# Welcome to Tonto National Forest

The Tonto National Forest spans 2.9 million acres of rugged, beautiful Arizona, offering unique landscapes including Sonoran Desert Saguaro cacti and flat lands that slowly give way to the highlands of the Mogollon Rim. This variety in vegetation and range in altitude— from 1,300 to 7,900 feet— offers outstanding recreational opportunities throughout the year, whether it's lake beaches or cool pine forests.
