# Posey Lake Campground

This campground is adjacent to Posey Lake, a small fishing lake. Posey Lake has a boat ramp for non-motorized boating and two docks. There are two hiking trails that start in the campground. Access to the Great Western Trail from the campground allows hiking, biking, and horse riding.

## General Information

**Facilities**

- [Posey Lake Campground map](https://www.fs.usda.gov/media/238189)
- 21 campsites with picnic tables, fire rings/grills, and bear box
- 1 group site (up to 35 people) with picnic tables, fire ring/grill, and bear proof storage box
- Drinking water (Memorial Day - Labor Day)
- Vault toilets
- No garbage disposal, please pack it out

### Seasons of Use

Late May - Early September

### Operational Hours

Open 24 hours/day, Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. until 6:00 a.m., no generators during that time, open late spring to late fall. Check in: 2:00 p.m.; Check-out: 11:00 a.m.

### Restrictions

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfires in developed fire pits only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Dogs must be on leash
- ATV’s and non-street legal UTVs are allowed at the day use area on the south end of the lake and at campsites 2, 3, and 4. Street legal UTVs are allowed throughout the campground but must stay on roads and spurs.
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $16 per night

Group Site: $65 per night (1-35 people)

- for current pricing and availability, visit [**www.recreation.gov**](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/231916?tab=seasons)

### Pet Information

Dogs must be on leash.

### Contact Information

**Contact Name:** Escalante Interagency Visitor Center

**Contact Phone:** [(435) 826-5499](tel:(435) 826-5499)

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 37.934839

**Longitude:** -111.694129

### Directions

From Escalante, turn north on 300 East. At 0.7 miles, the road will fork, stay right.  After 3.5 miles the road will turn to dirt and is now called Forest Road 153 (FR 153). At 13.6 miles the road will fork, turn left on FR 154 and follow signs to Posey Lake Campground.

Posey Lake is within walking distance from the campground. There is a boat launch and two floating docks.  The lake is fairly small but being out on a boat is ideal for fishing and bird watching.
