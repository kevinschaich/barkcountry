## Forest Order

**UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE**

**UNITED STATES FOREST SERVICE**

**NATIONAL FOREST OF NORTH CAROLINA**

**Croatan National Forest**

**Order Number 08-11-03-26-01**

Pursuant to 36 CFR 261.50 (a) and (b), for the protection of public health and safety, the following acts are prohibited due to Hurricane Florence recovery activities on the Croatan National Forest. These prohibitions are effective until May 1, 2029, or until rescinded by an authorized Forest Service official, whichever occurs first.

1. Being on Neusiok Trail from Pinecliff Recreation Area Trailhead to posted Pinecliff Equestrian Trail detour. 36 CFR 261.55(a)
2. Going into or being upon the following areas:


   - Fishers Landing Recreation Area
   - Neuse River Recreation Sites (Flanners Beach)
     - Flanners Beach Picnic Area
     - Flanners Beach Campground
   - Pinecliff Recreation Area
   - Siddie Fields Recreation Area

36 CFR 261.53(e and f), 36 CFR 261.52(e)

3. Being on the National Forest System roads (50-feet each direction from the centerline of each road):


   - FSR 132—Pinecliff Road at MM0.67
   - FSR 141—Fishers Landing Road
   - FSR 167—Siddie Fields Road

36 CFR 261.54 (e)

Pursuant to 36 CFR, Section 261.50(e), the following persons are exempt from this Order:

1. Persons with a special use permit or contract specifically authorizing the otherwise prohibited act or omission.
2. Any Federal, State, or local officer, or member of an organized rescue or fire fighting force in the performance of their official duty.

Executed in Asheville, North Carolina, this 22nd day of June, 2026.

/s/

James E. Melonas

Forest Supervisor

National Forests in North Carolina

A violation of these prohibitions is punishable by a fine of not more than $5,000 for an individual or $10,000 for an organization, or imprisonment for not more than 6 months, or both. 16 USC 551 and 18 USC 3559 and 3571.
