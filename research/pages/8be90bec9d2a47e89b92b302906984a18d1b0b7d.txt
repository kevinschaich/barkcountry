# Traveling with Pets

|     |
| --- |
| ![dog in a kennel](https://www.nps.gov/cave/planyourvisit/images/CCTC-kennel-183x161_1.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)In the concession-run kennel, only water is provided, but you may bring in bedding for your pet's comfort. <br>NPS<br>Be aware that desert temperatures, thorny vegetation, and predators may be a serious threat to your pet's well-being.<br>Please consider the following points before deciding to bring a pet:<br>- Pets are not allowed in the cavern, on unpaved park trails on the surface, off road, during the Bat Flight Program, or in the visitor center.<br>  <br>- Pets need to be on a leash no longer than six feet in length when outside your vehicle.<br>  <br>- **You are not allowed to leave your pet unattended in vehicles**. They must be kenneled; more information regarding kenneling below. <br>  <br>- Pet etiquette and park regulations require that you always clean up after your pet and dispose of waste in trash receptacles.<br>  <br>#### Kennel Information<br>The park concessioner, Carlsbad Caverns Trading Company, operates a kennel service where you may leave your pet in a temperature controlled environment while you tour the cavern. The kennel is for day use only—no overnight stays. <br>**Fee:** $15/day<br>**Vaccine Requirements**<br>- **For dogs:** Rabies, Distemper, Bordetella (kennel cough), and Canine Influenza.<br>- **For cats:** Rabies, Distemper, and Panleukopenia. <br>- Owners are required to show a copy of the pet's vaccination record with the expiration date when boarding a pet. No exceptions. **_Collar_** _**tags** don't meet this requirement and won't be accepted._<br>- **_Please be prepared with pet vaccine information when checking in._**<br>**Operating Hours:** 9:00 am–3:30 pm<br>**Last Time to Drop-Off Pet:** 2:15 pm<br>**Last Time to Pick-Up Pet:** 3:30 pm <br>Reservations are not accepted. This service is provided on a first-come, first-served basis. <br>They rarely run out of cage space, but if they do, the larger cages go first. There are three sizes of cages, length x height x width:<br>49" x 37" x 44"<br>47" x 24" x 32"<br>47" x 24" x 24"<br>Pets are kept in cages relative to their size, such as a small dog in a small cage. Water is provided for the pet, but the owner must provide food or bedding if they feel it's needed.<br>The concessioner accepts no responsibility for injuries that might occur during a pet's stay in the kennels.<br>Employees do not handle pets and do not remain in the kennel room with the pets.<br>If you have more specific questions, contact the Carlsbad Caverns Trading Company at 575-785-2281. |
