![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nt/woodbuffalo/2024-changes/01_11-nodrone/No-Drone_nobkgd.png)
Using recreational drones in Wood Buffalo National Park is prohibited. Operating a drone within park boundaries may face law enforcement action and a fine of up to $25,000.

Spanning the Alberta-Northwest Territories border, the largest national park in Canada protects a large swath of the Northern Boreal Plains ecosystem, the largest herds of wood bison in the world, vital whooping crane habitat, extraordinary examples of karst and caves, and the Peace-Athabasca Delta. This is the traditional territory of the Dene, Cree, and Métis and is home to many spectacular species, including whooping cranes, bison, wolves, and more.

### [Discover the world’s largest Dark Sky Preserve](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/woodbuffalo/activ/star)

The sky gets plenty dark in the North through fall, winter and spring until, that is, the Milky Way spills across the sky like a starry river. If you’re lucky, the Northern Lights will light up the night sky.

### [Nahanni National Park Reserve](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/nahanni)

Remote granite pinnacles lure top alpinists, wilderness river tripping opportunities attract paddlers, interpreters share cultural and natural history with river trippers, campers and day flight visitors.

### [Nááts'įhch'oh National Park Reserve](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/naatsihchoh)

In the headwaters of Tehjeh Deé (South Nahanni River) Nááts'įhch'oh National Park Reserve is a place where culture and nature are intertwined. Nááts'įhch'oh offers whitewater paddling and off-the-grid hiking in the Northwest Territories for experienced adventurers.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nwt/thaidene-nene-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220102)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/thaidene-nene)

### [Thaidene Nëné National Park Reserve](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/thaidene-nene)

Located at the eastern end of Great Slave Lake in the Northwest Territories, Thaidene Nëné National Park Reserve is part of a larger group of protected areas around the East Arm and Artillery Lake regions. _Thaidene Nëné_ means ‘Land of the Ancestors’ in the Dënesųłiné—or Chipewyan—language.
