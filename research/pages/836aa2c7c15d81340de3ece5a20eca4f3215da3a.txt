## More places to discover with Parks Canada

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ns/agb-site-640x360-2022.jpg?modified=20221126220531)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/grahambell)

### [Alexander Graham Bell National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/grahambell)

Meet the man who would forever transform the modern world. Feel Alexander Graham Bell’s remarkable legend come to life while touring full-scale replicas, touching his personal artefacts and taking part in exhilarating behind-the-scenes tours.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ns/fol-site-640x360-2022.jpg?modified=20221126220531)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/louisbourg)

### [Fortress of Louisbourg National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/louisbourg)

Mingle with Louisbourg’s lively 18th century citizens, hearing their stories and tasting their food while soldiers march, cannons blast, children play and women mend clothes on a typical 1740s day in the French fortified town.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ns/marconi-site-640x360-2022.jpg?modified=20221126220531)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/marconi)

### [Marconi National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/marconi)

Discover how the wireless age began at a museum chronicling inventor Guglielmo Marconi’s achievements in carrying out the world’s first transatlantic wireless telegraph experiments in Nova Scotia at the turn of 20th century.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ns/spc-site-640x360-2022.jpg?modified=20221126220533)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/stpeters)

### [St. Peters Canal National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/stpeters)

Located in one of Canada’s oldest European settlements, St. Peters Canal links the Atlantic Ocean with the Bras d'Or Lakes as well as Canada’s historic trading past to Cape Breton Island’s vibrant future.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ns/canso-site-640x360-2022.jpg?modified=20221126220531)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/canso)

### [Canso Islands National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/canso)

Wander an interpretive trail to unveil vital history. Uncover the secrets of an 18th century settlement. Be amazed by the war-torn remains of a stone fortress. Canso Islands National Historic Site brings to life the stories of Atlantic Canada.
