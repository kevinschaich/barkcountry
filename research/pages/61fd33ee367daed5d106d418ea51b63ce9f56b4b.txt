## Forest Order

### **UNCOMPAHGRE NATIONAL FOREST**

### **OURAY & GUNNISON RANGER DISTRICT**

Pursuant to 16 U.S.C. § 551 and 36 C.F.R. § 261.50(a) and § 261.50 (b), the following acts are prohibited within the Uncompahgre Wilderness of the Gunnison and Ouray Ranger Districts of the Grand Mesa, Uncompahgre and Gunnison National Forests, Hinsdale, Ouray and Gunnison Counties, State of Colorado (the “Restricted Area”) and on and on National Forest System (NFS) Trails located within the Restricted Area (the “Restricted Trails”).

The Restricted Area and the Restricted Trails are depicted on the attached map, hereby incorporated into this Order as Exhibit A.

This Order is necessary to protect physical and biological resources of the Uncompahgre Wilderness, and to protect the human experience of Wilderness character, solitude, and the untrammeled condition of the landscape.

### PROHIBITIONS

1. Entering or being in the Restricted Area with a group size of more than 15 people. A combination of stock and people shall not exceed 25 per group, with the maximum amount of people per combined group limited to 15. 36 CFR § 261.58(f)
2. Camping within 100 feet of any NFS trail or any lake, stream, pond, river, or other similar body of water. 36 CFR § 261.58(e)
3. Building, maintaining, attending or using a fire, campfire, or wood-burning stove:
1. Within 100 feet of any NFS trail or any lake, stream, pond, river, or other similar water body; or
2. Above tree line. 36 CFR § 261.52(a)
4. Storing equipment, personal property, or supplies. 36 CFR § 261.57(f)
5. Hitching, tethering, hobbling or fencing a horse or other saddle or pack animal in violation of posted instructions. Instructions to include, but may not be limited to:
1. Prohibiting hitching, tethering, hobbling, or fencing within 100 feet of any NFS Trail or any lake, stream, pond, river, or similar water body; or
2. Prohibiting tying directly to trees except during loading and unloading. 36 CFR § 261.58(aa)
6. Possessing a dog that is: (i) off leash; (ii) not under direct verbal control of the dog’s owner or handler at all times; or (ii) disturbing or damaging wildlife, other animals, people or property. 36 CFR. § 261.58(s)
7. Shortcutting a switchback in a trail. 36 CFR § 261.55(e)
8. Possessing or using a wagon, cart, or other vehicle, including a wheelbarrow, bicycle or game cart. 36 C.F.R. §261.57(h).
9. Possessing, storing, or transporting any plant material, such as hay or straw, unless it is certified weed free or has been chemically or mechanically processed to destroy viable seeds (e.g., pelletized feed and rolled grains)36 C.F.R. §261.58(t).

### EXEMPTIONS

Pursuant to 36 C.F.R. § 261.50(e), the following persons are exempt from this Order:

1. Persons with Forest Service Permit No. FS-7700-48 (Permit for Use of Roads, Trails, or Areas Restricted by Regulation or Order), specifically exempting them from this Order or a written determination by a Forest Service authorized officer that a permit is not required under 36 C.F.R. § 251.50(e)(1) or (2).
2. Any Federal, State, or Local Officer, or member of an organized rescue or firefighting force in the performance of an official duty.

### DESCRIBED AREA, ROADS AND TRAILS

The Described Area is all NFS lands within the boundary of the Uncompahgre Wilderness (as shown in Exhibit A) located in Hinsdale, Gunnison, and Ouray Counties, Colorado, in the Ouray and Gunnison Ranger Districts of the Grand Mesa, Uncompahgre and Gunnison National Forests.

### PURPOSE

The purpose of this Order is to protect natural resources and wilderness character.

### IMPLEMENTATION

1. This Order shall be in effect from May 14, 2026 at 0900 through May 14, 2029 at

0900, unless rescinded.

2. A map identifying the Described Area is attached and made part of this Order as Exhibit A. For a digital version of this Order and Exhibit A, go to [https://www.fs.usda.gov/alerts/gmug/alerts-notices](https://www.fs.usda.gov/alerts/gmug/alerts-notices).
3. A violation of the above prohibitions is punishable as a Class B misdemeanor by a fine of not more than $5,000 for individuals and $10,000 for organizations, or by imprisonment for not more than six months, or both. 16 U.S.C. § 551; 18 U.S.C.

§§ 3559, 3571, and 3581.

4. Contact the Ouray Ranger District in Montrose, Colorado, (970) 240-5300, the Gunnison Ranger District in Gunnison, Colorado, (970) 641-0471 and Grand Mesa, Uncompahgre and Gunnison National Forest Supervisor’s Office in Delta, Colorado, (970) 874-6600, for more information about this Order.
5. This Order supersedes any previous Orders prohibiting the same or similar acts in the same Described Area.

Signed and issued on May 13, 2026.

Chad Stewart Forest Supervisor

Grand Mesa, Uncompahgre and Gunnison National Forests
