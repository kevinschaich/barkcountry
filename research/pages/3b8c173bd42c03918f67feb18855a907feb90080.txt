# Pactola Visitor Center

Current Conditions

Hours: 9:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Sunday, Monday, Friday, Saturday
Closed on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday

The Pactola Visitor Center serves as an educational and interpretive hub for the Black Hills National Forest.  It is open during the summer and early fall seasons and is often used by local school groups and youth organizations for a variety of field trips.

**What You Will Find**

- Staff available to answer questions and provide helpful publications
- Exhibits and displays highlighting natural and cultural resources of western South Dakota
- Seasonal programs and special events at the visitor center, observation decks, and other Forest sites
- For details on programs and youth activities, please contact the visitor center at (605) 343-8755

**Black Hills Parks and Forests Association Store**

Inside the visitor center, the [Black Hills Parks and Forests Association](http://www.blackhillsparks.org/) (BHPF) operates a store offering:

- Educational books and field guides
- Trail maps, posters, CDs, and DVDs
- Nature games, clothing, plush animals, and souvenirs

Purchases support the educational programs and conservation efforts of the Forest. For more information, please call (605) 745-7020.

Looking for more Smokey Bear products? Check out the [BHPF Online Store](https://blackhillsparks.org/product-category/smokey/).

**Contact Information**

For more information about the visitor center, please contact the Recreation Program at the Forest Supervisor’s Office by [email](http://www.fs.usda.gov/contactus/blackhills/about-forest/contactus) or (605) 673-9200.

## Actively Learn about the Forest

The Pactola Visitor Center is perhaps one of the most beautiful visitor centers in the Black Hills.  It is situated on a rocky outcropping that overlooks the Pactola Reservoir.  Many of its exhibits and displays date back to the early 70s; however, their interpretive information remains valid and provides visitors with a vintage educational experience.  Combined with multiple outdoor picnic tables and benches, as well as various family programs, this site offers guests a relaxing and engaging opportunity in the "heart" of the Black Hills.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

The Pactola Visitor Center normally opens for the season on the Friday of Memorial Day Weekend and closes on Labor Day.  If staffing is available, weekend opening dates are often available through September.

### Operational Hours

**Summer 2026 Hours**

- Sunday ~ 9:30 a.m. - 5 p.m.
- Monday ~ 9:30 a.m. - 5 p.m.
- Tuesday ~ Closed
- Wednesday ~ Closed
- Thursday ~ Closed
- Friday ~ 9:30 a.m. - 5 p.m.
- Saturday ~ 9:30 a.m. - 5 p.m.

### Passes and Permits

The Pactola Visitor Center does sell the annual Motorized Trails Permits through its cooperative partner - Black Hills Parks and Forests Association.

### Fee Site and Info

There are no fees or passes required to visit the Pactola Visitor Center.

### Pet Information

Only service animals are allowed within the Pactola Visitor Center.  However, small pets are allowed on the grounds of the facility, as long as they are on a leash (and under control) at all times.  Waste bags are also available in a nearby pet exercise area.

## Getting There

### Directions

Pactola Visitor Center

Black Hills National Forest

22995 Hwy 385

Rapid City, SD 57702

Phone: (605) 343-8755

From Hill City, South Dakota, travel north on Hwy 385 for about 21 miles.  The visitor center is located on the west side of the highway, overlooking the Pactola Reservoir.

### Travel Considerations

In 2026, Hwy 385 south of the Pactola Visitor Center is under construction.  Visitors may experience delays at sporadic times during the summer and early fall seasons.  This project is being managed by the South Dakota Department of Transportation (DOT).

### Parking

A paved parking lot provides easy access by passenger vehicles, along with extended parking sites for large campers, boat trailers, and buses.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

The restrooms are only available when the Pactola Visitor Center is open and available to the public.

### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

Drinking water is only available in the nearby restrooms.  Please be advised that drinking water is also available inside the facility through bottled water being sold by the official partner of the Forest - [Black Hills Parks and Forests Association](https://blackhillsparks.org/).

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

Multiple picnic tables are available on the grounds of the Pactola Visitor Center.  Some are located in the front garden area of the facility, while two tables and some benches are available on a platform that overlooks the Pactola Reservoir.
