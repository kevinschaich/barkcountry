# Plan your visit

## Gwaii Haanas National Park Reserve, National Marine Conservation Area Reserve, and Haida Heritage Site

##### How to Pronounce Gwaii Haanas in Xaayda Kil

This translates to Islands of Beauty

Gwaii Haanas is a protected area on the southern half of Haida Gwaii, approximately 100 kilometres off the northern coast of British Columbia. Gwaii Haanas has no roads and is accessible only by boat or seaplane.

Most visitors explore this area with one of our licensed [tour operators](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/gwaiihaanas/visit/voyagistes-operators). Guided trips range from one-day zodiac adventures to multi-day kayak, sail, or motorboat excursions. Tours tend to book well in advance, so planning is key.

A smaller percentage of our visitors explore Gwaii Haanas independently. Independent visitors require a [reservation](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/gwaiihaanas/visit/tarifs-fees) and must participate in a visitor [orientation](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/gwaiihaanas/visit/orientations-orientations) prior to entering Gwaii Haanas.

### Directions

Gwaii Haanas can only be reached by sea or by air. Generally, small craft travel the inside waters of Carmichael Passage and Dana Passage from Moresby Camp to Gwaii Haanas. It takes about two days, weather permitting, to travel by kayak from Moresby Camp to the northern border of Gwaii Haanas. A popular alternative is transportation by a licensed [tour operator](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/gwaiihaanas/visit/voyagistes-operators).
