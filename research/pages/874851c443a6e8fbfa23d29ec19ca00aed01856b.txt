# The Pines Campground

The Pines is a rustic campground in a wooded setting, located adjacent to Barbour's Creek, a stocked trout stream. Most of the campsites are designed for tents, but small recreation vehicles can be accommodated. Jeep enthusiasts can experience some of the most challenging 4-wheeling in Virginia at nearby Potts Mountain Jeep Road.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

April 1 - December 7

### Operational Hours

Open April 1 - November 1, Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. until 6:00 a.m.

### Restrictions

When recreating on Forest Service Lands, follow the safety and outdoor ethics recommendations.

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

### Pet Information

Please, keep your pet on a leash when in a recreation area or campground. The law requires dog owners to keep their dog on a leash with a length no greater than 6 feet and under physical control when within a recreational area, such as a campground or day use area (36 CFR 2616(j)).

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 37.604984

**Longitude:** -80.0766742

### Directions

From New Castle, VA: Follow SR 311 north for approximately 4 1/2 miles. Turn right on SR 611. Follow it for 5 miles and turn left on SR 617. Travel 5.5 miles to the campground entrance on left. Driving distance from New Castle is about 15 miles; estimated time is 30 minutes.

From Covington, VA: Take SR 18 to SR 615. Turn left and follow to SR 617; campground is on right.
