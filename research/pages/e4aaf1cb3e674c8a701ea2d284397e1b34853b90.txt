## Forest Order

**UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE**

**UNITED STATES FOREST SERVICE**

**NATIONAL FOREST OF NORTH CAROLINA**

**UWHARRIE NATIONAL FORESTS**

**ORDER NO. 08-11-10-26-01**

Pursuant to 36 CFR 261.50 (a) and (b), the following acts are prohibited within the Uwharrie National Forest in caves and mines within the area shown on the attached map. This closure is effective from the date signed by the Forest Supervisor until December 31, 2035 or until terminated by an authorized Forest Service official, whichever occurs first.

1. Entering any cave or abandoned manmade underground structure, commonly referred to as a

"mine." 36 CFR 261.53(a) (b) &(e)—for the protection of threatened, endangered, rare, unique,

or vanishing species. Protection of special biological species. And for public health and safety.

The term "cave" means any naturally occurring void, cavity, recess, or system of interconnected passages which occurs beneath the surface of the earth or within a cliff or ledge (including any cave resource therein, but not including any vug, mine, tunnel, aqueduct, or other manmade excavation) and which is large enough to permit an individual to enter, whether or not the entrance is naturally formed or manmade. Such term shall include any natural pit, sinkhole, or other feature which is an extension of the entrance (Federal Cave Resources Protection Act, 16 USC § 4302(1)).

The above prohibition applies to areas shown on attached map within the Uwharrie National Forest.

Pursuant to 36 CFR 261.50(e) the following persons are exempt from the above prohibitions:

1. Any persons with written authorization or permit by a District Ranger or Forest Supervisor specifically authorizing an otherwise prohibited act or omission. Authorization or permit must be on person at all times while within closure area.
2. Any Federal, State, or local Forest Officer, or member of an organized rescue or fire fighting force in the performance of their official duty.
3. Persons engaged in any of the following activities which have been authorized in writing by a permit, contract, or agreement:

a. Mineral use, 36 C.F.R. Part 228 (36 C.F.R. §§ 228.1—228.116);


Executed in North Carolina this 24th day of August, 2026.

/s/

James Melonas

Forest Supervisor

National Forests in

North Carolina

A violation of these prohibitions is punishable by a fine of not more than $5,000 for an individual or $10,000 for an organization, or imprisonment for not more than 6 months, or both. 16 USC 551 and 18 USC 3559, 3571, and 3581.
