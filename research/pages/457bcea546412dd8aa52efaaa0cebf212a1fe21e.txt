# Homochitto

There are numerous recreational activities available to pursue on the Homochitto National Forest. Homochitto, meaning “big red”, is the local Indian name for the river. Homochitto National Forest is rich in wildlife, offering deer, turkey and small game hunting, including  Wildlife Management Areas-Caston Creek and Sandy Creek. Traditional activities like hunting, fishing, camping and hiking abound. Or you may decide to mountain bike some of the South's finest trails. Follow the links below and chose your favorite activity.

## General Information

Homochitto National Forest is rich in wildlife, offering deer, turkey and small game hunting, including two Wildlife Management Areas.
