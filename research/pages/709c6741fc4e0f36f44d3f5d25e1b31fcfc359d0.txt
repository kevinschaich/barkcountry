# Charity Valley East Trailhead

The Charity Valley East Trailhead is located approximately 3 miles west of Markleeville, California off Hot Springs Road, about a mile before you reach Grover Hot Spring State Park. Provides access to trails including the Charity Valley Trail which connects to Blue Lakes Road to the west.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

May - October, as weather and conditions allow

### Operational Hours

Open 24 hours/day.

### Restrictions

- Dogs must be on a leash at trailhead.

### Passes and Permits

- A free [California Camp Fire Permit](https://readyforwildfire.org/permits/) issued by CalFire is required for campfire, barbecue, and portable stove use.
