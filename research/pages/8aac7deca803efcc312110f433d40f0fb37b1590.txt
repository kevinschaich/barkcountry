For your safety, please stick to designated park trails. <br>NPS Photo<br>### DANGER: Exit Glacier Canyon Risk of Outburst Flood<br>A closure for safety is in place from the toe of Exit Glacier to the end of the canyon where Exit Creek opens into the Outwash Plain. The canyon is susceptible to unpredictable glacier outburst flooding events which can produce sudden surges of water combined with large ice chunks, rock, and other debris.<br>For your safety please stick to designated park trails. Designated trails do not go through any outburst flood susceptible areas.<br>Because glacier outbursts are caused by the hydrology within the glacier, not prevailing weather conditions, a glacier outburst can happen at any time. Visitors accessing the Outwash Plain should be aware that water levels may rise quickly. Outburst flood waters often carry large amounts of ice, rock, and other debris. The entire Outwash Plain is susceptible to flooding. Please be aware of your surroundings and seek high ground immediately if water levels begin to rise. <br>![A young brown bear walks by in the distance as two people raise their arms in the foreground](https://www.nps.gov/kefj/planyourvisit/images/KEFJ_2011_Miscellaneous_0545.JPG?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Always be bear aware! <br>NPS Photo<br>### TRAIL SAFETY: [Be Bear Aware](https://www.nps.gov/kefj/planyourvisit/bear-safety.htm)<br>- Carry bear spray<br>- Talk loudly<br>- Hike in groups<br>- Do not surprise a bear<br>- Dogs and other [pets are prohibited on trails](https://www.nps.gov/kefj/planyourvisit/pets.htm) at all times<br>- Dogs and other pets may add to the stress of bears or other wildlife<br>**If you see a bear:**<br>- Back away<br>- Give it lots of space<br>- Don't get between a sow and cubs<br>- Plan to wait if there is a bear near the trail - You are no longer in a hurry!<br>![Orange cones line the side of a road.](https://www.nps.gov/kefj/planyourvisit/images/IMG_2569-2.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Please be respectful when parking. Hiking poles can be helpful for balance on steep sections of trail. If present, follow orange flags that mark where the trail is.
