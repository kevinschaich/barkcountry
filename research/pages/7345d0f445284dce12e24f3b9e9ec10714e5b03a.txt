# Roubaix Lake

Roubaix Lake is a small lake located northwest of Rapid City and South of Deadwood, just west of US Highway 385. The lake provides opportunities for swimming, fishing and ice skating.

See the [Roubaix Lake Campground webpage](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/recreation/roubaix-lake-campground) for camping information.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Available year round ... with limited services in the shoulder seasons.

### Fee Site and Info

See day-use fee information

### Pet Information

Dogs in developed recreation sites, such as campgrounds, picnic sites, and day-use areas, must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet at all times. Furthermore, dogs are not allowed on any swim beaches on the Forest.  For the safety and comfort of fellow recreationalists, the Forest encourages guests to avoid leaving pets unattended at campsites and/or in parked vehicles.

## Getting There

### Directions

To access Roubaix Lake Day-Use Area from Deadwood, South Dakota, travel south on US Highway 385 for approximately thirteen miles. Turn right (west) onto FSR 255 and travel about one mile to the campground / day-use area entrance.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

A toilet facility is located near the swim beach at the day-use site.

NOTE: Toilet facilities are also available in nearby Roubaix Lake Campground and can be used by recreation day-users. However, guests may experience limited services from approximately October 1 through May 15 (shoulder season).

### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

Water is available in nearby Roubaix Lake Campground and can be used by hikers.

Please be advised that water is not available from approximately October 1 through May 15 (shoulder season).  Depending on weather conditions, water availability may change in the spring and fall seasons, due to cold temperatures and the potential for freezing waterlines.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

Multiple picnic tables are available at the day-use site and can be used on a first come first served basis.
