[Lodging](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/lodging.htm)

![Cedar Grove Lodge. Photo by Kiel Maddox.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/seki/crop16_9/B79D1F8C-1DD8-B71B-0B50A5DDE5553999.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

![A park map](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/seki/crop16_9/FF26A1BB-1DD8-B71B-0BDE88CF849ABCA6.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

[Bears and Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/bear_bc.htm)

Important information about bears and food storage.

[General Safety](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/safety.htm)

![Western Pacific Rattlesnake curled in pine needles at base of charred tree.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/seki/crop16_9/3E79B331-FA30-93CD-06C922693AAB5296.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Whether you trek into wilderness or stay close to your car, review tips on staying safe in these parks.

[Pets](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/pets.htm)

Wondering if you should bring your four-legged friend on vacation? Read more here...

[Accessibility](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/accessibility.htm)

![An elderly man standing up from a wheelchair next to a ranger.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/seki/crop16_9/9101A19C-C6D9-5992-78DAD84B7CF6680E.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

We offer recreational opportunities suited for a wide range of abilities, including exhibits, walking, camping, and shuttle transportation.

[Nature](https://www.nps.gov/seki/learn/nature/index.htm)

![A bobcat sits among dry shrubs](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/seki/crop16_9/9BBC78A5-1DD8-B71B-0BC2E2EC4062855C.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)
