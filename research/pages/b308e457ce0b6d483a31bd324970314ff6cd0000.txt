# Prince Albert National Park

Located in central Saskatchewan, Prince Albert National Park showcases the amazing ecological diversity found in the transition between prairie fescue grasslands and the lakes and wetlands of the mixed wood boreal forest.

[![A man cleans his boat at the Waskesiu Marina beside Waskesiu Lake in Prince Albert National Park.](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/sk/princealbert/WET4/2024/ais/ais-640-24.jpg?modified=20240517210251)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/princealbert/nature/conservation/aquatiques_aquatic)

### [Aquatic invasive species](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/princealbert/nature/conservation/aquatiques_aquatic)

All watercraft must have a mandatory aquatic invasive species (AIS) watercraft certification permit before entering Prince Albert National Park waters to help stop the spread of AIS.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/sk/sk-grasslands-640.jpg?modified=20221126215444)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands)

### [Grasslands National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands)

At Grasslands National Park, expanses of dinosaur fossils harken back to a time before history. Tipi rings are testament to First Nations communities, and ruins of prairie homesteads tell of settlers intent on taming the prairie.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ab/ab-elk-island-pn-np-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126221034)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/elkisland)

### [Elk Island National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/elkisland)

Experience first-hand the story of the bison and how it was saved from near extinction at Elk Island National Park, where an active conservation program replenishes herds around the world.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/sk/sk-batoche-640.jpg?modified=20221126215443)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/sk/batoche)

### [Batoche National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/sk/batoche)

Experience life as a Métis settler in the village community of Batoche and discover the history of the last battle of the Northwest Rebellion/Resistance of 1885, scene of Louis Riel’s last stand.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/mb/pnmr-rmnp-2-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220835)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/mb/riding)

### [Riding Mountain National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/mb/riding)

Explore 3000 km2 of thrilling outdoor possibilities in Riding Mountain National Park where the boreal forest, aspen parkland and fescue prairie meet.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/sk/sk-walsh-640.jpg?modified=20221126215444)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/sk/walsh)

### [Fort Walsh National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/sk/walsh)

Experience life with James Morrow Walsh and his NWMP troops in the 1870s. Barter for buffalo robes at the trading post, smell sweet lodge pole pines on a hike through the Cypress Hills.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/sk/sk-motherwell-640.jpg?modified=20221126215444)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/sk/motherwell)

### [Motherwell Homestead National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/sk/motherwell)

Hear prairie songbirds sing and smell the sweet hay as you help feed the horses. Re-connect with Canada’s homestead life and bake bread with flour ground from grain harvested from the golden fields outside.
