## Forest Order

**Order Number: 02-02-03-25-01**

**U.S. Department of Agriculture, U.S. Forest Service**

**Bighorn National Forest**

**Medicine Wheel Ranger District**

**Medicine Wheel Area Closure**

Pursuant to 16 United States Code (U.S.C.) § 551, and 36 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) § 261.50(b), the following are prohibited on National Forest System (NFS) lands in the Medicine Wheel Ranger District, Bighorn National Forest described below and shown on the attached map incorporated into this Order as Exhibit A and B (the “Described Area”):

**PROHIBITIONS**

1. Camping within the Medicine Wheel Recreation Area (Exhibit A). 36 CFR 261.58(e)
2. Possessing or using a motor vehicle off National Forest System roads within the Medicine Wheel Recreation Area (Exhibit A). 36 CFR 261.56
3. Building, maintaining, attending or using a fire, campfire, or stove fire within the Medicine Wheel Recreation Area (Exhibit A). 36 CFR 261.52(a)
4. Going into or being upon the fenced enclosure of the Medicine Wheel Area (Exhibit B). 36 CFR 261.53(c)
5. Bringing into or possessing an animal in the Medicine Wheel Recreation Area (Exhibit A). 36 CFR 261.58(s)
6. Bringing into or possessing an animal in the fenced enclosure of the Medicine Wheel Area or on the footpath immediately outside the rope fence that surrounds the Medicine Wheel Area (Exhibit B). The legal location is T56N, R92W, Section 16. 36 CFR 261.58(s)
7. Parking or leaving a vehicle in violation of posted instructions in the Medicine Wheel Recreation Area (Exhibit A). 36 CFR 261.58(g)

**EXEMPTIONS**

Pursuant to 36 C.F.R. § 261.50(e), the following persons are exempt from this Order:

1. Persons with a Forest Service permit specifically authorizing the otherwise prohibited act or omission.
2. Any Federal, State, or local officer, or member of an organized rescue or firefighting force, when involved in the performance of an official duty.
3. Persons who are traditional Indian religious practitioners or traditional Indian peoples, meaning those Native Americans who practice a religion whose origin and interpretation are from within a traditional American Indian culture or community are exempt from Prohibitions 1, 3, 4, and 7.
4. Persons using a guide or service animal or persons bringing in or possessing an animal upon a leash that is not longer than six feet are exempt from Prohibition 5.
5. Persons using a guide or service animal are exempt from bringing a animal onto the footpath immediately outside the rope fence that surrounds the Medicine Wheel Area but are subject to the rest of Prohibition 6.

**DESCRIBED AREAS**

The Medicine Wheel Recreation Area encompasses the parking lot and kiosk along Forest System Road 12 northwest to the fence line past the Medicine Wheel site as shown in Exhibit A.

The Medicine Wheel Area includes the actual Medicine Wheel including the trail around the site on the outside of the fence as shown in Exhibit B.

**PURPOSE**

The purpose of this Order is to protect the cultural resources in the Medicine Wheel National Historic Landmark according to the Medicine Wheel Historic Preservation Plan.

**IMPLEMENTATION**

1. This Order shall be in effect from June 23, 2025 at 12:00 a.m. through December 31, 2030 at 11:59 p.m., unless rescinded.
2. Maps identifying the Described Area is attached and made part of this Order as Exhibits A and B. For a digital version of this Order and Exhibits, go to [https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/bighorn/alerts](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/bighorn/alerts)
3. Unless otherwise expressly defined in this Order, the terms used in this Order are defined by the regulations of the Secretary of Agriculture, 36 C.F.R. Chapter II, Parts 200-299. If there are terms in this Order that are not expressly defined in the Order or 36 C.F.R. Chapter II, Parts 200-299, their meaning shall be determined by their plain language definitions.
4. A violation of the above prohibitions is punishable as a Class B misdemeanor by a fine of not more than $5,000 for individuals and $10,000 for organizations, or by imprisonment for not more than six months, or both. 16 U.S.C. § 551; 18 U.S.C. §§ 3559, 3571, and 3581.
5. Contact the Bighorn National Forest Supervisor’s Office in Sheridan, Wyoming, (307) 674-2600, for more information about this Order.
6. This Order supersedes any previous Orders prohibiting the same or similar acts in the same Described Area.

Signed and issued on June 23, 2025.
