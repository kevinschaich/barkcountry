Lake Clark preserves the ancestral homelands of the Dena'ina people, an intact ecosystem at the headwaters of the largest sockeye salmon fishery in the world, and a rich cultural wilderness.

[A Day on the Lake Clark Coastline](https://www.nps.gov/lacl/planyourvisit/bear-viewing.htm)

![Two-toned water meets an uneven shore with rivers disappearing into forest and up into mountains.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/FD8840D5-CC44-69F1-C09CF5777CB1A772.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Watch the park's coastal orientation film. Learn more about bear viewing.

[A Trip to Crescent Lake](https://www.nps.gov/lacl/planyourvisit/crescent-lake.htm)

![A bear stands at the edge of a pebble beach facing a boat of fishermen in front of mountains.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/FDE63DF4-C9D9-62BD-C0542D217488301A.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

The park's most visited destination is deep in the wild. Here's how to prepare for a safe trip to this remote and scenic location.

[A Visit to Twin Lakes](https://www.nps.gov/lacl/planyourvisit/visit-proenneke-cabin.htm)

![Calm blue water reflects a log cabin with a cache and American Flag surrounded by coniferous trees.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/FE1D5A46-AEE3-124B-A981DC73AD8054FD.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Plan your trip to Twin Lakes and Proenneke Cabin.

[Start Your Adventure](https://www.nps.gov/lacl/planyourvisit/directions.htm)

![A float plane flying in air. ](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/B26CC275-1DD8-B71B-0BC6DBA49F12B838.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

There are no roads here. Traveling to the park requires a plane or a boat. Here's how to start your adventure.

[Backpacking in Alaska](https://www.nps.gov/articles/backpackingak.htm)

![A hiker with vibrant green backpack walks on autumnal tundra toward the hills.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/FE433DD1-082A-118E-6A8F26CF500A1BF5.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Get acquainted with what makes backpacking in Alaska unique and familiarize yourself with these backcountry tips before you plan your trip

[Hunting](https://www.nps.gov/lacl/planyourvisit/hunting.htm)

![A man with a rifle sits on a steep, rocky slope](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/F9B41AC6-1DD8-B71B-0BBA9775C65E98DF.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Sport hunting and trapping are permitted in the national preserve. State of Alaska rules and regulations apply.

[Subsistence is More than Sustenance](https://www.nps.gov/lacl/learn/historyculture/subsistence.htm)

![Rows of drying salmon fillets hang from wooden rods in smoke house.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/FE9CC87C-FE73-FCE7-CE07AF8148911471.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Subsistence, and all it entails, is critical to sustaining both the physical and spiritual culture of modern Alaskans.

[Connected to the Land](https://www.nps.gov/lacl/learn/historyculture/denainaculture.htm)

![A group of people of all ages pose in front of a camp fire with water and mountains behind them.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/FFD09DB1-E182-5738-8AFC1497D377E795.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Qizhjeh Vena/Lake Clark is the ancestral homelands for the Dena'ina Athabascan people. Discover more about people that call this area home.

[Monitoring Sockeye on the Newhalen River](https://www.nps.gov/lacl/learn/nature/monitoring-sockeye-salmon.htm)

![A person counts salmon atop a metal tower overlooking a wide river lined with trees.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/FE86202C-FC38-97DC-808EE66B13FCB170.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Each year the park monitors sockeye salmon returning to Lake Clark via the Newhalen River in order to understand salmon population trends.

[Lesson Plans for 6-12th Grade Teachers](https://www.nps.gov/lacl/learn/education/classrooms/curriculummaterials.htm)

![A person stands in a river looking through a research device with snowy mountains and clouds beyond.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/006A7A72-C906-AED4-0907A12E4067D791.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Bring Lake Clark to your classroom by using one of our curriculum-based lesson plans.

[Become a Junior Ranger!](https://www.nps.gov/lacl/learn/kidsyouth/junior-ranger.htm)

![A ranger stands in front of two children in green shirts, holding a Junior Ranger Booklet.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/lacl/crop16_9/005C5F1D-D254-E8D5-5AB93F0150327927.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

"Junior Ranger Journey" and "Where There Are Bears" are now available to print at home.
