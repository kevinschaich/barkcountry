![Landscape seen from Grand Canyon Lodge. Two massive reddish peaks at sunset.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/grca/crop16_9/F073CDFD-1DD8-B71B-0B173E16689B9D27.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

NO SERVICES. Campground, store and gas station are closed. Scenic roads closed for repair through November 3.

[Park Overview](https://www.nps.gov/grca/learn/management/statistics.htm)

![Jagged limestone pillars frame sunset-lit peaks in a vast canyon.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/grca/crop16_9/48C30444-DAFA-948B-61252F7BF069E069.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Fun facts about the geology, geography, climate, history, and the park's natural and cultural resources.

[Homeland of Tribal Communities](https://www.nps.gov/grca/learn/historyculture/associated-tribes.htm)

![graphic shows names of tribes around a circle with words "Tribes Call the Canyon Home".](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/grca/crop16_9/58DA334D-9C03-119B-2CF16D8970FC920E.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

11 tribes have cultural connections to the lands and resources now found within Grand Canyon National Park.

[Special Use Permits (SUP)](https://www.nps.gov/grca/getinvolved/sup.htm)

![Two holding hands during a wedding ceremony with the canyon in the background.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/grca/crop16_9/5D3E4D26-D612-EF1E-B1EFCE5920B8670F.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Special events held within the park like weddings, Rim-to-Rim extended day hikes, and large gatherings require special use permits.

[Filming and Photography Permits](https://www.nps.gov/grca/learn/management/filming.htm)

![A ranger is interviewed near the canyon rim with a reporter and a videographer. ](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/grca/crop16_9/2E108CB0-A818-D4BE-10FF1CB67A938A11.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

A special use permit is required for certain filming and still photography activities within the park.

[Commercial Use Authorization (CUA)](https://www.nps.gov/grca/getinvolved/cua.htm)

![Two large tour buses and two transit vans are parked in a loading zone.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/grca/crop16_9/B9CD5943-968A-1B6D-F459FC087BEFD371.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

CUAs are required for road-based tours, guided backcountry trips and all other commercial activities for visitors within the park.
