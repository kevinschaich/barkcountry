# Activities and experiences

## Forillon National Park

Forillon's mountain peninsula has plenty of activities and experiences to offer on sea and land: hiking, camping, wildlife watching, cruises, kayaking and more. Park lovers will learn a great deal about nature and culture through exhibitions and lively heritage sites.

Immerse yourself in the moment to the rhythm of nature!
