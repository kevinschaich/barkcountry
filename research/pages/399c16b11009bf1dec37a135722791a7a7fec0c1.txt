# Activities and experiences

## Quttinirpaaq National Park

Board a charter to Tanquary Fiord and explore Quttinirpaaq’s vast landscape on foot or on skis. Visit ancient cultural sites around Kettle Lake, walk along the 4500-year-old Muskox Way or embark on a multi-day trek around the Ad Astra and Viking ice caps.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nu/quttinirpaaq/WET4/index/randonnee-hike-640x360-2.jpg?modified=20231215210919)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/quttinirpaaq/activ/randonnees-hiking)

### [Hike the top of the world](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/quttinirpaaq/activ/randonnees-hiking)

Embark on a day hike or multi-day trek from Tanquary Fiord.

### [Spring ski touring](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/quttinirpaaq/activ/ski)
