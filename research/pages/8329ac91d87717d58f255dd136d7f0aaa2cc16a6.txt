# Up Up 40-Foot Lookout Tower

Current Conditions

Opens June 11, 2027.

NOTE: Phone and vehicle GPS mapping directions are not correct. Please use our directions below.

This lookout is a 40-foot wooden tower perched on a beautiful rock pinnacle. A hiking trail is available for those that wish to explore the Eagle Peak roadless area. The lookout has a fantastic vista for views of the St. Regis River drainage and the high alpine country.

View the site through the [interactive map](https://arcg.is/1D85uv1).

## General Information

Accommodations include propane lights and a wall-mounted heater. There are no cooking facilities or refrigeration. At the base of the tower is a fire ring and a picnic table. One twin bed with mattress and cover is available. Up to four people may occupy the tower. You can drive directly to the tower.

### Seasons of Use

Open June 12, 2026 - October 6, 2026.

### Operational Hours

Arrival is no earlier than 2:00pm. Departure is no later than 11:00am.

### Restrictions

No smoking in tower.  Discourage bringing children because the tower is 40 feet in height with exterior open stairway.

Please visit the [Forest's Alerts page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/lolo/alerts) for information on restrictions, in particular please review the [Food/Wildlife Attractant Storage Order](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/alerts/r1-2023-02-ncde-food-and-wildlife-attractant-storage-order) to help reduce the likelihood of human-to-bear conflicts.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 47.332456

**Longitude:** -115.343394

### Directions

Up-Up 40' Lookout Tower is located 80 miles west of Missoula on I-90 Ward Creek Exit 26. Exit 26 is only accessible from I-90 eastbound. If traveling west bound go to the Drexel exit 25 and return eastbound. Travel on road 889 for 3.2 miles, turn right on road 3816 and travel 14 miles to Up Up Lookout.

### Parking

Parking is extremely limited to vehicle maximum limit. Suitable for two standard vehicles or one small trailer and a standard vehicle.
