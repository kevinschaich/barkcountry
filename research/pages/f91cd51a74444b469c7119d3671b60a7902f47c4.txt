# Pacific Rim National Park Reserve

**Wolves in the area: closures, dog prohibitions, and warning in effect**

- [See closures, prohibitions and warning](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/bulletins)
- [Dog safety and pet restrictions](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/visit/chiens-dogs)
- [Wildlife safety](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/nature/faune-wildlife)

Lying along the west coast of Vancouver Island in the traditional territories of the Nuu-chah-nulth First Nations, Pacific Rim National Park Reserve boasts kilometres of long sandy beaches, unspoiled temperate rainforests and adventures that will delight the explorer in all.

[![La silhouette de deux personnes se profile au coucher du soleil sur la plage ](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/WET4/homepage/Pac-Rim---LBU-beach-sunset-1.jpg?modified=20221117210426)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/activ/plage-long-long-beach)

### [Long Beach Unit](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/activ/plage-long-long-beach)

Storm watching, beach-walking, cycling and more. There is something for everyone.

[![Rough coastline of Vancouver Island](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/WET4/activ/WCT/WCT-Unit---640-x-360.jpg?modified=20240605165032)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/activ/ssco-wctu)

### [West Coast Trail Unit](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/activ/ssco-wctu)

Back-country options on the west coast of Vancouver Island, including Cape Beale Headlands and the iconic West Coast Trail.

[![Two people paddling their kayaks in the Broken Group Islands](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/WET4/homepage/Pac-Rim---BGI-Kayakers-1.jpg?modified=20221117210426)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/activ/broken-group)

### [Broken Group Islands Unit](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/activ/broken-group)

Enjoy a day or overnight adventure in the sheltered bays and shell beaches of Barkley Sound. The islands are only accessible by boat.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/bc/Gulf-Islands-NPR.jpg?modified=20221126220951)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/gulf)

### [Gulf Islands National Park Reserve](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/gulf)

Scattered throughout the Salish Sea, the Gulf Islands teem with wildlife, a haven for rare species and threatened eco-systems and a playground for hikers, campers, cyclists, boaters and kayakers.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/bc/gwaiihaanas-homebanner_640x360.jpg?modified=20221127214854)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/gwaiihaanas)

### [Gwaii Haanas National Park Reserve, National Marine Conservation Area Reserve, and Haida Heritage Site](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/gwaiihaanas)

Amid lush rainforest islands and seas rich with wildlife, Gwaii Haanas harbours rare sites of carved poles and longhouses, making this park culturally important to the Haida people who co-manage cooperatively manage the region with Parks Canada.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/bc/FortRoddHillNHS.jpg?modified=20221126220951)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/bc/fortroddhill)

### [Fort Rodd Hill National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/bc/fortroddhill)

Tour through secret bunkers, military command posts and original 19th century buildings at Fort Rodd Hill, a west coast artillery fortress on active duty from 1895 to 1956. Be touched by the personal stories of soldiers and their families. Camp overnight in a group-friendly oTENTik.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/bc/FortLangleyNHS.jpg?modified=20221126220951)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/bc/langley)

### [Fort Langley National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/bc/langley)

Experience life as a 19th century voyageur at Fort Langley, where the colony of British Columbia was born. Mingle with fur traders, pan for gold, learn the blacksmith’s art and camp in an HBC-themed oTENTik.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/bc/GulfofGeorgiaCanneryNHS.jpg?modified=20221126220951)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/bc/georgia)

### [Gulf of Georgia Cannery National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/bc/georgia)

Tour a historic Fraser River salmon cannery, through a herring reduction plant and interactive fishing displays. Learn about local marine life on the Fish Wall and be inspired by the industrious fishery of BC’s past.
