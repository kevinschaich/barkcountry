# Ivvavik National Park

![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nt/woodbuffalo/2024-changes/01_11-nodrone/No-Drone_nobkgd.png)
Using recreational drones in Ivvavik National Park is prohibited. Operating a drone within park boundaries may face law enforcement action and a fine of up to $25,000.

Ivvavik, meaning ‘a place for giving birth, a nursery,' in Inuvialuktun, the language of the Inuvialuit, is the first national park in Canada to be created as a result of an Indigenous land claim agreement – the [Inuvialuit Final Agreement (1984)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/ivvavik/gestion-management/plan). The park protects a portion of the calving grounds of the Porcupine caribou herd and represents the Northern Yukon and Mackenzie Delta natural regions.

### [Pingo Canadian Landmark](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nt/pingo)

Pingo Canadian Landmark protects a unique arctic landform: ice-cored hills called pingos. Rising out of the flat tundra, these hills provide a distinctive backdrop to the community of Tuktoyaktuk, Northwest Territories.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nwt/nwt-aulavik-pn-np-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220102)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/aulavik)

### [Aulavik National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/aulavik)

Located in Canada’s Northwest Territories, Aulavik is among the country’s most remote national parks. But it rewards adventurers with untouched tundra, pristine rivers, archaeological sites and ample wildlife, from muskoxen to seals and other marine mammals.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/yt/yt-vuntut-pn-np-640x360.jpg?modified=20221125212558)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/vuntut)

### [Vuntut National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/vuntut)

Explore untouched northern landscapes and learn the story of the Vuntut Gwitchin people and their relationship to the land and animals of the northern Yukon.
