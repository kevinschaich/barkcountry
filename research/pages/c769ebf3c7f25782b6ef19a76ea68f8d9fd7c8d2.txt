# Welcome to Payette National Forest

The Payette National Forest in west central Idaho offers the visitor over 2.3 million acres to enjoy. Everything from the deep recesses of Hells Canyon to peaks reaching elevations of almost 9,500 feet. Dry desert grasslands compliment heavily forested acres. Gateways to the Frank Church-River of No Return Wilderness allow the visitor to experience the solitude of seldom traveled trails.

![A photo taken from inside a cave, looking out towards a creek and snowy mountains.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/legacy-media/payette/In%20the%20Spotlight-%20Big%20Creek%20Cave.jpg?h=be2253ff)
