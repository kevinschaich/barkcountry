# Pets

|     |
| --- |
| ![Dog at Everglades](https://www.nps.gov/ever/planyourvisit/images/IMG-0829.jpeg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)A dog in front of a Florida Panther statue <br>NPS Photo A. Alcantara<br>## Pets are Allowed in Limited Areas in Everglades National Park<br>_Due to the concern for wildlife management issues, Everglades' regulation allows the closing of an area to the use of pets and_ [service animals](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/service-animals.htm) _, if it is determined that these pose a direct threat to the health or safety of people or wildlife._<br>_Pets present in areas not permitted open themselves to predation by wildlife, as well as posing a significant danger to the handler and other visitors._<br>### Where Can I Bring my Pet?<br>Pets must be kept on a leash (maximum of 6 feet in length) at all times. Do not leave pets unattended. Please pick up after your pet and dispose in a sealed container. <br>### Pets are permitted in the following areas:<br>- Roadways open to public vehicular traffic<br>- Roadside campground and picnic areas<br>- Maintained grounds surrounding public facilities and residential areas<br>- Private boats<br>### Pets **not** permitted in the following areas:<br>- All trails - boardwalk, paved and unpaved<br>- Unpaved roads<br>- Shark Valley Tram Trail/Road<br>## Service Animals<br>To learn more about bringing a service animal to the park, [visit the Service Animals in National Parks page](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/service-animals.htm).<br>_For additional information call the general park information phone 305-242-7700 or read the Everglades Compendium listing current park-wide laws and policies._ |
