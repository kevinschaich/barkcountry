# Welcome to Idaho Panhandle National Forests

The Idaho Panhandle National Forests create a tapestry of land and water in the panhandle of North Idaho.

Vast lakes and miles of rivers support a world-class fishery. Rich in wildlife, the forest is home to large game such as elk and deer, as well as species such as grizzly bears, wolves, and caribou.

![Large machine processing logs in the snow](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/idahopanhandle/image/processinglogs_u75.jpg?h=072c2169)

Find information on past, current and planned timber sales. Bid on open sales. Find previous bid results, periodic sale announcements, and outyear project planning for the Idaho Panhandle National Forests.

[Timber Sales Information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/idahopanhandle/timber-sales)

The Coeur d'Alene Nursery is a full-service facility that provides bare root and containerized plant stock for publicly owned lands. Along with seed cleaning and storage, the Nursery also provides stock quality testing for the land manager.
