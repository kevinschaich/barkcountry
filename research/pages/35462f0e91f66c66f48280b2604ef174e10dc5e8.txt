# Sly Creek Campground

Current Conditions

Scheduled to close for the season November 1, 2026.

Sly Creek Campground has 30 units. Campground has drinking water, vault toilets, and garbage service. Each camping unit has a table and a fire ring.

Trailers up to 22 feet can be accommodated, however no hookups are provided. Maximum stay is 14 days. Sly Creek Reservoir usually opens around May through September. Boat ramp open all year.

## Reservations

Campground first come first, serve basis.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Generally open May through September.

### Restrictions

- 14 day limit
- 8 people per site
- 2 vehicles per site
- OHV use prohibited in campground
- [Be Bear Aware](https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/bears)

[https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/bears](https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/bears)
