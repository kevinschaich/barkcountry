# Cooper Creek Recreation Area

Cooper Creek Recreation Area is located along the banks of  Cooper Creek in the North Georgia mountains. Campers can experience all types of recreation activities, including camping, hiking, trout fishing, and hunting. Anglers may fish Cooper Creek and nearby Mulky Creek, both of which are trout streams for wild and stocked trout.

## Reservations

[**Reservations are required to camp in this campground**](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/10156125) **.** Reservations can be made through the National Recreation Reservation Service (NRRS). The website ( [www.Recreation.gov](http://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/10156125)) offers convenient round the clock access to the Online Reservation Service or visitors may call NRRS at 1-877-444-6777 (toll free) between 10:00 a.m. and 12:00 a.m. Eastern Standard Time.

You may reserve camp sites the same day as you arrive and as far as six (6) months in advance. However, **reservations cannot be made on-site.** Please book them online or via the NRRS phone number.

## General Information

The campground offers two camping loops for a total of thirteen standard nonelectric campsites, and one standard nonelectric group site. Each campsite provides a picnic table, fire-ring, tent pad, lantern post, and gravel parking. Visitors are permitted up to two vehicles per campsite. Potable water is available from a hydrant in the center of each camping loop. Visitors are required to reserve campsites through Recreation.gov; there are no first-come first-serve sites available in this campground.

### Seasons of Use

Yearlong

### Operational Hours

Open 24 hours a day. Quiet hours 10pm to 6am.

### Restrictions

- 14 day stay limit
- Maximum 2 vehicles per campsite
- Dogs must be leashed at all times.

### Passes and Permits

The Interagency Senior and Access Passes and the Golden Age and Access Passports are accepted. These passes entitle the pass holder to a 50% discount for single family campsites. [Click here for detailed information on passes!](https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/passes-permits)

### Fee Site and Info

**Camping Peak Season: April 1 - October 31**

$15 per night/site

$30 Double Site (Site #24)

**Camping Non-Peak Season(no water): November 1 - March 31**

$7.50 per night/site

$15 Double Site (Site #24)

### Pet Information

Dogs and other pets must be kept on a leash at all times.

## Getting There

### Directions

From Blue Ridge, GA take US 515 north for 4 miles to GA Hwy 60; turn right and go 2 miles to Morganton, GA. In Morganton, turn right on GA Hwy. 60 south for 15.5 miles. Turn left on Forest Service Road 4, go 6 miles.

From Blairsville, GA take US 515 south 2.5 miles to Kiutuestia Creek Road. Turn left, go 1.3 miles. Turn left on Old 76 for 100 feet and then turn right on Mulky Gap Road. Go 9 miles to Cooper Creek Campground

From Dahlonega, GA take GA Hwy. 60 north for 26 miles; turn right on Forest Service Road 4, go 6 miles.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Restroom information is not available for this site.

### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

Potable water is available during peak season (April 1 - October 31) from two hydrants centrally located in each camping loop.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

Each campsite is furnished with a picnic table.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are not allowed at this site.

### Other Amenities

If cooper creek is booked please check out Deep Hole Campground which is 15 minutes South of Area. This campground has nine sites and is also a great place to catch fish along the Toccoa River. This campground can also be booked on recreation.gov or calling the reservation line. Blue Ridge, Blairsville, and Dahlonega GA, are all towns nearby to visit for all your supply needs. If you don't want to drive into town, visit the Cooper Creek Store which is about 5 miles south of the campground as they'll have all your camping supplies.

## Recreation Opportunities

### Hiking

[![Day Hikes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/opportunity-icons/day_hike_icon.svg)Day Hikes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/chattahoochee-oconee/recreation/opportunities/hiking)

### Day Hikes Info

There are several hiking trails that start in or near the campground and all are interconnected, so forming a loop is a breeze. The Yellow Mountain Trail is the longest at 3.2 miles, so these are great little trails for taking the kids or the inexperienced hiker on. The Duncan Ridge Trail isn’t far up the road if you would like to do a more challenging hike.

Yellow Mtn. Trail – 3.2 miles

Connector Trail – 0.4 miles

Mill Shoals Trail – 0.9 miles

Shope Gap Trail – 0.6 miles

Cooper Creek Trail – 1.7 miles
