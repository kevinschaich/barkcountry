# Hiking

Hiking is a wonderful way to see and experience the many wonders of our nation’s forests. Visit your closest ranger district office for maps to help you plan your route based on your ability, available time and interest. Being prepared includes knowing how to be a responsible hiker. Learn more on our [Responsible Recreation](https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/responsible-recreation) page.
