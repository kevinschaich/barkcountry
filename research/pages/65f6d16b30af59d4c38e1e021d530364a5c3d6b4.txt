# Upper Tonto Creek Campground

Current Conditions

Firewood sales are not available at this time.

There are numerous activities to explore in the Upper Tonto Creek Campgrounds. The Lower Tonto Creek Campground has a large open-space parking lot and paved walkway makes it accessible with only a light hike to the creek. The Upper Tonto Creek Campground is tucked away from the main highway where the Tonto and Horton Creeks merge. Families may enjoy fishing at both Tonto and Horton Creeks or pass time hiking through nearby Horton Creek Trail or Derrick Trail. Both of these trails access the Highline National Recreation Trail (#31).

# Upper Tonto Creek

Part of [Tonto National Forest](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/1096)

## Overview

The Upper Tonto Creek is a small campground tucked away from the main highway where the Tonto and Horton Creeks merge. 6 Campsites are available for reservation only.

**This location is available only through reservation May 1through November 3rd.**

Campground map available [**here**](https://cdn.recreation.gov/public/2024/04/02/16/32/10215934_ba24f33b-3a52-4bb7-85a1-a4cb61e35329.pdf) .

## Facilities

This campground contains 6 campsites, 3 picnic sites, and a double vault toilet.

## Natural Features

The union of Tonto and Horton Creeks is at the north end of the Upper Tonto Creek campground. Fishing in both creeks is a popular sport.

## Recreation

Families may enjoy fishing at both Tonto and Horton Creeks or pass time hiking through nearby Horton Creek Trail or Derrick Trail. Both of these trails access the Highline National Recreation Trail (#31).

## Contact Info

This location is unstaffed. To speak with our main office for general information, please call: (928) 474-7900.


## Important Notices

- Sites are small in this campground so trailers have to be short or only over camper type mounts.

- This campground is small with 6 sites sitting above Tonto Creek. The campground road is tight with no turn around options.

- Check in time is 2:00pm and check out time is 11:00am. Quiet hours are from 10:00pm to 6:00am.


## Directions

<p>This site is 17 miles northeast of Payson. From Payson, travel 16 miles east on State Hwy. 260. Just east of mile marker 268 at Kohl’s Ranch, turn left (north) on Forest Road (FR) 289. Travel one mile and turn right into the site.</p>

## General Information

- 14-day stay limit.
- No OHV recreational riding within the campground.
- Dogs are allowed and must be kept on a leash.
- Please use trash receptacles or pack it in, pack it out.
- All campgrounds have posted Rules & Regulations at entrance kiosks. Please read and observe all rules.
- Sites are small in this campground so trailers have to be short or only over camper type mounts.
- Quiet hours 10:00 pm to 6:00 am (running generators are not permitted during quiet hours).
- Bear activity has increased due to drought conditions. For more information please visit [bebearaware.org](https://bebearaware.org/)

### Seasons of Use

Open Season: May - October

### Operational Hours

Check in: 2:00 p.m.; Check-out: 12:00 p.m.; Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. until 6:00 a.m.
