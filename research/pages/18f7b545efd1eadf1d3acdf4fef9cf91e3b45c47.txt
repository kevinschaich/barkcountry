# Twin Knobs Campground

Family-friendly Twin Knobs Campground offers 200 campsites developed for tent and RV camping. Each site has a picnic table and campfire ring. RV sites range from electric-only to full hookups and a dump station is available. Bathhouses with showers and flush toilets are spread throughout the campground.

During your stay, you will have access to all the amenities and facilities of the 700-acre Twin Knobs Recreation Area including a boat ramp, campground store, sandy swimming beach, volleyball courts, basketball goals, horseshoe pits and scenic trails.

## General Information

The boat ramp will become unsafe and/or inaccessible if lake levels rise above 733 feet. [Check current lake levels before your visit.](https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/crlk2)

### Seasons of Use

2nd Friday in March - 1st Sunday in November

### Operational Hours

Check-in: 2 p.m.

Checkout: 11:00 a.m.

Only registered campers are allowed at Twin Knobs after 10 p.m.
