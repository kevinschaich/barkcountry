### Protected area. Enriching nature experiences.

Parc national du Lac-Témiscouata is a harmonious blend of water and woods and an essential stop on the Lands and Forests of Acadia circuit. This park protects a representative sample of the natural region of Monts Notre-Dame. Hiking, cycling, boating, and discovery activities are a great way for visitors to experience the spirit of this place and fully appreciate the scope of an area rich with 10,000 years of human history, all in an unspoiled natural setting. This park is nestled along the largest and most majestic lake in the region, Lac Témiscouata. Created by the power of glaciers, it invites calm and contemplation.

**Curious-by-nature trail**

A must for families! Located at the Grand Lac Touladi, this independent 0.7 km circuit designed for children and their families includes 6 discovery stations, each recounting a moment in the history of the park territory. Through physical challenges and discovery games, children will be given a chance to let loose while learning more about the park. Imitating beavers, log drivers, Indigenous peoples, and eagles, they will have to build a hut, leap from trunk to trunk, hunt caribou, and soar across a zipline.

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/tem/accordeon/tem_act_longue_rando.jpg)

Parc National du Lac-Témiscouata is an ideal destination for families and beginner hikers looking to try their hand at long-distance hiking. Crossed by the Sentier national, the park offers a wide variety of ecosystems to explore. Choose from one of the five trails available, all known for their accessibility and proximity to services and parking lots.

The trails and lodging facilities (hut and rustic shelter) are open from May to October. For more information or bookings, contact us at 418-855-5508 or 1-800-665-6527.

Five trails are available, offering one- to three-night outings with daily hikes ranging from 8 km to 18 km.

A shuttle service is also available. To request this service, call 418-855-5508, ext. 4434.

### [![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/tem/accordeon/tem_act_peche.jpg)Day Fishing](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/tem/index.dot?language_id=1\#Item1411875)

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/tem/accordeon/tem_act_peche.jpg)

Petit Lac Touladi, Grand Lac Touladi and Touladi river are offered for day fishing. For fishing of Touladi lakes, only personal watercraft that do not require towing are permitted (canoe, kayak, stand-up paddleboard, rowboat without trailer, personal motors of 9.9 hp or less).

- Fishing rights needed.
- Provincial fishing license required (not sold in the park).
- Personal watercraft with 9.9 hp boat motor or less that do not require towing are permitted from May 15 to June 15, 2026 on Petit and Grand Lac Touladi.
- Dock and boat-washing station near the Petit-Lac-Touladi Visitors Centre.
- Personal boat washing is mandatory.
- It is prohibited to get within 300 m of the bald eagle nest and to land on the islands.
- Registering catches is mandatory.
- On Lac Témiscouata, only provincial regulations apply.

The fish species are speckled trout and perch.

The daily catch and possession limit per angler on these bodies of water is:

- Speckled trout : 7 (new catch and possession limit)
- Perch: 50

Period: May 15 to September 10, 2026

Please come on site on your day of fishing to make a reservation. No reservation in advance.

Come and have fun on Grand lac Touladi or brave the waters of Lac Témiscouata (Anse-à-William and Grands-Pins) on a stand-up paddle board.

Hawaiian in origin, stand-up paddling is an activity that combines surfing and kayaking. The board, tailor-made for calm water, is longer, wider and thicker than standard surfboards designed for waves and therefore more stable. Standing on a big surfboard, paddle in hand, you can test your balance. It’s fast, easy to learn and guaranteed fun!

[Rental](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/tem/tarifs.dot#cat_39) includes the board, the paddle, and a life jacket.

### [![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/tem/accordeon/tem_act_observationfaune.jpg)Wildlife watching](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/tem/index.dot?language_id=1\#Item5596189a-f4d5-4c1c-ab8b-f6c5e3abc9ee)

#### Curé-Cyr hut

Located near the sandy shore, Grey-Owl trail, Anse-à-William services (0.8 km) and 4.7 km from the Saint-Juste entrance (on foot or by bike). In winter, this hut is accessible by snowshoeing or backcountry skiing (via Saint-Juste entrance). It sleeps four people and overlooks Lac Témiscouata.

The shelter is equipped with a wood-burning stove, a table with benches and chairs, and four beds. You'll need to provide drinking water, cooking utensils, and lighting. A dry toilet is located close by. Wood is provided to heat the stove. In winter, outdoor fires are prohibited.

## Park access authorization

There is a limit to the number of visitors that can enter a national park on any given day. It is therefore **strongly recommended** that you purchase your right of access online before a visit or that you acquire an annual pass. This guarantees you access to the park, will ensure a smoother arrival at the site and avoid unnecessary travel when the maximum capacity is reached. It can be purchased up to 30 days before a visit. You may be denied access to a sector, a trail, or an activity if a parking lot is full or if the maximum capacity has been reached.

## Important

### Don't move firewood

A single piece of firewood can destroy millions of trees. Invasive insects and diseases can hide inside it. Buy it locally. Burn it on site. [Find out more This link will open in a new window.](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/plant-health/forestry/hazards-moving-firewood)
