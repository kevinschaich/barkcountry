# Lower Bluster Campground

Small, minimally developed campground located near Jarbidge, Nevada.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

June - October, as weather and conditions allow.

### Operational Hours

Open 24 hours/day

### Restrictions

- Dogs must be on a leash at campground.

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

No fee

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 41.8398

**Longitude:** -115.4251

### Elevation

6,500 feet

### Directions

Located approximately 3.5 miles south of the town of Jarbidge, Nevada

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Vault toilet(s)


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are allowed at this site.
