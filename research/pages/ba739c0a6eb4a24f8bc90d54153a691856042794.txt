# Resumidero Camping Area

Resumidero is a popular primitive camping area.  Located in a large alpine meadow, Resumidero is a local favorite and used by numerous recreationists for camping, picnicking, and group gatherings.

NM Resumidero Campground

````

START TOUR

**Uses:**  Picnicking, camping, and parking for access to San Pedro Parks Wilderness through Vega Redonda trail 43 and Corralitos Trail 452 (on the west side of the meadow). **Facilities:** portable toilets during summer months only, picnic tables, fire rings.

- **Special Instructions:** The existing "acequia" (irrigation ditch) is utilized by residents for irrigation of farmland.  PLEASE DO NOT POLLUTE THE WATER IN THE CANAL.
- Users must pack out their trash.

## General Information

### Restrictions

- Motorized vehicles or mechanized equipment are prohibited in the San Pedro Parks Wilderness.
- Call Coyote Ranger District for current fire restrictions
- All pets must be on a leash
-   Please adhere to all posted rules and regs

### Fee Site and Info

No Fee

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 36.11445053

**Longitude:** -106.7469193

### Directions

Travel on SH 96 to FR 103, the on FR 103 for 9 miles to FR 93.  Another 2 miles on FR93 will lead visitors to the campground
