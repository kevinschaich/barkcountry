# Two-Mile Connector Trail

Current Conditions

Weather and snow melt will affect open and closing. Please call the Moab Ranger District for current conditions, 435-259-7155.

The Two-Mile Connector Trail #980 is 0.5 miles long. This trail connects Forest Road #745 and Forest Road #746. This short trail makes it easier to create loops in the Two-Mile area within the Forest Service Boundary. Other loop options include using State Trust Land open trails. This trail can be combined with a variety of other trails and roads to create multiple loop routes. Other trails to consider combining to make a loop include: #979 ( [Lower Two-Mile](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/manti-lasal/recreation/trails/lower-two-mile-trail)), #983 ( [Trinity Canyon](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/manti-lasal/recreation/trails/trinity-canyon-trail)), #981 ( [Upper Two-Mile](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/manti-lasal/recreation/trails/upper-two-mile-trail)) as well as various State Trust Land Trails and Roads. Forest Roads to consider combining with this trail to make a loop include: #072 (Lower Two-Mile), #743, #744, #745, #746, #747, #748, #201 (Hang Dog), #202 (Hop Creek), #203 (Dry Draw) as well as various State Trust Land Trails.

**Elevation Change:**

South West End (Road #745): 7,988 feet / 2,435 meters

North East End (Road #746): 8,248 feet / 2,514 meters

## Specific Trail Information

### Trail Type

**Standard/Terra Trail** — A trail that has a surface consisting predominantly of the ground and that is designed and managed to accommodate use on that surface.

## General Information

**Latitude:** 38 24' 8.9"N

**Longitude:** 109 6' 53.6"W

**Datum:** NAD83

**Position Format:** Degrees Minutes Seconds

### Water Available

Water is unavailable on this trail. Users should carry water.

### Restrictions

Open to: Hiking, Mountain Biking, Horseback Riding, and Off Highway Vehicles (OHV) less than or equal to 50 inches wide.

## Getting There

### Directions

From La Sal, head east on UT-46 and continue 7.0 miles. At Old La Sal, turn left onto Co Rd #125/Forest Road #072 (Lower Two Mile) and travel approximately 6.1 miles. Turn left onto Forest Road #743 and travel 2.3 miles. Turn right onto Forest Road #744 and travel 0.4 miles. Turn right onto Forest Road #745 and travel 0.5 to Two-Mile Connector Trail #980.

## Additional Information

### Operated By

Forest Service
