Ranger Stella sends a friendly reminder that pets must be on a leash while recreating in Mammoth Cave National Park. <br>NPS Photo<br>## Where can I visit with my pet?<br>To help everyone enjoy the park and for the safety of you, your pet, and park wildlife, the following regulations apply in Mammoth Cave National Park. <br>- Pets are not permitted in caves or park buildings. <br>  <br>- Pets must be leashed (up to 6 feet) at all times. <br>  <br>- Leashed pets are allowed on all surface trails in the park. <br>  <br>- Pet-friendly lodging available in the Woodland Cottages.<br>## Tips for a great visit with your pet<br>- Do not leave pets unattended outside or inside your vehicle. <br>  <br>- During the summer, high temperatures can affect your pet, ensure your pet has enough water and snacks for a hike in the park. <br>  <br>- Pet waste must be immediately collected and disposed of in the nearest trash can. <br>  <br>- Pets and their owners may encounter horse riders on park backcountry trails. Please move yourself and your pet to the side of the trail and keep your pet calm while the riders pass by.   <br>## Day-Boarding Kennel<br>The self-service outdoor kennel operated by [The Lodge at Mammoth Cave](https://mammothcavelodge.com/lodging-mammoth-cave/) can provide a safe place for your pet while you are on your cave tour. Kennels services are available for both large and small dogs or cats during daylight hours. Please note that proof of vaccination must be provided upon kennel entry. Dogs need proof of current inoculation against rabies, DHLP, Bordetella, and parvo.<br>Call (844) 760-2283 for more information about kennel services. <br>![A service animal becomes a Junior Bark Ranger.](https://www.nps.gov/maca/planyourvisit/images/unnamed-5_3_2.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)<br>NPS Photo<br>## Service Animals<br>In accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), service animals are authorized entrance to all park caves and facilities open to the general public. <br>Find out more information on [accessibility at Mammoth Cave National Park](https://www.nps.gov/maca/planyourvisit/accessibility.htm).<br>[Learn about B.A.R.K.
