# Appalachian Ranger District, Pisgah National Forest

Experience high elevation forests, grassy balds and sweeping mountain views along the northern reaches of Pisgah National Forest. Visitors can explore areas around Roan Mountain, Max Patch and the Appalachian Trail while hiking, camping fishing. Enjoy the district's approximately 161,400 acres across Haywood, Madison, Buncombe, Yancy, Mitchell and Avery Counties.

**Things to Do**

The Appalachian Ranger District offers a variety of outdoor recreation opportunities, from quiet hiking experiences gushing waterfalls. Whether you’re hiking, biking, horseback riding, or exploring by vehicle, there are options for all experience levels.

Find more information about [recreation opportunities here](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/recreation/opportunities). Visitors can view a list of all trails, campgrounds and recreation areas in the complexes section below.

**Camping**

The Appalachian Ranger District has several camping options, including developed campgrounds and dispersed camping.

Some campgrounds offer amenities like restrooms and picnic tables, while others provide a more primitive experience.

Learn more about camping and how to reserve a site [here.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/recreation/camping-cabins)

**Forest Maps**

[View The Appalachian Ranger District maps](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/maps-guides) for roads, trails, and recreation areas.

**Closures**

Check [seasonal closures](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/about-area/faqs/seasonal-closures) before you go, or [temporary conditions on alerts.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/alerts)

Passes & Permits

Some recreation areas and activities require passes or fees. If you don’t see what you need on recreation.gov, [check here for additional pass information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/passes).

Visit here for [information on permits](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/permits) for foraging, special uses, firewood, mineral collection and much more.
