# Hole-in-the-Ground Campground

Current Conditions

Site closed due to damage from the Park Fire. Entering the area is prohibited until further notice.

True to its name, Hole-in-the-Ground is tucked away in a steep, tree-covered draw alongside Mill Creek. A developed campground with 13 designated sites, there is a maximum of 8 people per site at this campground.  Campfire rings and tables are provided. Only shorter travel trailers recommended.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

This campground is generally open from May to September. Open season subject to change based on weather and other factors.

### Restrictions

- Fee must be paid upon arrival and prior to each night of camping, Fee regulations are strictly enforced
- Quiet hours are 10pm to 6am
- Campfires allowed only in provided fire rings
- 1 vehicle and 8 persons allowed in each campsite
- Pets must remain on a leash or confined at all times
- Site must be occupied each night after fee is paid or equipment is set up/left in a campsite
- Fireworks and tiki torches prohibited

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $12 per night

### Pet Information

Dogs must remain on leash at all times
