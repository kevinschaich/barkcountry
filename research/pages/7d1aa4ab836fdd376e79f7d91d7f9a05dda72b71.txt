# Pattee Canyon Disc Golf Course

Current Conditions

Closes on Friday 10/30/2026.

Near the picnic area and parking lot, this basket course is wooded, fairly level, and beginner friendly.

## Reservations

Disc golf course is open June 15- October 31.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

June 15- October 31.

### Restrictions

Please visit the [Forest's Alerts page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/lolo/alerts) for information on restrictions, in particular please review the [Food/Wildlife Attractant Storage Order](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/alerts/r1-2023-02-ncde-food-and-wildlife-attractant-storage-order) to help reduce the likelihood of human-to-bear conflicts.

### Pet Information

Must be physically restrained on a 6 foot leash.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 46.827944

**Longitude:** -113.92518

### Directions

Trail starts at the Northeast corner of the parking lot.

### Parking

Follow signage to disc golf course parking.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Restrooms are not available at this site.

### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are not available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are not allowed at this site.
