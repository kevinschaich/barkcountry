# Welcome to Rogue River-Siskiyou National Forest

Located in **southwestern Oregon and extending into California**, the Rogue River-Siskiyou National Forest ranges from the crest of the Cascades Range, west into the Siskiyou Mountains, and includes southern portions of the Coastal Range, extending nearly to the Pacific Ocean. Oregon is comprised of a magnificent variety of geological wonders, and the Rogue River-Siskiyou National Forest is a part of that splendid diversity!
