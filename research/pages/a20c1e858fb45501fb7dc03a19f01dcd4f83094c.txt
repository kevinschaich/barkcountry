Current conditions vary as winter turns to spring. Check park alerts for updates.

[Hiking](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/hikingtrails.htm)

![Adults and children with jackets and backpacks hike along a trail. Wooden trail sign in foreground.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/blca/crop16_9/1ED817B2-D7FE-D5C3-A49F0F6D44B3DD17.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Hiking trails range in distance and difficulty. Some have steep drop offs, rocky terrain, and steps.

[Camping](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/camping.htm)

![A blue tent and light green tent are pitched on dirt between juniper and pinyon pine trees.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/blca/crop16_9/1F8C19FE-969B-A392-B40239F57BE50264.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Decide which campground is right for you - whether you're driving a RV or pitching a tent.

[Inner Canyon Use](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/innercanyon.htm)

![three hikers scrambling up steep rock ledges, viewed from above](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/blca/crop16_9/0C239A8A-1DD8-B71B-0BC0DC7FE9B5865C.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Learn about the wilderness area of the Inner Canyon. Information on permits, regulations, routes, etc,

[Geology](https://www.nps.gov/blca/learn/nature/geology.htm)

![Four dark canyon walls with a blue and cloudy sky. The perspective is looking up at the walls.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/blca/crop16_9/1EB136F4-D8E9-D7D4-3EA8516D9C91B962.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

The geologic story of Black Canyon of the Gunnison is one of uplift, volcanism, and the erosive force of water.

[Animals](https://www.nps.gov/blca/learn/nature/animals.htm)

![A black bear face with a brown fur looks through green vegetation.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/blca/crop16_9/E3BCDCAC-9FD9-FA65-9E16C116B714C1B9.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Animals live in different habitats from canyon rim to river and require specific lifestyles to be successful.

[Wilderness](https://www.nps.gov/blca/learn/wilderness.htm)

![View of a river running through a steep canyon. Vegetation lines the riverbank.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/blca/crop16_9/66F3C469-C45E-8696-FAEDD9CDF7D13BEE.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Learn about Black Canyon of the Gunnison Wilderness, a federally designated wilderness area.

[2025 South Rim Fire](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/2025-south-rim-fire.htm)

![Burned and treeless landscape with canyon and blue sky in background](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/blca/crop16_9/D6BE4A8E-0015-A4C1-CB58B20DCC4CB87A.jpg?width=1300&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Learn about the 2025 South Rim Fire at Black Canyon of the Gunnison National Park.

[One River System, Two Park Experiences](https://www.nps.gov/cure)

![Distant rocky pinnacles rise above a large reservoir with sagebrush in the foreground.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/blca/crop16_9/7EB1E51B-A5AD-0959-A71BFE89AA748832.jpg?width=1300&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)
