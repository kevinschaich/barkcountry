# Worthen Meadow Campground

Worthen Meadow Campground has 28 campsites on the shores of Worthen Meadow Resevoir. Trailheads at the campground accesses the Popo Agie Wilderness.

Stay limit is 16 days.

Maximum spur length is 24 feet .

Food storage is required in this campground - be prepared to store your food properly. Bear boxes available.

## Reservations

This site is first come, first served.

## General Information

### Operational Hours
