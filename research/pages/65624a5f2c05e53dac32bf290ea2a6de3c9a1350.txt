# Laird Lake Campground

This small campground is near Laird Lake and comprised of 4 campsites and is located near the [Wild and Scenic Elk River](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/elk-river), and along the [Wild Rivers Coast Scenic Bikeway](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/wild-rivers-coast-scenic-bikeway).

Laird Lake is a small lake at the headwaters of the Elk River. It is stocked annually with several thousand catchable rainbow trout and was recently rehabilitated to remove exotic Umpqua pikeminnow that were illegally introduced.

## General Information

First come, first served.

### Restrictions

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfires in developed fire pits only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Dogs must be on leash
- OHV/ATV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days

### Fee Site and Info

No fee required.

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in the campground. All pets must be kept on a leash, and owners are required to clean up pet waste.

### Latitude / Longitude
