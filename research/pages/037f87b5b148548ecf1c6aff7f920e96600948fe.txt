# Safety and Outdoor Ethics

First time visiting the Nebraska National Forests & Grasslands? Help protect our beautiful forests and grasslands by recreating responsibly, safely and ethically. The best way to do this is _**KNOW BEFORE YOU GO**_. Check out the following resources that will help you fulfill the first principle of [Leave No Trace](https://lnt.org/why/7-principles/) – _**Plan Ahead and Prepare**_.

The Nebraska National Forests & Grasslands want visitors’ experiences to be memorable and safe. Be aware of and prepared for safety concerns before you head out.

## Trip Planning

1. Visit [**Recreation**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/recreation) to find an activity and area to visit.
2. Continue reading below for trip planning resources and considerations before you head out.

### Fire

Please visit our [**Fire**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/fire) page for all fire-related information. Here you can check fire restrictions to see if the area you're visiting allows campfires, check local fire bans that may apply to National Forest System lands and check our alerts to monitor wildfire and prescribed burn closures.

### Roads, Trails, Closures

- Our [**MVUMs**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/maps-guides) are a great resource for road and trail closure information and to see what types of vehicles we allow on our roads.
- Visit [**Current Conditions**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/conditions) to see road, weather, and fire conditions.
- Visit the [**Alerts**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/alerts) page to see prohibitions, closures, and regulations for the Forest.

### Passes, Permits, Reservations

- Visit [**Recreation**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/recreation) to see if the area you're visiting requires a [pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/passes) or reservation.
- Looking for permits for events, photography, firewood collection, or other special use? Visit [**Permits.**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/permits)
- Make reservations at [**recreation.gov.**](https://www.recreation.gov/)

### Camping

- View a list of campgrounds available on our [**Campgrounds page**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/recreation/camping-cabins).
- Visit [**recreation.gov**](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/1056) for campground reservations and more.

### Recreation

- Be sure to carry a current map of the area and have more than one way to navigate. Download [**maps**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/maps-guides) **,** purchase at your local ranger district office or buy maps [**online**](https://store.usgs.gov/).
- Have a backup plan- Have an alternate plan in case trailhead parking lots, campsites, etc. are full.
- Leave No Trace! Pack it in, pack it out.
- Prepare for unpredictable weather! Severe weather occurs regularly and unexpectedly. In summer, beware the heat and sudden thunderstorms; in winter, beware the extreme cold and winds.
- Always be prepared for your trip with water, food, and other necessary supplies.
- Notify others when entering and leaving the Forests & Grasslands.
- Stop in at the local ranger district office for updates, warnings, and specific safety precautions.
- Always treat water found along the trail if you decide to drink it. Any water found is not safe for human consumption. Due to high summer temperatures and lack of shade, care must be taken to avoid heat related injuries. Bring plenty of water when you visit!
- You might not have cell service! Many areas on the Forests & Grasslands don't have cell coverage, especially in remote areas.
