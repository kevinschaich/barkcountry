# Welcome to Kaibab National Forest

If you are anywhere near Arizona, you won't want to miss the chance to visit us as the forest surrounds the northern and southern portions of the majestic Grand Canyon. From canyons to prairies, peaks to plateaus, the Kaibab National Forest offers layers of opportunity for peace, solitude and discovery.

![Smoke dominates the sky above the forest at the edge of a meadow at sunset, a sign reading "Kaibab National Forest" sits in the foreground.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/kaibab/image/Photos_20250728_SmokeBehindKNFSign_CliffBerger_000597.jpg?h=405b5128)

Find out about the incredible work being done on the nearly 204,500 acres affected by the 2025 fires on the North Kaibab Ranger District.

[Learn more here](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/kaibab/fire/white-sage-and-dragon-bravo-fires)

![A picnic table in a developed campground](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/legacy-media/kaibab/Demotte%20CG.jpg?h=6462987b)

Camping, cabins, hiking, scenic drives, fishing and snow play—the Kaibab has opportunities for everyone year round.

[Start Exploring](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/kaibab/recreation)

![Map showing all three districts of the Kaibab National Forest in relation to one another.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/kaibab/image/KNF%20general%20map.jpg?h=5f2c4f29)

Be prepared with maps in your format: interactive, downloadable, and print, including Motor Vehicle Use maps and travel aids.

[Maps & Guides](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/kaibab/maps-guides)

![Image of cut up firewood](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/kaibab/image/Firewood%20Card%20image.jpg?h=5f2c4f29)

The Kaibab offers opportunities for firewood collection as well as other forest products such as corral poles, fence posts, and stays, Christmas trees, wilding transplants, pine cones and more.

**Fuelwood Permits can be purchased at the District offices or on the** [**E-Permits website**](https://epermits.fs2c.usda.gov/forest/0307).

[Get a Permit](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/kaibab/permits)

![Two runners on a rolling dirt road in the desert.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/kaibab/image/Grand%202%20Grand%20Ultra%20Marathon%202012%20%281%29.jpg?h=ba53e8e0)

Examples of activities that require special use permits includes recreation events, large group gatherings (outside of reserved group sites), communication towers, power lines, pipelines, roads, outfitter/guide businesses and commercial endeavors.

[Special Uses](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/kaibab/permits)
