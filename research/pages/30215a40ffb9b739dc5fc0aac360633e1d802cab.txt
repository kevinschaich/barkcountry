# White Mule Trailhead

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Day use only; no overnight camping allowed

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

### Pet Information

Please follow the guidelines below to ensure your pets remain welcome to visit the Forest in the future:

- Leave vicious or unusually noisy dogs at home.
- Clean up after your pet.
- When out on the trails, be sure you leash your dog whenever you approach other hikers, horses, pack stock, wild animals, or other pets. Horses and pack stock may spook at an inquisitive or boisterous dog, injuring the rider.  Hikers may not enjoy meeting your dog running loose on a narrow, steep trail.

By practicing good dog etiquette and being a conscientious dog owner, you know you are doing your part to keep National Forests designated as "dog friendly" places.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 43.55861111

**Longitude:** -122.3408333

### Directions

From Roseburg, OR, take OR-138 east / northeast Diamond Lake Boulevard. Travel east for 72.5 miles. Turn left onto National Forest Service Road 2610/Birds Point Road. In 4.3 miles turn right to stay on National Forest Service Road 2610/Birds Point Road or towards Lemolo Lake. Around 0.6 miles the trailhead will be on the left side of the road.
