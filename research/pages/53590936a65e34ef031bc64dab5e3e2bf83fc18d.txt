# Plan your visit

## Wapusk National Park

Churchill, Manitoba, 1,400 kilometres northeast of Winnipeg, is the “gateway” community to Wapusk National Park. Visit the [Parks Canada Visitor Centre](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/mb/wapusk/activ/centre) to better understand the park, its history and the diversity of the Churchill area.

### Directions

**By rail**

There is regular train service to Churchill and Gillam. Please contact [VIA Rail](https://www.viarail.ca/en) for details about their service.

**By air**

Calm Air provides scheduled direct flights to Churchill and Gillam from Winnipeg and Thompson, Manitoba. Please contact [Calm Air](https://www.calmair.com/) for details on flights.

**By road**

There is no road access to Churchill.

Gillam is accessible by Provincial Road 280 from Thompson.

**By licensed tour operators**

Access to Wapusk National Park is mainly by [licensed commercial tour operators](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/mb/wapusk/visit/services#licensedtouroperators) in Churchill. **Visitors are strongly encouraged to use the services of a licensed commercial operator when travelling in Wapusk National Park as there are no roads into the park.**
