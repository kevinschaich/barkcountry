[Essential Acadia: Top 5 Things To Know](https://www.nps.gov/articles/top-5-things-to-know-about-visiting-acadia.htm)

![Ranger leads visitors walking single file through a field with a coastline and mountain in distance](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/acad/crop16_9/326CC43B-949E-37E5-3E829F7B4DF88863.jpg?width=1300&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Whether this is your first visit to Acadia, or you've been coming with your family for generations, check out this list to learn the latest.

[Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/index.htm)

![people board an island explorer bus](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/acad/crop16_9/CF5DB705-E10B-DD80-3C23BED986FE815C.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Details of your visit start to come together here including camping, fees, reservations, operating hours, transportation info and more.

[Learn About the Park](https://www.nps.gov/acad/learn/index.htm)

![Woman in a field along coastline writes in a note pad](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/acad/crop16_9/CF001A88-E714-28C9-B4F0290CC5F9E382.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Learn about park history, nature, and management practices.

[Get Involved](https://www.nps.gov/acad/getinvolved/index.htm)

![People standing in a carriage road holding rakes and tools](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/acad/crop16_9/CEC128DB-A609-8008-8527CC46DC4BF296.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Learn how to volunteer, work with us, become an artist in residence, report a wildlife sighting, and other ways to get involved in Acadia.

[Entrance Pass Required](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/fees.htm)

![Visitor hikes on a trail past a park boundary and entry pass sign](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/acad/crop16_9/1A3D38C5-F5BC-AFBC-50C20CB48A544B01.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

A park entrance pass is required year-round. Vehicles must display a pass clearly visible through the windshield. Buy & print a pass online.

[Vehicle Reservations](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/vehicle_reservations.htm)

![Ranger leans out of booth window to scan a device held by a driver of a vehicle.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/acad/crop16_9/5D269E5B-04AF-7C6C-24EE6DE4FEDB1D2B.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Vehicle reservations are required to drive Cadillac Summit Road from late-May to mid-October. Buy reservations online. Not sold at the park.

[Camping Takes Planning](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/camping.htm)

![Photo of a white and red vintage trailer under trees at a campsite. ](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/acad/crop16_9/1A28CD81-0EC7-BFC0-3E38D337D09EB11E.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

All campsite reservations must be made online in advance. There is no backcountry camping or overnight parking allowed in Acadia.

[Wabanaki Nations](https://www.nps.gov/acad/learn/historyculture/wabanaki.htm)

![Two women kneel in tall grass](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/acad/crop16_9/684A2A8D-0E58-A092-6304E419305865E6.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Learn more about the heritage, culture, and continuing presence of tribes known collectively as Wabanaki or "People of the Dawnland"

[Get the Most Out of An Off-Season Visit](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/winter.htm)

![Park sign along a snow covered road](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/acad/crop16_9/C6AD8794-1DD8-B71B-0B26472B8607312A.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

There's actually a lot to do and enjoy in Acadia during the off season, from November through April.

[Accessibility](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/accessibility.htm)

![Smiling man in wheelchair parked on path with mountain and pond in background](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/acad/crop16_9/6FFD948F-0A7F-F2DC-1C691094469D6EB5.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Acadia National Park is committed to making its facilities, landscapes, programs, and information accessible to people of all abilities.
