# Kootenai Creek Trailhead

Current Conditions

Stay on main trail for approximately the first 1/2 mile. The trail runs through private land, please respect property boundaries of the owner.

Kootenai Creek Trailhead has adequate parking for both cars and trailers. It offers access to Kootenai Creek Trail #53 and the Selway-Bitterroot Wilderness. Facilities include a vault toilet, and hitching rails.

Stay on main trail for approximately the first 1/2 mile. The trail runs through private land, please respect property boundaries of the owner.

## General Information

[**Food Storage Required**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/bitterroot/alerts/bitterroot-national-forest-food-storage-order)

Please practice Leave No Trace Principles when visiting forest land

Pack it in Pack it out, no garbage service provided

No overnight camping

### Seasons of Use

Year round, snow dependent.

### Operational Hours

Day use. Overnight parking only allowed if not occupying vehicle.

### Restrictions

Stay on main trail for approximately the first 1/2 mile.

The trail runs through private land, please respect property boundaries of the owner.

Trail enters Selway Bitterroot Wilderness at mile 2.6.

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

### Pet Information

Pets must be under control.  Leashes are recommended to avoid user and wildlife conflicts. Properly dispose of animal waste bags. Do not put animal waste bags in vault toilets.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 46.53833333

**Longitude:** -114.1502778

### Elevation

3572

### Directions

From Stevensville take the Stevi cut-off road to Highway 93. Travel north 3/4 of a mile and turn west (left) onto Kootenai Creek Rd. Travel approximately 2 miles to the end and the trailhead.

### Parking

Lot large enough to accommodate stock trailers.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Vault toilet(s)


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are not available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are allowed at this site.

Limited amount of parking for stock trailers.
