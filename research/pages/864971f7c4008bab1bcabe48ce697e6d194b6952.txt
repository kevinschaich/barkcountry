# Welcome to Francis Marion and Sumter National Forests

The Francis Marion and Sumter National Forests offer recreational activities as diverse as the terrain--from [hiking](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation/opportunities/hiking) to explore mountain waterfalls to [paddling](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation/opportunities/water-activities) in low country swamp wilderness.

Four ranger districts span the state of South Carolina: [Andrew Pickens](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation/andrew-pickens-ranger-district-0) in the mountains, [Enoree](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation/enoree-ranger-district-0) and [Long Cane](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation/long-cane-ranger-district-0) in the piedmont, and [Francis Marion](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation/francis-marion-ranger-district-0) on the coast.

Mountain bike the epic [Forks Area Trail System](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation/trails/forks-area-trail-system) on the Long Cane, or paddle and fish the [Wild and Scenic Chattooga River](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation/chattooga-wild-and-scenic-river) on the Andrew Pickens district. Saddle up and explore a [horse trail](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation/opportunities/horse-riding-and-camping) on any district, or unload your ATV or dirt bike and hone your skills on one of four [OHV trails](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation/opportunities/highway-vehicles-ohv). Explore the Intracoastal Waterway from Buck Hall boat launch on the Francis Marion or paddle, hunt or fish the three rivers on the Enoree Ranger District. Nine rifle ranges will help maintain your aim for hunting season.

Find these sites and other hiking trails, campgrounds, picnic areas and more from our [Recreation webpage](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/francismarionsumter/recreation) or the [interactive visitor map](https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/a695e1dbdeab4e8ba5c6ad4fd06a0ba0?minx=-9768439&miny=3854148&maxx=-8985723&maxy=4206982&exploremenu=no).
