# Welcome to the Beaverhead-Deerlodge National Forest

The forest supports communities with timber, minerals, grazing lands, and stunning scenery that invites endless recreation. From trekking the Anaconda-Pintler or Lee Metcalf wilderness areas to driving the Gravelly Range Road or Pioneer Mountains Scenic Byway—or simply camping beneath the pines—the Beaverhead-Deerlodge offers adventure in every direction.

![A man rides his mountain bike near a lake on the Beaverhead-Deerlodge National Forest.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/beaverhead-deerlodge/image/USDA%20Photo%20Preston%20Keres%2C%20man%20mountain%20biking%20near%20Wade%20Lake%2C%2011JUL2018.jpg?h=104728fb)
