# Whiskey Springs Campground

Whiskey Springs Campground is quiet and secluded and can accommodate most trailers lengths. (Trailers longer than 28 feet in length are not recommended.) The 34-site campground is a short distance from Willow Lake Recreation Area, and numerous hiking trails are within easy access, including the Mt. McLoughlin Trail.

This campground is located on the site of the original supply road (Old Military Road) for Fort Klamath. A one mile interpretive loop trail is located within this campground.

# Whiskey Springs Campground

# WHISKEY SPRINGS CAMPGROUND

Part of [Rogue River-Siskiyou National Forest](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/1108)

## Overview

Whiskey Springs Campground is nestled within the forest providing shady campsites that are flat and located along a loop . This campground has a classic "forest feel" to it.

## Facilities

The campground has vault toilets and drinking water available.

## Natural Features

This campground is located on the site of the original supply road (Old Military Road) for Fort Klamath.

## Recreation

A one mile interpretive loop trail is located within this campground.

## Nearby Attractions

Nearby Willow Lake is located off of the Butte Falls highway with an expansive 927 acres of recreational activities including hiking trails, swimming, fishing, camping and boating,

## Contact Info

For facility specific information, please call (541) 560-3400.


## Important Notices

- Caution: Bears frequent the area; be aware, keep a clean camp and store all food items in closed containers.
- One vehicle is included in campsite fee, an additional fee for extra vehicles must be paid at campground.

- First-come, first-served sites are available.
- Off-road vehicles are not allowed in the campground.
- For current conditions please call the High Cascades Ranger District at (541) 560-3400


## Available Activities

- Day Use Area - Day Use Area
- Hiking - Hiking
- Fishing - Fishing
- Camping - Camping

## Directions

From Butte Falls, OR, take County Rt. 821 south 9 miles to campground sign. Turn left after sign onto Forest Rt. 3065 and go 0.3 miles to another campground sign. Turn left at sign and go 0.3 miles to campground.

## General Information

### Restrictions

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfires in developed fire pits only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Dogs must be on leash
- OHV/ATV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $16 per night

Extra Vehicles: $5 per vehicle per day

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in the campground. All pets must be kept on a leash, and owners are required to clean up pet waste.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 42.49279

**Longitude:** -122.41763

### Directions

From **Medford, OR**, travel north approximately 16 miles on Highway 62 going through Eagle Point to the Butte Falls / Willow Lake turnoff.  Turn east (right) and travel 16 miles to the town of Butte Falls, OR.  Continue through Butte Falls on County Road 821 for 9.1 miles to Forest Road 3065.  Turn north (left) and travel 0.3 mile on Forest Road 3065 to the campground entrance to the north (left).
