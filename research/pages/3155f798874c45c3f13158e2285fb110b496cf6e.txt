# Horsethief Lake Campground

**A Delightful Setting**

Located 10 miles south of Hill City, South Dakota, Horsethief Lake Campground is a delightful setting for visitors seeking recreation and relaxation in Black Hills National Forest. This campground is 2 miles from Mount Rushmore National Memorial and adjacent to Horsethief Lake. Sites are tucked into a forest of towering ponderosa and lodgepole pine.

See the [layout of the campground](https://www.fs.usda.gov/media/62967) to find your perfect camping site.

## General Information

Campsites include:

- Standard Nonelectric
- Tent Only Nonelectric
- Walk To
- Overnight

**Don't Move Firewood:** Prevent the spread of tree-killing organisms by obtaining firewood at or near your destination and burning it on-site. For more information visit [**dontmovefirewood.org.**](http://www.dontmovefirewood.org/)

### Seasons of Use

Late spring to early fall

### Operational Hours

Horsethief Campground closes each year during the o [ff-season](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/recreation/season-campground-information).

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $30 per night

### Pet Information

A pet fee of $2 per pet per night is required upon check in. Pets must be leashed, under control and attended at all times.

- Dogs must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet in campgrounds, picnic areas, and other developed recreation sites.
- Dogs are not allowed on swim beaches.
- For everyone’s safety and comfort, please don’t leave pets alone at campsites or in parked vehicles.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 43.8951038

**Longitude:** -103.4842324

### Elevation

4959

### Directions

From Hill City, SD take U.S. Rt. 16/385 south 3 miles to State Rt. 244. Turn left onto Rt. 244. Travel 5.9 miles to the campground sign. Turn right at sign into campground.

Drinking water is available but you CANNOT hook up for filling.

Picnic tables are available at this site.
