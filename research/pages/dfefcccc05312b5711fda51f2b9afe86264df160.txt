# Hunting & Shooting

Hunting and fishing activities are regulated by the Idaho Department of Fish and Game. Follow the state laws and regulations pertaining to hunting and fishing seasons, dates and licensing. For licenses, regulations and detailed game unit information, visit the Idaho Department of Fish and Game [website](https://idfg.idaho.gov/).
