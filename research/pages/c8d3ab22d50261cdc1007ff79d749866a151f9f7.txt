# Terra Nova National Park

Explore the wonder of Canada’s most easterly national park. Terra Nova is a magical place where the land and sea compete for your attention, where the island boreal forest reveals its secrets as you hike a trail, and where you can experience an evening of theatre under the stars.

Reservations will open for long term camping in Newman Sound on February 9, 2026 at 8:30 am NT. **These sites may only be booked by calling 1-877-737-3783.**

Reservations will open for all other camping on February 11, 2026 at 8:30 am NT

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nl/nl-signalhill-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220707)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/signalhill)

### [Signal Hill National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/signalhill)

Signal Hill celebrates St. John’s rich communications and military history, amidst spectacular city and ocean views. Enjoy military and musical performances, stirring ceremonies, coastal hikes, dramatic fortifications, and the iconic Cabot Tower.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nl/nl-capespear-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220707)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/spear)

### [Cape Spear Lighthouse National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/spear)

Atop a headland at North America’s easternmost point, Cape Spear Lighthouse overlooks a parade of drifting icebergs and waters teeming with whales and porpoises. Its restored interior offers a glimpse into 19th century lightkeeping along Newfoundland’s unforgiving coast.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nl/nl-castlehill-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220707)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/castlehill)

### [Castle Hill National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/castlehill)

An impressive fort stands sentinel above a seaside town. Stone walls and cast iron cannons are testaments to a turbulent past. This is Castle Hill National Historic site—bastion to the struggle for a continent.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nl/nl-hawthornecottage-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220707)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/hawthorne)

### [Hawthorne Cottage National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/hawthorne)

Hawthorne Cottage was the Newfoundland home of legendary Arctic explorer Captain Bob Bartlett. See exhibits and artefacts from his voyages while admiring the nearly 200-year-old cottage, a fine example of the picturesque architectural style in Newfoundland.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nl/nl-ryanpremises-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220707)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/ryan)

### [Ryan Premises National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/ryan)

On Bonavista’s scenic harbour, knowledgeable interpreters, diverse exhibits and displays within the Ryan Premises’ restored buildings bring to life a 19th century Newfoundland fishing enterprise and tell the tale of Canada’s East Coast fisheries dating back 500 years.
