# Sirmilik National Park

Amid an expansive landscape of glaciers, valleys and red-rock hoodoo spires, nesting seabirds crowd the cliffs of Bylot Island, high above icy waters teeming with narwhal and beluga whales. Sirmilik is an area rich in wildlife that has sustained Inuit for thousands of years.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nu/sirmilik/WET4/index/hoodoos-640x360.jpg?modified=20240104182118)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/sirmilik/activ/randonnees-hiking)

### [Explore glaciers and hoodoos](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/sirmilik/activ/randonnees-hiking)

Hike or ski through the diverse terrain of Bylot Island and the Borden Peninsula.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nu/sirmilik/WET4/activ/narval-narwhal-640x360.jpg?modified=20240104181535)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/sirmilik/activ/faune-wildlife)

### [Floe edge safari](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/sirmilik/activ/faune-wildlife)

See polar bears, whales, seals and birds where ice meets open water.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nu/sirmilik/WET4/index/ours-bears-640x360.jpg?modified=20240104195818)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/sirmilik/info)

### [Auyuittuq National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/auyuittuq)

Home to spectacular natural landmarks like the Akshayuk Pass, Thor Peak and the Penny Ice Cap, Auyuittuq is the “land that never melts” in Inuktitut.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nu/qausuittuq-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220409)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/qausuittuq)

### [Qausuittuq National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/qausuittuq)

A home for the endangered Peary caribou and a traditional Inuit hunting and fishing area on Bathurst Island in Nunavut’s High Arctic.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nu/quttinirpaaq-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220410)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/quttinirpaaq)

### [Quttinirpaaq National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/quttinirpaaq)

Inuktitut for “land at the top of the world”, Quttinirpaaq is Canada’s northernmost national park, a vast arctic wilderness on Ellesmere Island.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nu/ukkusiksalik-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220410)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/ukkusiksalik)

### [Ukkusiksalik National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/ukkusiksalik)

An area rich in arctic wildlife and Inuit history where hundreds of archaeological sites dot the landscape surrounding Wager Bay.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nu/epaves-wrecks-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220409)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nu/epaveswrecks)

### [Wrecks of HMS Erebus and HMS Terror National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nu/epaveswrecks)

Learn about Sir John Franklin’s legendary 1845 expedition and the role of Inuit knowledge in the discoveries of the shipwrecks.
