# Guffy Campground

Current Conditions

Guffy Campground is located 6 miles from Inspiration Point and Angeles Crest Highway (CA-2).

[**General Camping Info**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/angeles/recreation/camping-cabins): Find helpful guidance and tips for an enjoyable trip to the forest!

[**Be Bear Aware!**](https://wildlife.ca.gov/HWC/Black-Bears): Bear safety is critical, for the safety of your group and/or you, as well as our bears. Help keep bears wild by following these tips.

**Overview of Site:**

Guffy Campground is open year-round. However, it is hike-in only from early/mid-November to late-April/early-May due to seasonal closures of Blue Ridge Road (Forest Service Road 3N06).

Guffy Campground is located 6 miles from gate at Inspiration Point and Angeles Crest Highway (CA-2) and 3 miles from Blue Ridge Campground.

**Elevation:** 8,300-feet in elevation

**Amenities:** Six campsites with picnic tables and campfire rings. Vault (non-flush) toilets. _No water available. Please bring enough water for cooking, cleaning, drinking, and extinguishing campfires._

**Hiking:** Access to the Pacific Crest Trail

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Open year-round, but gate at Inspiration Point is closed seasonally to vehicles during winter months.

### Restrictions

[**Know Fire & Other Restrictions Before You Go!**](http://fs.usda.gov/r05/angeles/alerts): These conditions can affect your entire trip. For example, _Extreme_ and _Critical_ fire use restrictions mean no campfires and no portable cooking systems/stoves. Bring cold food and no S'mores.

### Passes and Permits

A [California Campfire Permit](https://permit.preventwildfiresca.org/) is required.

Adventure Pass or Interagency Pass required for each vehicle.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $5 per night

Day Use:

$5 per vehicle per day

$30 per annual

$5 per secondary annual

**Fees:**

**Camping -** No camping fee but an Adventure Pass or America the Beautiful Pass is required for each vehicle

**Day-use -** Adventure Pass or America the Beautiful Pass required for each vehicle = $5/day or $30/year

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 34.34166667

**Longitude:** -117.6544444

### Directions

Located on Blue Ridge Road 6 miles from the intersection of Blue Ridge Rd. and Angeles Crest Highway (the intersection is 1.5 miles west of Big Pines). The road into the campground is a rough dirt road - after the first 5 miles a four-wheel drive is required.
