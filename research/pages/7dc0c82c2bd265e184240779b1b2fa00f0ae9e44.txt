# Beaver Creek Campground

Beaver Creek is a small (8 sites) campground on the banks of scenic Beaver Creek. There are vault restrooms but no water. There is no fee for camping at Beaver Creek.

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Open 24 hours/day

### Restrictions

Water and garbage service are not provided. Bring enough water for drinking, cooking and washing as well as extra water to drown campfires each day. Please take all trash, garbage and dog droppings with you when you leave. Fires permitted in fire rings only subject to local restrictions.  Dogs on leash only. Camping limit 14 days.

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

No fee

### Pet Information

- **Leash Rules:** Required (max 6 feet) in developed sites, trailheads, and often, as mentioned on [this Forest Service webpage](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/klamath/safety-ethics), on busy, narrow, or steep trails to protect wildlife and other users.
- **Safety & Control:** Pets must be restrained or under strict voice control at all times, according to regulations . Uncontrolled dogs that harass wildlife or people may be removed.
- **Restrictions:** Pets are generally prohibited in swimming areas.
- **Camping:** Pets cannot be left unattended at campsites.
- **Service Animals:** Service dogs are permitted in all areas, even where pets are restricted

### Latitude / Longitude
