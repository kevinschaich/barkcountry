# Alerts

Be sure to check the Alerts page before recreating on the Boise National Forest. Simply knowing the Forest Orders, current conditions, alerts, and closures prepares you for a pleasurable outdoor experience with no surprises! This webpage also provides current regional information about restrictions, closures, alerts, and advisories.
