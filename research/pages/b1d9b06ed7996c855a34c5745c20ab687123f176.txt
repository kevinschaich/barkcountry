# Sand Mountain Trailhead: Trail \#1175

Trail #1175 is a motorized, single track, motorcycle trail that is 9.5-mile out-and-back, near Clark, Colorado and provides access to the top of Sand Mountain. Generally considered a challenging route, it takes an average of 5 hours, 26 minutes to complete. This trail is great for hiking and it's unlikely you'll encounter many other people while exploring. The best times to visit this trail are July through August.

Dogs are welcome and may be off-leash in some areas.

## Getting There

### Directions

Directions from Steamboat Springs, go west on US Hwy 40 to County Road 129. Travel 28 miles north of Steamboat Springs, turn left onto County Road 62, 4 1/2 miles. Turn right on FS 42 Road, turn left, south, on 480 Road, rough 4x4 road, take right on 480.1A to the end of road.
