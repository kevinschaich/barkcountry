Hot Springs has something to offer for the whole family, from hiking trials to architecture and ancient springs. Hot Springs was first protected for its therapeutic waters and designed to be the nation's destination for health. Today the park offers forested solitude mixed with urban comforts. Rangers offer a sneak peek at those stories while showing you places to explore, sites to see, and even fresh spring water to drink.<br>## Top Things to Know<br>01. **There are no opportunities to soak in the thermal springs outdoors.**<br>    <br>    <br>    Soaking in the springs is only permitted in two of the bathhouses, the [Buckstaff](https://www.buckstaffbaths.com/) and the [Quapaw](http://quapawbaths.com/). The Arlington Hotel and Spa also offers spa services. Please contact them for more information.<br>02. **We are an urban park.**<br>    <br>    <br>    Nestled in the heart of downtown Hot Springs, the National Park is surrounded by shops, diners, roads, and other attractions. There is a network of [hiking trails](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/trails.htm) and beautiful mountain overlooks for those looking to escape the feeling of city life that is also a part of the Park. The national park visitor center is located in the Fordyce Bathhouse at 369 Central Avenue. The city of Hot Springs visitor center is at 629 Central Avenue. <br>03. **There is no entrance fee.**<br>    <br>    <br>    Everybody is welcome to walk through and enjoy the park as often as they please.<br>04. **Reservations are required at Gulpha Gorge Campground.**<br>    <br>    <br>    Reservations must be made in advance for all 40 sites at Gulpha Gorge through [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/247559). All sites have full RV hook-ups and are $34 per night. Reservations can be made up to six months in advance.<br>05. **There are metered parking and parking garages in town.**<br>    <br>    <br>    There is metered street parking throughout downtown and at the Exchange Street Parking Garage, at 128 Exchange Street. For more information, visit the [city of Hot Springs parking website](https://www.cityhs.net/1845/Parking).<br>06. **There is a cell phone tour on bathhouse row.**<br>    <br>    <br>    With several stops all along the Row, you can learn about the park's history any time of day.<br>07. **Pets are welcome!**<br>    <br>    <br>    Pets are allowed throughout the park and on all trails, just not in federal buildings. Please keep your pet on a leash and pick up after them.<br>08. **It is hot and humid in the summer.**<br>    <br>    <br>    Stay hydrated, bring plenty of water, wear a hat, use a parasol, and stay cool.<br>09. **The City of Hot Springs has fun events all year long.**<br>    <br>    <br>    From moonlit bike rides to bathtub races to art walks and everything in-between, there's always something fun happening in town. Be sure to check out the City's [Upcoming Events](https://www.hotsprings.org/) page for more information.<br>10. **Arkansas is the "Natural State", enjoy it beyond our Park!**<br>    <br>    <br>    With seven other NPS units and fifty-two state parks, you'll find plenty to do throughout the state. Visit the [Arkansas State Parks website](https://www.arkansasstateparks.com/) and the [Arkansas NPS website](https://www.nps.gov/state/ar/index.htm) to learn more.<br>[Basic Information](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)<br>![Visitors receive information from a park ranger at the visitor center front desk](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/hosp/crop16_9/6E2A4554-DFAD-5527-38E8E32630D97845.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)<br>Learn the basics before you visit!<br>[Directions & Transportation](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/directions.htm)<br>![A man looking for direction on a trail](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/hosp/crop16_9/B1CE0820-D98E-0D56-5E6DA05BC9B42683.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)<br>Get travel directions and view a park map.<br>[Calendar](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/calendar.htm)<br>![A park ranger speaks to a group of visitors on a tour of the Fordyce Bathhouse](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/hosp/crop16_9/6E663BEF-E902-E74A-95965B597F8C9DC4.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)<br>Check out our calendar of events!<br>[Things To Do](https://www.nps.gov/hosp/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)<br>![An areal view of bathhouse row during golden hour ](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/hosp/crop16_9/B152AB7F-F5ED-2402-1308D161A3AC0B03.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)<br>Explore, Learn, and Relax!
