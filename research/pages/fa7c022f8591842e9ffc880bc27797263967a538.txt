# Pets

|     |
| --- |
| ![A leashed dog on the Prairie Vista trail.](https://www.nps.gov/wica/planyourvisit/images/20190925_182517.jpg?maxwidth=1300&autorotate=false)A dog on a leash on the Prairie Vista Trail, one of two pet friendly trails in the park. <br>NPS Photo / Callie Tominsky<br>Pets are welcome at Wind Cave National Park within the following limits:<br>- **At all times pets must be restrained on a leash no longer than six feet in length.** Leashes protect dogs from becoming lost and from potential encounters with wildlife as well as protecting park wildlife and plants.<br>- **Pets should not be left unattended.** Summer sunshine poses a threat to pets left in vehicles. On a sunny day, even in temperatures as low as 60°F, temperatures inside a vehicle can reach dangerous levels. On an 85°F day the temperature inside a car, even with the windows cracked, can reach over 100°F (38°C) within 10 minutes.<br>- **Pet owners are responsible for removing pet waste** from campgrounds, picnic areas, parking lots, roads, and other developed areas.<br>- **Pets are not permitted in the back-country areas of the park.** This includes areas near park roadways and most of the trails – see below for exceptions.<br>#### Where CAN I go with my pet?<br>- Grassy areas near the visitor center.<br>- The Elk Mountain Campground.<br>- Both the [Prairie Vista Trail and Elk Mountain Campground Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/pet-friendly-hikes-wica.htm).<br>#### Where CAN'T I go with my pet?<br>- Ranger-led programs and cave tours.<br>- Public buildings including the visitor center.<br>- The backcountry or any trails except those listed above.<br>#### Pet Boarding in Hot Springs and Custer:<br>- **Fall River Veterinary Clinic:** (605) 745-3786; Must have proof of vaccinations.<br>- **Southern Hills Animal Clinic:** (605) 673-4996<br>## More Information<br>For more information about pet rules and regulations at Wind Cave, read the [36 Code of Federal Regulation (CFR) 2.15 in the Superintendent's Compendium.](https://www.nps.gov/wica/learn/management/lawsandpolicies.htm) |
