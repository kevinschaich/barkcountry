# Rouge National Urban Park

Access to Zoo Road Day Use Area and Rouge Beach and Marsh is currently closed.

Please visit the [important bulletin](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/securite-safety/bulletins) page for more information.

Explore one of the largest urban parks in North America, conveniently located in the Greater Toronto Area. Discover the natural wonders of Canada through the park's rich assembly of forests, creeks, farms and trails as well as marshland, a beach on Lake Ontario and human history spanning 10,000 years.

**Please note:** Parking lots in Rouge National Urban Park may close temporarily in inclement weather.

Flooding from the Rouge River may occur during times of heavy rain or snowmelt. Visit the [TRCA Flood Forecasting and Warning Centre](https://trca.ca/conservation/flood-risk-management/flood-forecasting-warning/) to check for any active flood messages. Always exercise caution around water.

### Contact Rouge National Urban Park

**General inquiries:**

416-264-2020

[rouge@pc.gc.ca](mailto:rouge@pc.gc.ca)

**For emergencies within the park:**

Call 911

**Hours of operation**

Rouge National Urban Park is free and open to the public 365 days a year.

Parking lots are open from 7:30 am to 9 pm daily.

Visitors are responsible for their own safety.
