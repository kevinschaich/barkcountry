# Gros Morne National Park

### Visitor Centre Closure on Monday November 2

Please be advised that the Gros Morne National Park Visitor Centre in Rocky Harbour will be closed on Monday November 2. It will reopen for regular off-season hours on Tuesday November 3.

Thanks for your understanding!

Soaring fjords and moody mountains tower above a diverse panorama of beaches and bogs, forests and barren cliffs. Shaped by colliding continents and grinding glaciers, Gros Morne’s ancient landscape is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nl/grosmorne/WET4/GrosMorne-tablelands-640x360.jpg?modified=20221125152908)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nl/grosmorne/activ/experiences/tablelands)

### [The Tablelands: See the Earth Naked](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nl/grosmorne/activ/experiences/tablelands)

Don’t miss the internationally known Tablelands, a rare outcrop of the Earth’s mantle pushed skyward half a billion years ago. Explore on your own, with a guide or with the Parks app.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nl/grosmorne/WET4/GrosMorne-western-brook-pond-640x360.jpg?modified=20221125152908)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nl/grosmorne/activ/experiences/western-brook)

### [Cruise the freshwater fjord of Western Brook Pond](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nl/grosmorne/activ/experiences/western-brook)

Behold the spectacular glacier-carved land-locked fjord, waterfalls cascading from 2000 feet that often turn to mist before reaching the pond, billion year-old cliffs, and frequent wildlife sightings.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nl/grosmorne/WET4/GrosMorne-gros-morne-mountain-640x360.jpg?modified=20221125152907)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nl/grosmorne/activ/experiences/randonnee-hiking/mgm-gmm)

### [Trek to the panoramic views atop Gros Morne Mountain’s summit](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nl/grosmorne/activ/experiences/randonnee-hiking/mgm-gmm)

Trek to the top of Gros Morne Mountain, the park’s highest point, on a challenging, one-day, 17-kilometre loop. Hike from lowland to alpine terrain amid wildflowers and wildlife, enjoying panoramic views from the summit. To protect wildlife, the mountain is closed to hikers beginning on May 1 and re-opens on June 28.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

### [Port au Choix National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/portauchoix)

See prehistoric artifacts from four ancient Aboriginal cultures that inhabited Newfoundland’s rugged northwest coast. Visit an ancient burial ground, hike coastal trails and see prehistoric artifacts including slate spears, harpoon tips and ivory daggers.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nl/LAnse-aux-Meadows-NHSlocation640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220707)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/meadows)

### [L'Anse aux Meadows National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/meadows)

Tour the only known Viking site and the earliest known evidence of European presence in North America. Experience Norse life and see original 11th century artifacts at this UNESCO World heritage site.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nl/Red-Bay-NHS-location-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220708)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/redbay)

### [Red Bay National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/redbay)

Red Bay was once the largest and most important commercial whaling station in the world. See the restored chalupa, explore archaeological treasures and original artifacts from the period at this UNESCO World Heritage site.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nl/Torngat-Mountains-NP-location-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220708)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nl/torngats)

### [Torngat Mountains National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nl/torngats)

Amid jagged peaks and vast glacial valleys, polar bears and caribou roam the Torngat Mountains, for centuries the homeland of Inuit who today now welcome visitors to experience a dramatic landscape where nature and culture meet.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nl/AUKMM-NPR-location-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220707)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nl/mealy)

Located in Labrador, the glacially-rounded, bare rock summits of the Mealy Mountains reach up to 1180 meters to overlook Lake Melville. ### [Hopedale Mission National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nl/hopedalemission)

A complex of large, wooden buildings constructed by the Moravian Church at Hopedale, Labrador stand starkly silhouetted against the rocky shoreline of the vast, barren landscape. Official recognition refers to the cultural landscape comprised of the mission buildings on their shoreline site.
