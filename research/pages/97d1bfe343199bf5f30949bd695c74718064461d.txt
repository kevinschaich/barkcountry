It became a National Forest on March 4, 1907. On July 1, 1908 Grantsville National Forest and Salt Lake National Forest were added. In 1973 Wasatch was combined administratively with <b>Cache National Forest</b>, creating <b>Wasatch-Cache National Forest</b>. In descending order of acreage, the <b>Wasatch National Forest</b> portion is located in Summit, Tooele, Salt Lake, Davis, Uinta (Wyoming), Duchesne, Wasatch, Morgan, Utah, Weber, and Juab counties in Utah except Uinta, which is in southwestern Wyoming. Its total area was 908,731 acres (3,677.50 km<sup>2</sup>), comprising 56.44% of the combined Wasatch-Cache's 1,610,184 acres (6,516.18 km<sup>2</sup>) as of 2008. There are local ranger district offices in Kamas and Salt Lake City in Utah, and in Evanston and Mountain View in Wyoming. Administrative headquarters reside with the combined Uinta-Wasatch-Cache National Forest in South Jordan, Utah.</p>"}
