# Point Pelee National Park

At the southernmost point of the Canadian mainland - Point Pelee National Park, experience nature like never before. Whether you cycle, paddle, or hike Canada’s second smallest and most ecologically diverse national park, you’ll be immersed in an unforgettable eco-adventure.

## Visiting Point Pelee National Park

### [Activities and experiences](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ)

Things to do all year round including: birding, hiking, canoeing and kayaking, cycling, swimming, camping and more.

### [Plan your visit](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/visit)

How to get here, hours of operation, fees, directions, park rules, rental information and more to help plan your visit.

### [Parks Canada App](https://parks.canada.ca/voyage-travel/app)

An interactive digital map of Point Pelee National Park is available on the Parks Canada App.

### [Camping and overnight accomodation](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/otentik)

Camping in oTENTik A-frame cabins located in Camp Henry. Enjoy a comfortable & convenient stay available all year round.

### [Fees](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/visit/tarifs-fees)

Prices of admissions including: daily admissions, seasonal passes, Parks Canada Discovery Passes, facility rentals, and camping.

### [Safety and guidelines](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/securite-safety)

Plan ahead and prepare for your visit with important bulletins, information on ticks, unsafe swimming conditions, paddling safety and more.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

### [Fort Malden National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/on/malden)

Discover the history of the War of 1812 and the Rebellions of 1837-38 with costumed interpreters, historic buildings, and more at Fort Malden.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/on/on-southwold-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126215936)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/on/southwold)

### [Southwold Earthworks National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/on/southwold)

Walk where Canada's earliest inhabitants did and imagine the Attiwandaron longhouses and palisade walls that once stood at Southwold Earthworks.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/on/on-hmcs-haida-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126215935)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/on/haida)

### [HMCS Haida National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/on/haida)

Discover HMCS Haida National Historic Site in Hamilton, Ontario. Learn about the ship's role in the Second World War, the Korean Conflict and more.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/on/Woodside-National-Historic-Site-640.jpg?modified=20221126215936)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/on/woodside)

### [Woodside National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/on/woodside)

Discover the Victorian childhood home of Canadian Prime Minister William Lyon Mackenzie King at Woodside National Historic Site in Kitchener, Ontario.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/on/640x360-ON-FG-fort-george.jpg?modified=20221126215933)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/on/fortgeorge)

### [Fort George National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/on/fortgeorge)

Discover the history of The War of 1812 by speaking with costumed interpreters, touring historic buildings, and watching demonstrations at Fort George.
