# Wyandotte Campground

Current Conditions

Scheduled to close for the season October 11, 2026.

This campground has 30 campsites, 12 of which are reservable.  Campground amenities include water, flush toilets and trash service along with paved roads. Campsite amenities include picnic tables, fire ring and paved parking pad. Fishing, day hiking, nature viewing and water recreation are some of the local attractions.

## Reservations

[Reservations available at recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/251446)

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Generally mid-June to fall.

### Operational Hours

Check in: 2:00 p.m.; Check-out: 12:00 p.m.

### Restrictions

- 14 day limit
- 8 people per site
- 2 vehicles per site
- OHV use prohibited in campground
- [Be Bear Aware](https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/bears)

[https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/bears](https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/bears)

### Passes and Permits

[Senior and Access passes honored for single campsites.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/passes-permits)

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $23 per night

Double Site: $38 per night

### Pet Information

Dogs are to be on leash at all times.
