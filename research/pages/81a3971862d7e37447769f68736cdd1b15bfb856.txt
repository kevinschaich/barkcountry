# Mahogany Cove Campground

**Overview**

The Mahogany Cove Campground is located in a grassy meadow along the Beaver Canyon Scenic Byway (Hwy-153), just 12 miles from Beaver, Utah. At 7,200 feet elevation this area is popular for visitors to enjoy hiking, mountain biking and fishing in the nearby Beaver River.

**Facilities**

The campground consists of one large group site, which is made up of seven individual sites that can accommodate a total of 150 people and 20 vehicles. Reservations for the group site are posted at the campground in advance. If the entire site is not reserved, individual sites can be used on a first-come-first-served basis. The first site has several picnic tables, a large grill and campfire ring. The other six sites each contain one picnic table, fire ring and grill. Vault toilet bathrooms and drinking water are available. Roads and parking areas within the campground are gravel and a large meadow is located just inside the entrance.

**Natural Features**

Stands of mahogany, pinion, juniper and ponderosa pine trees provide partial shade. The campground offers an overlook just outside the campground displaying beautiful views of Beaver Canyon.

**Recreation**

Fishing for rainbow and brook trout is popular on the Beaver River, which is just a short drive down the canyon below the campground. Beaver Canyon Scenic Byway curves past the campground, offering 17 miles of scenic driving through the beautiful Tushar Mountains. Numerous lakes and streams provide fishing, boating and canoeing opportunities.

# Mahogany Cove Campground

# MAHOGANY COVE CAMPGROUND

Part of [Fishlake National Forest](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/1032)

## Overview

Mahogany Cove Campground is located in a grassy meadow along the Beaver Canyon Scenic Byway just 12 miles from Beaver, Utah, at an elevation of 7,200 feet. Visitors enjoy hiking, mountain biking and fishing in the nearby Beaver River.


## Facilities

The campground consists of one group site made up of seven individual sites that can accommodate a total of 150 people and 20 vehicles. Reservations are posted at the campground in advance. If the entire site is not reserved, the individual sites can be used on a first-come, first-served basis.

The first site has several picnic tables, a large grill and campfire circle. The other six sites each contain one picnic table, fire ring and grill. Vault toilets and drinking water are provided. Roads and parking spurs are gravel. A large play field is located just inside the entrance.

## Natural Features

Stands of mahogany, pinion, juniper and ponderosa pine trees provide partial shade. The campground offers pretty views of the Tushar Range. An overlook just outside the campground has views of Beaver Canyon.


## Recreation

Fishing for rainbow and brook trout is popular on the Beaver River, which is just a short drive below the campground. Hiking, mountain biking and hunting are popular activities in the area.


## Nearby Attractions

Beaver Canyon Scenic Byway curves past the campground, offering 17 miles of scenic driving through the beautiful Tushar Mountains. Numerous lakes and streams provide fishing, boating and canoeing opportunities.


## Important Notices

- SR-153 wraps around three sides of the campground; exercise caution with children and pets.

- Street legal non-street legal vehicles are allowed for access and egress only.

- There are no electricity, water, or sewer hookups at the campground.

- This is a 'pack it in-pack it out' facility. No garbage services are available. Please bring your own garbage bags.

- Camping is limited to 16 days.

- Any vehicles or trailers that can not fit in the designated site must be relocated to another site purchased at full site price. Check area map for available free parking

## General Information

_Hwy-153 wraps around three sides of the campground; please exercise caution with children and pets._

### Seasons of Use

May - October

### Restrictions

**Restrictions**

- OHVs are not allowed to operate in the campground and must remain on trailers for transport to OHV trailheads.
- Dogs must be on a leash.
- Fireworks are prohibited on all National Forest lands.
- [16-day camping limit](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/fishlake/alerts/16-day-camping-limit-occupancy-and-use-order-0408-25-01)

### Passes and Permits

[**Passes**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/fishlake/passes)

America the Beautiful Passes (Senior, Access, and Military) are honored at Campgrounds managed by High Country Recreation on the Fishlake National Forest and offer a 50% discount on most single sites. _Some restrictions apply_. Federal Recreation Passes can be purchased at any ranger district office or at [Recreational Passes \| USGS Store](https://store.usgs.gov/recreational-passes).

- Pass must be valid. No expired passes will be accepted.
- Must present card to campground host for first-come-first-serve sites.
- Card information may be added at checkout for valid sites when making reservations through Recreation.gov.

### Fee Site and Info

Fees are subject to change annually. Current rates can be found on Recreation.Gov. Please visit [Mahogany Cove Campground](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234615) for specific site pricing and availability. Fees are also posted within the campground and can be found on the kiosk next to the pay tube.

- If campsites have not already been reserved and paid for online, fees for first-come-first-served sites need to be paid within 30 minutes of arrival.
- Fees for FCFS sites can be deposited in the pay tube located next to the kiosk.



_Please Note: All campgrounds on the Fishlake National Forest are managed by High Country Recreation under the terms and conditions of a concession permit that was issued to them by the Forest Service in 2020. Email:_ [_highcountryrec@gmail.com_](mailto:highcountryrec@gmail.com)


### Pet Information

- Pets must be leashed at all times within this recreation area. This policy is in place to ensure the safety and enjoyment of all visitors and to protect wildlife.
- Clean up dog waste and dispose of it properly.
- Keep dogs from chasing, harassing, and disturbing wildlife, livestock or other visitors.
