## Pets in the Park

PETS ON LEASH

Welcome to Mount Rainier National Park! We hope you and your pet enjoy your visit. In 
order to protect wildlife, other visitors, and your animal, please respect the following rules:

• You may walk your pet along roads and in parking lots, campgrounds and picnic areas. 
Pets are not permitted on park trails or in off-trail areas. Keep it leashed at all times and 
clean up after it.

• Pets are not permitted in buildings, at ranger programs, or in the backcountry.

• Your pet is welcome in campgrounds as long as it is crated, on a leash, or otherwise 
under physical restraint at all times, and remains quiet.

• Pets are not permitted in park inns.

• Never leave your pet unattended in a campground or outside buildings.

The temptation to track and chase wild animals can be overwhelming for even the most obedient 
pet. Because Mount Rainier is a national park, the wildlife who call it “home” receive special 
protection. Remember that a leash can protect your animal, too. Every year pets are lost in the park 
and never found. Don’t let that happen to your pet!

Ask a ranger about trails *outside* Mount Rainier National Park where you can hike with your dog. 
Please help us protect this special place by following these rules.
