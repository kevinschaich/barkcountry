# Pacific Crest Trail - Snoqualmie North Trailhead

Provides access to: [Pacific Crest Trail](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mbs/recreation/groups/pacific-crest-national-scenic-trail-recreation) 2000 Snoqualmie Pass North. Heading North from the trailhead, the Pacific Crest Trail enters [Alpine Lakes Wilderness](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mbs/recreation/alpine-lakes-wilderness-mt-baker-snoqualmie) and junctions with Commonwealth Basin Trail 1033 after 2.5 miles.

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Open 24 hours/day

### Restrictions

**Restrictions**:

- Trail is closed to bicycles and motorized vehicles.
- Dogs must be on a leash.
- The Pacific Crest Trail travels through designated Wilderness where wilderness regulations apply.

### Passes and Permits

[Recreation Passes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mbs/passes) honored for day use: Digital Passes, Northwest Forest Pass, and Interagency Recreation Passes. _Washington State Discover Pass is not valid at US Fee Sites._

Wilderness Permits are required for day use and overnight trips in [Alpine Lakes Wilderness](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mbs/recreation/alpine-lakes-wilderness-mt-baker-snoqualmie). Permits are free and available on a self-issue basis at trailheads.

### Fee Site and Info

Day Use: $5 per vehicle per day

$30 per annual

Day use fee payment options: Scan & Pay on-site or display a valid [Recreation Pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mbs/passes).

_\*Suggestion: Download the_ [_recreation.gov app_](https://www.recreation.gov/mobile-app) _before your trip to have the ability to scan & pay digitally on-site._

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 47.42800618

**Longitude:** -121.41346959

### Elevation

3,000 feet

### Directions

From North Bend travel east on I-90 to exit #52. Turn north. Take the first immediate right into the Pacific Crest Trail 2000 parking lot. A stock loading lot is located to the left. The road continues straight to the general hiking access and parking lot.

### Parking

There is space for about 70 cars at the trailhead parking area.

### [Pacific Crest National Scenic Trail Recreation](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mbs/recreation/groups/pacific-crest-national-scenic-trail-recreation)

The Pacific Crest National Scenic Trail (PCT) extends 2,650 miles from Canada to Mexico. Although some hikers have traveled the length in one trip, many take the trail in segments over a period of years. The northern section of the Pacific Crest National Scenic Trail is rugged and it is the wettest section along the entire route. Make sure to plan ahead and be prepared.
