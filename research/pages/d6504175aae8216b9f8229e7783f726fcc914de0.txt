# Little Hole Boat Launch Area

Little Hole boat launch on the Green River, Section B.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

This boat launch area is open year-round.

### Fee Site and Info

$5/vehicle/day, $15/vehicle/week, $35/vehicle/year

### Pet Information

Dogs must be on a leash

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 40.910719

**Longitude:** -109.315144

### Elevation

5560

### Directions

From Vernal, Utah, take U.S. 191 north for 47 miles. Turn off U.S. 191 just north of Dutch John onto Forest Road 075. Travel east about 6 miles to the boat launch site.

From Manila, Utah, take Highway 44 for 28 miles. Turn left on U.S. 191 and go north for about 11 miles. Turn off U.S. 191 just north of Dutch John onto Forest Road 075 and go east about 6 miles to the boat launch site.

From Rock Springs, Wyoming, take I-80 west toward Green River about 6 miles to the junction with U.S. 191/Flaming Gorge Road. Take U.S. 191 south for about 62 miles to Dutch John. Turn off U.S. 191 just north of Dutch John onto Forest Road 075 and go east about 6 miles to the boat launch site.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Restrooms Available.


### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

Available May through September

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Other Amenities

Vault restrooms are open year-round, flushing restrooms open May through September.
