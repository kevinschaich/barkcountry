# Adventure awaits on the Klamath National Forest

The forest includes the Klamath National Wild & Scenic River and several [**wilderness**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/klamath/wilderness), [**botanical**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/klamath/recreation/botanical-areas), and [**geologic areas**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/klamath/natural-resources/geology). The forest is a haven for campers, hikers, wildlife watchers, hunters, fishermen, mountain bikers, white water enthusiasts, geologists, botanists, and naturalists.

![Hiker on Trail](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/klamath/image/Hiker%20on%20trail.png?h=7a498e30)

With nearly one million acres for your enjoyment, relaxation, and exploration, you can experience the solitude of the wilderness or enjoy camping near others in developed campgrounds.

![Stack of chopped Firewood](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/klamath/image/Firewood%20Stack.png?h=7a498e30)

Cutting firewood for personal use is a popular pastime for many people who live near the Klamath National Forest. Make sure you have a permit and "Know Before You Go." Firewood cutting restriction information can be obtained by phoning 530-841-4588 at any time.

![Wildland firefighter sprays water on grass fire.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/klamath/image/54284068808_f1b057d030_c.jpg?h=97f17f4d)

The Klamath National Forest posts closures, alerts, and critical fire information for your safety.

## Butte Valley National Grassland

![Butte Valley National Grasslands Sign with Mount Shasta in Background](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/klamath/image/ButteVallyGrasslands-web.jpg?h=bb45b0fb)

Butte Valley National Grassland occurs in a scenic basin within the southern Cascade Range, located in northern California. The National Grassland is 18,425 acres in size and is the only national grassland in California.

[More Information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/klamath/about-area/butte-valley-national-grassland)
