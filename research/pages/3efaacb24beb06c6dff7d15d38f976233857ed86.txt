# Porcupine Campground

Porcupine Campground is open to self-supported camping as long as weather permits after season closures (no trash collection, water, or cleaning). It is located about two miles north of U.S. Highway 14A, the Medicine Wheel Passage scenic byway, off Porcupine Road 13. The campground is near Jaws Trailhead.

## Reservations

[Make Reservations Here](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/250021)

This site can be reserved by calling Toll Free 1-877-444-6777 (International 518-885-3639 or TDD 877-833-6777) or [on-line](http://www.recreation.gov/camping/Porcupine_Campground_Wy/r/campgroundDetails.do?contractCode=NRSO&parkId=115839&topTabIndex=Search).

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

June - Labor Day, weather dependent

### Restrictions

14 day stay limit

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $20 per night

Limit 8 people per site.

Two non-towed vehicles allowed per site. The first vehicle is included in the basic fee; the second vehicle will be charged an extra fee at the campground.

### Pet Information

Dogs must be on a leash.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 44.83105897

**Longitude:** -107.8589005

From Lovell, WY, take Hwy 14A-- Medicine Wheel Passage Scenic Byway-- east for about 32 miles and turn left on Forest System Road (FSR) 13. Travel for another 2 miles.
