# Doe Point Campground & Day Use

This remote and picturesque 30-site campground is in the midst of old-growth forest and adjacent to [Fish Lake](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/fish-lake), offering some of the best fishing in the area. Of those 30 sites, 5 are walk in tent sites. All sites have fire rings.

A boat launch for Fish Lake (speed limit 10 mph) is located at adjacent [Fish Lake Campground](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/fish-lake-campground), and many amenities are offered at nearby [Fish Lake Resort](https://fishlakeresort.net/). Numerous hiking trails are within easy access, including the Fish Lake, High Lakes, Brown Mountain, and the famous Pacific Crest Trail, as well as the [Sky Lakes Wilderness](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/sky-lakes-wilderness-rogue-river-siskiyou-national-forest) and Mt. McLoughlin Trail systems.

Some sites have a lake view, and there is plenty of shade from the fir and pine trees for those hot afternoons.  Fish Lake is a reservoir used for irrigation, possibly resulting in low water levels in August and September.

# Doe Point Campground

# DOE POINT CAMPGROUND

Part of [Rogue River-Siskiyou National Forest](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/1108)

## Overview

Doe Point Campground is located 39 miles east of Medford, Oregon in the midst of an old growth forest and adjacent to Fish Lake. There are 30 campsites, that can accommodate trailers, rvs and tents and 5 sites that are tent camping only.

Fish Lake Resort is within walking distance of the campground and provides access to a convenience store, cafe and lodging. The lake has a 10 m.p.h. speed limit and there is a boat launch at Fish Lake Day Use Area and at the Resort.

## Facilities

Drinking water and Fush toilets with wash basins are provided as well as drinking water. Each site has a picnic table and fire ring.

## Natural Features

Fish Lake is a reservoir with 483 acres surface area and is located at 4,639 feet elevation. The lake is supplied by over 45 underground volcanic streams. The southern shore provides really good views of Mount McLoughlin and is bordered by a 2,000 year old lava flow from Brown Mountain. The northern shore accommodates 2 campgrounds and a resort.

## Recreation

Doe Point Campground offers biking, fishing, swimming, paddle boating and hiking opportunities.

There is easy access to Fish Lake Trail, High Lakes Trail, Brown Mountain Trail and the Pacific Crest Trail. Sky Lakes Wilderness and Mount McLoughlin Trail system are less than an hour drive away.

## Nearby Attractions

Campgrounds nearby include adjacent Fish Lake Campground and North Fork Campground.

Lake of the Woods Resort is 8 miles east of Doe Point Campground and provides opportunity for dining, swimming, hiking and boating.

Crater Lake National Park is 53 miles north of this campground.

## Contact Info

For facility specific information, please call (541) 865-2735.


## Important Notices

- Caution: Bears frequent the area. Be aware, keep a clean camp and store all food items in closed containers.
- One vehicle is included in campsite fee, an additional fee for extra vehicles must be paid at campground.

- First-come, first-served sites are available.
- Off-road vehicles are not allowed in the campground.
- For current conditions at Doe Point Campground please call the High Cascades Ranger District at (541)560-3400.


## Directions

From Medford, OR, take OR-140 (Lake of the Woods Hwy) about 20 miles to Fish Lake Campground. From Klamath Falls, take OR-140 (Falls Hwy) about 4 miles past Lake of the Woods to the Campground. Fish Lake Campground is adjacent to, but not part of Fish Lake Resort.

## General Information

Fish Lake is a reservoir sits at 4,639 feet elevation in the southern Cascades. The lake is supplied by more than 45 underground volcanic streams.

The southern shore provides great views of Mount McLoughlin and is bordered by a 2,000 year old lava flow from Brown Mountain.

### Seasons of Use

May - October

### Restrictions

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfires in developed fire pits only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Dogs must be on leash
- OHV/ATV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $20 per night

Additional Vehicle Fee: $9 per vehicle per night

Day use:

$5 per vehicle per day

**Current rates as of 2026 are:**

Overnight Use:

- Single site: $25 per night
- Walk-in: $12.50 per night
- Extra vehicle: $5  per day

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in the campground. All pets must be kept on a leash, and owners are required to clean up pet waste.
