### Protected area. Enriching nature experiences.

A true natural jewel of the Abitibi-Témiscamingue region, Parc national d'Aiguebelle is home to a variety of geological phenomenon with evocative names: giant's kettle, pillow lava...A 22-metre-high suspended walkway offers a thrilling crossing over a gigantic fault. From this vantage point you can admire scenery that has been deeply marked by the work of billions of years. A wide range of discovery activities explore a diversity of wildlife species: moose, beaver, mink, heron and osprey, to name just a few. In winter and summer alike, Parc national d'Aiguebelle is the perfect spot to take your time!

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/aig/accordeon/aig_canot.jpg)

By canoe, the must-sees are definitely the majestic La Haie and Sault fault lakes. These twin lakes offer a guaranteed change of scenery. On an excursion, you’ll discover the most impressive aspects of the park’s geology and geomorphology, but also its plant and animal life. There are some spots set up for picnicking or relaxing.

Paddle on Lac Patrice into the heart of the park, an oasis of peace in the boreal forest. This is an ideal place for a peaceful adventure, where calmness reigns everywhere. Le Patineur rustic shelter offers a fabulous view of the lake. If you’re lucky, you may get a chance to see the Northern Lights or the largest cervid of our forests, the moose.

Watercraft are also available on vast Lac Loïs where you can visit the lake’s many islands and check out the Great Blue Heron colony. Lac Matissard is also accessible by canoe. Rental is available at the Ojibway campground information kiosk. Go to Le Garde-Feu convenience of Abijévis campground for more information.

### [![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/aig/accordeon/aig_act_arriere_pays_ete.jpg)Expedition in the Backcountry](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/aig/index.dot?language_id=1\#Itemc8d144f8-8a58-4ebd-a52a-80ad767e9e07)

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/aig/accordeon/aig_act_arriere_pays_ete.jpg)

Since people already think of Abitibi as a land on the edge of civilization, imagine the backcountry of Parc national d’Aiguebelle: an intense concentration of sovereign nature.

#### Summer

Enter a world of dense vegetation where your chances of seeing wildlife are tenfold. This is the undisputed territory of Ruffed Grouse, black bear, beaver, moose, and Canadian lynx. River otter frolic around the many clear-water lakes nestled in the hollows of the Abijévis hills.

Discover Lac aux Rochers, a phenomenon in itself because the elevation makes it invisible to its neighbour, Lac aux Geais, which is just a few steps away. Then get aligned with the twin hills, where magnificent views will leave you speechless. The most daring adventurers will continue on to Lac de l’Indien to set up camp.

[Summer backcountry area (in French only)](https://www.sepaq.com/dotAsset/09fd5790-a69f-484b-b843-b63e451b0fff.pdf)

#### Winter

The park bathes in this deeply peaceful setting, which gives you a sense of being alone in the world. On one of the frozen lakes, more than anywhere else, there’s an excellent probability of coming across a moose, unless the wolves put on a howling concert to the rhythm of….your accelerated heartbeat.

Will you dare to tread on the thick layer of snow that covers the Aiguebelle territory?

[Winter backcountry area (in French only)](https://www.sepaq.com/dotAsset/54b29170-3249-49dd-a1bb-fc65433d6125.pdf)

#### Reservation Terms and Conditions

To go into the backcountry, it is mandatory to have an access permit. See [Reservation Terms and Conditions](https://www.sepaq.com/quoi-faire/expedition-arriere-pays-hiver.dot#consignes) for all the important details.

Period: year-round (access permit required)

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/aig/accordeon/aig_act_sup.jpg)

All summer long, you can enjoy paddle surfing on Matissard and Loïs lakes. Of Hawaiian origin, paddle surfing is an activity that combines surfing and kayaking. Designed for calm waters, the board is larger (longer, wider, and thicker) and therefore more stable than the traditional surfboard devised for waves. Safe and accessible, paddle surfing is offered at the Abijévis and Ojibway campgrounds.

In order to make its trails accessible to persons with reduced mobility, Parc national d’Aiguebelle has acquired outdoor equipment for persons with reduced mobility. The equipment can be rented free of charge at the rental centre upon presentation of the CAL card and upon availability.

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/aig/accordeon/raquette_aig.jpg)

Only the crunch of your snowshoes digging into the snow and your breath turning the crystalline air into a fine mist graze the silence of Aiguebelle. On Lac La Haie or from the top of La Trompeuse hill, every step you take leaves your mark among those of the hare, Ruffed Grouse and moose that traverse these astonishing landscapes. A total of 40 kilometres, divided into four marked trails, give you a chance to take the full measure of your human dimension in this northern whiteness. Do you see the pretty rustic shelter in the distance? This could be your base camp for one or several nights. Two hours, two days, two weeks…take all the time you need in the exhilarating winter purity of Parc national d’Aiguebelle.

## Park access authorization

There is a limit to the number of visitors that can enter a national park on any given day. It is therefore **strongly recommended** that you purchase your right of access online before a visit or that you acquire an annual pass. This guarantees you access to the park, will ensure a smoother arrival at the site and avoid unnecessary travel when the maximum capacity is reached. It can be purchased up to 30 days before a visit. You may be denied access to a sector, a trail, or an activity if a parking lot is full or if the maximum capacity has been reached.

## Important

### Don't move firewood

A single piece of firewood can destroy millions of trees. Invasive insects and diseases can hide inside it. Buy it locally. Burn it on site. [Find out more This link will open in a new window.](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/plant-health/forestry/hazards-moving-firewood)
