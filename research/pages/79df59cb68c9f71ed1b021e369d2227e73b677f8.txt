# Tipsu Tyee Campground

Tipsu Tyee is located along the shore of Applegate Lake and is accessible only by the Payette Trail #970 or by boat. Persons accessing this site by boat may find current information about lake levels helpful.

5 campsites (tent-sites)

[![Vicinity map of lower Applegate Lake ](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r06/rogue-siskiyou/image/Lower%20Applegate%20Lake%20Map.jpeg?h=b6236d98)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/tipsu-tyee-campground#)

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Open 24 hours/day

### Restrictions

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfires in developed fire pits only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Dogs must be on leash
- OHV/ATV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in the campground. All pets must be kept on a leash, and owners are required to clean up pet waste.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 42.03613

**Longitude:** -123.12453

### Directions

From Jacksonville, OR, travel South on Highway 238 to Ruch, OR, and turn left on to Upper Applegate Road. Continue about 15 miles to Applegate Lake and turn left on County Road 959 (traveling across the dam). Continue along County Road 959 about 3 miles and turn right on to Forest Road 100. Continue about 0.5 mile along Forest Road 100 to Squaw Arm Parking Area. Hike about 1.25 mile west along Payette Trail to the Campground.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Vault toilet(s)


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Other Amenities

- Forest Service-approved fire rings. Know current fire restrictions before you go by visiting the Alerts and Notice page: [Alerts](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/alerts)
- There is no on-site garbage disposal. Please pack out what you pack in. For more on Leave No Trace Ethics, please visit [lnt.org](https://lnt.org/).
