# Bienville Wildlife Management Area

Bienville Wildlife Management Area is under the management of Mississippi Department of Wildlife, Fisheries, and Parks. For information concerning permits or if you have other questions, contact the local office at 601-859-3425 or go to: [MDWFP - Bienville](https://www.mdwfp.com/wildlife-hunting/wildlife-management-areas).

## General Information

### Passes and Permits

$15 permit fee

## Getting There

### Directions

I-20, Exit 77, Take Hwy 13 north to Hwy 80. Turn right on Hwy 80 and go approx. 4 miles and turn left at Forest Service sign saying National Forest Service Trail. Go approx 6 miles, headquarters on left.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Restroom information is not available for this site.

### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.
