# Recreation Opportunities

[![Camping and Cabins image](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/medium/public/r03/cibola/image/Camping%20in%20Mountainair.jpg?itok=HTArMmsg)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/camping-cabins "Camping and Cabins")

### [Camping and Cabins](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/camping-cabins)

Plan a forest escape with camping or cabin stays perfect for unwinding in nature.


[![red bicycle frame with mounted water bottles](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/bike-closeup.jpg?h=3fe45f0e)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/biking "Biking")

### [Biking](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/biking)

The Cibola National Forest is a popular mountain biking destination with biking opportunities across nearly all the Recreation Regions.


[![A child in a chair with a fishing pole on the banks of a pond](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/fishing-child.jpg?h=a53b61ac)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/fishing "Fishing")

### [Fishing](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/fishing)

Find a fishing location and be sure to follow all laws near you.


[![A hiker walks through a field of yellow wildflowers between pine trees](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/hiking-wildflowers.jpg?h=a1e1a043)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/hiking "Hiking")

### [Hiking](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/hiking)

Roughly 800 miles of trails are available across the Forest and Grasslands for visitors to explore.


[![Two people ride horses up a trail in a pine forest amid wildflowers](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/horse-riding-sandia-trail.jpg?h=a1e1a043)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/horse-riding-and-camping "Horse Riding and Camping")

### [Horse Riding and Camping](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/horse-riding-and-camping)

Many multiple use trails offer the opportunity for riding!


[![Child practices archery](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/Play%20in%20the%20Rain%20Day-Archery%20Practice%2C%20Willamette%20National%20Forest.jpg?h=c3635fa2)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/hunting-shooting "Hunting & Shooting")

### [Hunting & Shooting](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/hunting-shooting)

Find a hunting location and be sure to follow all laws near you.


[![Two people in a blue off-highway vehicle on a forest](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/ohv-offroad.jpg?h=a1e1a043)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/highway-vehicles-ohv "Off-Highway Vehicles (OHV)")

### [Off-Highway Vehicles (OHV)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/highway-vehicles-ohv)

Trails that allow for OHV recreation can be found across the Cibola National Forest and National Grasslands.


[![A child looks a petroglyphs while writing in a notebook](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/outdoor-learning-child.jpg?h=c0b350a8)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/outdoor-science-and-learning "Outdoor Science and Learning")

### [Outdoor Science and Learning](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/outdoor-science-and-learning)

Opportunities for learning are all around you!


[![Two picnic tables in a forest on a sunny day](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/picnic-recreation.jpg?h=a1e1a043)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/picnicking "Picnicking")

### [Picnicking](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/picnicking)

Scenic locations, from remote to semi urban, provide perfect picnicking spots!


[![A road points towards rocky mountain faces](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/drive-sandias.jpg?h=c3635fa2)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/scenic-drives "Scenic Drives")

### [Scenic Drives](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/scenic-drives)

While traveling to your recreation destination, scenic opportunities exist to take in the local landscapes.


[![Lake Marvin boating site](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/Lake%20Marvin%20Boating%20Site.JPG?h=c7d97968)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/water-activities "Water Activities")

### [Water Activities](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/water-activities)

Developed sites for water activities exist on the National Grasslands where you can enjoy boating and fishing.


[![An adult and a child share a sled in the snow](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/cibola/image/snow-sledding.jpg?h=1490aae3)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/winter-sports "Winter Sports")

### [Winter Sports](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/cibola/recreation/opportunities/winter-sports)

One ski area, and multiple other sites for winter recreation, allow for endless possibilities!
