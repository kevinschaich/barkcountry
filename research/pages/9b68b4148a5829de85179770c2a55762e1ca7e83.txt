# Pets

| --- |
| ![a laughing woman riding a bike, attached to a tiny dog by a leash](https://www.nps.gov/dena/planyourvisit/images/pets-2.png?maxwidth=1300&maxheight=1300&autorotate=false&format=webp)Pets must be leashed at all times. They are welcome on roadways and parking lots, but neither on trails nor off-trail, in the wilderness. <br>NPS Photo / Kent Miller<br>![a gray dog and a black dog sitting on a dog bed](https://www.nps.gov/dena/planyourvisit/images/pets.png?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Sometimes your pets are happier staying at home. <br>NPS Photo / Elizabeth Beavers<br>## Keeping Your Pet Safe and Happy<br>Pets are welcome in certain areas in Denali National Park and Preserve, but to protect other visitors, wildlife and your pets themselves, please remember these regulations:<br>- Pets must be leashed (6' or shorter leash) at all times.<br>- You must clean up your pet's poop.<br>- You may not leave a tethered pet unattended.<br>- Pets may be walked on the park road, in parking lots, or on campground roads.<br>- **In general, pets are not permitted on park trails**, nor off-trail in the wilderness. The exceptions to this rule are the Roadside Hiking Trail and the Roadside Bike Trail, where they may be walked on leash. Check out a [map of hiking trails](https://www.nps.gov/dena/planyourvisit/dayhiking.htm) to orient yourself.<br>- Pets are not permitted on any buses in Denali.<br>Please note, there is a temporary closure prohibiting pet goats in the park. There is a chance that they can pass diseases to park wildlife, particularly Dall sheep. [Contact the park superintendent for more details](https://www.nps.gov/common/utilities/sendmail/sendemail.cfm?o=4F80D5BAA2C0BAAC91A91AA1ED12A5A9439352AF52A69FD15810B8&r=/dena/planyourvisit/pets.htm).<br>## Pet Boarding<br>We suggest you contact the [local Chamber of Commerce](https://discoverdenali.org/) for information on kennel services in the area.<br>## Service Animals<br>Qualified [service animals](https://www.nps.gov/dena/planyourvisit/serviceanimals.htm) assisting people with disabilities are allowed throughout the park. If you wish to visit the Sled Dog Kennels with a qualified service animal, please contact a park ranger in advance for more information. |
