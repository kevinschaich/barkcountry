# Camping and overnight accommodations

## Fundy National Park

**Reserve your campsite!**

[1‑877‑RESERVE (737‑3783)](tel:1-877-737-3783)

[Reserve online](https://reservation.pc.gc.ca/)

Whether you prefer to camp in a tent, in a trailer close to amenities, to travel to a wilderness site, we have the experience you are looking for!

### Campgrounds and backcountry sites

Fundy National Park has five front country campgrounds, each providing a unique camping experience. You will find amenities such as washrooms, showers, electrical, sewer and water hookups all within close proximity to other Park services and facilities. You'll find the campground maps here: [Maps and brochures](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nb/fundy/visit/cartes-maps)

As a backcountry traveler, you have your choice of eight backcountry campsites located at Goose River, Marven Lake, Tracey Lake or Chambers Lake.

### Private accommodations

**[Fundy Highlands Motel and Chalets](https://www.fundyhighlandchalets.com/)** operates out of Fundy National Park and offers commercial accommodation options from a central location in the park. Please call or [reserve online](https://www.fundyhighlandchalets.com/) ahead of your visit to ensure availability.

**Web:** [https://www.fundyhighlands.com/](http://www.fundyhighlands.com/)

**Phone:**(506) 887-2930 **or** 1-888-883-8639 (toll-free)

**Email:** [info@fundyhighlands.com](mailto:info@fundyhighlands.com)

### Fees

Fees vary during the peak and shoulder season. Please consult our [detailed fees list](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nb/fundy/visit/tarifs-fees).

- Don't move firewood - Importing firewood is prohibited
- Pets must be kept on a leash at all times
- It is illegal to feed wildlife in national parks
- Keep your campsite clean, animals are attracted by human food and waste
- Excessive noise will not be tolerated, day or night
- Quiet time is from 11 pm to 7 am
- Please refer to our [Hours of operation](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nb/fundy/visit/heures-hours) page for a complete list of operating dates/hours and also check the [General Campground Information](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nb/fundy/activ/camping/regles-rules) page for more information
