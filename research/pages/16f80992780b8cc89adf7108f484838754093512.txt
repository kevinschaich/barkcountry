# Recreation

A myriad of diverse recreation opportunities are available year-round on the Uinta-Wasatch-Cache National Forest. Scenic driving, camping, picnicking, hiking, mountain biking, fishing, boating, wildlife viewing, skiing, and other adventures await visitors.

If your plans include a trip to Pineview or Causey Reservoir be aware of closures that may affect your access. Check out the [Pineview Reservoir Recreation Complex Reconstruction](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/99671b5b29834d43bb2bd6fccae1ab8e) and [Causey Reservoir Area Improvement Projects.](https://arcg.is/4jy8z2) Also Ogden City is implementing the [Pineview Reservoir Pipeline Replacement Project](https://ogdencitypipeline.com/) beginning this fall.
