# Prairie Dog Hunting

In _**Nebraska**_, hunt prairie dog colonies on the Oglala National Grassland. Rules and regulations are here:

-  Nebraska Game and Parks Commission at [outdoornebraska.gov](https://outdoornebraska.gov/) or call 402-471-0641.

In _**South Dakota**_, colonies exist on the Buffalo Gap and Fort Pierre National Grasslands. Rules and regulations are here:

- South Dakota Game, Fish and Parks at [https://gfp.sd.gov/hunt/](https://gfp.sd.gov/hunt/) or call 605-223-7660.

Several areas on [Buffalo Gap National Grassland are _permanently closed_ to prairie dog hunting](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/nebraska/alerts/permanent-prohibition-shooting-prairie-dogs-conata-basin).

## Prairie Dogs in the Grassland Ecosystem

Prairie dogs are a keystone species in grassland ecology. They serve as the food source for the endangered black-footed ferret. Their burrows are used by burrowing owls.
