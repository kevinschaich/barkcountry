# Welcome to Cleveland National Forest

The Forest provides various terrains and recreational opportunities, including camping and picnic areas, hiking, exploring on horseback, trail running, mountain biking, and scenic drives in the beautiful state of California.

![A male and female and their pet dog stand by a trailhead sign.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/cleveland/image/52687024205_f6656ece72_o.jpg?h=0765e871)

Discover campgrounds, trailheads, and more! Remember to [**#RecreateResponsibly**](https://www.recreateresponsibly.org/primary).

[Locations and Trails](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/cleveland/recreation)

![Fuels Crew poses in new gear](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/cleveland/image/Select%20Projects%20%284%20of%204%29.jpg?h=71976bb4)

Throughout Southern California, the USDA Forest Service is working to reduce wildfire risk.

[Wildfire Risk Reduction](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/fire/wildfire-risk-reduction)

![Wooden Signpost on the Pacific Crest Trail](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/cleveland/image/PCT%20sign.jpg?h=a5eb5da0)

[Plan your trip for the PCT](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/cleveland/recreation/groups/pacific-crest-trail-recreation) and stay informed on [current conditions](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/cleveland/conditions) and [alerts](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/cleveland/alerts) along the way.

[Information and Permits](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/cleveland/recreation/groups/pacific-crest-trail-recreation)
