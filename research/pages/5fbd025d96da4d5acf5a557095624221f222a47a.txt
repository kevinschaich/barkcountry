# Mill Pond Campground

This lies along Sullivan Creek and offers great opportunities for hiking on a looped trail system and connects to Mill Pond Historic Site. The Sullivan Creek Trail #565 connects the campground with the [Mill Pond Flume Trail #520](https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/colville/recreation/hiking/recarea/?recid=67938&actid=50), Floodplain Trail #523, [Elk Creek Trail #560](https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/colville/recreation/hiking/recarea/?recid=67922&actid=50), and [Mill Pond Historic Site](https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/colville/recreation/camping-cabins/recarea/?recid=71047&actid=29).

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

May 24 - Labor Day Weekend

### Operational Hours

Check in: 2:00 p.m.; Check-out: 12:00 p.m.; Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. until 6:00 a.m.

### Restrictions

Washington state fishing license required. See Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife at [http://wdfw.wa.gov/](http://wdfw.wa.gov/) for more information.

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfire in developed fire ring only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Pets must be on leash
- OHV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days
- Food and wildlife attractants must be properly stored at all times

### Passes and Permits

Camping: 50% discount for single site camping fees, with any of these passes: Interagency Senior, Interagency Access, Golden Age and Golden Access.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $29 per night

Day Use:

$10 per vehicle per day

- Camping: $31/single site
- Extra vehicle: none
- Day use: $10
