# Visiting with pets

## Rouge National Urban Park

Your four-legged friends are welcome in Rouge National Urban Park. Just remember all dogs are required to be on a leash, or under physical control at all times. As a courtesy to other visitors, please keep your dog on a leash, even at the beach. Remember to stoop and scoop.
