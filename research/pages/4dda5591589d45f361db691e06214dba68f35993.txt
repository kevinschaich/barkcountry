# Welcome to Lassen National Forest

### The Crossroads of California

Lassen National Forest is a total of 1.2 million acres or 1,875 square miles in Northern California. It lies at the heart of one of the most fascinating areas of California, called the Crossroads. Here the granite of the Sierra Nevada, the lava of the Cascades and the Modoc Plateau, and the sagebrush of the Great Basin meet and blend, extending across seven counties: Lassen, Shasta, Tehama, Butte, Plumas, Siskiyou, and Modoc.
