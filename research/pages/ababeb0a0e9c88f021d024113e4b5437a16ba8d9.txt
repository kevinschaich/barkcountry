# Fish Creek Campground

Current Conditions

No camp host on site. Open year-round, but might not be accessible because of snow. The Fish Creek Pavilion is an on-site picnic area that can be reserved for day use. The nearby Fish Creek Group Site is a primitive dispersed camping site that can be reserved for up to 75 people.

Fish Creek Campground is in the heart of a 4-season recreation area and offers 10 campsites that accommodate tents and recreational vehicles. There are two accessible toilets available. Paved concrete walks provide access to the Fish Creek Pavilion, water spigot, bulletin board, and a paved circular bonfire area. The paved parking lot provides access from the pavilion to the restrooms. A Camp Host is on site during the peak summer season. Many trails are available for a wide range of activities, such as snowmobiling, skiing, and hiking.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

The campground is open year-round, but might not be accessible because of snow.

### Operational Hours

Open year-round, but might not be accessible because of snow.

### Restrictions

Garbage services not available. Pack It In, Pack It Out.

### Passes and Permits

Idaho State Park N' Ski sticker for ski and snowshoe parking during the winter trail grooming season.

### Fee Site and Info

$12.00 per night. There is an additional fee of $3 for each extra vehicle.

## Getting There

### Elevation

5000'

### Directions

From Grangeville, go south about 8 miles on Forest Road 221.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Restroom information is not available for this site.

### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.
