[White Sands Essentials](https://www.nps.gov/articles/white-sands-essentials.htm)

![a white sand dune ridge runs into the distance covered in shrubs with a golden sunset.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/whsa/crop16_9/EB8331AD-FBB5-488E-7EA3E8C5904AA8E4.jpg?width=1300&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Interested in visiting White Sands National Park? Knowing these essentials will help you plan an incredible experience at the park.

[Safety](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/safety.htm)

![A visitor faces away reading safety sign about bringing water when hiking in the park.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/whsa/crop16_9/1746F4E0-B99D-FE0A-84DD12955F2122A7.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Understand what it takes to be safe when visiting the park.

[Ranger Programs](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/ranger-programs.htm)

![a ranger in flat hat stands in front of large group of visitors. Pointing into the distance.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/whsa/crop16_9/C9612B60-0C9F-7E33-BFD3F4638AAC9987.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Throughout the year, a variety of ranger lead programs allow visitors to experience and learn about this incredible place.

[Plan Your Visit](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)

![two people hiking on white sand dunes.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/whsa/crop16_9/C6FB21E4-F1A7-6445-6F27414ED85E2CF8.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Planning ahead helps you have the best experience possible.

[Military Testing Park Closures](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/park-closures.htm)

![Park closure sign on gate. ](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/whsa/crop16_9/18F2C9E0-1DD8-B71B-0B69C6EF63E10C88.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Occasionally the road into the park can close due to missile testing. Look here for the latest information.

[Accessiblity](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/accessibility.htm)

![a boardwalk extends into the distance among white sands dunes. A shade paviliion can be seen ahead.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/whsa/crop16_9/10059146-D1DA-DCFA-A7C0C09F48E0377D.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Discover how White Sands National Park is working to provide everyone with accessible experiences at the park.

[Fossilized Footprints](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/learn/nature/fossilized-footprints.htm)

![Multiple human footprints fossilized in hard sediment cross the ground. Individual toes can be seen.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/whsa/crop16_9/1BDAD941-FD67-B52E-5E1CDE5F5F649377.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Trackways in the park give us a glimpse into the lives of ice age animals and the people that lived alongside them in the Tularosa Basin.

[Junior Ranger](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/learn/kidsyouth/junior-ranger.htm)

![Ranger and junior ranger high fiving.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/whsa/crop16_9/BA475113-1DD8-B71B-0BF41ED73BC1B5C0.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Do you want to become a Junior Ranger? The program is available for all ages and can be completed during even a short visit.
