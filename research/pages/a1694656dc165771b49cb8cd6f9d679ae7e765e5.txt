# Office Trail

The Office Trail is located behind the Bienville National Forest office (information center). Along this .75-mile interpretive trail, visitors will have an opportunity to learn about multiple use management on the national forest and how we work to improve habitat for endangered species. The trail was completed in April 2000 and is dedicated to the Senior Community Service Employment Program enrollees, who did most of the work constructing it.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

January

### Restrictions

Pets must be on leash. No alcoholic beverages allowed. No camping on or near trail. No all-terrain vehicles allowed on trail. No horses allowed on trail.
