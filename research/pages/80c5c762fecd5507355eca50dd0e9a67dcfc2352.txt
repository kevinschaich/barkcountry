# Apache Canyon Spur OHV \#103 Trail 24W06

This is a connector OHV trail that connects from Apache Canyon OHV #103 Trail 24W05.

It is a Four-wheel drive route in sandy wash with two short side roads into the Badlands. Some private land in the wash. Use Apache Canyon Road to get around dead-end route.

## Specific Trail Information

### Trail Number

24W06

### Trail Type

**Standard/Terra Trail** — A trail that has a surface consisting predominantly of the ground and that is designed and managed to accommodate use on that surface.

### Restrictions

- Pack out what you pack in and [**Leave No Trace!**](https://lnt.org/why/7-principles/)
- Dogs MUST be on a leash not longer than six feet, or otherwise under physical restrictive control \[ [36 CFR 261.16(j)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/chapter-II/part-261/subpart-A/section-261.16)\]. Read more info about [pet ethics](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/wilderness/ethics).
