# Fee Areas on the Kaibab - 2026 Information

### Locations

Cataract, [Dogtown](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/233956), [Kaibab](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/233957), and [White Horse](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/233955) lakes ( _Williams Ranger District_)

Tusayan-Montane Campground ( _Tusayan Ranger District_)

Jacob Lake and DeMotte campgrounds, Jacob Lake Group & Picnic areas ( _North Kaibab Ranger District_)

### Tusayan-Montane Campground

Tusayan-Montane Campground is operated by the Kaibab National Forest and is located just south of Grand Canyon National Park and the Town of Tusayan.

Reservations for Tusayan-Montane Campground are available now on [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234488) for stays beginning May 15th.

First come, first serve camping will be available May 1-15 and September 30th-October 31st.

## 2026 Season Concession Site Dates and Fees

Reservations for Kaibab National Forest campgrounds operated by [Recreation Resource Management of America (RRMofA)](https://rrmofa.com/) are **available now on Recreation.gov for stays beginning May 15th**. If weather conditions and staffing permit, campgrounds will open earlier and be available first come, first serve. Sites will be open in the following order:

- Kaibab Lake, Cataract Lake, Dogtown Lake, White Horse Lake ( _Tusayan Ranger District_)
- Jacob Lake Campground, Jacob Lake Group & Picnic areas, DeMotte Campground ( _North Kaibab Ranger District_)

All sites will remain open through October 31st, except White Horse Lake, which will close October 15th. Some Kaibab Lake loops may also close October 15th based on demand. Sites will be first come, first serve beginning September 17th.

Camping and day-use fees are the same as they were in 2025.

[Make Camping Reservations at Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/1094)

Day-use fees for Cataract, Dogtown, Kaibab, and White Horse lakes will be assessed beginning May 1st. The day-use fee is $8 per vehicle per day. An annual pass can be purchased for $50 for Williams-area residents and $100 for non-residents.

It's important to note that camping and day-use fees are separate from state fishing licenses required by the Arizona Game & Fish Department. **Fishing licenses can be obtained at** [**www.azgfd.com**](https://www.azgfd.com/).

Day-use fees are not charged outside of RRMofA’s operating season.

### 2025 Fee Investments

The fees collected by Recreation Resource Management of America are invested directly into the Forest’s concession-operated campgrounds under the Granger-Thye Act authority.

In 2025, fee dollars, including 100% of day-use fees, were used for new grills and tables and upgraded boat ramp access at Cataract Lake, significant water system upgrades at all campgrounds, and new signage and speed bumps.

These and future projects are part of a nationwide effort to modernize recreation sites, increase access and safety, and enhance visitor experiences.

### Kaibab Lake Pavilion Use

Beginning in 2026, the pavilion at Kaibab Lake will be reservable on [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/1094) for $40 per day for up to 5 vehicles/25 people.

Additional vehicles would be subject to the established day use fee.

Pavilion reservations are needed to manage vehicle parking and improve event planning.

Larger special events should be coordinated by contacting RRMofA at 928-537-8888.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the day use Fees?

Fees are used to generate revenue that directly supports operations, maintenance, and improvements at those sites, ultimately enhancing the visitor experience and preserving natural resources.

### Why are residents being charged a day fee?

All users of the facilities will be charged a fee; local residents will receive a discount on the season pass.

### Who is considered a “local resident”?

Someone whose government ID shows their address in Williams, Ash Fork, and Parks. If you live in these areas but your ID does not have a Williams, Ash Fork, or Parks address, you can present a current utility statement with your ID to confirm residency.

### Can Flagstaff be added as a local area?

This is at the discretion of the concessionaire. They have indicated the local area does not extend to Flagstaff. You can contact them directly at 928-537-8888.

### Do I get a “locals discount” for a single day pass?

No, the 50% off is only for the season pass.

### Is there a senior or veteran discount?

The concessionaire has not informed the Forest Service of any discount other than the local resident discount for a season pass. You can ask the concessionaire directly at 928-537-8888.

### Which lakes have a day-use fee?

All of the Forest Service managed lakes with a concessionaire in the Williams Ranger District - Cataract, Dogtown, Kaibab, and White Horse. The Santa Fe and City reservoirs are managed by the City of Williams, not the Forest Service.

### Is the fee per person or per vehicle?

In general, the day use pass is “per vehicle” and carpooling is encouraged. However, this is at the discretion of the concessionaire and may not apply to overpacked or oversized vehicles.

### How long are the Day and Season Passes valid?

Day use passes are good for the day they are purchased (they expire at midnight). Season passes are good through the concession operating season of the current year. Operating season varies. It is typically May through October.

### What happens after the concessionaire season ends?

The fee will be collected during the operational season of the concessionaire, typically between May and October.  In the off-season, the Forest Service does not plan to collect this fee, as there will be limited services over the fall and winter.

### Will gates be left open during the off season for fishing & plowed during snow?

Gates to the campgrounds will be closed as usual for the off-season, which sometimes limits access for Day Use Areas such as at White Horse Campground; other gates to Day Use Areas have typically been left open.  There are no plans at this time to change that approach in the off-season, or to start plowing to these sites.

### Can I fish during the day and come back at night?

Yes, you may fish during the day and come back at night. However, Cataract Lake opens at 6:00 am and closes at 9:00 pm. If you return to Cataract after closing, the gate will be closed and you’ll need to walk in.

### Is it free if I walk in?

Yes. A day pass is not required for walk ins, as long as you don’t use the site’s amenities such as trash cans, picnic tables, water, restrooms, etc.

### Is the pass good for all the Lakes in Williams?

The season pass is good for all _Forest Service lakes managed by the concessionaire_ in the Williams area. Santa Fe and City reservoirs are managed by the City of Williams and not the Forest Service.

### Can I use a single-day pass at more than one lake in the same day?

**For instance, if I buy a single-day pass at Cataract but go to Kaibab later that day, will I need to buy another pass at Kaibab?**

Your day-use pass is good for all the lakes. If you buy a day pass and move to another lake that same day, you do not need to buy another pass.

### Is the pass good for camping too?

No. Day-use passes (single-day and season) are not valid for camping.

### Can I purchase a day-use pass at the Kaibab National Forest offices?

No. The day-use passes are sold by the concessionaire, not the Forest Service. The concessionaire is issuing passes only at the recreation sites.

### Will I be charged a fee to drop someone off?

A fee will be charged once a vehicle passes the “Fee Area” sign at the campground.  A fee will not be assessed to individuals that walk into the fee area unless they use the facilities such as restrooms, picnic tables, grills, water, trash cans, etc.

### Will schools or other organizations have free access for functions?

Schools and other educational organizations can request an exemption from the fees from the concessionaire.  Their ability to accommodate a request will be dependent on date and time (like whether it occurs on a holiday or holiday weekend), and the number of participants. The Forest Service does not issue exemptions. Those seeking a fee exemption should request one from RRMofA as far in advance as possible by calling 928-537-8888.

### What is the difference between the fees for my fishing license and day-use pass?

Purchasing a fishing license through the Arizona Game and Fish Department (AZGFD) directly contributes to the management of AZ fisheries, such as fish stocking and habitat improvement projects. The fees charged by RRMofA are used by the concessionaire and the Forest Service to maintain and improve infrastructure at the sites, such as boat ramps, picnic tables, fire rings, and restrooms. The concessionaire can be contacted at 928-537-8888. To learn more about fishing in AZ, visit [www.azgfd.gov/fishing.](https://www.azgfd.com/fishing-2/)

### What are the fees used for?

Revenues are applied directly toward operating costs and funding improvements at the sites.

### When did you decide to start charging for day use?

The campgrounds in the Williams area have been concessioned for over 20 years and the ability to charge day use fees has been a part of all concession permits. The previous concessionaire chose not to implement day-use fees during their tenure. The current concessionaire, Recreation Resource Management of America (RRMofA), first exercised their ability to charge day-use fees in 2025 when they received the permit to operate the sites.

### Why can’t they take our America the Beautiful Passes?

This is at the discretion of the concessionaire. Questions can be referred directly to the concessionaire at 928-537-8888.

### Why is the Black Bear Snack Shack not allowed at White Horse anymore?

The Snack Shack’s presence was an arrangement involving the previous concessionaire. The snack shack could come to a new arrangement with the new concessionaire to operate there. The Forest Service cannot independently permit the snack shack within the concession-run campground.
