### Protected area. Enriching nature experiences.

Plaisance—the name evokes the pleasantness of a landscape broken by bays, ponds, and marshes. In spring, Plaisance puts on a performance with thousands of Canada Geese. Come summer, it's a paradise for ducks, herons, ospreys, and dozens of other species. It's also the Ottawa River's soft breeze that makes the tall grasses bend and toss in a wave-like roll across the Petite Presqu'île, not far from the campground.

### [![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/pla/accordeon/pla_act_chutes_moulin.jpg)Les Chutes du Moulin](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/pla/index.dot?language_id=1\#Item929e1890-85ec-484b-9cc3-1a15f5bbc6bc)

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/pla/accordeon/pla_act_chutes_moulin.jpg)

Featuring a vertical drop of over 35 metres, the Chutes du Moulin waterfall is simply awe-inspiring! You'll be fascinated by this enchanting setting during your entire hike. The Chutes du Moulin site was the birthplace of the Petite-Nation's industrial era. Interpretation panels on the site will show you what life was like in the old village of North Nation Mills.

**Address:** 100, rang Malo, Plaisance (QC) J0V 1S0

**N.B.** The Chutes du Moulin site is earmarked to be included in the future expansion of Parc national de Plaisance. Management of the site was entrusted to Sépaq in spring 2024 by the Ministère de l'Environnement, de la Lutte contre les changements climatiques, de la Faune et des Parcs (MELCCFP).

Please note that leashed dogs are allowed on the Chutes du Moulin site.

#### A hiking tour

A 2 km round-trip trail leads to the observation deck. Picnic tables and interpretation services are also available on site.

#### A watercraft tour

The team suggests a canoe or kayak trip on Rivière de la Petite-Nation all the way to the foot of the falls. Once there, you’ll follow a short trail to the observation deck, where a breathtaking view awaits you. Watercraft can be rented at the Parc national de Plaisance Discovery and Visitors Centre (Presqu'iles sector) during service building opening hours.

#### Site access fees:

- $9.25 per adult
- Free for children 17 and under

Access to the Chutes du Moulin site is included for all holders of a daily access permit or a valid annual pass for Parc national de Plaisance.

Period (Chutes du Moulin Registration Centre): April 18 to November 1, 2026

The parking lot is not cleared of snow in winter, and for safety reasons, the lookout will be closed as soon as the first snow falls.

### [![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/pla/accordeon/pla_act_surf.jpg)Stand Up Paddling](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/pla/index.dot?language_id=1\#Item8c093963-dd34-497b-9fbb-ec548aa3386e)

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/pla/accordeon/pla_act_surf.jpg)

Explore the waters of Rivière Petite-Nation and Baie de la Pentecôte and surf standing up on a board with a paddle (Stand up paddle surfing). Originally a Hawaiian sport, stand up paddle surfing combines surfing and kayaking. This board, designed for calm water, is bigger (longer, wider and thicker), and therefore more stable than traditional surf boards designed for waves. Standing on this big board with paddle in hand, you can test your balance. It’s fast and easy to learn, and guaranteed fun!

Stand up paddle boards can be [rented](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/pla/tarifs.dot#cat_39) by the hour, half day or full day at the Discovery and Visitors Centre.

The park offers more than 100 campsites, either serviced or semi-serviced for tents and recreational vehicles. The camping areas are all located in the Presqu’Îles sector.

#### La Cédrière campground

Located less than 5 km from the Discovery and Visitors Centre and less than 1 km from La Carapace Visitors Centre, this camping area offers 15 semi-serviced campsites and 6 1-service campsites (electricity).

#### Les Marmottes campground

Les Marmottes campground features 45 two-service campsites (water and electricity), located less than 5 km from the Discovery and Visitors Centre and close to La Carapace Visitors Centre.

#### L’Érablière campground

L’Érablière campground features 29 two-service campsites (water and electricity) and is located about 5 km from the Discovery and Visitors Centre and close to La Carapace Visitors Centre.

#### La Baie campground

La Baie campground features about 20 semi-serviced campsites and a dozen two-service campsites (water and electricity). The camping area is located about 5 km from the Discovery and Visitors Centre and close to La Carapace Visitors Centre.

#### RV area

The Fer-à-Cheval RV area offers sites designed specially for trailers and motorhomes on their own. This RV area is completely open and unwooded with a grassy surface. No services are offered on the sites, but a water point, a shower and toilets are located at the Discovery and Visitors Centre. Use of the dump station is possible in the Marmottes sector.

**Important**: dogs are allowed in the sector. If you need to use the showers or dump station in the campground, please note that dogs are not allowed in this sector of the park.

Please note that due to rehabilitation work on the Petite‑Nation Canal bridge, the tranquility of the site may be affected from Monday to Friday, between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m.

## Park access authorization

There is a limit to the number of visitors that can enter a national park on any given day. It is therefore **strongly recommended** that you purchase your right of access online before a visit or that you acquire an annual pass. This guarantees you access to the park, will ensure a smoother arrival at the site and avoid unnecessary travel when the maximum capacity is reached. It can be purchased up to 30 days before a visit. You may be denied access to a sector, a trail, or an activity if a parking lot is full or if the maximum capacity has been reached.

[Daily Access](https://www.sepaq.com/en/reservation/purchase/daily-access) [Annual Card](https://www.sepaq.com/en/reservation/national-parks/annual-card)

## Important

### Don't move firewood

A single piece of firewood can destroy millions of trees. Invasive insects and diseases can hide inside it. Buy it locally. Burn it on site. [Find out more This link will open in a new window.](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/plant-health/forestry/hazards-moving-firewood)
