# Vista Linda Campground

This popular campground is open year round. The campground is located in the scenic San Diego Canyon, with spectacular red rock canyon and mesa views. Come prepared for warm days and cool nights during the summer months and cold winter temperatures. The Jemez River, adjacent to the campground, offers excellent fishing opportunities (valid State fishing license required) and wildlife viewing.

**Uses:** Overnight camping, hiking and fishing.

**Facilities:** The campground has 13 units with grills. Host is present on-site.  Amenities include drinking water, paved access and vault toilets. No hook-ups or dump station available. Tents, RV's and trailers up to 40 feet and pull through capability.

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Check out time is 11:00 AM, check in time is 1:00 PM. Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM. to 7:00 AM. (No generator use during quiet hours). 14-day stay maximum.

### Restrictions

Dogs must be leashed at all times while within campground area.

### Passes and Permits

Interagency Senior Pass holders (Lifetime and Annual) receive a 50% discount on single unit campsite fee.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $20 per night

Starting 5/9/2025 the site will be $20.00 per site, per night, for each campsite. Only two (2) vehicles are permitted at each camp site, this includes the tow vehicle or RV.

### Pet Information

Pets must be on a leash at all times while in the campground.
