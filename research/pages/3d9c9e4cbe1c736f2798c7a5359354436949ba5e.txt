# Activities and experiences

## Point Pelee National Park

Whether you love to hike a wooded trail, photograph a stunning sunset, glimpse rare bird species during spring and fall migration, paddle a canoe through a wetland brimming with life, picnic on a sandy beach, be dazzled by a kaleidoscope of dragon flies, blue jays and birds of prey or crunch through the pristine snow of a wintry landscape – Point Pelee National Park has it all in every season.

### [Calendar of events](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/calendrier-calendar)

Take part in a wide range of events, special activities, and memorable experiences in Point Pelee.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/on/Pelee/WET4/activ/640x360-pagayez-paddling.jpg?modified=20221115213023)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/pagayez-paddling)

### [Canoeing and kayaking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/pagayez-paddling)

Enjoy a peaceful canoe or kayak in the internationally recognized wetlands at Point Pelee National Park.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/on/Pelee/WET4/activ/640x360-otentik.jpg?modified=20221115213023)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/otentik)

### [oTENTik camping](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/otentik)

Camping in oTENTik A-frame cabins located in Camp Henry. Enjoy a comfortable & convenient stay available all year round.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/on/Pelee/WET4/activ/640x360-randonnee-hiking.jpg?modified=20221115213024)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/randonnee-hiking)

### [Trails](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/randonnee-hiking)

Find trail descriptions for the over 12 km of hiking, walking, and cycling trails at Point Pelee National Park.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/on/Pelee/WET4/activ/640x360-oiseaux-birding2.jpg?modified=20221117155047)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/oiseaux-birds)

### [Bird watching](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/oiseaux-birds)

Observe bird migration at Point Pelee National Park. With more than 390 species of birds recorded, Point Pelee is the perfect location for birding.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/on/Pelee/WET4/activ/640x360-velo-cycling.jpg?modified=20221115213024)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/velo-bicycling)

### [Bicycling](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/pelee/activ/velo-bicycling)

With 12 km of bike trails and the 9 km roadway, cycling is a great way to explore Point Pelee National Park.
