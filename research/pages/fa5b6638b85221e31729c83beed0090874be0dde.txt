## Forest Order

FOREST ORDER

USDA FOREST SERVICE

GUNNISON and RIO GRANDE NATIONAL FORESTS

GUNNISON, DIVIDE and SAGUACHE RANGER DISTRICTS

### La Garita Wilderness Area  Occupancy & Use

Pursuant to 16 USC § 551 and 36 CFR § 261.50(a) and § 261.50(b), the following acts are prohibited within the La Garita Wilderness of the Gunnison, Divide and Saguache Ranger Districts of the Gunnison and Rio Grande National Forests, Hinsdale, Saguache and Mineral Counties, State of Colorado (the “Restricted Area”) and on all National Forest System (NFS) Trails located within the Restricted Area (“the Restricted Trails”).

The Restricted Area and the Restricted Trails are depicted on the attached map, hereby incorporated into this Order as Exhibit A.

This Order is necessary to protect physical and biological resources of the La Garita Wilderness, and to protect the human experience of Wilderness character, solitude, and the untrammeled condition of the landscape.

#### PROHIBITIONS:

1\. Entering or being in the Restricted Area with a group of more than 15 people or more than 10 stock animals. 36 CFR § 261.58(f)

2\. Camping:

a. Within 100 feet of any NFS trail or any lake, stream, pond, river, or other similar body of water;

b. Within 300 feet of Machin Lake; or

c. Within the Wheeler Geologic Area.

36 CFR § 261.58(e)

3\. Building, maintaining, attending or using a fire, campfire, or wood-burning stove:

a. Within 100 feet of any NFS trail or any lake, stream, pond, river, or other similar water body;

b. Above tree line;

c. Within 300 feet of Machin Lake; or

d. Within the Wheeler Geologic Area.

36 CFR § 261.52(a)

4\. Storing equipment, personal property, or supplies. 36 CFR § 261.57(f)

5\. Hitching, tethering, hobbling or fencing a horse or other saddle or pack animal in violation of posted instructions. Instructions to include, but may not be limited to:

a. Prohibiting hitching, tethering, hobbling, or fencing within (i) 100 feet of any NFS

Trail or any lake, stream, pond, river, or other similar water body, or (ii) within 300 feet of Machin Lake.

b. Prohibiting tying directly to trees except during loading and unloading.

36 CFR § 261.58(aa)

6\. Possessing a dog that is: (i) off leash; (ii) not under the direct verbal control of the dog’s owner or handler at all times; or (iii) disturbing or damaging wildlife, other animals, people or property. 36 CFR § 261.58(s)

7\. Shortcutting a switchback in a trail. 36 C.F.R .§ 261.55(e)

#### EXEMPTIONS:

Pursuant to 36 CFR § 261.50(e), the following persons are exempt from this Order:

1\. Persons with a special use authorization or other Forest Service authorization specifically exempting them from the effect of this Order.

2\. Any Federal, State, or local office, or member of an organized rescue or firefighting force in the performance of an official duty.

This Order shall be in effect when signed until rescinded or until May 31, 2026, whichever occurs first.

Executed in Delta, Colorado this 7th day of June 2021.

CHAD STEWART

Forest Supervisor

Grand Mesa, Uncompahgre and Gunnison National Forests

Executed in Monte Vista, Colorado this 3rd day of June 2021.

DAN DALLAS

Forest Supervisor

Rio Grande National Forest

A violation of these prohibitions is punishable as a Class B misdemeanor by a fine of not more than $5,000 for an individual or $10,000 for an organization, or imprisonment for not more than 6 months, or both. 16 USC § 551 and 18 USC §§ 3559, 3571, 3581.
