# Otto/Harris Lake Trail

The Otto/Harris Lake Trail is an 8-mile hiking trail that connects the lakes and wraps around each lake. Otto and Harris Lakes are quiet, isolated lakes and each has back country campsite.

The ½ mile portage into Otto Lake is suitable for portage wheels to transport a small boat or canoe.

## Specific Trail Information

### Trail Number

10044C

### Trail Type

**Standard/Terra Trail** — A trail that has a surface consisting predominantly of the ground and that is designed and managed to accommodate use on that surface.

## General Information

**Camping**

Otto has 2 backcountry sites and Harris has 1 backcountry site.

**Features**

- Otto and Harris Lakes are quiet, isolated lakes.
- The almost 9 mile trail is primitive in most areas.
- Both lakes have walleye, panfish, and northern pike fish species.
- Otto Lake is 168 acres with a maximum depth of 27 feet. The portage into Otto Lake is suitable for portage wheels to transport a small boat or canoe. All-terrain vehicles (ATVs) are not allowed.
- Harris Lake is 67 acres with a maximum depth of 38 feet.
- Otto Lake has two back country campsites. Harris Lake has one.

**Safety**

You have the responsibility to be adequately prepared for a trip in the wilderness with

- good footwear,
- ample water,
- emergency supplies, and
- sufficient time to make the trip.

You should be self sufficient: there is no cell phone coverage, and due to the nature of the trail, there is no easy way to get assistance in, nor any easy way to get people out in emergencies.

**Travel**

- A compass and accurate map are essential.
- Bring reliable maps. Maps can be purchased at some Forest Service offices, area businesses and outfitters, or directly from the map companies.
- If you get lost, don’t panic. Sit down, relax and think. Chances are that you will figure out your location in a few minutes.
- If you plan to use a Global Positioning System (GPS) for navigating, be sure you also bring a map and compass as a back up in case your GPS unit fails.
- Dress in layers and be prepared for every kind of weather situation.

### Water Available

Boil or filter water.

### Restrictions

**When visiting, please remember**

- ATVs only allowed from parking area to Harris Lake.
- Only use facilities that are provided.

**Pets/Dogs**

- Dogs are allowed on hiking trails, as long as they are kept on a leash. Unleashed dogs can pose a danger to your pet, the natural resources of the area, and other forest visitors.
- Do not allow them to interact with wildlife.

## Getting There

### Directions

- Drive east of Hoyt Lakes on County 110 (Forest Highway 11) for 13.4 miles.
- Turn left (east) on County 16/Forest Highway 11 for 4 miles.
- Turn right (south) on Forest Road 416 for 4 miles to parking area for Otto Lake.
- Another way into Harris Lake is from the south on a road called Northeast Grade. This road is rough and prone to flooding. High-wheel vehicles or ATVs are advised.
