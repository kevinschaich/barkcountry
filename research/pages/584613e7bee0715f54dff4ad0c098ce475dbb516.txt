# Welcome to the Midewin National Tallgrass Prairie

The Midewin National Tallgrass Prairie in Illinois is the largest island in the archipelago of protected areas that collectively comprise the Chicago Wilderness. It includes full array of species and natural processes typical of tallgrass prairies in the region, including the reintroduction of bison.

Turning back the clock from a landscape dominated by rusting munitions factories and abandoned ammunitions bunkers into a 20,283-acre pristine tallgrass prairie makes the Midewin a compelling vision for landscape scale restoration.
