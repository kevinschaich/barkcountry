# Chinook Campground

Current Conditions

Water system is off for the season.

Forested campground situated along the Secesh River at an elevation of 5,650 feet. Each of nine sites includes a picnic table, fire ring, and several are equipped for stock use. There is typically potable water available June - September.

There are no hookups or trash receptacles. Please pack out your trash.

Local activities include fly fishing, mountain biking, and hiking. The Secesh River Trail #080 and Loon Lake Trail #081 both comprise a portion of the popular Loon Lake Loop and depart directly from the campground. The historic town of Warren, as well as Burgdorf Hot Springs are within easy driving distance.

## Reservations

All sites are available on a first-come, first-serve basis.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

June - September, depending on snow levels

### Operational Hours

Check in: 2:00 p.m.; Check-out: 12:00 p.m.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $10 per night

Double Site: $15 per night

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in all campgrounds, picnic areas, and on trails. We recommend you **keep your dog on a leash**. Never leave them unattended. Dogs allowed to roam freely can frighten or injure wildlife, disturb other forest visitors, and create impacts to natural resources.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 45.214049

**Longitude:** -115.808702

### Elevation

5,708 feet

### Directions

From McCall, follow the Warren Wagon Road (21) north for 36 miles. Turn right at the Chinook Campground sign and follow Forest Road #378 for one mile to the campground.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Accessibility

The toilet at the adjacent trailhead is fully accessible from the parking lot.

### Restrooms

Vault toilet(s)


### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

Potable water is typically available in the summer. It's always a good idea to travel with extra water for drinking and cooking.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are not allowed at this site.
