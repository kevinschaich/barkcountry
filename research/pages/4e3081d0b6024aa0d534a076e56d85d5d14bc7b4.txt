# Buffalo Campground

Buffalo Campground is nestled along the banks of the Buffalo River, about 26 miles south of West Yellowstone, Montana, in beautiful Island Park, Idaho. Situated within the Caribou-Targhee National Forest’s Ashton/Island Park Ranger District, this campground rests at an elevation of 6,200 feet, accessible via U.S. Highway 20, just north of Pond’s Lodge on the east side of the highway.

**Natural Features:** A mixed forest of lodgepole pine and aspen drapes the campground, casting partial shade over its sprawling sites, while summer wildflowers burst across nearby meadows. The Buffalo River flows alongside, it's clear waters teeming with brook and rainbow trout, a serene ribbon winding through the volcanic caldera landscape of Island Park.

The river beckons with canoeing, tubing, and world-class trout fishing, complemented by an accessible fishing dock. Two miles west, the Island Park Reservoir offers boating, jet-skiing, and more angling, while the Mesa Falls Scenic Byway, seven miles south, unveils stunning vistas at Upper and Lower Mesa Falls.

**Facilities:** The campground offers 117 single-family sites, four double-family sites, and one group site for up to 150 people, each equipped with picnic tables and campfire rings. Vault toilets, drinking water, and trash collection are available, with electric hookups in one loop and a host on-site. Roads and parking spurs are paved, welcoming RVs up to 50 feet.

**Season**: Late May-Early September

**After season**: Open ( **weather dependent**) first-come/first-served; no fee, no toilets, no water.

## General Information

This campground lies adjacent to the Buffalo River.  The flat, shallow river is a favorite with floaters and fisherman.  The campground does provide parking for fisherman.

Canoeing, tubing and trout fishing are popular activities.

Two miles to the west of Buffalo Campground is the Island Park Boat Ramp for the Island Park Reservior.  Activities on the reservoir include fishing, jet-skiing, waterskiing, tubing and boating.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 44.4251

### Directions

From West Yellowstone, Montana, take Highway 20 south for 26 miles to the campground (less than1/4 mile north of Ponds Lodge on the east side of the highway).  From Idaho Falls, ID take Highway 20 north 80 miles to Ponds Lodge, the entry way to the campground is on the east side of Highway 20 across from Ponds Lodge.
