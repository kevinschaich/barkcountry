# Pattee Canyon Group Picnic Area

Current Conditions

Closes for reservations on Friday 10/31/2026.

Nov. 1 to June 14 the site is walk-in only, and first come-first served.

Set in a wooded canyon east of Missoula at 4,100 feet, the Pattee Canyon Picnic Area is a popular spot for family picnics. Because this picnic area is in the Pattee Canyon Recreation Area, there's plenty to do before enjoying a relaxing lunch. Knock yourself out hiking or biking a trail then have a picnic lunch and rest under the shade of the forest...pure bliss!

View the site through the [interactive map](https://arcg.is/198Ore0).

## Reservations

[Make Reservations Here](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234481)

Use of group picnic sites are available by reservation only from June 15-October 31; fee required. Visit [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234481?tab=info) to make reservations.

## General Information

The loop roads are paved and the group sites have garbage receptacles and accessible toilets. This area has picnic tables, fire rings, and grills. No overnight camping is permitted. When the picnic area gates are closed people are welcome to park outside and walk in during the hours of 6 a.m. to 9 p.m.

### Seasons of Use

June 15 - October 31. From Nov. 1 to June 14, access to the Pattee Canyon Group Picnic Area is limited to walk-in only and the group use picnic sites are available first-come, first-serve.

### Operational Hours

Day use only, 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. Area closed 9 p.m. to 6 a.m.

### Restrictions

Please visit the [Forest's Alerts page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/lolo/alerts) for information on restrictions, in particular please review the [Food/Wildlife Attractant Storage Order](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/alerts/r1-2023-02-ncde-food-and-wildlife-attractant-storage-order) to help reduce the likelihood of human-to-bear conflicts.

No overnight camping.

### Fee Site and Info

Day Use:

Group Picnic Area Site A: $100 per day

Group Picnic Area Sites B,C: $50 per day

Reservations can be made at [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234481).

### Pet Information

Must be physically restrained on a 6 foot leash.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 46.825591

**Longitude:** -113.927811

### Directions

From Interstate 90 in Missoula, take the Orange Street exit #104 and travel south. Turn left onto West Front Street and go 0.2 miles to South Higgins Avenue, then turn right. Continue on South Higgins Avenue for about 2.7 miles to Pattee Canyon Road, then turn left. On Pattee Canyon Road, travel 4 miles heading (east) up the canyon. Look for the Pattee Canyon Picnic Area sign and entrance.

### Parking

This area shares a parking lot for the Pattee Canyon Disc Golf Course. Please use designated parking spots for picnic sites.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Accessible vault toilets


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

There are 14 individual picnic table sites and 3 group picnic sites.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are not allowed at this site.
