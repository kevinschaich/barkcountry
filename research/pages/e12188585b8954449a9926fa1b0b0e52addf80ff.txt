# Pets

|     |
| --- |
| Please be aware that having a pet with you will limit your activities in Great Basin National Park. Kennel services are not currently available in the neigboring town of Baker, NV. The nearest kennel services are found in Ely, NV about 60 miles from the park.<br>## Park Pet Regulations<br>Any animal that is not a trained service animal is considered a pet and must abide by all of the following rules.<br>- Pets must be kept on a leash 6 feet in length or shorter at all times. This is for the protection of the pet, wildlife, and other visitors.<br>- Pets are not permitted in Lehman Caves or at evening programs.<br>- **Pets are not permitted on trails** or in the backcountry of the park, except leashed pets on the [Lexington Arch Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-to-lexington-arch.htm) or the trail between Baker and the Great Basin Visitor Center.<br>- Leashed pets can only be exercised in the campgrounds, in front of the visitor centers, and along roads.<br>- Pet owners are required to immediately remove and properly dispose of fecal matter deposited by their pets. Deposit bags of fecal matter in a dumpster.<br>- Pets may not be left unattended at campsites or tied to trees or other fixtures.<br>## Why are there restrictions on pets in National Parks?<br>A national park is a refuge for native wildlife. Restrictions are intended to prevent stress and molestation of wildilfe, to protect pets from wildlife that may attack or harm them, and to provide an enjoyable experience for those visitors without pets. Domestic animals can also spread diseases to other wildlife through feces and other bodily fluids. <br>[![NPS horse trailer parked](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/grba/crop16_9/54311EBA-A5D1-8338-6195B750B5D2643C.jpg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Horseback Riding](https://www.nps.gov/grba/planyourvisit/pack-animals.htm) <br>Horses and other pack animals (mules, burros, and llamas) are welcome in the backcountry of Great Basin. Please follow regulations.<br>[![A black dog with strap saying "Guide Dog" lies between the legs of a visitor sitting on a bench.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/grba/crop16_9/1DCC5200-EB6D-2AA5-CC7802431DBF6441.jpg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Service Animals](https://www.nps.gov/grba/planyourvisit/service-animals.htm) <br>Learn about bringing your service animal to the park |
