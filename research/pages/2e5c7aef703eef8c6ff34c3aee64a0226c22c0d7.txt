# Signal Mountain Trail

The Signal Mountain Trail starts on Larimer County Road 63E, drops down to meet and follow Pennock Creek. The trail then ascends to follow the Comanche Peak Wilderness boundary, passes the Lookout Mountain Trail (#934) that connects to Danner Pass Trail (#926), and ultimately meets the Bulwark Ridge Trail (#928-1) on Signal Mountain.

## Specific Trail Information

### Trail Number

#928-2

### Trail Type

**Standard/Terra Trail** — A trail that has a surface consisting predominantly of the ground and that is designed and managed to accommodate use on that surface.

## General Information

Peak Elevation: 11,260'

You can find more information at [Poudre Wilderness Volunteers](https://pwv.org/trails/find-a-trail).

### Restrictions

Dogs must be on a hand-held leash. No motorized or mechanized equipment. Camps, campfires and stock, where allowed, at least 200 feet from water and trails. Group size limited to 12 people or stock animals combined. Certified weed-free hay is required for stock.

## Getting There

### Parking

Signal Mountain Trailhead accommodates approximately roadside parking for 4 vehicles.

### Directions

From Fort Collins take Highway 14 west 36 miles and turn south onto County Road 63E. Follow CR 63E for approximately 14 miles. The trailhead is on the left side of the road approximately 1.5 miles past the intersection of Buckhorn Road and CR63E.
