# Croatan National Forest

Welcome to the Croatan National Forest, one of four National Forests in North Carolina and the only true coastal forest in the East. In eastern North Carolina, the Croatan National Forest is a place where pine forests, saltwater estuaries, pocosins and winding rivers come together to create one of the most unique landscapes in the Southeast. Bordered on three sides by tidal rivers and the Bogue Sound, the forest is defined by water. The Croatan is located between New Bern, Morehead City and the Crystal Coast, the forest offers opportunities for hiking, camping, fishing, paddling, hunting and wildlife viewing. Visitors can enjoy up to 167,687 acres that make up the Croatan National Forest, spanning across Craven, Carteret and Jones Counties. Explore longleaf pine ecosystems, spot carnivorous plants and rare wildlife, and experience the quiet beauty of coastal wetlands that help support both recreation and conservation across the region.

**Things to Do**

Croatan National Forest offers a variety of outdoor recreation opportunities, from fishing to waterfront hikes. Whether you’re hiking, biking or exploring by vehicle, there are options for all experience levels.

Find more information about [recreation opportunities here](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/recreation/opportunities). Visitors can view a list of all trails, campgrounds and recreation areas in the complexes section below.

**Camping**

Croatan National Forest has several camping options, including developed campgrounds and dispersed camping.

Some campgrounds offer amenities like restrooms and picnic tables, while others provide a more primitive experience.

Learn more about camping and how to reserve a site [here.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/recreation/camping-cabins)

**Forest Maps**

[View Croatan National Forest maps](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/maps-guides) for roads, trails, and recreation areas.

**Closures**

Check [seasonal closures](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/about-area/faqs/seasonal-closures) before you go, especially for OHV trails and [temporary conditions on alerts.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/alerts)

**Passes & Permits**

Some recreation areas and activities require passes or fees. If you don’t see what you need on recreation.gov, [check here for additional pass information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/passes).
