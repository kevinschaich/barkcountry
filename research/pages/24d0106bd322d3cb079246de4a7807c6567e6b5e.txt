# Pines Campground

Pines Campground is located 25 miles southeast of Walden in the Gould Area, adjacent to the South Fork of the Michigan River, a scenic mountain stream. It sits at an elevation of 9,200 feet. Pines Campground has 11 campsites and each campsite has a fire grate and picnic table. The campground offers a vault toilet. Open season is June to late October.

- Due to the beetle kill campsites offer very little to no shade.
- Campers should come prepared with their own water as there is no potable water.

This site was approved for a fee change - [Click here for more information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/mbrtb/recreation/mbrtb-recreation-site-fee-changes-information)

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

June (weather dependent) - Mid to Late October (weather dependent)

### Operational Hours

_**This Campground and boat launch is open seasonally and closes mid-late October annually, (weather dependent.)**_

### Restrictions

Dogs must be on leash in campground.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $20 per night

Double Site: $40 per night

Additional Vehicle Fee: $5 per vehicle per night

$20/site, exact cash or check only, payment instructions are found on the kiosk.

### Pet Information

Dogs must be kept on a leash.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 40.4912

**Longitude:** -106.00714

### Elevation

9200

### Directions

From Walden, go east on Highway14 for 22 miles to Gould. Take County Road 21 (Forest Road 740) south 4 miles to the campground. Follow the signs.
