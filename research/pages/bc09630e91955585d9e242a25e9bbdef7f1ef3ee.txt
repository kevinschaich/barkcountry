# Four Mile Campground

Located along side the South Fork Salmon River, these 4 campsites offer access to an extensive trail system. Fishing in the South Fork of the Salmon River. Trail access is provided at a nearby trailhead, which is equipped with a stock ramp and hitching rack.

## Reservations

All sites are available on a first-come, first-serve basis.

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Check in: 2:00 p.m.; Check-out: 11:00 a.m.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $10 per night

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in all campgrounds, picnic areas, and on trails. We recommend you **keep your dog on a leash**. Never leave them unattended. Dogs allowed to roam freely can frighten or injure wildlife, disturb other forest visitors, and create impacts to natural resources.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 44.86299

**Longitude:** -115.691541

### Directions

From McCall, go 30 miles northeast on Forest Road 48 (Lick Creek Road), then 11 miles south on FR 674 (South Fork Road).

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Vault toilet(s)


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are not allowed at this site.

The adjacent trailhead is equipped with a stock ramp and hitching rack.
