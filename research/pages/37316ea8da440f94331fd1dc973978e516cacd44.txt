# Mingan Archipelago National Park Reserve

The Mingan Archipelago, located on the North Shore of Quebec, is home to the largest concentration of monoliths in Canada. While whales and seals frolic at large, the thousands of islands and islets fascinate visitors with their arctic-alpine plants and colonies of birds.

### [Forillon National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/forillon)

At the tip of the Gaspé Peninsula, between the sea and the mountains, Forillon National Park is home to a variety of ecosystems and human stories. Camping, hiking, kayak, whale watching tour... Forillon creates colourful experiences and memories!

### [Pointe-au-Père Lighthouse National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/qc/pointeaupere)

The Pointe-au-Père navigational aid station, among the most important in Canada, helps us better understand the complexity of the St. Lawrence River and the role played by its lighthouse, one of the tallest in the country. A visit to the lighthouse and the new exhibition River Sentinels are two must-sees!

### [Saguenay–St-Lawrence Marine Park](https://parks.canada.ca/amnc-nmca/qc/saguenay)

Watch whales and learn about the fascinating mammals and their habitat. The Saguenay–St. Lawrence Marine Park is recognized as one of the best places in the world for whale watching.
