# Pets

If you have a dog, a cat or other animal as a pet, please be advised that only **dogs** are allowed under certain conditions. Take note of the following important information before you plan your trip.

[Rules](https://www.sepaq.com/animaux/annexes/regles-encadrement.dot)

Easily recognizable  are allowed everywhere.

You have questions? Start by checking the [frequently asked questions list](https://www.sepaq.com/questions-frequentes/animaux-domestiques.dot) or [contact our representatives](https://www.sepaq.com/nous-joindre/index.dot).

![Dog](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/animaux/chien.jpg)

## National parks

Since May 17, 2019, dogs are **allowed** in all Québec national parks, **except in the Anticosti and Île-Bonaventure-et-du-Rocher-Percé national parks**. However, dogs are **only authorized in certain places**.

The places where dogs are allowed vary from one national park to another and from one season to another. **Note**: dogs are generally allowed on the summer trails from early May to late October. To learn the precise period during which dogs are allowed, please check on the opening periods of the summer and winter trails of the park you wish to visit. Consult the list of places where dogs are authorized in each park:

## Wildlife reserves

Dogs **are allowed** in wildlife reserves, but **only on certain places** marked for that purpose.

## Sépaq Anticosti

Pets **are not allowed** at Sépaq Anticosti.

## Tourist establishments

### Aquarium du Québec

Pets **are not allowed** at Aquarium du Québec. Note that easily recognizable **assistance dogs** and **guide dogs** are allowed without restriction in all buildings and other locations accessible to visitors.
