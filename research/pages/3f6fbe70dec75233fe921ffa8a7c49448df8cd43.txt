# Activities and experiences

## Terra Nova National Park

Whether exploring on your own, at the Visitor Centre, or in one of our interpretive programs, you find a new rhythm—the rhythm of nature.

It’s in the beat of your feet on a hiking trail. It’s the crackling campfire and in the sky where the stars have never shone so bright. It’s the lapping of the water pulling on your paddle. It’s the thumping of your heart as you crest a summit.
