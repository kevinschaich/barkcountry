# Welcome to Manti-La Sal National Forest

The Manti-La Sal National Forest stretches from central Utah to southeastern Utah and into Colorado. The 1.3-million-acre forest is managed for multiple uses such as range, timber, minerals, water, wildlife, and recreation. The Manti-La Sal is made up of three primary land areas: the Manti Division, the La Sal Division at Moab, and the La Sal Division at Monticello. Today the forest offers people a retreat from the hurry of modern life.
