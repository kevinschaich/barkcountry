# Welcome to Tahoe National Forest

Tahoe National Forest — in the northern Sierra Nevada — extends from the foothills across the Sierra crest to the California-Nevada state line. Includes more than 850,000 acres of public land, intermixed with 350,000 acres of private land.

![A Tahoe National Forest Sign](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/tahoe/image/DSC03676.JPG?h=790be497)
