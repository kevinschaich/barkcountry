# Sibley Lake Campground

Sibley Lake Campground is open to self-supported camping as long as weather permits after season closures (no trash collection, water, or cleaning). It is located just off U.S. Highway 14, the Bighorn Scenic Byway, and is the only Bighorn National Forest campground that offers a loop with electrical hookups. Fishing opportunities abound in nearby Sibley Lake and numerous streams.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Memorial Day - Labor Day, weather dependent

### Restrictions

14 day stay limit, Electric Motors Only on Sibley Lake

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $23 per night

Single Site Electric: $28 per night

14 sites have electrical service.

Limit 8 people per site.

Two non-towed vehicles allowed per site. The first vehicle is included in the basic fee; the second vehicle will be charged an extra fee at the campground.

### Pet Information

Dogs must be on a leash.
