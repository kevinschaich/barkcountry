## General Information

### Operational Hours

Day-use hours are 6 a.m. - 10 p.m.

### Restrictions

[**Know Fire & Other Restrictions Before You Go!**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/angeles/newsroom/releases/know-you-go-angeles-national-forest): These conditions can affect your entire trip. For example, _Extreme_ and _Critical_ fire use restrictions mean no campfires and no portable cooking systems/stoves. Bring cold food and no S'mores.

### Passes and Permits

Adventure Pass or America the Beautiful Pass required.

### Fee Site and Info

Day Use:

$5 per vehicle per day

$30 per annual

Day Use: $5/vehicle/day.

Adventure Passes are Honored: $30/annual; $5/secondary annual.

https://www.fs.usda.gov/detailfull/r5/passes-permits/?cid=stelprdb5208699&width=full

Interagency Recreation Passes are Honored.

store.usgs.gov/recreational-passes

Please be prepared to pay with CASH or CHECK.

### Pet Information

Dogs must be on leash.

36 CFR 261.8 (d) Possessing a dog not on a leash or otherwise confined.

$150 fine
