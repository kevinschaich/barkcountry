# Trail conditions

## Mount Revelstoke National Park

### Brushing and chipping work is underway on the Meadows in the Sky Parkway.

For safety, work areas will be closed to all users including pedestrians and cyclists.

The parkway will be:

• Open to Columbia Viewpoint (12 km) until Monday,  Oct. 19.

• Open to Monashee Viewpoint (8 km) until Oct. 25.

• Closed above the kiosk from Oct. 26–29.

Once work is complete, the parkway will be accessible for non-motorized users.

* * *

To protect wildlife, pets and visitors, dogs are not allowed at the Summit Area or in the backcountry of Mount Revelstoke National Park. Dogs are welcome below Columbia Viewpoint on the Meadows in the Sky Parkway, but must remain on a leash at all times. [Find dog-friendly areas and additional information](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/revelstoke/visit/chiens-dogs).

To ensure the safety of bears and people, Snowforest Campground reservations are limited to hard-sided camping units only. Hard-side camping means a truck-camper, motorhome or recreational vehicles.
