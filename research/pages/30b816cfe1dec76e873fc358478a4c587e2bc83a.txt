# Benham Falls West Day Use & Trailhead

This site provides access to the [**Deschutes River Trail**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/deschutes/recreation/trails/deschutes-river-trail). A trail goes approximately 50 yards down to a viewing platform overlooking scenic Benham Falls. Dogs must be **on leash** at all trailheads throughout the year and on the Deschutes River Trail from May 15 to September 15.

**Recreation Fee Site:** A recreation pass is required. The recreation fee is $5 per vehicle per day or may be covered by a recreation pass. Passes can be purchased at a local [**Forest Service office**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/deschutes/offices) or through a [**vendor**](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/dashboards/2d1b34fe80e948c5be01b3366d14119a).

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Day use only; no overnight camping allowed

### Restrictions

Dogs must be **on leash** at all trailheads throughout the year and [**on the Deschutes River Trail**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/media/226900) from May 15 to September 15.

### Passes and Permits

**Recreation Fee Site:** A recreation pass is required. Please check [**here**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/passes) for more information about recreation passes. _Acceptable passes include:_

- **Northwest Forest Passes**(Oregon & Washington)
  - [Day ePass](https://www.discovernw.org/mm5/merchant.mvc?screen=PROD&product_code=20281)($5)
  - [National Forest Day Pass](https://www.discovernw.org/mm5/merchant.mvc?screen=PROD&product_code=09944)($5)
  - [Annual Pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/detail/r6/passes-permits/recreation/?cid=fsbdev2_027010) ($30)
- **Local Pass**
  - [Digital Recreation Day Pass](https://www.recreation.gov/sitepass/74418) ($5) _(Day Pass for Forest Service fee sites ONLY in Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area, Deschutes National Forest, Mt. Hood National Forest, and Siuslaw National Forest)_
- **Interagency Passes**
  - [4th Grade Interagency Pass](https://everykidoutdoors.gov/pass.htm) (free for current fourth graders)
  - [Interagency Annual Pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/detail/r6/passes-permits/recreation/?cid=fsbdev2_027020) ($80)
  - [Interagency Senior Lifetime Pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/detail/r6/passes-permits/recreation/?cid=stelprdb5353054) ($80)
  - [Interagency Annual Senior Pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/detail/r6/passes-permits/recreation/?cid=stelprdb5353054) ($20)
