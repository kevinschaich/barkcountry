# Activities and experiences

## Mingan Archipelago National Park Reserve

The Mingan Archipelago offers an exceptional opportunity to reconnect with nature. The islands offer activities such as: camping, hiking, observation of monoliths, wildlife and plants. Experience an authentic outdoor adventure, filled with nordicity.

Featured


[![Sea excursion](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/qc/mingan/Activites/mer-sea-640.jpg?modified=20260519145923)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/activ/mer-sea)

### [Sea excursions](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/activ/mer-sea)

The park reserve is only accessible by boat. Make sure to reserve your transportation with a boat operator recognized by Parks Canada.

[![Standup paddleboarding around Mingan Archipelago](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/qc/mingan/Activites/planche-sup-640.jpg?modified=20260519145923)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/activ/aventure)

### [Nautical activities](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/activ/aventure)

Surrounding waters are a great place to practice nautical activities such as standup paddleboard and sea kayak.

[![Hiking in the forest at the Mingan Archipelago](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/qc/mingan/Activites/randonne-hiking-640.jpg?modified=20260519145924)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/activ/randonnee_hiking)

### [Hiking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/activ/randonnee_hiking)

There are more than 80 km of hiking trails to discover, distributed on over 9 different islands.

[![A woman with her daugther camping in the woods](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/qc/mingan/2015_ric-Lajeunesse-287.jpg?modified=20260519150542)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/activ/camping)

### [Camping and overnight accommodations](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/activ/camping)

Spend the night in one of our oTENTik tents, Ôasis accommodations, camping or 4-star accommodation.

[![Interpretive activities](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/qc/mingan/WET4/activ/640x360/mingan-interpretation-640.jpg?modified=20230620112341)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/activ/activites_interpretation)

### [Interpretive activities](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/activ/activites_interpretation)

Take part in the discovery activities offered to you by our animator guide to reinforce your knowledge of the riches of the Mingan Archipelago.
