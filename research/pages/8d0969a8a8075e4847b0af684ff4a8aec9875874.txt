# Reservoir Lake Campground and Picnic Area

Current Conditions

Drinking water is currently unavailable at this campground.

This high‑elevation lakeside campground offers quiet scenery and convenient access to the water. Reservoir Lake Campground and its day‑use picnic area sit along the lake’s south shore and include vault toilets, drinking water and a boat ramp.

Visitors can enjoy scenic hiking trails, as well as fishing, swimming and boating. The site includes 16 designated campsites, accessible restrooms and drinking water. Parking aprons average 15 by 61 feet and are gravel. Interior roads are narrow in places, with trees close to the roadway.

## Reservations

First come-first served.

## General Information

- Pets are allowed if on a leash. This is a pack it in, pack it out campsite. Please limit your stay to 16 days.
- Reservoir Lake is 11 acres and is non-motorized only. Boat ramp is primitive.

### Seasons of Use

Annually from mid-June through September, weather dependent.

### Operational Hours

Annually from mid-June through September, weather dependent.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $15 per night

There is a $15.00/night fee for this campground, with an additional $5.00 fee for extra vehicles.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 45.12381

**Longitude:** -113.44822

### Elevation

7,000

### Directions

From Dillon go South on Interstate 15 for 19 miles. Take Highway 324 west 17 miles. Turn onto Forest Road 181 and continue for 18 miles following signs to the lake.

### Travel Considerations

Campground not accessible by wheeled vehicles during winter months.  From Dillon, winter parking lot is approximately 5 miles from the campground.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Vault toilet(s)


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

Drinking water is currently unavailable at this campground.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Other Amenities

Bear-resistant food storage boxes are available, but there are no garbage services here.
