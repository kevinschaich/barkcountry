# Bulletins

## Rouge National Urban Park

### Share the trail: Help keep Rouge National Urban Park safe and enjoyable for everyone

#### Issued: September 14, 2026

Parks Canada reminds visitors that everyone has a role to play in protecting Rouge National Urban Park. By sharing trails responsibly and following [visitor guidelines,](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/securite-safety/regles-rules) visitors help protect wildlife, sensitive habitats, agricultural lands and cultural resources, while ensuring the park remains safe and enjoyable for future generations.

- Stay on designated trails and authorized routes.
- Respect and follow all posted signage and direction of Parks Canada staff.

- Keep dogs on a leash and under control at all times.
- [Leave no Trace™](https://can01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fparks.canada.ca%2Fpn-np%2Fon%2Frouge%2Fsecurite-safety%2Ftrace&data=05%7C02%7Caila.gardner%40pc.gc.ca%7C1fcf9c0e52de4539d6f708df05113d01%7C6f45dd51039e4566bcc55b700537cb87%7C0%7C0%7C639235243766204741%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=6eIqp0jX3WHxprOOGwwcpx3KqOJyPaksuTz%2BoSwTnKE%3D&reserved=0) of your visit by packing out all garbage and helping keep trails and natural areas clean. Leave plants, flowers, animals, rocks and other natural and cultural features undisturbed.
- Only use bicycles and permitted e-bikes on designated multi-use trails and authorized routes.
- When cycling, yield to other trail users, travel at a safe speed, and use your voice or bell to alert others to your presence.

**Recreational off-road vehicles are not permitted**

Recreational off-road vehicles, including all-terrain vehicles (ATVs), dirt bikes, side-by-sides, snowmobiles and other motorized recreational vehicles are not permitted on trails or lands within Rouge National Urban Park. Any off-road vehicles must remain on authorized roadways and operators must comply with Ontario’s _Off-Road Vehicles Act_ and municipal regulations. Individuals found operating recreational off-road vehicles within the park may be subject to enforcement action under applicable laws and regulations.

Unauthorized off-road vehicle use can damage sensitive habitats, disturb wildlife, create unsafe conditions for visitors, impact neighbouring communities and cause damage to trails and infrastructure.

**Reporting concerns**

If you observe unsafe behaviour, damage to park resources, unauthorized off-road vehicle use or other violations of park regulations, do not approach or confront individuals.

To report concerns to Parks Canada:

- Call **9-1-1** in an emergency
- For urgent safety concerns or suspicious activity, contact Parks Canada Dispatch at **1-877-852-3100**
- For non-urgent concerns, contact the Rouge National Urban Park information line at **416-264-2020** or email us at [rouge@pc.gc.ca](mailto:rouge@pc.gc.ca)

When reporting, provide as much information about the incident as possible, including:

- Location;
- Time;
- A description of individuals or vehicles involved; and
- All or partial licence plate information, if it can be obtained safely.

Parks Canada thanks visitors for respecting park regulations, sharing the trail responsibly, and helping protect the landscapes of Rouge National Urban Park. Together, we can ensure the park remains safe and enjoyable for everyone.
