# Activities and experiences

## Kouchibouguac National Park

Discover a national park where lush mixed-wood forests lead to colourful salt marshes and warm ocean beaches. Offshore, golden sand dunes foster calm seas. At night, this Dark Sky Preserve is a celestial masterpiece; in winter, it is a snowbound fun zone.

[![Une famille sur la plage, sur une dune](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nb/kouchibouguac/WET-2022/Kellys/kellys-24b-640.jpg?modified=20230417185955)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nb/kouchibouguac/activ/plages-beaches)
