Read park alerts, check the weather, and find information on road or trail closures.

![A tent with inflatable canoe on the edge of the Little Missouri River.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop16_9/54D46150-D631-5E2E-C339F59596987013.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

The park's primitive campgrounds are located in shady cottonwood groves at the edge of the Little Missouri River.

[Experience the Wild Side](https://www.nps.gov/thro/learn/nature/index.htm)

![A mother and baby bison lit up by a sunset glow.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop16_9/5194B98A-E383-693F-C176CF7E992EC1BF.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Wildlife abounds at TRNP! The park's prairie ecosystem is teeming with a variety of mammal, bird, reptile, and insect species.

[Visiting with Pets](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/pets.htm)

![A black dog looks directly at the camera.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop16_9/5F864E2B-F654-3EF4-65610646FF309FED.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Learn where pets can and can't go and how to keep your four-legged friends safe while visiting the park.

[Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/hiking-and-trail-information.htm)

![A ranger-led hike follows a trail into the badlands.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop16_9/5FF2C33E-E1ED-9968-695C4FA2C7CBB42C.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Hiking trails range from short, paved paths to strenuous wilderness routes. Find a trail that's right for you.

[Leave No Trace](https://www.nps.gov/articles/leave-no-trace-seven-principles.htm)

![Hikers stand at the end of a trail overlooking barren grasslands.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop16_9/51BFDCF7-FA37-230B-8A2B73EC0551A75B.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Follow these seven principles to keep national parks special places to visit and for future generations to enjoy.

[Become a Junior Ranger!](https://www.nps.gov/thro/learn/kidsyouth/junior-ranger.htm)

![Ranger hat, binoculars, and Junior Ranger books.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop16_9/5EC8043D-9F1E-E303-3E23565F641DE9C9.jpeg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

[Activities for Youth](https://www.nps.gov/thro/learn/education/classrooms/curriculum-materials.htm)

Are you looking for some fun and educational activities for your children? Check out our curriculum materials!
