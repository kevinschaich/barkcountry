Legally two separate national forests—the <b>Chequamegon National Forest</b> and the <b>Nicolet National Forest</b>—the areas were established by presidential proclamations in 1933 and have been managed as one unit since 1998. The national forest's land, trees and vegetation are part of the North Woods ecoregion that prevails throughout the upper Great Lakes region.
