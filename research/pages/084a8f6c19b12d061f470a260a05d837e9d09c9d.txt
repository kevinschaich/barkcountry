# Welcome to Coronado National Forest

Experience all four seasons during a single day's journey through Southern Arizona. Enjoy spectacular views from the mountains, wander through the desert among the giant saguaro cactus and colorful wildflowers in the morning, linger over lunch beside a mountain stream or lake, and play in the snow all before the day's end.

![Lower Thumb Rock Picnic Area in Nogales](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/coronado/image/NOGCNF_FeeSite.JPG?h=3ef413b6)
