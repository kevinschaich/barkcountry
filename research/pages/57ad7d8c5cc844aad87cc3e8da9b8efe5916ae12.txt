# Quosatana Campground

Quosatana Campground is located along the famously mighty [Wild and Scenic Lower Rogue River](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/wild-and-scenic-lower-rogue-river). The campground offers 43 sites and plenty of space for everyone to enjoy their time along the river and surrounding areas. Boat or shore fish from this beautiful 15-acre campground, which is spread out over a large river bar.

## Reservations

All sites are first come, first serve.

**This site is fully cashless**.Visitors will need to pay on-site using the [**Scan and Pay**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/learn-more-scan-and-pay) feature of the Recreation.gov app. Download the [**Recreation.gov app**](https://www.recreation.gov/mobile-app) and add a valid payment method _before_ heading out on your adventure.

## General Information

### Restrictions

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfires in developed fire pits only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Dogs must be on leash
- OHV/ATV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $20 per night

Additional Vehicle fee: $5 per vehicle per night

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in the campground. All pets must be kept on a leash, and owners are required to clean up pet waste.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Accessibility

3 of the campground's 43 sites are accessible.

### Restrooms

Flushing toilets


### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Other Amenities

- Day Use area with a play area
- Campground Host onsite
- Firewood available for purchase
- Forest Service-approved fire rings. Look up current fire restrictions before you go by visiting the [Alerts and Notices](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/alerts).
- Boat launch
- Fish cleaning station
- Garbage disposal
- RV dump station
