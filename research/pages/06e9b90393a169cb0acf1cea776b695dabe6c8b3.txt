# Pet Regulations

|     |
| --- |
| Pet regulations in the park are dictated by the Code of Federal Regulations and the Superintendent's Compendium. If you choose to bring a pet with you, please be aware of the following restrictions.<br>The following rules apply in **all** national parks:<br>- Pets must be physically restrained at all times, either in a carrier, cage, or on a leash not to exceed 6 feet in length.<br>- Pets cannot be left unattended, or tied to an object, at any time unless in a designated area.<br>- Pet excrement must be collected and disposed of by the owner of the pet.<br>- Pets are not allowed on any trails or backcountry areas.<br>- Pets cannot be brought into public buildings, public transportation vehicle, locations designated as a swimming beaches, or any area closed to possession by the superintendent. This does not apply to service animals.<br>- Pets cannot make unreasonable noise - considering location, time of day or night, or impact on park users - or frighten wildlife by barking, howling, or making other noise.<br>- Pets are not allowed at any astronomy program.<br>Great Basin National Park allows **one exception** to the above rules:<br>- Leashed pets _are allowed_ on the Lexington Arch Trail. |
