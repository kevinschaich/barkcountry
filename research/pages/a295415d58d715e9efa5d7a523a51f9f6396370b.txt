# Red Hills Lake

Red Hills Lake is located a short distance from Toledo Bend Reservoir. Completed in 1940 by the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC), this area offers camping, fishing and swimming on a picturesque 19-acre lake.

## General Information

**Red Hill Lake is OPEN for the season effective 05/01/2026.**

### Seasons of Use

May 1- October 31

### Operational Hours

Red Hill Lake is OPEN for the season.

Quiet time 10:00pm- 6:00 am

### Passes and Permits

[Activity pass](https://www.recreation.gov/activitypass/AP2682)

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $6 per night (1 vehicle limit per site)

Single Site with electric hook-up: $10 per night (1 vehicle limit per site)

Day Use:

$3 per vehicle per day

$30 per annual

**CAMPING**- $6 per campsite per night, $10 per night for sites with electric hookups (one vehicle per campsite).

**DAY-USE FEE** \- $3 per vehicle, per day.

### Pet Information
