# Check Out The New Trail Clearing Status App from the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests

**Release Date:** May 29, 2026

**Contact Information:** cyrus forman    [cyrus.forman@usda.gov](mailto:cyrus.forman@usda.gov)

**KAMIAH, ID, May 29, 2026 —** This National Trails Day (June 6th), a new app is debuting that keeps forest users up to date with recent trail maintenance. In real time, crews working on the 4 million acres of the Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests will be in the field with not just hand tools, but also tablet computers, which they are using to input real time data on their work. This app was created by Eyler White, GIS Program Manager for the forests, who, “believes this tool will be a great resource for Forest Service employees and forest users.  You can quickly see which trails have been cleared most recently.  You can select a trail to read comments about the maintenance work that was done.”

White explained that this tool is part of a bigger trend in Geographic Information Systems (GIS): “GIS is in everything we do nowadays.  Leveraging GIS for visitor services allows us to serve the public efficiently and effectively. The idea for this tool came from David Hood on the Bitterroot National Forest.  He had seen a similar app from the Bob Marshall Wilderness.

As trail crews go into the field, they will log their data and accomplishments. When they return to their offices, this information will go into the app, which will be updated for the public regularly. “Accurate information improves all recreation experiences; We hope that this app will enhance the visitor experience on our thousands of miles of trails and highlight the hard work of our Forest Service and partner trail crews who provide safe access to Idaho’s most cherished places. The public is also always welcome to call our ranger districts for even more information about recreation conditions and opportunities,” said Angela Edwards, Acting Recreation Program Manager.

The app lives on the Forests home page at [this link](https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/fcc4757ca5cc4c3e969a8a8bb5833432).  And happy trails to you this National Trails Day!
