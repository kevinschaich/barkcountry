# Braley Pond Day Use Area

Braley Pond Day Use Area is a very popular access to multiple trailheads including Ramsey's Draft Wilderness. Outdoor enthusiasts will enjoy this area for picnicking, fishing, or camping overnight in the adjacent dispersed camping site.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Open Year-Round

### Operational Hours

Open Year Round Day Use Only: Sunrise to Sunset

### Restrictions

No dispersed camping allowed in picnic area. When recreating on Forest Service Lands, follow the [safety and outdoor ethics](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/gwj/safety-ethics) recommendations.

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

### Pet Information

Keep your pet on a leash when in a recreation area or campground. The law requires dog owners to keep their dog on a leash with a length no greater than 6 feet and under physical control when within a recreational area, such as a campground or day use area (36 CFR 2616(j)).

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 38.28611111

**Longitude:** -79.30194444

### Directions

From Staunton, VA: Follow US 250 West to Churchville. Continue West on US 250 for 10 miles past Churchville and turn right onto SR 715. This road turns into Forest Development Road (FDR) 96. Braley Pond is 1 mile off of US 250.
