# Forillon National Park

### Seasonal closure

Activities have ended for the 2026 season. Thank you for visiting!

Visitors must ensure their own safety and continue to comply with current regulations.

For any emergency involving first aid, a missing person, a rescue, a wildfire, a wildlife issue, or a spill or pollution incident, speak to a park ranger on site or contact the Parks Canada Dispatch Center at [1-800-762-1422.](tel:1 800 762-1422)

For any emergency, call 911.

[More information on safety and guidelines](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/forillon/securite-safety)

### Do not feed wildlife in the park

Feeding wildlife is strictly prohibited in the park. Animals that become accustomed to human food pose a safety risk to campers, visitors and road users.

If you witness illegal activity in the park, such as feeding or harassing wildlife, call Parks Canada's dispatch service at 1-888-762-1422 (open 24 hours a day).

[More information on visitor guidelines](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/forillon/info/reglements-regulations)

Covering almost 245 km2, Forillon National Park offers a host of experiences between sea, cliffs and forest. Its history is as fascinating as its wildlife and flora. Watch the whales blow or the imprint of a fossil. Walk the Les Graves trail, climb up to see lakes or test your mettle against the crests of the Appalachians. Listen to fishing stories at the Grande-Grave heritage site. Dive into the marine world or catch your dinner at the end of the wharf!

Embrace the moment and feel the rhythm of nature!

### [Battle of Restigouche National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/qc/ristigouche)

Relive the last naval battle between France and England for possession of American territory. As well, discover the remains of the wreck of the Machault which has been under water for over 200 years! Follow an employee in period costume so you don't miss out on any of the history!

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/qc/qc-pointe-au-pere-lhn-nhs-2-640x360.jpg?modified=20241211183706)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/qc/pointeaupere)

### [Pointe-au-Père Lighthouse National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/qc/pointeaupere)

The Pointe-au-Père navigational aid station, among the most important in Canada, helps us better understand the complexity of the St. Lawrence River and the role played by its lighthouse, one of the tallest in the country. A visit to the lighthouse and the new exhibition River Sentinels are two must-sees!

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/qc/qc-saguenay-amnc-nmca-640x360.jpg?modified=20221208210710)](https://parks.canada.ca/amnc-nmca/qc/saguenay)

### [Saguenay–St-Lawrence Marine Park](https://parks.canada.ca/amnc-nmca/qc/saguenay)

Watch whales and learn about the fascinating mammals and their habitat. The Saguenay–St. Lawrence Marine Park is recognized as one of the best places in the world for whale watching.
