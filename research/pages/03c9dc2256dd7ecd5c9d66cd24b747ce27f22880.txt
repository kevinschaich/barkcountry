# Welcome to Modoc National Forest

### Your adventures await — embrace the journey.

From California's northeastern corner, enjoy a stunning vacation destination featuring diverse landscapes. Like the volcanic Cascades or the picturesque Warner Mountains. Or the lush forests, expansive ranges, and the unique Devils Garden.

Explore the forest from scenic drives and inviting picnic areas, to campgrounds with breathtaking vistas.

Embrace the wonder of star gazing in a dark sky sanctuary. Immerse yourself in the beauty of abundant wildlife. And embark on adventures through hiking, fishing, OHV, obsidian collecting, and more.

![Recreation Alert ](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/modoc/image/Obsidian%202026-04-08%20150253.jpg?h=8771458a)

Collecting obsidian with a valid permit runs from July 1 through Aug. 31, 2026.

## Modoc National Forest is Recruiting RAC Members

![RAC Recruitment ](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/modoc/image/Screenshot%202026-09-02%20141916_0.png?h=c79ebf9f)

Modoc National Forest is looking for new members for its Resource Advisory Committee (RAC), as required by the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act. Recruitment will close around September 30, 2026. The RAC helps foster collaboration and advises the National Forest on project proposals funded by the Act.

[Committees](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/modoc/working-with-us/committees)

![Wild horses on the Modoc National Forest](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/modoc/image/Modoc%20NF%20-%20WildHorses%20by%20Madeleine%20Levy%20July2026%20-3.jpg?h=0d27ee61)

The Devil's Garden Plateau Wild Horse Territory spans 500 square miles of rugged terrain, just outside Alturas, California — the largest wild horse territory managed by the Forest Service.

This territory also includes work with the **Double Devil Wild Horse and Burro Corrals.**

## Public Feedback Opportunity

### Survey on Forestwide Trails Project

Image

[![IMBA Survey](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/medium/public/r05/modoc/image/Screenshot%202025-07-15%20124035.jpg?itok=-qd6xA45)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/r05/modoc/image/Screenshot%202025-07-15%20124035.jpg)

Your input helps shape the future of trails and recreational development in the Modoc National Forest.

After several in-person public meetings in 2025, four geographic zones were identified for trail improvements: Medicine Lake, Cedar Pass, Crowder Flat, and Ash/Rush Creek.

- Each of these zones feature unique landscapes with a range of recreation opportunities including hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding, motorized access, and winter activities. These concepts may include interpretive routes too.
- In each area, preliminary trail concepts have been designed in partnership with Trails Solutions LLC. The designs focus on enhancing accessibility to key points of interest, accommodating various skill levels, and improving current recreational experiences highlighted by community stakeholders.
