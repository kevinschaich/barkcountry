# Morrison Jeep Trail Closure Area

## An area closure will begin on Monday for public safety

**Release Date:** June 18, 2026

**Cody, Wyo., June 18, 2026—** Be aware that the Shoshone National Forest is implementing a closure area around the Morrison Jeep trail outside of Clark Wyoming. This closure is to protect the safety of the public and employees alike during blasting operations on the Morrison Jeep Trail switchbacks.

The closure area prohibits being in an area that is roughly one mile up and downstream from the beginning of the switchbacks, between the Clarks Fork of the Yellowstone River and the Dillworth Bench. Specifically, it is all areas between the west bank of the Clarks Fork of the Yellowstone River and the Dillworth Bench in Township 56 North, Range 104 West, Sections 26, 27, 34, and 35. It will be in place from 12:01am on June 22nd, 2026, until 11:59pm on June 28th, 2026.

Please note that this is a change from the current closure that allows access to the river along the lower trail, NFSR 119 Friday through Sunday weekly. This access will be restored in time for the Independence Day weekend; the switchbacks will remain closed until work is completed later in the season.

The Morrison Jeep Trail was damaged during the severe flooding that affected the area in 2022 and the lower section, NFSR 119, has been closed since then. With help from disaster relief funds and amazing partnerships this area will be returned to the destination for the community that it once was.
