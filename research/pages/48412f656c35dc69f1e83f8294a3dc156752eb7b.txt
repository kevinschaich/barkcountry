# Cook Lake Recreation Area

Current Conditions

B Loop is open, all other loops are closed

**Recreation and Relaxation at Cook Lake**

Cook Lake Recreation Area is one of the Black Hills National Forest’s best-kept secrets. It offers picnic sites, camping, fishing, kayaking, hiking, and more. Because it’s tucked away from the busy central Black Hills, visitors enjoy fewer crowds and a more peaceful nature experience.

The campground sits on a hillside above the lake, while picnic areas line the shoreline with easy access from nearby parking lots. Floating docks make fishing easier, and a shoreline trail provides a gentle hike—perfect for families with young children. Wherever you go, the lake’s calm waters and surrounding forest create a tranquil setting for outdoor lovers.

Plan your visit and [reserve your campsite](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/231835?fbclid=IwAR28efZiEqnezI8_w8Ibb4K-i776UisKlnNA62SSyMhbW3HVNF184AVnYXk) online at Recreation.gov.

## A Hideaway for Outdoor Enthusiasts ... Any Time of Year

Cook Lake Recreation Area offers some stunning scenery throughout the year.  For many, this site provides numerous opportunities for recreation, outdoor enjoyment, relaxation, and an appreciation for public lands.

## Reservations

[Make Reservations Here](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/231835)

Advanced reservations are available for dates starting in mid-May through early September (peak season). Reservations are allowed up to 180 days in advance.

NOTE: Shoulder season dates are not reservable; all camping sites are available on a first come, first serve basis.

## General Information

Cook Lake Campground within the Recreation Area closes on December 15 of each year.

### Operational Hours

Cook Lake Campground closes for the off-season on December 15.

### Passes and Permits

Refer to [Forest Passes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/passes) for more information.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $28 per night

Peak Season: $28/night - See [day-use fee information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/passes/forest-recreation-passes-discounts)

Shoulder Season (October through December): $15/night

[Off Season Campground Information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/recreation/season-campground-information)

### Pet Information

- Dogs must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet in developed areas, including campgrounds, picnic sites, and day-use areas.
- Dogs are not allowed on swim beaches anywhere in the Black Hills National Forest.
- For everyone’s safety and comfort, do not leave pets unattended at campsites or in parked vehicles.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 44.5945096

**Longitude:** -104.4118458

### Directions

From Sundance, WY, take US 14 west or exit 185 off Interstate 90 to US 14 west, for 1.4 miles to Cook Lake Recreation Area sign at Forest Route 838. Turn right onto FR 838 and go 12.9 miles to FR 843. Turn right and go 5.4 miles to another Cook Lake Recreation Area sign on FR 842. Turn left and go 1.1 miles to campground.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Vault (4)


### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

- **Water is not available from about Oct. 1 through May 15.**
- In spring and fall, water service may change based on weather. Cold temperatures can freeze water lines, so availability is not guaranteed during these seasons.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

Each campsite offers one fire grill and one picnic table.
