# Banff National Park

Rocky Mountain peaks, glacial lakes, and adventure come together in Banff National Park - Canada’s first national park and the flagship of the nation’s park system. Banff is part of the Canadian Rocky Mountain Parks UNESCO World Heritage Site.

![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/ab/banff/WET4/No-Drone.png) Using [recreational drones](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/info/permis-permit/drone) in Banff National Park is prohibited. Anyone caught operating a drone within park boundaries may result in law enforcement action and a fine of up to $25,000.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ab/ab-cave-basin-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126221034)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ab/caveandbasin)

### [Cave and Basin National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ab/caveandbasin)

As the birthplace of national parks in Canada, the Cave and Basin shares stories about one of the finest and most extensive systems of natural and cultural heritage places in the world.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ab/ab-musee-banff-museum-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126221034)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ab/banff)

### [Banff Park Museum National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ab/banff)

Explore the oldest natural history museum in western Canada and its 5,000+ vintage botanical and zoological specimens — from bears and bighorns, to birds and bees.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ab/ab-rocky-mountain-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126221034)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ab/rockymountain)

### [Rocky Mountain House National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ab/rockymountain)

Discover a historic trading post along the North Saskatchewan River and immerse yourself in stories of Indigenous Peoples and legendary explorers.
