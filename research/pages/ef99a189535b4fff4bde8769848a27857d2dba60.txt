# Being bear aware on the Canfield Mountain Trail System

**Release Date:** July 21, 2017

**Contact Information:** Shoshana Cooper    [smcooper@fs.fed.us](mailto:smcooper@fs.fed.us)

We are asking the public to be aware that a sow bear with a cub(s) has been reported on Forest Trail #4 (Cave Trail) in the Canfield Mountain trail system in Coeur d’Alene. The Coeur d’Alene River Ranger District has posted advisory signs to notify people that a bear is in the area.

The district is closely monitoring the situation with Idaho Fish and Game, with a focus on balancing the needs of the bear with the recreation interest of our public. Public safety is our number one priority. At this point, it appears that the bear is exhibiting “typical” bear mannerism, and has not demonstrated aggressive behavior.

An encounter with a sow and cubs can be extremely dangerous, as mother bears are very protective of their cubs. The Idaho Panhandle National Forests encourages visitors to the Canfield Trail system to be mindful that there is a bear in the area and to be “bear aware.” Be sure to make plenty noise to notify the bear of your presence, carry bear spray, and keep dogs leashed.
