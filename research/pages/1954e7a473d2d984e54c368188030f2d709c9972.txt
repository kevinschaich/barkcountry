### Protected area. Enriching nature experiences.

Parc national d'Oka is not far from Montréal. This park has a rich historical and natural heritage. The Calvaire, with its four oratories and three chapels, is a unique complex of historic buildings in America. In Grande Baie and Rivière aux Serpents, land and water come together to give birth to ecological jewels: the wetlands. Winter and summer alike, the top of the Calvaire d'Oka trail provides an exceptional view of Lac des Deux Montagnes and the Adirondacks. Soothing and magnificent...

### [![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/oka/accordeon/oka_observation_faune.jpg)Wildlife Watching](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/oka/index.dot?language_id=1\#Itemba8f7579-b33e-4005-bdca-7d30942fc1ee)

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/oka/accordeon/oka_observation_faune.jpg)

From wetlands to mature forests, Parc national d’Oka has a variety of habitats ideal for the wildlife typical of Southern Québec. Slip into your hiking shoes; now it’s up to you to discover it as you explore the park!

A visit to Parc national d'Oka provides every attentive observer with an opportunity to discover the abundant and diverse wildlife of southern Quebec. Looking for birds? Visit Pointe aux Bleuets or La Grande Baie marsh. To reach La Grande Baie marsh, you'll cross the hickory maple grove and the silver maple swamp, two exceptional and unforgettable forest ecosystems. Once you arrive at the stretch of calm water, watch for birds of prey hunting fish or ducks splashing about. There's no shortage of action at the marsh! During the spring and fall migrations, take a walk along the floating bridge or use the observation tower as a vantage point at this must-visit spot to watch for species that only stop here on their way to their distant destination! Add this visit to your agenda!

During a hike on La Sauvagine or La Grande Baie trails, you’re almost sure to come across white-tailed deer grazing peacefully. Stop a moment near Lac la Sauvagine or Rivière aux Serpents, where beavers, musk rats, turtles and mink are active. Three other trails also cut across the park through the maple forest, an enchanting place frequented by woodpeckers, racoons and many other species!

Before leaving, get the [List of birds (in French only)](http://www.sepaq.com/pq/oka/decouvrir/portrait.dot?language_id=2) in the park. Over 200 species are on it! You’ll also find the park’s [List of mammals, amphibians and reptiles (in French only)](http://www.sepaq.com/pq/oka/decouvrir/portrait.dot?language_id=2).

Our park warden-naturalists offer many discovery activities that focus on plant and animal life.

### [![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/oka/accordeon/oka_hippocampe.jpg)Equipment for persons with reduced mobility](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/oka/index.dot?language_id=1\#Item4299654e-4444-400a-8ed4-02eebd4bcc56)

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/oka/accordeon/oka_hippocampe.jpg)

In order to make its trails accessible to persons with reduced mobility, Parc national d'Oka has acquired outdoor equipment for persons with reduced mobility. The equipment can be rented free of charge at the rental centre upon availability.

- **New!** Three‑wheeled beach wheelchair with multiple recline options.
- Ski-Vel: equipment on which a wheelchair (not included) can be mounted to allow access to snow walking trails (available in winter).
- Kartus wheelchair: specialized wheelchair designed to run in duo on multifunctional trails (available in summer).
- Hippocampe wheelchair: wheelchair adapted for swimming, sand, and snow (available in summer and winter).
- Opair tricycle: special cycle that allows a person with reduced mobility to be transported on the front (available in summer).

### [![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/oka/accordeon/oka_act_peche_blanche.jpg)Ice fishing](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/oka/index.dot?language_id=1\#Iteme0858cc9-2aa7-44cc-a480-45476c1af599)

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/oka/accordeon/oka_act_peche_blanche.jpg)

Come and fish in the stunning winter scenery of Parc national d'Oka. Lac des Deux-Montagnes is a popular spot for ice fishing. Many species of fish can be found here.

The activity is offered during the winter period, from January 10 to March 15, 2026, depending on ice and weather conditions.

Equipment rental is available daily at Le Littoral Visitors Centre, from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

The ice fishing kit includes a sled, 10 brimbals, a skimmer, a manual auger, a bucket, a ruler, frozen baits and all necessary documentation. Park access fees are not included (free of charge for children in the company of their family). Fishing licence required.

If you'd like to warm up by the fire, enjoy a hot chocolate or use the bathrooms, Le Littoral Visitors Centre is just a 5-minute walk from the fishing site. Put on your warm clothes and bring your chairs for a unique experience.

Are you a first-time ice fisherman and would like some guidance? We offer introductory sessions every Saturday morning at 10 a.m. Please arrive at least 15 minutes early at the Le Littoral Visitors Centre.

Overnight guests staying in cabins at the park can enjoy a 20% discount on the day ice fishing package.

Reservations can be made on site from Monday to Friday. Reservations are required by phone for Saturdays and Sundays (450-479-8365, ext. 6500).

## Park access authorization

There is a limit to the number of visitors that can enter a national park on any given day. It is therefore **strongly recommended** that you purchase your right of access online before a visit or that you acquire an annual pass. This guarantees you access to the park, will ensure a smoother arrival at the site and avoid unnecessary travel when the maximum capacity is reached. It can be purchased up to 30 days before a visit. You may be denied access to a sector, a trail, or an activity if a parking lot is full or if the maximum capacity has been reached.

## Important

### Don't move firewood

A single piece of firewood can destroy millions of trees. Invasive insects and diseases can hide inside it. Buy it locally. Burn it on site. [Find out more This link will open in a new window.](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/plant-health/forestry/hazards-moving-firewood)
