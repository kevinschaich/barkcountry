# Mill Creek Canyon

Current Conditions

Cannot make reservations for picnic areas after 9/30 First-come First-Serve

The upper gate (winter gate) closes on November 1st every year.

The Mill Creek Canyon road is groomed for cross country skiing at least once a week.

The groomed trail is also open to fat bikes, but please stay off the classic ski track.

## Active Alerts

[Mill Creek Canyon Upper Area, Road, Picnic Site and Trailhead Closure](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/uinta-wasatch-cache/alerts/mill-creek-canyon-upper-area-road-picnic-site-and-trailhead-closure)

## General Information

[>>Learn more about Salt Lake County Passes for Mill Creek Canyon](https://www.saltlakecounty.gov/parks-recreation/parks-trails/millcreek-canyon/)

### Fee Site and Info

Mill Creek fees  $5.00 Daily, $3.00 Senior, $50.00 Annual, $30.00 Annual Senior.

### Pet Information

- Dogs must be leashed in the entire canyon on even-numbered days and may be off-leash on odd days ONLY.
- Dogs must be leashed on all days in developed areas: picnic grounds, parking lots, roads, and housing developments.
- Dogs are not allowed in Big Cottonwood Canyon just over the ridge.
