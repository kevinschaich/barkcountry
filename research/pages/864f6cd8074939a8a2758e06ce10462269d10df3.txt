# Noisy Creek Campground

Within walking distance to the lake, the campsites in this campground are nice and private. [Noisy Creek Trail #588](http://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/colville/recreation/recarea/?recid=67948) can be accessed from the campground and the lake provides many wildlife viewing and recreational opportunities.  Campsites are suitable for both tent and RV camping.

## General Information

Other Nearby Recreation Opportunities

- [Noisy Creek Group Campground](http://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/colville/recreation/recarea/?recid=74082)

### Seasons of Use

May 24 - Labor Day Weekend

### Operational Hours

Boat launch is not open year-round.

### Restrictions

Washington state fishing license required. See Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife at [http://wdfw.wa.gov/](http://wdfw.wa.gov/) for more information.

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfire in developed fire ring only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Pets must be on leash
- OHV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days
- Food and wildlife attractants must be properly stored at all times

### Passes and Permits

- Camping: 50% discount for single site camping fees, with any of these passes: Interagency Senior, Interagency Access, Golden Age and Golden Access. Discount not available on double or group sites.
- Day use: $45 Concessionaire Season Pass accepted. No other recreation passes honored.

### Fee Site and Info

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 48.78861111

**Longitude:** -117.2813889

### Directions

From the junction of State Highway 20, follow State Highway 31 north for 3 miles to the junction of the Sullivan Lake Road (County Road 9345).  Follow Sullivan Lake Road for 8.2 miles tothe campground entrance which lies on the south end of Sullivan Lake.
