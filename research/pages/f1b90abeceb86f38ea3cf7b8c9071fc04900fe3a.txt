# Nash Fork Campground

This campground was once decommissioned due to pine beetle infestation, but with a partnership with Common Outdoor Ground, Forest Service employees were able to clean up the campground and get it in working order in 2021. There are 27 sites, all with access to a picnic table, fire ring, metal barbecue grill and vault toilets. Dogs must be leashed in the campground. The campground still doesn't have many trees, but it is just as beautiful as before with the Nash Fork Creek meandering through the campground.

The campground is convenient to the Snowy Range Scenic Byway (State 130). There are 102 recreational lakes in the area, with an extensive network of trails linking them including Brooklyn Lake, which is located 3 miles north of campground. Open season is early June to early October.

This site was approved for a fee change - [Click here for more information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/mbrtb/recreation/mbrtb-recreation-site-fee-changes-information)

## Reservations

Reservations can be made at [recreation.gov.](https://www.recreation.gov/)

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Early July (weather dependent) - Early October (weather dependent)

### Operational Hours

Open seasonally, weather dependent.

### Restrictions

Dogs must be leashed in the campground. Can accommodate trailers up to 22 feet.

### Passes and Permits

No permits required, but must have a valid fishing license purchased through Wyoming Fish and Wildlife.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $20 per night

$20 per site. No Credit cards accepted. Must have exact change or pay by check.

### Pet Information

Dogs must be kept on a leash.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 41.35876

**Longitude:** -106.23573

### Elevation

10,200 ft

### Directions

From Laramie, travel west 25 miles to Centennial on WY 130, Nash Fork is west 7.8 miles from Centennial to Brooklyn Lake Rd/ Forest Route 317. Turn right onto FR 317 and go 0.1 miles, then turn left into campground.
