# Clear Lake Day Use Area

Current Conditions

For current conditions, please contact the local ranger station.

Enjoy a picnic in the historic shelter built by the Civilian Conservation Corps in the 1930s or near the wooded edge of the beautiful, pristine Clear Lake.

The [McKenzie River National Recreation Trail](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/willamette/recreation/trails/mckenzie-river-national-recreation-trail-3507) and [Clear Lake Resort](https://www.linncountyor.gov/parksrec/park/clear-lake-resort) are nearby.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Open year-round.

### Operational Hours

Day use only: open 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

### Restrictions

- Non-motorized boating only.

### Fee Site and Info

$10.00 per vehicle

The Northwest Forest Pass and interagency recreation passes are valid at this location. Passes can be purchased at Forest Service offices or on [recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/activitypass/AP235).

### Pet Information

Within developed recreation sites, dogs must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet, or otherwise under physical restrictive control. 36 CFR 261.16(j)

### Rental Information

[Clear Lake Resort](https://www.linncountyor.gov/parksrec/park/clear-lake-resort) offers cabin rentals, boat rentals, restaurant, grocery items, snacks, fishing tackle.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 44.36803

**Longitude:** -121.9965

### Directions

From McKenzie Bridge,OR, travel east on Highway 126 for 18 miles to the Clear Lake Day Use Area.

### Travel Considerations

Trailers are not recommended.
