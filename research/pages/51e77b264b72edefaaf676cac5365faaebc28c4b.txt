# Activities and experiences

## Kootenay National Park

Enjoy the scenic drive and experience something new around every turn. Plan a multi-day backcountry trek or relax in scenic campgrounds. Soak in the mineral waters of Radium Hot Springs or challenge yourself to a full-day hike with fossils older than dinosaurs! The choice is yours.

[![hikers looking at fossils](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/kootenay/WET4/activ/activ/burgess/burgess-hike-640x360.png?modified=20221126055337)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/burgess)

### [Burgess Shale fossil hikes](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/burgess)

The Burgess Shale is one of the most significant fossil finds on earth. Learn about the Burgess Shale, explore fossils virtually, or plan a guided hike.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/kootenay/WET4/modules/Features/KNP-2019-PC_ZL_20190818-RESIZE-640x360.jpg?modified=20240705034443)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/route-drive)

### [Scenic Drives](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/route-drive)

Download the guided audio tour app, enjoy scenic viewpoints, a picnic, or a short self-guided interpretive walk.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/kootenay/WET4/modules/Features/KNP-2022-AVW-SimpsonRiver-HIKING-RESIZE-640x360.jpg?modified=20240705040700)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/randonnee-hike)

### [Hiking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/randonnee-hike)

With more than 200 kilometres of trails, Kootenay National Park offers countless possibilities for the day stroller and expert backpacker alike.

[![Two hikers on a bridge crossing Marble Canyon](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/kootenay/WET4/activ/places/marble-canyon-bridge-hikers-640x360.jpg?modified=20230626195413)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/places)

### [Points of Interest](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/places)

Explore the top things to see on your drive through the park. Take a break along the way and enjoy something new around every curve.

[![Family at campsite picnic table](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/kootenay/WET4/activ/camping/frontcountry-camping-family-640X360.jpg?modified=20230626195423)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/camping)

### [Camping](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/camping)

Find your perfect basecamp at Redstreak, Mcleod Meadows or Marble Canyon campground.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/kootenay/WET4/activ/camping/KNP-2019-HIKING-ROCKWALL-PC-ZL-RESIZE-640x360.jpg?modified=20240705040606)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/arrierepays-backcountry)

### [Backpacking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/arrierepays-backcountry)

Kootenay National Park offers extensive backcountry experiences to visitors looking to explore further into the park.
