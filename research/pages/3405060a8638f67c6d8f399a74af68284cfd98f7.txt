# Watauga Point Picnic Area

Current Conditions

Watauga Point bathrooms are WINTERIZED for the season and the general area is open to drive-thru traffic only. You may make reservations for the covered pavilion and the Shady Area in the Passes section or the Fee Site and Info section for the 2027 season.

Watauga Point Picnic Area is located on the southern shore of Watauga Lake in Carter County. Large shade trees scattered throughout grassy areas provide a park setting for picnicking. In addition to picnic facilities, there is a gravel trail that loops through the nearby forest. A large pavilion and large shady area with tables are available to rent for your family gatherings or other events. There is not a designated swim area.  Swimming is at your own risk.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

4/6/2026 - 10/13/2026

### Operational Hours

Watauga Point bathrooms are WINTERIZED for the season and the general area is open to drive-thru traffic only.

### Restrictions

- Watauga Point is for Day-use only, from sunrise to sunset.  No overnight camping.
- Alcohol is prohibited.
- No burning of plastic or other non-wood products except charcoal in grills.
- Pets must be on a maximum 6 ft. leash at all times, and pick up after them.
- Food storage restrictions apply at all areas on the Cherokee National Forest. All food and beverages must be stored in an **approved bear-proof container** or your locked vehicle at all times unless being eaten, prepared, or transported.
- It is your responsibility to read, know, understand, and follow all specific rules and regulations which are also posted at the day-use area.

### Passes and Permits

You may reserve the covered pavilion, the shady area, or both [HERE](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/121940)

### Fee Site and Info

There is no day-use fee charged at this site. To reserve the covered pavilion or the Shady area, for $30 each per day, you may do so [HERE](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/121940).  Please make sure what you are reserving is correct.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 36.3205

**Longitude:** -82.0862

### Directions

From Elizabethton, take US 19E/321 south for five miles to Hampton. Turn left on US 321/TN 67 south for five miles, then turn left into Watauga Point.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Flush toilet(s)


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

Picnicking is allowed on a first come, first serve basis on the general grounds, outside of the covered pavilion and Shady Area.
