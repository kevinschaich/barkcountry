# Hardin Ridge Recreation Area

Current Conditions

Camping will close for the season on October 25, 2026 at noon, and reopen in early April, 2027. Boat ramp, trails and day use areas will remain open without any fees until early April, 2027.

Hardin Ridge Recreation Area is located on Monroe Lake south of Bloomington, Indiana. This recreation area offers​ six campground loops, two picnic areas and three shelterhouses,​ two of which are available for reservation, a three​ lane boat launch, a swim beach with shelter and showerhouse,​ hiking trails, and two rental cabins on the lake. The​ campground loops offer modern restroom and shower​ facilities. This area is operated by a concessionaire under a​ special use permit issued by the Forest Service from early ​April through mid to late October.

### Seasons of Use

Trails are open year round.

Campsites available beginning early April through late October

### Operational Hours

Campground quiet hours are from 10:00 pm to 6:00 am

### Restrictions

Beach Rules

- No glass containers allowed on the beach
- No pets allowed on the beach

Firewood Restrictions

- Any firewood brought onto National Forest System land must meet ONE of the [firewood requirements on Hoosier National Forest](https://www.fs.usda.gov/detail/hoosier/notices/?cid=FSEPRD856963).
- Use of on-site firewood must only be from dead, downed material. Standing trees, living or dead, may not be cut or defaced.

NO HUNTING IS ALLOWED IN THE RECREATION AREA. A complete list of rules and regulations are posted on bulletin boards in the campground and are provided upon check-in at the recreation area.

### Passes and Permits

Trail Permits

- Hoosier National Forest trail permits are NOT required to ride bikes on the 2-mile long Hardin Ridge trail that runs parallel to the main campground road.

Fishing and Boating

- Indiana state fishing license is required for fishing.
- Indiana launch permit required for launching boats on Lake Monroe.
- Boaters are required to follow Indiana state boat operating and registration requirements.

Day-Use and Annual Passes

- Day-use or annual passes are required April through October and are available at the gatehouse, or instructions provided if gatehouse is not staffed.
- Day-use and annual passes may be used at Hardin Ridge, Indian-Celina, and Tipsaw Lake Recreation Areas.
- Day-use and annual passes do not cover separate camping fees.

Interagency Passes

- Interagency Access, Senior and Military-Lifetime pass holders can [receive discounts](https://www.fs.usda.gov/detail/hoosier/passes-permits/recreation/?cid=fsbdev3_017577) on base camping fees. No discounts are provided for day-use fees.

### Fee Site and Info

Peak Season (Early April - Late October)

Day Use: $8 per day

Day Use Annual: $50

See Recreation.gov for current rates and applicable discounts on over-night visits

Non-Peak Season(Campground and Bathhouses Closed): Late October - Early April

Day use: Free for trail and boat ramp use

Day Use Fees

- Required from April through October.
- $8/day for each vehicle
- Buses are $5 for the driver plus $1/person  (no passes accepted)
- $50 Annual passes available
- No federal pass discounts are available for day use (expanded amenities) fees

Camping Fees

- Walk-to and primitive sites - $24/night
- Standard Electric sites (single) - $33/night
- RV Electric - $38/night
- Double sites with electric - $52/night
- Cabin with electric - $64 per night
- Campsites are allowed one vehicle. $5/ night for extra vehicles
- Discounts are available to access and senior pass holders for single sites only.

Shelter Rental  - April through October

- with electric - $59/day
- non-electric - $50/day
