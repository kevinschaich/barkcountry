| ## Theodore Roosevelt National Park<br>You can explore the park day and night, any time of year. [Visitor center hours](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm) vary by season. In winter, there are occasional [road closures](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/roadclosures.htm) for snow and ice. [Campgrounds](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/camping.htm) are open year-round.<br>There are no restaurants or hotels in the park, but [food and lodging](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/goodsandservices.htm) can be found in nearby communities. [Pets](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/pets.htm) are welcome to visit but must remain on a leash and cannot go on trails. <br>- [![Two children look out into the badlands from an overlook.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop1_1/60088238-0895-C650-598571ADACECD464.jpg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Places to Go](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/placestogo.htm) <br>  <br>The park's 3 separate units offer a variety of experiences. Where will your adventure take you?<br>  <br>- [![A hand holds an array of credit-card sized national park passes](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop1_1/56AB43B6-A17C-4A37-19C180B64A276021.jpg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Fees and Passes](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/fees.htm) <br>  <br>Learn about no contact transactions to reduce person-to-person interactions.<br>  <br>- [![A tent with inflatable canoe on the edge of the Little Missouri River.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop1_1/54D46150-D631-5E2E-C339F59596987013.jpg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Sleep Under the Stars\<br>\<br>Camping](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/camping.htm) <br>  <br>The park's primitive campgrounds are located in shady cottonwood groves at the edge of the Little Missouri River. <br>  <br>- [![A ranger-led hike follows a trail into the badlands.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop1_1/5FF2C33E-E1ED-9968-695C4FA2C7CBB42C.jpg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Wander in Wonder\<br>\<br>Hiking Trails](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/hiking-and-trail-information.htm) <br>  <br>Hiking trails range from short, paved paths to strenuous wilderness routes. Find a trail that's right for you.<br>  <br>- [![Twenty vehicles parked in a gravel lot at a trail head surrounded by trees and layered buttes.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop1_1/33B156C0-B3F5-C537-58118D850A06FED0.jpeg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Reduce Crowding; choose another loca\<br>\<br>Reduce Crowding](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/maps.htm) <br>  <br>Be flexible and try to reduce crowding at popular destinations. If you experience a crowded location, try finding another spot in the park <br>  <br>- [![Lightning crackles amid dark skies.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop1_1/55F826E9-F43B-BB49-D8B454D4098327C8.jpeg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Current Conditions](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/conditions.htm) <br>  <br>Read park alerts, check the weather, and find information on road or trail closures. <br>  <br>- [![A bison cow lays down with two calves.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop1_1/6015620F-0935-683A-7B4A9EC362B7192F.jpg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Wildlife Watching](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/wildlife-viewing.htm) <br>  <br>Theodore Roosevelt National Park is one of the best places to readily view the most iconic animals of the West. <br>  <br>- [![A black dog looks directly at the camera.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop1_1/5F864E2B-F654-3EF4-65610646FF309FED.jpg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Visiting with Pets](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/pets.htm) <br>  <br>Learn where pets can and can't go and how to keep your four-legged friends safe while visiting the park. <br>  <br>- [![international symbols for lodging and dining in front of a badlands scenic backdrop](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/thro/crop1_1/7A4E66BD-E0A1-D945-DDE058E5D9F3EBD1.jpg?width=640&quality=90&mode=crop)\<br>\<br>Nearby Lodging and Dining](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/eatingsleeping.htm) <br>  <br>The park's only lodging is primitive camping and there are no dining facilities in the park. But nearby communities have you covered.<br>  <br>The following resources may also be helpful as you plan your next adventure in the park:<br>- [Fun for Familes](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/justforkids.htm) \- activities for people visiting the park with children, including information on the Junior Ranger Program.<br>- [Directions](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/directions.htm) \- driving directions to the park's three units, including how to get to the remote Elkhorn Ranch Unit.<br>- [Getting Around](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/gettingaround.htm) \- find out how to get from place to place within the park.<br>- [Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/things2do.htm) \- a listing of activities to enjoy during your next visit.<br>- [Safety](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/safety.htm) \- learn how to avoid common dangers in the park<br>- [Accessibility](https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/accessibility.htm) \- find out what facilities and programming are offered to meet your individual needs |
