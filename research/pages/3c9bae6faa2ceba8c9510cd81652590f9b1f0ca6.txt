# Welcome to the Flathead National Forest

In the Rocky Mountains of Northwest Montana, just south of the Canadian border and Glacier National Park, lies the 2.4-million-acre Flathead National Forest. With abundant lakes, rugged wilderness, wild rivers, over 2,000 miles of trail, campgrounds, ski areas, and year-round beauty, this forest is a premiere natural landscape of the American West.

![A backpacker takes in the view](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/flathead/image/hiking-recreation-hero.jpg?h=e4272402)

Find out what recreation opportunities are available on the Flathead National Forest, check out our online maps, or try the interactive visitor map.

![A black bear walking along a river ](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/flathead/image/wildlife-safety-hero-2_0.jpg?h=4c5c077f)

The Flathead National Forest is home to both grizzly bears and black bears. Find out more about recreating safely in bear country.
