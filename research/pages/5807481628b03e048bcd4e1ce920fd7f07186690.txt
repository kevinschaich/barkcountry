# Trailhead: South Coldwater

Current Conditions

03/04/2025: Parking is not available at the trailhead because it is beyond the closed gate on State Route 504. You can park at the Hummocks Trailhead and walk up the road to access the South Coldwater Trailhead.

**Provides access to**: [South Coldwater Trail #230A](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/giffordpinchot/recreation/trails/trail-230a-south-coldwater)

There is no vault toilet at this site. Vault toilets nearby at [Coldwater Lake Picnic and Boating Site](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/giffordpinchot/recreation/site-coldwater-lake-picnic-and-boating-area).

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Day use only: open sunrise to sunset

### Restrictions

- Pets must be on leash at all times.

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

### Pet Information

Dogs allowed on leash.

### Contact Information

**Contact Name:** Mount St. Helens National Volcanic Monument Administrative Headquarters

**Contact Phone:** [(360) 449-7800](tel: (360) 449-7800)

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 46.285997

**Longitude:** -122.252288

### Directions

From Toutle, WA take State Highway 504 east for approximately 36 miles.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.
