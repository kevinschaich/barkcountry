# Adventure awaits on the Stanislaus National Forest

Established in 1897, the Stanislaus National Forest is one of the oldest national forests. Explore the breath-taking Central Sierras with 78 lakes, 811 miles of streams and rivers, and more than 1,000 miles of hiking trails. All in, the 898,000 acres are the perfect destination for whatever your next adventure holds — hiking, biking, fishing or camping.

![A wildland firefighter uses a drip torch to carry out a prescribed burn](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/stanislaus/image/05252023-FS-BMC-002.jpg?h=1a914fb8)

Be ready when smoke's in the air.

![Compass Logo](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/stanislaus/image/STFLAndscapeLogo.png?h=32f163a2)

Focused along California’s Highway 108, the 305,000-acre planning area spans federal, state and private land. Plans include treating up to 120,000 acres of national forest.

![A wooden bridge spanning a granite outcropping with the Stanislaus River flowing beneath.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/stanislaus/image/Pinecrest-Lake-Loop-Stanislaus.jpg?h=790be497)

Our staff shares images of all the work on the forest and the incredible landscape we call home.
