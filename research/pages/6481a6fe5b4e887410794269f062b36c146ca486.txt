# Mount Revelstoke National Park

Take a cool stroll through a lush rainforest on a hot summer day. Stand at the point of no return, where champions once launched themselves down a world-famous ski jump, and imagine the thrill of flight. Climb the only mountain in the national park system that you can summit just a short walk from your car – all at Mount Revelstoke National Park.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/revelstoke/WET4/bulk-banners/640x360-meadows-parkway-feature.jpg?modified=20230809183612)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/revelstoke/activ/promenade-parkway)

### [Meadows in the Sky Parkway](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/revelstoke/activ/promenade-parkway)

Drive 26km of paved road to the summit to visit Mount Revelstoke's world-renowned wildflower meadows.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/bc/glacier-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220951)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/glacier)

### [Glacier National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/glacier)

Glacier is known for steep alpine hikes, lush cedar forests and unparalleled backcountry skiing. At the heart of the park, history comes alive at Rogers Pass, the key to completion of Canada’s transcontinental railway.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/bc/rogers-pass-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220951)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/bc/rogers)

### [Rogers Pass National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/bc/rogers)

Discover the role Rogers Pass played in the transcontinental Canadian Pacific Railway. Follow an abandoned rail line to the ruins of a Victorian-era resort, where legendary Swiss mountain guides forged popular trails and climbing routes.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/bc/yoho-640x360.jpg?modified=20230210184900)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/yoho)

### [Yoho National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/yoho)

In the shadow of the Great Divide, Yoho’s towering rockwalls, spectacular waterfalls and soaring peaks reveal the secrets of ancient life, the power of ice and water and the stories of plants and animals that continue to evolve today.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/bc/kootenay-640x360.jpg?modified=20230210184859)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay)

### [Kootenay National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay)

With diverse terrain embracing everything from arid grasslands to glaciers, Kootenay National Park offers the full Rocky Mountain experience along the historic Banff-Windermere Highway. Take a scenic drive or stay and explore the park’s treasures.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ab/ab-banff-pn-np-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126221033)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff)

### [Banff National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff)

Founded in 1885, Banff is Canada’s first national park and part of the first national park system in the world. With its soaring peaks, azure lakes and abundant wildlife, this Rocky Mountain park attracts millions of visitors every year.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/ab/JasperNP_most-places_640x360.jpg?modified=20221126221035)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper)

### [Jasper National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper)

Abundant in natural beauty, wildlife, lakes, waterfalls and mountains, Jasper is a wild and wondrous place to visit year-round.
