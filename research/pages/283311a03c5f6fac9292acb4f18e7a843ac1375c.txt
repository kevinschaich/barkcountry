# Browns Lake Campground

Browns Lake Campground is adjacent to Browns Lake, a high elevation lake in a setting of forested mountains, offering 18 campsites with picnic tables and fire rings. A small day use, picnic area close to the campground has views of the lake, swimming access, and a primitive boat launch/beach area.

Browns Lake Trail No. 320 is an easy one-mile, non-motorized hiking trail that follows the south and east side of the lakeshore and ends at a fish viewing platform. The trailhead is located near the road to the boat launch. The trail winds through a patch of old growth cedar and provides excellent views of the lake. There is a fish viewing platform on the east end of the shore.

Browns Lake is fly fishing only. Motorized boats are not allowed. Shoreline access is good throughout most of the perimeter of the lake.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Memorial Day Weekend - October 31

### Operational Hours

Check in: 2:00 p.m.; Check-out: 12:00 p.m.; Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. until 6:00 a.m.

### Restrictions

Washington state fishing license required. See Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife at [http://wdfw.wa.gov/](http://wdfw.wa.gov/) for more information.

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfires in developed fire ring only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Pets must be on leash
- OHV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days
- Food and wildlife attractants must be properly stored at all times
- Non-motorized lake use only
- Browns Lake is fly fishing only

### Passes and Permits

Camping: 50% discount for single site camping fees, with any of these passes: Interagency Senior, Interagency Access, Golden Age and Golden Access. Discount not available on double or group sites.

Day use: Concessionaire Season Pass accepted. No other recreation passes honored for day use fee.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $29 per night

Day Use:

$10 per vehicle per day

Season Pass for Colville and Idaho Panhandle: $52

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 48.4363341

**Longitude:** -117.1959819

### Directions

Browns Lake Campground lies 25 miles northeast of Newport.

From Newport, WA:  Follow State Highway 20 north for 15 miles to the town of Usk. Cross the bridge over the Pend Oreille River on the east edge of town and follow the Kings Lake Road (County Road 3389) 4 miles to the junction of Forest Road 5030000 (Browns Lake). Travel north on 5030000 for 4 miles to the campground entrance.
