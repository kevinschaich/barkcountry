# Fishlake Hightop (North) Trailhead

**Overview**

FishLake Hightop (North) Trailhead is located 32.5 miles from salina Utah. The last 3 miles of road is not maintained for passenger cars; the best access is by using high clearance vehicles.

**Facilities**

Vault toilet

**Natural Features**

This trail includes scenic expansive sagebrush views with dense pine trees forests.

**Recreation**

Popular activities in the area include ATV/UTV riding, fishing, hiking, biking, horseback riding and wildlife watching.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

June - October

### Restrictions

- Horse and foot trail only
- No e-bikes or electric motorcycles allowed
- Please keep dogs on a leash

### Pet Information

- Pets must be leashed at all times within this recreation area. This policy is in place to ensure the safety and enjoyment of all visitors and to protect wildlife.
- Clean up dog waste and dispose of it properly.
- Keep dogs from chasing, harassing, and disturbing wildlife, livestock or other visitors.
- Yield to other hikers, horses, and other trail users by stepping off the trail and controlling your dog.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 38.6853677

**Longitude:** -111.711104

### Directions

The Fishlake Hightop (North) Trailhead is  48.5  miles east of Richfield, Utah. From Richfield travel east on I-70 for 24.2 miles, exiting on the Gooseberry Road (Exit 63). Travel south on the Gooseberry Road (FR460). Turn right (west) on FR056 for 0.4 miles and turn left on FR350 for 2.9 miles. The trailhead is on the left.

### Parking

Dirt parking area

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

None


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are not available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are allowed at this site.

- No horse hitching posts or corrals
- Horse trails available nearby
- Stay on designated trails
- Use certified weed-free hay to prevent invasive species
- Tie horses to trees in a way that won't damage the tree.
