| We gladly welcome well-behaved pets to the park! Pets are allowed anywhere outdoors, provided they are properly restrained. Service animals are permitted inside the visitor center.<br>If your pet would like to become a B.A.R.K. ranger, stop by the visitor center's entrance desk during open hours and show the ranger photos or videos of your pet following all the principles of B.A.R.K on the park grounds. Your pet will earn a special keepsake prize! At this time, B.A.R.K. tags are only available in-person and cannot be mailed. <br>![a woman holds a black cat who is wearing a bandana and a leash. The gateway arch is in the background.](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/images/zeb.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Don't be feline left out! Adventurous kitties like Zeb, pictured, are also welcome outdoors at the park and at our summer B.A.R.K. Ranger events. Cats must be restrained on leashes or in strollers, bags, or backpacks. <br>NPS Photo.<br>### 2027 B.A.R.K. Ranger Events:<br>Check back soon, we look forward to adding event dates in early 2027. <br>B.A.R.K. Ranger events are always free and open to everyone. Dogs must be leashed and friendly. All events will start at 10:00 a.m. at the park entrance. Since B.A.R.K. Ranger events are held outdoors, they will be cancelled in case of inclement weather. Check out our [park calendar](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/calendar.htm) or [email us](https://www.nps.gov/common/utilities/sendmail/sendemail.cfm?o=4180C8A894D0AE8395AE16BDD919BBBE089A4999&r=/jeff/planyourvisit/pets.htm) for more information.<br>![Black and brown dog sitting on grass with a wooden BARK ranger tag on it's blue collar.](https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/images/JaxBadgecropped.JPG?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Brand new B.A.R.K. Ranger Jackson smiling with his dog tag. <br>NPS Photo: D. Reissing<br>## B.A.R.K. Ranger Principles<br>### B = Bag Your Poo<br>Keep our park a clean and healthy place to visit. Trash containers are located throughout the park. Please remember that dog feces is not a natural fertilizer. Runoff from dog feces can contaminate our park and can also spread diseases such as parvovirus, giardia, and roundworms.<br>### A = Always Wear a Leash<br>Even a well-behaved dog can behave unpredictably when visiting an unfamiliar environment. Please stay on a leash no more than 6 feet long at all times. This keeps everyone safe.<br>### R = Respect Wildlife<br>You are visiting the homes of other animals when walking around the park, so please be respectful. No chasing any squirrels, rabbits, opossums, or other creatures you may encounter.<br>### K = Know Where You Can Go<br>Pets are allowed in all the outdoor areas of the park, but only service animals can go inside buildings. Dogs may not swim in the reflecting pools or root around in garden beds. Pets should never be left unattended anywhere in the park. Pets should also never be left unattended in vehicles, especially during summer.<br>[![B.A.R.K.
