# Prince Edward Island National Park

**Infrastructure Work at Ross Lane Beach**

From October 19, 2026, through spring 2027, coastal access at Ross Lane Beach will not available while work on roadways, parking areas, and dune crossing infrastructure is undertaken. [Read more](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/pe/pei-ipe/bulletins).

**Infrastructure Work in Cavendish area of PEI National Park**

From October 14, 2026, to approximately December 2026, road resurfacing work will be undertaken on the Cavendish Campground perimeter road. [Read more](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/pe/pei-ipe/bulletins).

**Infrastructure Work in Greenwich, PEI National Park**

Beginning October 19, 2026, road and parking lot improvement work will take place between the Greenwich Visitor Information Centre and the Greenwich Dunes Trail trailhead and is expected to continue until approximately December 2026. [Read more](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/pe/pei-ipe/bulletins).

Gentle surf strokes sandy beaches alongside red cliffs and wind-sculpted dunes. Cycle a seashore path, savour a picnic by a lighthouse and spot heron wading in coastal bays. Hike woodlands and overlook ponds watching for red fox, waterfowl and warblers, then head to one of many beaches to build spectacular sandcastles. At sunset, roast marshmallows over a campfire listening to tales and songs - Prince Edward Island National Park is a giant playground for kids of all ages.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/pei/pei-dalvay-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126215731)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/dalvay)

### [Dalvay-by-the-Sea National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/dalvay)

Surrounded by sand dunes, beaches and a lake, Dalvay-by-the-Sea is a peek into a luxurious Victorian estate. Explore the interior, cycle the shoreline, stroll the grounds or simply relax in an Adirondack chair with a view.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/pei/pei-green-gables-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126215731)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/greengables)

### [Green Gables Heritage Place](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/greengables)

Be charmed by an encounter with Canada’s iconic redheaded sweetheart, Anne of Green Gables. Relive the fictional orphan’s youthful adventures at the Island farmstead that inspired author Lucy Maud Montgomery.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/pei/pei-ardgowan-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126215731)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/ardgowan)

### [Ardgowan National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/ardgowan)

Stroll the serene and grand grounds surrounding Ardgowan, the former Charlottetown home of William Henry Pope, one of Canada’s Fathers of Confederation who resided and entertained in this cottage-style house during the Charlottetown Conference of 1864.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/pei/pei-province-house-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126215732)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/provincehouse)

### [Province House National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/provincehouse)

The birthplace of Confederation and the seat of Prince Edward Island's provincial legislature since 1847, Province House National Historic Site is a Charlottetown highlight. Stroll the grounds to experience the magnificent neo-classical architecture of this majestic building and view interpretive panels.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/pei/pe-skmaqn-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126215731)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/skmaqn)

### [Skmaqn–Port-la-Joye–Fort Amherst National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/skmaqn)

Established in 1720, Port-la-Joye was the entry point for European settlers coming to Île Saint-Jean to embark on a new life. There are centuries of history to discover in this historic location, declared a national historic site in 1967.
