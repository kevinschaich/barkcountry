# Shongelo Lake

Current Conditions

Shongelo is a fee area and is open seasonally from May through September.

This small recreation area is located 5 miles north of Raleigh, MS and 21 miles south of Forest, MS. Shongelo Lake is a 5-acre lake offering bank fishing. A swimming area, seven picnic sites, picnic shelter and hiking trail make this site appealing for family day use enjoyment.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

March 1-December 15

### Operational Hours

8 a.m. to 7 p.m.

### Restrictions

No alcoholic beverages are allowed. Pets must be on a leash.

### Passes and Permits

Passes Honored - Access, Senior, and MS Adventure

Passes can only be purchased at National Forests in Mississippi Ranger District Offices.

### Fee Site and Info

Day Use:

$3 per vehicle per day

$5 day use (includes picnicking, swimming and hiking).

A fee station is located at the entrance gate. No attendant will be on duty. Users should make sure they have correct currency before arriving at the recreation area.
