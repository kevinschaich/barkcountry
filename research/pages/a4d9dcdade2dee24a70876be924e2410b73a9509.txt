Yosemite is a busy place! You may find yourself stuck in traffic, waiting at the entrance, or unable to find parking. Plan accordingly.

So many places to go and so little time! Learn more about all the areas and attractions in Yosemite.

Thinking about bringing your four-legged friend on vacation? Learn more about Yosemite's pet policies.

One app, every park at your fingertips. The NPS App offers tools to explore more than 400 national parks nationwide.

[Waterfalls](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/waterfalls.htm)

![View of Vernal Fall](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/yose/crop16_9/F2D3A178-1DD8-B71B-0BDEA717E310E9BF.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Yosemite is home to countless waterfalls. The best time to see waterfalls is during spring, when most of the snowmelt occurs.

[Camping](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/campgrounds.htm)

![Tent and visitor](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/yose/crop16_9/E31E6090-1DD8-B71B-0BB171E528A00012.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Camping is extremely popular in Yosemite. Plan ahead and figure out what options you have for this enjoyable activity.

[Lodging](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/lodging.htm)

![Lodging](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/yose/crop16_9/F8434FC1-A255-027E-390EBCFCB7A45325.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Looking for lodging? Learn more about our popular overnight accommodations.

[Wilderness Permits](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/wildpermits.htm)

![Two tents and campers overlook the high country, with Half Dome and a lake in the background.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/yose/crop16_9/56CD3D55-E4EF-1B16-0DBD63EB2F5193A4.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Wilderness permits are required for all overnight hikes. Learn how and when to apply for a permit.

[Half Dome Permits](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/hdpermits.htm)

![Hikers going up the Half Dome cables](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/yose/crop16_9/279F8F08-EB57-9B20-A70D57C8C875C3A3.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Permits to hike to the top of Half Dome are required when the cables are up and are distributed by lottery. Learn how to apply for a permit.
