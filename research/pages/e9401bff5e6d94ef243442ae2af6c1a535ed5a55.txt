# Corral Creek Pasture/South Hay Creek (Dog Mountain) Division Fence

The proposed project is a one mile fence to exclude the South Fork of Hay Creek not within the S. Hay Creek Riparian Pasture from use while cattle are on the Corral Springs pasture of the Dog Mountain Allotment to better manage the riparian area.

## Project Summary

### Project Overview

**Current Status:**

Completed

**Location Summary:**

The fence will run from the NE corner of the S. Hay Creek Riparian Pasture to the southern edge of the Dry Valley-Sparrow private Property in Section 1 of T 40 S R 16 E.
