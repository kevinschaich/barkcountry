# Vuntut National Park

Remote and unspoiled Arctic wilderness, a First Nations history dating back millennia and the setting for one of the planet’s great animal migrations await the few who make the trek to Vuntut National Park.

Featured


[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/yt/vuntut/WET4/info/Features/John-Tizya-centre-feature.jpg?modified=20221121210502)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/vuntut/visit/serv/tizya)

### [The John Tizya Centre](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/vuntut/visit/serv/tizya)

Take a tour of the exhibits, listen to audio recordings of elders’ stories, admire traditional tools, learn about the magnificent Porcupine Caribou Herd, and find out what life was like in the ancient Beringian landscapes of the last ice age.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/yt/vuntut/WET4/info/Features/Old-Crow-Flats-feature.jpg?modified=20221121210503)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/vuntut/nature/habitats)

### [Van Tat - Old Crow Flats](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/vuntut/nature/habitats)

The Old Crow Flats is a vast wetland complex of shallow lakes and small streams, important to the Vuntut Gwitchin for subsistence hunting and other traditional practices.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/yt/vuntut/WET4/info/Features/PCH-feature.jpg?modified=20221121210502)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/vuntut/nature/caribou)

### [Porcupine caribou herd](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/vuntut/nature/caribou)

The Porcupine Caribou Herd is one of the largest caribou herds in North America. The herd is central to the culture and way of life of the Vuntut Gwitchin.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

### [Kluane National Park and Reserve](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/kluane)

With vast icefields and 17 of Canada’s 20 highest peaks, Kluane offers outstanding alpine scenery, rich First Nations culture and history, and a mix of extreme adventure and accessible outdoor recreation.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/yt/yt-ivvavik-pn-np-640x360.jpg?modified=20221125213622)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/ivvavik)

### [Ivvavik National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/yt/ivvavik)

Rafters from around the world meet up in Ivvavik National Park. The Firth River slices through canyons and mountain valleys to the Arctic Ocean.  A fly-in base camp offers hikers access to an Arctic landscape of tors, peaks and rolling hills untouched by the last Ice Age.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/yt/yt-chilkoot-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221125212558)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/yt/chilkoot)

### [Chilkoot Trail National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/yt/chilkoot)

Hike a legendary trail through the northern Coast Mountains, from Alaska’s Taiya Inlet to the headwaters of the Yukon River in northern British Columbia.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/yt/yt-ss-klondike-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221125212558)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/yt/ssklondike)

### [S.S. Klondike National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/yt/ssklondike)

Steam-powered sternwheelers were the Yukon’s link to the outside world for almost a century. The S.S. Klondike National Historic Site in Whitehorse honours these vessels, so vital to the settlement of the North.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/yt/yt-klondike-lhn-nhs-640x360.jpg?modified=20221125212558)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/yt/klondike)

### [Klondike National Historic Sites](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/yt/klondike)

Step back into the days of the 1898 Klondike Gold Rush. Prowl a paddlewheeler. Explore goldfields. Magical Dawson City is a frontier town where history mingles with everyday life.
