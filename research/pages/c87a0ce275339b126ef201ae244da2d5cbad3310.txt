# Hanna Campground

Current Conditions

Walk in tent sites are open during the off season

**Adjacent to Spearfish Creek**

Hanna Campground is adjacent to the spruce-lined east Spearfish Creek and has 13 sites available during the regular season. The campground is within the Spearfish Canyon management area, but not located directly within the Canyon proper. The drive in side of the campground for RV's and trailers is closed for the season but there are walk-in tent sites that are open and available year-round (Please Note: the tent sites are available for use when the gate is closed). Parking for the tents are the two road pullouts near the foot bridges. Sites are spread across a wide meadow with a pine-covered mountain surrounding. This is a scenic and peaceful place to camp. The Spearfish Canyon Scenic Byway begins north of the campground at State Rt. 14A. There are limited groceries and gas at Cheyenne Crossing, north of the campground.

## Tucked Away in the Northern Hills

Hanna Campground "wows" most everyone that stays within the Northern Hills Ranger District. It is surrounded by mountainous scenery and captivates the attention of any outdoor enthusiast. Although close to Spearfish Canyon Scenic Byway, it is far enough away to maintain a quiet, somewhat secluded, setting.

## Reservations

Reservations are not available for Hanna Campground. All sites are available on a first come, first served basis. Other campgrounds within the Black Hills National Forest that allow for advanced reservations can be found at [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/1057).

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Available year round ... with limited services in the shoulder seasons.

### Passes and Permits

Refer to [Forest Passes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/passes/forest-recreation-passes-discounts) for more information.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $21.50 per night

Peak Season: $24 / night (Approximately Mid-May through Late September)

Shoulder Season: $15 / night (Approximately Late September through Mid-May)

[Off Season Campground Information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/recreation/season-campground-information)

NOTE: Please be advised that Hanna Campground closes for the season on September 30 each year. Walk-in tent sites are available year-round with limited services in the shoulder seasons. Furthermore, the Forest does not provide any maintenance or snow plowing services during the winter months.

### Pet Information

Dogs in developed recreation sites, such as campgrounds, picnic sites, and day-use areas, must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet at all times. Furthermore, dogs are not allowed on any swim beaches on the Forest.  For the safety and comfort of fellow recreationalists, the Forest encourages guests to avoid leaving pets unattended at campsites and/or in parked vehicles.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 44.2750067

**Longitude:** -103.8507145

### Directions

To access Hanna Campground from Lead, South Dakota, travel south on US Highway 85 for approximately eight miles to just past the intersection of Cheyenne Crossing and US Highway 14A. Turn left (south) onto FSR 196 and travel for about two miles to the campground entrance.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Accessibility

The Forest renovates this campground using funds generated from campground fees.  The design and improvements are prioritized to meet federal accessibility standards. The updates also enhance access for modern-day RVs and campers, improve fire safety, and reduce long-term maintenance costs.

### Restrooms

Outhouse


### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

Water is not available from approximately October 1 through May 15 (shoulder season).  Depending on weather conditions, water availability may change in the spring and fall seasons, due to cold temperatures and the potential for freezing waterlines.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

Each campsite offers one fire grill and one picnic table.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are not allowed at this site.

### Other Amenities

An orientation board is located at the campground entrance and provides information and maps related to the loops and sites, nearby amenities, fees, rules and regulations, etc.
