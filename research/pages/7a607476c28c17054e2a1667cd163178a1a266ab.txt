# Peninsula Dispersed Campground and Recreation Area

Current Conditions

Closes 10/19/2026.

24 dispersed camping sites along the Clark Fork River near fishing, rafting, and swimming opportunities. No services available besides two vault toilets.

View the site through the [interactive map](https://arcg.is/19WeOX2).

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Open year-round. Restrooms close in October for winter season.

### Restrictions

Please visit the [Forest's Alerts page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/lolo/alerts) for information on restrictions, in particular please review the [Food/Wildlife Attractant Storage Order](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/alerts/r1-2023-02-ncde-food-and-wildlife-attractant-storage-order) to help reduce the likelihood of human-to-bear conflicts.

### Pet Information

Dogs must be under control at all times.

## Getting There

### Directions

From I-90 take St. Regis Exit 33, travel approximately 11.5 miles northeast on Highway 135 and turn right and follow the road to the campsites. From Highway 200 turn southwest onto Highway 135 and travel 10.5 miles, passing Ferry Landing, and turn left into the Peninsula area following road signs.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Two vault toilets are available. The restrooms close in October for the winter season.

### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.
