# Camping at Pinnacles

|     |
| --- |
| ## **Pinnacles Campground Overview**<br>Located on the **east side** of Pinnacles National Park, the Pinnacles Campground offers a variety of camping options, including tent, group, and RV sites. **Please Note:** There are no connecting roads between the east and west entrances of the park, so the campground is only accessible from the east side.<br>Each tent and group site is equipped with a picnic table and fire ring, while most RV sites have electrical hookups and share community tables and barbecue pits. Water is available throughout the campground, and many campsites are shaded by oak trees. Amenities include coin-operated showers, a dump station, and a swimming pool, which is typically open from mid-April through the end of September, depending on weather conditions.<br>### **Pet Guidelines**<br>**[Pets are welcome](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/planyourvisit/pets.htm)** in the campground, as well as on paved roads and in parking lots, but they are not allowed on park trails. Pets must be on a **six-foot leash** at all times and should never be tied to an object or left unattended.<br>### **Campfire Regulations**<br>Campfires are permitted only during certain fire conditions. Please check the **[Current Conditions page](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)** for updates on fire regulations in the park. Note that **collecting firewood** is prohibited in Pinnacles National Park.<br>### **Help Protect Wildlife**<br>To keep both visitors and wildlife safe, please do not feed the animals in the park. For more information, visit the **[Keep Wildlife Wild page.](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/planyourvisit/kww.htm)**<br>### **Campground and Store Information**<br>The **Pinnacles Campground** and **Campground Store** are operated by **[Pinnacles Recreation Company](https://www.visitpinnacles.com/).** The Campground Store is open daily from **9:30 am to 5:00 pm**. For any questions regarding camping or the store, call **(831) 200-1722**.<br>### **Reservations**<br>- **Tent and RV sites** can be reserved up to **6 months** in advance.<br>- **Group sites** can be reserved up to **12 months** in advance.<br>To make a reservation, visit [**recreation.gov**](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234015) or call **(877) 444-6777**. |
