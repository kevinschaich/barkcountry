# Pike Community Group Campground

Current Conditions

The campground is open late May to mid-September, depending on weather.

This campground has a capacity for 100 people and provides picnic tables, group fire grates, drinking water, a campfire circle, trash dumpsters, vault toilets, a children's play area, a sand volleyball court, a softball field and horseshoe pits.

[![](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r02/psicc/image/20140314-FS-JC-1007.jpg?h=790be497)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/psicc/recreation/pike-community-group-campground#)

## Reservations

[Make Reservations Here](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/231872)

Call 877-444-6777 or visit [recreation.gov](http://www.recreation.gov/) at least four days in advance.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Late May to mid-September

### Operational Hours

Check in: 2 p.m.

Check out: 12 p.m.

Quiet hours: 10 p.m. to 8 a.m.

### Restrictions

**The following restrictions apply to this campground**:

- The site must be occupied within 24 hours of reservation.
- Dogs must be leashed at all times.
- No discharge of firearms or fireworks is allowed.
- Only licensed vehicles can operate inside the campground.
- Camping is not allowed behind a locked gate.

### Fee Site and Info

The fee is $150 per night for up to 100 people. This campground is available by reservation only through [recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/).

### Pet Information

Dogs must be leashed at all times.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 39.06800997

**Longitude:** -105.08951402

### Directions

From Colorado Springs, travel west on Highway 24 for approximately 18 miles to Woodland Park. From Woodland Park, travel 5 miles north on Highway 67. Pike Community Group Campground is located on the right side of the road just past the entrance to South Meadows Campground.

### Parking

There are 48 parking spaces available.
