# North Fork Trail

Current Conditions

Caution on North Fork Trail: Large animal carcass is visible from the trail. Predators may be present. Alternate trail option available from road.

[Temporary Reroute Map Available](https://www.fs.usda.gov/media/273172)

This trail crosses through the Comanche Peak Wilderness and into Rocky Mountain National Park. From the parking area the trail drops into a canyon and follows the North Fork of the Big Thompson River for several miles. Bridges have been placed at some of the stream crossings. The trail briefly passes through the private Cheley Camp, please do not trespass. Before the trail departs from the river it passes through large open meadows with historic cabins, commonly known as Deserted Village and then ascends to the border of Rocky Mountain National Park. Access the North Fork Trail from the Dunraven Trailhead graveled parking lot.

## Specific Trail Information

### Trail Number

#929

### Trail Type

**Standard/Terra Trail** — A trail that has a surface consisting predominantly of the ground and that is designed and managed to accommodate use on that surface.

## General Information

Peak Elevation: 8,970'

You can find more information at [Poudre Wilderness Volunteers](https://pwv.org/trails/find-a-trail).

### Restrictions

Dogs must be on a hand-held leash. No motorized or mechanized equipment. Camps, campfires, and stock, where allowed, at least 200 feet from water and trails. Group size limited to 12 people or stock animals combined. Certified weed-free hay is required for stock. No campfires – stoves only. Overnight stock use is prohibited.

## Getting There

### Parking

There is room for 23 vehicles.
