# Animals

Mammals of the Fishlake National Forest

### Please do not disturb or harass wildlife!

### Remember, when you venture into the Great Outdoors, treat the homes and backyard of wildlife with respect.

#### Key to species occurrence

**Po** = **Po** ssibly occurs on the Forest. This category includes species which are known to exist in close proximity to the Forest boundary, or which may accidentally migrate off a normal route onto the Forest.

**Pr** = **Pr** obably occurs on the Forest This category includes species which should occur on the Forest as indicated by species distribution date, but which have not been verified by actual sightings or other evidence.

**K** = **K** nown to exist on the Forest This category includes all species which have actually been verified as existing on the Forest.

An easily printable PDF version of this information may be found [here](https://www.fs.usda.gov/media/110423).
