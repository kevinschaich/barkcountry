# Newsome Campground

Current Conditions

Open year-round but might not be accessible because of snow.

Located near the historic Newsome Work Center, the Newsome campground provides nice, shaded camp sites. Beaver Creek flows next to the campground. The campground includes 2 metal fire rings, 1 picnic table, and a toilet.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

The campground is open year-round, but might not be accessible because of snow.

### Restrictions

Garbage services not available. Pack It In, Pack It Out.

### Fee Site and Info

No Fee.

## Getting There

### Elevation

4010 feet

### Directions

From State Highway 14, go north on Newsome Creek Road #1858 approximately 5.2 miles

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.
