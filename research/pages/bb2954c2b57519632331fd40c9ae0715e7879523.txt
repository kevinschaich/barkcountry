# Welcome to Shoshone National Forest

Named after the Shoshoni people who have called this area of northwestern Wyoming home for thousands of years, the Shoshone National Forest is filled with special places. With more than 1.4 million acres of congressionally designated wilderness, the Shoshone National Forest is an integral part of the 10-million acre Greater Yellowstone Ecosystem.

![Man on mountain bike in a forest looking down at a valley](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r02/shoshone/image/2024_RecreationCard_SNF_0.jpg?h=ea2c671e)

The Shoshone National Forest offers over 1,300 miles of trails, 32 campgrounds, and 11 picnic sites. Four scenic byways traverse the Forest, offering spectacular views of rugged mountain peaks, expansive plateaus awash in wildflowers, glaciers, rivers and streams, and unparalleled wildlife watching.

[Start Planning](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/shoshone/recreation)

![Woodpile](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r02/shoshone/image/WoodPile1_AdobeSI.jpg?h=ea2c671e)

Permits are needed for activities such as collecting firewood, cutting Christmas trees, commercial filming and other special events.

[Do You Have a Permit?](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/shoshone/permits)

![Shoshone National Forest Maps](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r02/shoshone/image/IMG_4814.jpg?h=8a862930)

The Shoshone National Forest publishes a variety of map products to help you plan and enjoy your visit. Maps and brochures are available online with even more options available at Shoshone National Forest offices.

[Find Your Map](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/shoshone/maps-guides)

![America the Beautiful passes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r02/shoshone/image/2025_Passes_SNF_xxx.jpg?h=a1e1a043)

America the Beautiful Passes are a suite of annual and lifetime passes that provides U.S. citizens and visitors an affordable and convenient way to access Federal recreational lands. Up to 100% of the Pass program's proceeds are used to improve and enhance visitor recreation services.

[Types of Passes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/shoshone/passes)
