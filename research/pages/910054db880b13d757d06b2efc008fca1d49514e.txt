# Discover Your National Forests

The Green Mountain and the Finger Lakes National Forests offer sprawling quintessential northeastern woodland settings in Vermont (Green Mountains) and New York (Finger Lakes).

Whether to hike, ski, or admire the fall foliage, diverse recreation opportunities draw in visitors year-round.

![Cut and stacked wood](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r09/gmfl/image/20240425-FS-FingerLakes-BAG-Caywood%20%2833%29.JPEG?h=916fe5ee)

Firewood permits are now available online! [**Purchase a firewood permit**](https://epermits.fs2c.usda.gov/forest/0920) through USDA E-Permits or learn more on our [firewood](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/gmfl/forest-products/firewood) page.

## Know Before You Go

![A narrow dirt and gravel road, flanked by trees whose leaves have begun to change color.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r09/gmfl/image/20211013-FS-GreenMountain-CS.JPEG?h=06812a4a)

Forest Roads vary; some may have portions with gravel and others may be constructed from the native soils in the area. These roads may be infrequently maintained with possible rocks, ruts, washouts, downed trees and encroaching brush.

![Forest road with yellow and brown leaves gathering on top.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r09/gmfl/image/20211001-FS-GreenMountain-BAG-ChittendenLeffertsPond%20%2888%29.jpg?h=199d8c1f)

GPS or a cell phone may not be reliable once you’re in the forest. It is important to carry and know how to use a map and compass when visiting.

![Potomac Road - Finger Lakes National Forest](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/legacy-media/gmfl/Potomac%20Road%20-%20Finger%20Lakes%20National%20Forest.jpg?h=10d202d3)

Most Green Mountain and Finger Lakes National Forest Roads are closed until mid-spring. Please refer to the [Motor Vehicle Use Maps](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/gmfl/maps-guides) for a list of seasonal and special vehicle designations.
