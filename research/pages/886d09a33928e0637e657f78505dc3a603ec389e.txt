# Tibble Fork Reservoir & Trailhead

Current Conditions

Forest Road 085 (Mineral Basin) Open

## General Information

The 085 Mineral Basin Road is closed.

### Seasons of Use

Year-round

### Restrictions

- Park in designated stalls only.
- No motorized use on banks or reservoir.
- Dogs must be on leash.
- No alcohol.
- No fires.
- No glass.

### Fee Site and Info

Day Use:

$10 per vehicle per three day

$20 per vehicle per seven day

$60 per vehicle per season

[**>>Purchase a Digital Day Use Pass**](https://www.recreation.gov/sitepass/74405)

[>>Learn more about passes.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/uinta-wasatch-cache/passes)

We honor all Interagency "America the Beautiful" National Parks and Federal Recreational Lands Passes, and Uinta-Wasatch-Cache National Forest Passes.

**Please have your pass on display while parked.**

### Pet Information

Dogs must be leashed in developed areas.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 40.48276928

**Longitude:** -111.6459663

### Parking

This is a heavily used location. Park only in designated parking stalls or designated over-flow areas. Do not park in no-parking zones, ticketing and towing is strongly enforced. Do not block gates, or flow of traffic.

Emergency vehicles always need access to respond to an emergency.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Vault


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.
