# Welcome to the Allegheny National Forest

_Established in 1923, the Allegheny National Forest is comprised of over half a million acres in the northwest corner of Pennsylvania._

Over 100 years ago, the USDA Forest Service brought new concepts in forest management to the Allegheny Plateau. The slogan, “Land of Many Uses”, captures our vision of a healthy, vigorous, working forest. Sustainable management has provided timber and wood products, watershed protection, access to underground minerals, ties to cultural history, a diversity of wildlife habitats, and outstanding outdoor recreation opportunities for present and future generations to enjoy.

## Get Involved

![A group of four wheelers traveling in a line down a dirt row surrounded by trees. ](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r09/allegheny/image/490262395_1107970848042175_2578634908288399877_n.jpg?h=c9c5d89e)

The Forest Service is proposing changes to recreation fees at various sites on the Allegheny National Forest. The majority of these fees remain local to operate, maintain, and improve these sites. The public is invited to comment on the proposed recreation fee changes through September 30, 2026.

[Learn More](https://arcg.is/0HnOnX)

![The grassy lawn at Kinzua Beach with a view of the reservoir and bridge.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r09/allegheny/image/IMG_0250.JPG?h=f8469d4c)

For decades, Kinzua Beach and Kinzua Point Information Center have been prime community touchpoints—providing visitors with space to gather, recreate, learn and connect with the forest. To ensure these spaces grow with the needs of our visitors, planning is essential for site longevity.
