# Plan your visit

## Quttinirpaaq National Park

Quttinirpaaq National Park is located on Ellesmere Island in the Qikiqtaaluk region of Nunavut, 800 km north of Resolute Bay. Commercial flights to Resolute Bay are available year-round. The park is most easily reached by chartered aircraft and is most accessible from May to August. Facilities in the park are limited. Visitors must be experienced and self-reliant. Contact us for guidance when planning your trip.

### Directions

**To Iqaluit, Nunavut**

Iqaluit is the hub for air traffic in Nunavut. [Canadian North](https://canadiannorth.com/) offers direct flights to Iqaluit from Ottawa, Montreal and Yellowknife.

**To Resolute Bay, Nunavut**

Canadian North offers scheduled flights from Iqaluit to Resolute Bay.

**To Quttinirpaaq National Park**

[Kenn Borek Air](https://borekair.com/) offers charter flights on a Twin Otter from Resolute Bay to the park.
