# Dutchman Campground

Current Conditions

Top loop is open, all other loops are closed.

Nestled in a shady Ponderosa pine forest on the northeast edge of Deerfield Reservoir, Dutchman Campground sits at 5,000 feet in the Black Hills National Forest. It’s the perfect spot for recreation and relaxation.

Campsites are widely spaced, and some offer sweeping views of the lake. Of the three campgrounds in the Deerfield Recreation Area, Dutchman is the most RV-friendly.

**Reserve Your Campsite**

- Book your stay at [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/231839).
- Check the [campground layout](https://www.fs.usda.gov/media/63198) to find your ideal site.
- See [off-season camping details](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/recreation/season-campground-information).

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. until 6:00 a.m.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Peak Season Single Site: $24 per night

[Off Season Campground Information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/blackhills/recreation/season-campground-information)

### Pet Information

- Dogs must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet in campgrounds, picnic areas, and other developed recreation sites.
- Dogs are not allowed on swim beaches.
- For everyone’s safety and comfort, please don’t leave pets alone at campsites or in parked vehicles.
