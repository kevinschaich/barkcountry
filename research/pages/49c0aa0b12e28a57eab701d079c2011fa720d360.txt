# Regulations

## Mingan Archipelago National Park Reserve

To ensure that visitors to Canada's national parks have unforgettable experiences now and in the future, the regulations aim to protect these special places and ensure that their stay is memorable and safe.

![Interdiction de grimper](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/securite-safety/~/media/12D224E7A52F486683C73D6E12BBCC97.ashx?w=46&h=46&as=1)

Rock climbing is forbidden in the park reserve, as is climbing or walking on the monoliths.

![Interdiction de prélever tout élément naturel](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/securite-safety/~/media/875374D83ED04BEE8EFA30187BF44A56.ashx?w=47&h=47&as=1)

It is strictly forbidden to damage, mutilate or remove natural resources (wildlife, vegetation, fossils, etc.).

![Interdiction de nourrir les animaux sauvages](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/securite-safety/~/media/03C7D0331B46447E87F452459B3CEE95.ashx?w=47&h=47&as=1)

It is forbidden to hunt, disturb, attract, feed or to have in your possession any wildlife.

![Interdiction de jeter des déchets](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/securite-safety/~/media/8340CB02DE7947C28E5E8F56BEAEBD4C.ashx?w=46&h=47&as=1)

No garbage may be left on the islands. It must be removed from the park reserve at the end of your stay.

![Symbole de feux de foyers](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/securite-safety/~/media/F207F9140775491FB126884FEB770040.ashx?w=46&h=47&as=1)

Fire are permitted only in fireplaces furnished by the park reserve and using wood provided for this

purpose. On the beaches, fires may only be built below the high tide mark, using available driftwood.

![Interdiction d'animaux domestiques](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/securite-safety/~/media/72F5FBF4A8464483A65857246523B491.ashx?w=46&h=47&as=1)

Pets are not allowed on the islands

![Symbole pour le camping ](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/securite-safety/~/media/A23AFEE1001C41EB9CA89CE5DB233F93.ashx?w=47&h=47&as=1)

A permit is required for all camping. Camping is authorized only on the site designated in the permit.

![Interdiction d'utiliser un bâtiment du parc comme dortoir](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/securite-safety/~/media/90313AD2D1C7457A8F14F02FC2CEFAAD.ashx?w=47&h=47&as=1)

It is forbidden to use service buildings for sleeping purposes.

![Interdiction de posséder une arme à feu](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/qc/mingan/securite-safety/~/media/7A0CF8972B4847AC950807E3C030FB60.ashx?w=47&h=47&as=1)

Firearms are prohibited in the park reserve.

_(Failure to comply with these regulations may result in legal action)_
