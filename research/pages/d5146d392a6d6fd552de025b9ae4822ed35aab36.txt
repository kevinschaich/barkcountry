# Little Grass Valley Recreation Area

Current Conditions

Please check status of individual recreation sites in the area for seasonal closures.

The Feather River Ranger District manages 8 family campgrounds around Little Grass Valley Reservoir. There are approximately 323 camping units. Campsites in Red Feather, Running Deer, Little Beaver parts of Loop A & B and the southern half of Wyandotte, and Horse Camp may be reserved through [www.recreation.gov](http://www.recreation.gov/) (1-877-444-6777). All other campgrounds are available on a first come, first served basis.

Each campground has piped water. Trailer sites are available, as well as walk-in tent campsites. Trailers up to 40 feet can be accommodated in some of the camping units. No hookups are provided. There are two RV dump stations, one near Tooms Boat Launch, and one near Red Feather Campground.  Facilities are usually open from mid-June to fall.

Little Grass Valley Recreation Area offers opportunities for many more recreation experiences for visitors. Three boat launch ramps provide access to the water for fishing, water-skiing, sailing, or simply touring on boats. There is a wheelchair accessible fishing ramp at the dam. Two swim beaches provide excellent swimming and picnicking opportunities. The Lakeshore Trail (13 miles) winds around the entire lake, open for horseback riding, mountain biking and hiking. Visitors may also enjoy watching wildlife or exploring nearby historic gold mining towns.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Mid-June to fall, conditions permitting.

### Operational Hours

Day use only; no overnight camping allowed

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

Interagency passes honored.  Fees vary, check individual recreation sites for fee information.

### Pet Information

Pets must be on leash and attended at all times.

### Latitude / Longitude

### Directions

From Quincy, take the La Porte-Quincy Road west from California State Highway 70, or travelling from Brownsville east on La Porte Road, travel to the intersection of the La Porte-Quincy Road with Little Grass Valley Road and follow the signs toward the lake.
