# Permits

![Sled dogs racing in snow](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/idahopanhandle/image/Inland%20Empire%20Sled%20Dog%20racing.jpg?h=269dbed4)

Special events or commercial use of public lands require a permit. This includes commercial activities such as filming or guiding, and non-commercial uses such as organized events or gatherings.

![Filmmakers and horses in a meadow](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/idahopanhandle/image/Filmmakers-horses_0.jpg?h=be2253ff)

The [EXPLORE Act](https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/house-bill/6492/text) provides guidance on when filming and photography permits are required on National Forest System Lands.

![Huckleberries on bush](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/idahopanhandle/image/Huckleberries%202.jpg?h=4521fff0)

Commercial gathering of huckleberries is not permitted on the Idaho Panhandle National Forests, but everyone is welcome to gather huckleberries for personal use.

![Morel mushrooms growing in dirt](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/idahopanhandle/image/Morel-1.jpg?h=7a8a8cdf)

The National Forest requires a free, mushroom harvesting permit in order to appropriately manage mushroom harvesting activities, monitor use, and protect resources.

![Stacked firewood](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/idahopanhandle/image/OIP.jpg?h=115fac94)

Firewood cutters must obtain a free permit and abide by the terms when cutting in Northern Region national forests.

![An adult and a child seeking a Christmas tree in the forest](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/idahopanhandle/image/Christmas%20tree.jpeg?h=91243204)

Cutting the family Christmas tree is a timeless tradition. You can purchase your tree permits online or at one of our offices between mid-October to the end of December.

![A horse rider leading a string of pack horses](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/idahopanhandle/image/Pack%20String.jpg?h=fb1daba4)

As partners in providing unique recreational experiences, commercial outfitters and guides have an integral role in fulfilling one of the agency's missions to serve the public.

![A historic recreation residence building](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/idahopanhandle/image/Rec%20Res%20photo.jpg?h=3b5d1417)

Summer homes, also referred to as recreation residences, have a long tradition on the Idaho Panhandle National Forests. Most are located around Priest Lake.
