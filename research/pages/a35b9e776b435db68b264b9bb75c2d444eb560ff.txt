# Best times to visit

## Qausuittuq National Park

Careful planning is essential for any trip to Qausuittuq National Park. As a newly established, remote park with limited facilities or services, visitors should review the following information to ensure a safe and enjoyable visit.

* * *

### When should I visit?

Qausuittuq National Park is open year-round, but the best times to visit are from late June to early August for summer activities and from mid-April to mid-May for winter activities. The park experiences 24-hour darkness from late October until mid-February. Visitors must be self-reliant and prepared for challenging conditions, including potential delays. Planning ahead is essential for a safe and successful visit to Qausuittuq.

### Getting here

- Visit the [Important Bulletins](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/qausuittuq/securite-safety/bulletins) page to check for area closures and restrictions in the park before planning your trip.
- For travel information, see the [How to Get Here](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/qausuittuq/visit/directions) page for a comprehensive overview of how to reach Qausuittuq National Park.
- The logistics of traveling to and from Qausuittuq National Park are complex. Start planning your trip well in advance and allow multiple days between the legs of your adventure.
