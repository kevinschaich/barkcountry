# Dog Lake Road closed Thursday for paving

**Release Date:** July 23, 2018

**Contact Information:** Tamara Schmidt    [tamaraschmidt@fs.fed.us](mailto:tamaraschmidt@fs.fed.us)

LAKEVIEW, Ore. – Forest Road 4017, commonly known as Dog Lake Road on the Lakeview District of the Fremont-Winema National Forest, will be closed this Thursday, July 26, for paving.

The road will be closed between Forest Service Roads 4017.101 and 4020.401, starting approximately 8.6 miles from the Fremont-Winema National Forest entry sign and 3.7 miles from the end of the county road during paving operations. All traffic – motorized and non-motorized, including walking – is prohibited for public and worker safety.

The Lake County Roads Department will be completing the paving project in partnership with the Forest.

A detour is available on Forest Service Road 3940, also known as Dent Creek Road. The road can be accessed from State Highway 140 north of Drews Reservoir.

Dog Lake Road was severely damaged due to a large, long term landslide which became active during the spring in 2017 when snowmelt and rain saturated soils. The road was closed as a result and reopened when conditions had stabilized enough for a temporary fix to restore public access.

The paving is the final step in the road repair.

The same section of road was impacted in 1995 and closed from mid-April through June of that year for repairs. At that time, the road was damaged due to severe erosion caused by heavy runoff from melting snowpack and wet spring storms.

For more information on the Fremont-Winema National Forest, visit [www.fs.usda.gov/fremont-winema](http://www.fs.usda.gov/fremont-winema), follow the Forest on Twitter @FremontWinemaNF or on Facebook at [www.facebook.com/R6FWNF](http://www.facebook.com/R6FWNF).
