The following listing of Included Timber by method of cut is for information only:

**a.** <u><strong>DESIGNATION BY PRESCRIPTION:</strong></u> Cutting Units **ALL** (6, 7, 9) are designated for cutting by 
the Designation by Prescription method.

1. **Cutting Units 6 and 7** - Remove diseased, suppressed, inferior trees to achieve a 
residual basal area of 50 – 60 ft<sup>2</sup>. Leave healthy longleaf pine with adequate spacing for growth. However, loblolly pine may be left to meet basal area requirements.

2. **Cutting Unit 9** - Remove diseased, suppressed, inferior trees to achieve a residual 
basal area of <u>30 – 40 ft<sup>2</sup></u>. Leave healthy longleaf pine with adequate spacing for growth. 
However, loblolly pine may be left to meet basal area requirements.

**b.** <u><strong>BOUNDARIES:</strong></u> Trees marked with three horizontal stripes of **ORANGE** paint at eye-level, and 
a spot below stump height are unit boundary trees and are not to be cut.  Where roads or streams 
or other obvious features can serve as boundaries, there may be no boundary trees marked.

5.  **PERIOD OF CONTRACT.**  The normal operating season covers the period January 1 to 
December 31.  Contract termination date is <u><strong>November 30, 2025.</strong></u> Extensions of this contract may 
be granted only when the purchaser has met specified conditions.  The purchaser must submit a 
general operating plan to the Contracting Officer for approval before operations begin or within 60 
days of sale award, whichever is earlier.  The plan must show how the purchaser plans to

6.  **PAYMENT.**  Payment for timber and any required deposits for slash disposal or road 
maintenance are required in advance of cutting.  Purchaser may provide an acceptable payment 
guarantee prior to cutting.  Payment for required deposits must be a cash payment.  The purchaser 
shall make advance deposits in accordance with Standard Provision 9.

The high bidder whose bid is accepted shall, at the time the contract is signed and returned by the 
bidder, make a downpayment pursuant to Title 36, Section 223.49, of the Code of Federal 
Regulations.  The Contracting Officer will notify the high bidder of the amount necessary to make 
this payment.  In no case shall the downpayment be less than 10 percent of the total advertised 
value and 20 percent of the bid premium.  After receipt of the downpayment and a satisfactory 
performance bond, and upon execution of the timber sale contract, the Forest Service will return 
the bid guarantee.  At time of award, a cash bid guarantee may be applied to the downpayment at 
the request of the purchaser.  The purchaser cannot apply the amount deposited as a 
downpayment to cover other obligations due on the sale until stumpage value representing 25 percent of the total bid value of the sale has been charged and paid for on the statement of 
account.

By the midpoint between award date and the termination date on contracts of more than one year 
but less than two years in length, the purchaser shall have paid for, or in lieu thereof, deposited 
cash in the greater amount of:  (1) 50 percent of the total estimated bid premium, or (2) 35 percent 
of the total estimated sale value at bid date, exclusive of required deposits, rounded up to the next 
$100.

For contracts two or more years in length, by the midpoint of the last normal operating season, or 
12 months from the initial periodic payment, whichever date is first, the purchaser shall have paid 
for, or in lieu thereof, deposited cash in the amount of 75 percent of the total estimated sale value 
at bid date, exclusive of required deposits, rounded up to the next $100.

7.  **PERFORMANCE BOND.**  A performance bond is required.  The penal sum of the bond will be 
10 percent of the total bid value of the sale, rounded up to the nearest $100 when the total bid 
value is $10,000 or less; and rounded up to the nearest $1,000 when the total bid value exceeds 
$10,000.  If an irrevocable letter of credit is used to secure the performance bond, the termination 
of the letter of credit must be at least 12 months past the contract termination date.

**o.** <u><strong>Cruise Methods:</strong></u> This sale is a designation-by-prescription (DxP) first thinning. Timber

weight tickets from approved, certified, scales must be paid for.

**p.** <u><strong>Purpose of Sale:</strong></u> This sale includes 140 acres of first thinning in upland forest. These actions 
are designed to improve upland and wetland habitat for the Carolina gopher frog populations. 
These harvests will heavily thin the ecotone between the wetlands and uplands to maintain a 
contiguous gradient between the wetlands and adjacent upland habitat type needed by gopher 
frogs dispersing from the wetlands to upland sites. Thinning the upland areas will restore and 
maintain forested corridors between bay wetlands necessary for dispersing juveniles to colonize 
other seasonal wetlands to prevent populations from becoming isolated.
