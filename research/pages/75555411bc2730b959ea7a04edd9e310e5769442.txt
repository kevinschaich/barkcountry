# Trestle Recreation Area

Located at the west end of the Village of Cloudcroft, the Trestle Recreation Area offers a day-use picnicking site with access to popular area trails. Water, restroom facilities, interpretive information, access to trails, picnic sites. This is a day-use facility only.

## Trestle Depot Recreation Area

[![Building with trees in background](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r03/lincoln/image/Trestle%20Depot.jpg?h=a1e1a043)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/lincoln/recreation/trestle-recreation-area#)

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Spring, Summer, Fall.

### Operational Hours

Currently open for day use.

### Restrictions

No camping, day-use area only

### Passes and Permits

- [New Mexico Enchantment Pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/passes/enchantment-pass)
- [Interagency Passes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/passes)

### Fee Site and Info

Day Use:

$5 per vehicle per day

$5 day-use fee. Pay onsite or use a day-use pass such as [New Mexico Enchantment Pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/passes/enchantment-pass) or Interagency Passes.

### Pet Information

Please pick up after your pets. All pets must be on a leash at all times.
