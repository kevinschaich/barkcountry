# Welcome to Custer Gallatin National Forest

A diverse and stunning landscape, 3+ million acres, critical to the Greater Yellowstone Ecosystem and stretching to the remote buttes and pine savannahs of southeastern Montana and South Dakota.

![Bear Encounter for CARD](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/legacy-media/custergallatin/Bear%20Encounter%20for%20CARD.png?h=966f4a91)

Montana is bear country. For the safety of humans and all native wildlife, be bear aware! A food storage order is in place. Learn how to properly hike, recreate and be bear aware.

[Being Bear Aware](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/custergallatin/safety-ethics/bear-aware)

![A stack of cut and split firewood.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/custergallatin/image/51881680612_7d40eafa54.jpg?h=2075fdba)

Many opportunities across the Forest are free. Activities such as forest products, firewood, commercial or special uses, require permits to help maintain and improve recreational amenities.

[Permits](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/custergallatin/permits)

![A photo of a boy looking at a map, standing in a forest.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r01/custergallatin/image/51873860348_99d6038147.jpg?h=2075fdba)

Whether looking for a general forest map, a supplementary map on forest access, forest orders or motor vehicle use maps for summer and winter we have you covered

[Find A Map](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/custergallatin/maps-guides)

![Mill Creek Cabin](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/legacy-media/custergallatin/Mill%20Creek%20Cabin_1.jpg?h=52d3fcb6)

From cabins to campgrounds, Recreation.gov offers numerous opportunities for people to explore, each with their own flair and flavor.

[Plan An Adventure](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/1017)

[View Projects](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/recreation/great-american-outdoors-act)
