# Upper Two Mile Trail

Current Conditions

Weather and snow melt will affect open and closing. Please call the Moab Ranger District for current conditions, 435-259-7155.

Upper Two Mile Trail #981 is 4.0 miles long and travels along Two-Mile Creek through ponderosa pine and Gambel oak forest. It begins at Forest Road #072 (Lower Two-Mile) and can be connected with open trails on State Trust lands. This trail ascends northward along the banks of Two-mile Creek. This trail can be combined with a variety of other trails and roads to create multiple loop routes. Other trails to consider combining to make a loop include: #979 ( [Lower Two-Mile](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/manti-lasal/recreation/trails/lower-two-mile-trail)), #983 ( [Trinity Canyon](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/manti-lasal/recreation/trails/trinity-canyon-trail)), #980 ( [Two-Mile Connector](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/manti-lasal/recreation/trails/two-mile-connector-trail)) as well as various State Trust Land Trails and Roads. Forest Roads to consider combining with this trail to make a loop include: #072 (Lower Two-Mile), #743, #744, #745, #746, #747, #748, #201 (Hang Dog), #202 (Hop Creek), #203 (Dry Draw) as well as various State Trust Land Trails.

**Elevation Change:**

South End (Road #072): 7,063 feet / 2,153 meters

North End (FS Bndry): 8,064 feet / 2,458 meters

## Specific Trail Information

### Trail Type

**Standard/Terra Trail** — A trail that has a surface consisting predominantly of the ground and that is designed and managed to accommodate use on that surface.

## General Information

**Latitude:** 38 21' 18.2"N

**Longitude:** 109 07' 10.5"W

**Datum:** NAD 83

**Position Format:** Degrees Minutes Seconds

### Water Available

Two-Mile Creek is an perennial stream but is unreliable. Users should carry their own water.

### Restrictions

Open to: Hiking, Mountain Biking, Horseback Riding, Motorcycle Trail Riding, and Off Highway Vehicles (OHV) less than or equal to 50 inches wide.

## Getting There

### Directions

From La Sal, head east on UT-46 and continue 7.0 miles. At Old La Sal, turn left onto Co Rd #125/Forest Road #072 (Lower Two Mile) and travel approximately 3.5 miles to Upper Two-Mile Trail #981 located on your left.

## Additional Information

### Operated By

Forest Service
