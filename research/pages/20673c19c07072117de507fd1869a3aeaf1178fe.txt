# Backbone Rock Recreation Area

Current Conditions

Backbone Rock Campground is closed for the season. Backbone Rock Pavilion is open to reservations until the first Monday in November, and the vault toilet in the day-use area is open until the third Monday in November. Please familiarize yourself with the Food Storage Order located in the Passes/Permits/Restrictions section of the website.

Backbone Rock Recreation Area is located along Beaverdam Creek in northern Johnson County. Backbone Rock gets its name from a spur ridge on Holston Mountain that abruptly ends at a bend in Beaverdam Creek.

A tunnel was drilled through the rock in 1901 to allow railroad access between Shady Valley and Damascus, VA. Motorists pass through what is known as "The Shortest Tunnel in the World" as they travel along TN 133. In the 1930s The Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) developed the recreation area for day use, constructing two picnic shelters and hiking trails that incorporated native stonework. The campground was added in the 1960s and rehabilitated in the mid 1990s.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

- **4/27/2026 - 10/13/2026**
- This campground does not have any Long-Term Camping programs.
- Camping in any location is limited to 14 consecutive days.  On the 14th consecutive day, the camper and party must leave that area/campground for a minimum of seven days before returning for another stay.

### Operational Hours

Backbone Rock Campground is closed for the season.  Backbone Rock Pavilion is open to reservations until the first Monday in November, and the vault toilet in the day-use area is open until the third Monday in November.  Gates are open at 7am and closed at 10pm. Guests are asked to leave the area before the gates are closed for the evening.  The hiking trails are open year-round, from sunrise to sunset.

### Restrictions

- Campsites are limited to not more than 5 people and 2 vehicles overnight. Double campsites allow for up to 10 individuals and 4 vehicles overnight.
- This campground does not have any Long-Term Camping programs.
- No alcoholic beverages are allowed in the area.
- No burning plastic or other non-wood materials except local wood in fire pits and charcoal in grills.
- Please adhere to the [**Cherokee National Forest Food and Refuse Storage Requirements**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/r08/cherokee/publication/Food%20and%20Refuse%20Storage%20Requirements.pdf) **.** Food storage restrictions apply at all areas on the Cherokee National Forest. All food and beverages must be stored in an **approved bear-proof container**, in your locked vehicle or hard-sided camper at all times, or suspended in the air, unless being eaten, prepared, or transported.
- Pets must be on a maximum 6 ft. leash at all times, and pick up after them.
- It is your responsibility to read, know, understand, and follow all specific rules and regulations which are also posted at the campground.

### Passes and Permits

A special use permit is required for groups of 75 people or more on National Forest lands.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $10 per night

Double Site: $20 per night

Camping is $10 for a single site (8 sites) and $20 for double site (2 sites).  One pavilion is reservable at $50 per day.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 36.59568504

**Longitude:** -81.81835727

### Directions

From Bristol, take US 421 South for 20 miles to Shady Valley. Turn left onto TN 133 for eight miles to Backbone Rock Recreation Area.  From Abingdon, VA, take US 58 east for 15 miles to Damascus. Turn right onto VA 716 for five miles to the recreation area (VA 716 becomes TN 133 at the state line).
