# Activities and experiences

## Gulf Islands National Park Reserve

With urban centers close by, the Gulf Islands are an easily accessible natural oasis. Sail or paddle a kayak on sheltered waterways through a scenic maze of islands thriving with wildlife from seabirds to whales. Explore the reserve’s 15 islands amid history and wilderness chiming with songbirds, hiking to mountaintop views of snowy ranges.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/bc/gulf/WET4/activ/1/Gulf-Islands---Feature-1----Hiking-small.jpg?modified=20221123185253)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/gulf/activ/randonnee-hiking)
