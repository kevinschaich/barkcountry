# Activities and experiences

## Thousand Islands National Park

### Regular fees apply as of September 8, 2026

September 7 is the last day of [free admission and discounted camping and overnight stays](https://parks.canada.ca/voyage-travel/conseils-tips/choisis-canada-choose/admission-camping) with Parks Canada and the Canada Strong Pass.

Thousand Islands National Park invites you to experience boating or paddle the St. Lawrence River’s blue-green waters past remote granite shoreline. Reserve a waterfront oTENTik or campsite for an overnighting in nature or take a scenic day hike along the river.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/on/1000/WET4/activ/activ-640.jpg?modified=20230920175950)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/experience)

### [Family and education programs](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/experience)

Take part in a wide range of special events and activities in Thousand Islands National Park.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/on/1000/WET4/modules/otentik_640.jpg?modified=20221121173406)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/camping/otentik)

### [Riverside oTENTik camping](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/camping/otentik)

Book your stay at an island or mainland oTENTik and relax in you waterfront accommodation.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/on/1000/WET4/activ/app-640.jpg?modified=20240509193632)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/app)

### [Self-guided audio tours](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/app)

Download the Parks Canada app and explore the park with self-guided tours.

## Island access

### [Boating the St. Lawrence](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/plaisanc-boater)

Explore the islands dotting the river as you cruise to docks and mooring cans.

### [Don't have a boat?](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/visit/directions/acces-access)

There are many ways to explore islands and inlets of the park, even if you don't own a boat.

## Highlights

### [Events and programs calendar](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/calendrier-even-calendar)

Take part in a wide range of special events and activities in Thousand Islands National Park.

### [Relax by the river at Mallorytown Landing](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/mallorytown)

Thousand Islands National Park welcomes you to relax and play on the breezy banks of the St. Lawrence River

### [​Camp on an island](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/camping)

Pitch your tent along the banks of the St. Lawrence River and take in the stars at night.

### [Red chairs](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/chaises-chairs)

Relax in Parks Canada's red chairs to take in iconic views.

### [Plan a group visit](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/1000/activ/group)

Enjoy Mallorytown Landing with your group while respecting park rules, nature and other visitors.
