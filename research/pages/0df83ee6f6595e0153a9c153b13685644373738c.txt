## Forest Order

Order Number: # 04-15-25-218

## US. Department of Agriculture, U.S. Forest Service   Caribou-Targhee National Forest  Palisades &Teton Basin Ranger Districts

**Occupancy and Use Dog Restrictions**

### PROHIBITIONS

Pursuant to 16 U.S.C. S 551 and 36 C.F.R. S 261.50 (a), the following is prohibited on the National Forest System (NFS) lands in the Palisades and Teton Basin Ranger Districts, Caribou-Targhee National Forest, described below and shown on the attached map incorporated into this Order as Exhibits A & B (the "Described Areas"):

1. Possessing a dog on or within 200 yards of a groomed ski trail from December 1 to April 15 annually on Teton Basin Ranger District and December 15 to April 15 on Palisades Ranger District. 36 CFR S 261.58(s)

### EXEMPTIONS

Pursuant to 36 C.F.R. S 261.50(e), the following persons are exempt from this Order:

1. Persons with Forest Service Permit No. FS-7700-48 (Permit for Use of Roads, Trails, or Areas Restricted by Regulation or Order), specifically exempting them from this Order or a written determination by a Forest Service authorized officer that a permit is not required under 36 C.F.R. S 251.50(e)(1) or (2).
2. Any Federal, State, or Local Officer, or member of an organized rescue or firefighting resource in the performance of an official duty.

### DESCRIBED AREAS

This Order applies to all NFS lands within the Palisades and Teton Basin Ranger Districts, Caribou-Targhee National Forest, located in both Idaho and Wyoming.

- South Valley Trails Area located on the Teton Basin Ranger District (shown on Exhibit A), Sections 19, 23, 24, 25, 26, 29, 30, and 32, Township 3 North, Range 45 East, Boise P.M. and Section 30, Township 3 North, Range 46 East, Boise P.M.
- Kelly Canyon Area located on the Palisades Ranger District (shown on Exhibit B), which includes Sections 25, 26, 27, 34, and 35, Township 4 North, Range 41 East, Boise P.M.

### PURPOSE

The purposed of this Order is to protect public health and safety.

### IMPLEMENTATION

1. This Order shall be in effect from January 1, 2025 at 1200 through January 1, 2030 at 1200, unless rescinded.
2. A map identifying the Described Areas is attached and made part of this Order as Exhibits A-B. For a digital version of this Order and Exhibits, go to [https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/caribou-targhee/alerts](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/caribou-targhee/alerts).
3. Unless otherwise expressly defined in this Order, the terms used in this Order are defined by the regulations of the Secretary of Agriculture, 36 C.F.R. Chapter Il, Parts 200-299. If there are terms in this Order that are not expressly defined in the Order or 36 C.F.R. Chapter Il, Parts 200-299, their meaning shall be determined by their plain language definitions.
4. A violation of the above prohibitions is punishable as a Class B misdemeanor by a fine of not more than $5,000 for individuals and $10,000 for organizations, or by imprisonment for not more than six months, or both. 16 U.S.C. S 551; 18 U.S.C. SS 3559, 3571, and 3581.
5. Contact the Caribou-Targhee National Forest Supervisor's Office 208-557-5900, for more information about this Order.
6. This Order supersedes any previous Orders prohibiting the same or similar acts in/on the same Described Areas.

Signed and issued on February 13, 2025.

MEL BOLLING

Forest Supervisor

Caribou-Targhee National Forest
