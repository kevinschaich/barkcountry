# Recreation

Recreation is one of the premier opportunities for visitors to the Lassen National Forest. So go explore, camp, hike, kayak, fish, or just sit beneath the towering pines on a sunny afternoon and relax. The Lassen National Forest welcomes you.

Where will you find your favorite place on the Lassen National Forest?  Enjoy the adventure that awaits!

**\*Please note that pets must always be restrained or on a leash while in developed recreation sites, and that pets (except guide dogs) are not allowed in swimming areas.**
