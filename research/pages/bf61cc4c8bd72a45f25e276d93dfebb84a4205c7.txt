# Auyuittuq National Park

On southeastern Baffin Island in Nunavut, the towering peaks of the Akshayuk Pass overlook a natural corridor through a landscape of sprawling glaciers, steep fiords and winding river valleys. Rich in Inuit culture and history, Auyuittuq is “the land that never melts” in Inuktitut.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nu/auyuittuq/WET4/index/randonnee-hiking-640x360.jpg?modified=20221115190835)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/auyuittuq/activ/randonnee-hiking)

### [Explore the Akshayuk Pass](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/auyuittuq/activ/randonnee-hiking)

Hike, ski or snowshoe through this 100 kilometre-long natural corridor.

### [Sirmilik National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/sirmilik)

### [Ukkusiksalik National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nu/ukkusiksalik)

An area rich in arctic wildlife and Inuit history where hundreds of archaeological sites dot the landscape surrounding Wager Bay.
