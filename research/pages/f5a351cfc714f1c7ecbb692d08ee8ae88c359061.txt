**Explore the Outdoors on the National Forests and Grasslands in Texas**

From hiking, camping, and horseback riding to fishing, paddling, and wildlife watching, the National Forests and Grasslands in Texas offer something for every outdoor enthusiast. With more than 675,000 acres of public land across East Texas, visitors can discover scenic trails, quiet lakes, and family-friendly recreation areas year-round.

![timber logging deck](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r08/texas/image/Salvage%20Sale%20SamHouston_0.jpg?h=80ee4936)

Click the link to find information about all timber sales currently being advertised on the National Forests and Grasslands in Texas.

[Current Timber Sales](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/texas/timber-sales)

![prescribed burning on national forest](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r08/texas/image/RX%20Fire%20Operations.jpg?h=ddb1ad0c)

The National Forests and Grasslands in Texas conducts prescribed burns throughout the year under specific weather and fuel conditions to meet ecological and safety goals. These controlled fires mimic natural fire cycles and help reduce the buildup of flammable vegetation.
