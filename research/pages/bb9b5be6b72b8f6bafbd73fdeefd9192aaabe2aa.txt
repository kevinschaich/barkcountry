| ## Internet & Cellular Access<br>Public WiFi is **not** available.<br>There is no WiFi is available at White Sands National Park. Cell service is limited around the park entrance and inside the dunes area. Some dead zones exist - remember text messages may be the best way to contact family or friends.<br>[Internet & Cellular Access Details](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm)<br>**Welcome to White Sands!**<br>The information below will help you find all the information you will need to help plan a safe and fun visit to White Sands National Park. Information in [different languages](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/other-languages.htm) is available to help you plan.<br>![White dune with blue sky above](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/images/WHSA_operatinghours_thumbnail.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)<br>NPS Photo<br>[Operating Hours & Seasons](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/hours.htm)<br>Park operating hours vary by season, and is occasionally closed for periods of time due to missile tests on the adjacent White Sands Missile Range. Visitors are encouraged to check the [Park Closure webpage](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/park-closures.htm) to verify that a closure is not in effect.<br>![Three different entrance passes](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/images/WHSA_Entrance_Passes_Thumbnail.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)<br>NPS Photo<br>[Fees and Passes](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/fees.htm)<br>Entrance fees are charged per vehicle to enter White Sands National Park. There are also individual passes available for those biking into the park. Children ages 15 and younger enter for free. The National Park Service has also designated various days throughout the year as Fee Free Days. Those interested in interagency park passes can purchase them at the fee station.<br>![David Bowie in costume filming Man Who Fell to Earth](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/images/WHSA_permits_thumbnail.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)<br>NPS Photo<br>### [Permits and Reservations](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/permitsandreservations.htm)<br>Applications for various special use permits are available, such as weddings, backcountry camping, and commercial filming and photography. Reservations are not needed to enter the park; however, some of our interpretive programs require advance reservations. Special rates are available for those bringing commercial bus tours to the park. School groups coming to the park for a curriculum-based activity may be eligible for an academic fee waiver. Visit the Academic Fee Waiver page at [Academic Fee Waiver - White Sands National Park (U.S. National Park Service)](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/academic-fee-waiver.htm) for more information.<br>![Sand being blown across dune](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/images/WHSA_wind_thumbnail.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)<br>NPS Photo<br>[Current Conditions](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)<br>The weather in the Tularosa Basin is usually quite nice with clear, sunny skies for approximately 330 days out of the year. Summers are hot with daytime temperatures averaging 97ºF (36ºC) in the summer and 60ºF (16ºC) in the winter. Most of our rainfall occurs during "monsoon season" from early-July through mid-October.<br>![Animal footprints next to human footprint in sand](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/images/WHSA_LNT_thumbnail.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)<br>NPS Photo<br>[Leave No Trace at White Sands](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/leave-no-trace.htm)<br>National parks, like White Sands National Park, are special places for all to enjoy now and into the future. It is important that we minimize our impacts and leave as little evidence as possible of our visits to forests, parks, and other protected places. Always pack-out anything you pack-in; including trash such as food wrappers and empty bottles. In addition to aesthetic concerns, there are health concerns for people and wildlife. It is easy to _Leave No Trace_ when you visit White Sands.<br>The White Sands National Park Leave No Trace principles are adapted from Leave No Trace Center for Outdoor Ethics’ Seven Leave No Trace Principles. Leave No Trace ethics can be applied in any natural setting to minimize human impacts on the environment.<br>![Man walking a dog on white sand with an orange sunset](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/images/WHSA_Pets_Thumbnails.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)<br>NPS Photo<br>[Park Regulations](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/park-regulations.htm)<br>Park regulations help keep you safe during your visit. These regulations include applicable federal, state, and park specific regulations.<br>![A man walking tan dog on leash in white sand](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/images/WHSA_pets_thumbnail.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)<br>NPS Photo<br>[Pets](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/planyourvisit/pets.htm)<br>Visitors are welcome to bring their pets to the park as long as they are kept on a leash. However, pets are not permitted in the visitor center or gift shop; service animals are allowed. Do not leave pets unattended or locked in your vehicle. Please leash your pets before opening your car doors. |
