# Trillium Lake Day Use

Trillium Lake, located about 40 miles southeast of Portland, is one of the most iconic locations in Mt. Hood National Forest. It's well-known for its beautiful scenery and abundant recreational opportunities. Trillium Lake Campground is also known for being a family-friendly place to camp in the summer.

Boating, swimming, and fishing are popular on 63-acre Trillium Lake. A small boat ramp is available within the campground and a larger ramp is available in the day use area. A fishing pier is also available, where anglers cast for a variety of trout. The 2-mile Trillium Lake Loop Trail circles the lake, and parts of it are accessible. Visitors also enjoy hiking and biking on other trails in the vicinity.

For information on the reservable amphitheater or the group picnic area, visit [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campsites/89687).

Between December and May, the lake is accessible to skiers and snowshoers via the [Trillium Sno-Park](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mthood/recreation/trillium-sno-park-trailhead) on US Hwy 26. Plan for a 4.4-mile round trip.

## Reservations

Reservations are required through [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campsites/89687) to use the Amphitheater or the Group Picnic Area.

No reservations are required for the main day use area.

## General Information

[Trillium Lake Campground](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mthood/recreation/trillium-lake-campground) and Day Use Area is operated by a concessionaire, Alaska Recreational Management (ARM), under a special use permit issued by Mt. Hood National Forest. Visitors should expect to see ARM employees on the road to Trillium Lake collecting fees.

**The day use fee for Trillium Lake Day Use is $10 and the only pass accepted is the $30 Annual Northwest Forest Pass. ("America the Beautiful" passes are NOT accepted in lieu of payment.)**

### Seasons of Use

May 1, 2026 to December 1, 2026

### Operational Hours

Day use only: open sunrise to sunset

### Passes and Permits

The only recreation pass accepted in lieu of day use fee payment is the **$30** [**Annual Northwest Forest Pass**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mthood/passes) **.**

$10 per vehicle per day

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 45.27027778

**Longitude:** -121.7347222

### Elevation

3,600 feet

### Directions

**From Portland, OR** \- Travel east on U.S. Hwy 26 for about 40 miles. Turn right 1 mile east of Government Camp on Forest Road 2656 (Trillium Lake Road). Continue 2 miles (past the campground turn-off) to the entrance day use picnic area or boat launch.
