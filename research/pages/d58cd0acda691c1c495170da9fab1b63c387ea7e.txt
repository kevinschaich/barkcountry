## General Information

### Operational Hours

Day-use hours are 6 a.m. - 10 p.m.

### Restrictions

[**Know Fire & Other Restrictions Before You Go!**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/angeles/newsroom/releases/know-you-go-angeles-national-forest): These conditions can affect your entire trip. For example, _Extreme_ and _Critical_ fire use restrictions mean no campfires and no portable cooking systems/stoves. Bring cold food and no S'mores.

### Passes and Permits

Adventure Pass/America the Beautiful Pass Required.

### Fee Site and Info

Day Use:

$5 per vehicle per day

$30 per annual

### Pet Information

Dogs must be on leash.

36 CFR 261.8 (d) Possessing a dog not on a leash or otherwise confined.

$150 fine

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Accessibility

One wheelchair accessible grill and picnic table available. First come first serve.

### Restrooms

Vault toilet(s)


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

No piped water available. Seasonal stream water (treat before using). However, water flow may be low or non-existent due to reduced rainfall. Please bring enough water for cooking, cleaning and drinking and extinguishing BBQ grill fires.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

26 Picnic sites - picnic tables & BBQ pedestal grills.
