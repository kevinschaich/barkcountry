# Riding Mountain National Park

## Help shape the future of Riding Mountain National Park and Wasagaming. [Learn more!](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/mb/riding/info/politiques-plans-policies)

Riding Mountain is home to a staggering diversity of living things. The windswept grasslands and deep, silent forests, of secretive wolf packs, of orchids and lilies, of lakes and wetlands singing with life. Riding Mountain National Park is in Treaty 2 Territory, where we work with Anishinabe, First Nations from treaties 2, 4, and 1. We honour, acknowledge, and recognize Indigenous contributions to the park, the province, and Canada.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/mb/pnmr-rmnp-est-east-640x360.jpg?modified=20221203012330)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/mb/eastgate)

### [Riding Mountain Park East Gate Registration Complex National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/mb/eastgate)

Canada’s last remaining early 1930s-style national parks entrance, the East Gate Registration Complex stands as a carefully maintained example of this form of traditional architecture.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/mb/theforks-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220835)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/mb/forks)

### [The Forks National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/mb/forks)

Delve 6,000 years into the past at Winnipeg’s “Meeting Place” while soaking up the bustling ambience. Learn how two great rivers at the heart of the continent connected the prairies to the world.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/mb/lowerfortgarry-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220835)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/mb/fortgarry)

### [Lower Fort Garry National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/mb/fortgarry)

Standing on the bank of the Red River for more than 180 years, Lower Fort Garry tells inspiring tales of innovation, discovery and struggle. Chat with a 19th century Red River settler and immerse yourself in the daily life of the Fort’s inhabitants.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/mb/rielhouse-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220835)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/mb/riel)

### [Riel House National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/mb/riel)

Travel back to spring 1886, six months after the death of Louis Riel. Visit his family home and discover what life was like for the Riel family and Métis who lived along the banks of the Red River.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/mb/standrewsrectory-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220835)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/mb/standrews)

### [St. Andrew's Rectory National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/mb/standrews)

Learn about the significance of the St Andrew’s Rectory, an excellent example of mid-19th century Red River Hudson’s Bay Company architecture. Imagine the day to day lives of the Reverend and his Red River settler parishioners in the 1800s.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/sk/sk-motherwell-640.jpg?modified=20221126215444)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/sk/motherwell)

### [Motherwell Homestead National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/sk/motherwell)

Hear prairie songbirds sing and smell the sweet hay as you help feed the horses. Re-connect with Canada’s homestead life and bake bread with flour ground from grain harvested from the golden fields outside.
