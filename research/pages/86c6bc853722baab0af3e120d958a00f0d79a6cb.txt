## Recreation on the Lincoln National Forest

Find your adventure in the Lincoln National Forest, where cool mountain forests, rugged canyons, and wide‑open desert meet. Whether you’re hiking, camping, fishing, wildlife watching, horseback riding, or enjoying designated motorized trails, this forest offers plenty of ways to get outside and explore responsibly.

In winter, snow transforms high‑country trails into quiet escapes perfect for snowshoeing, skiing, and sledding. History lovers can wander old mining towns and visit the birthplace of Smokey Bear.

Check the rules, know the conditions, tread lightly, and make the most of your time in this incredible landscape. Your forest is waiting, let’s take care of it together.

Call your [local National Forest office](https://fs.usda.gov/r03/lincoln/contact-us) to check on temperatures, conditions, the best place to go for your wishes, and much more.
