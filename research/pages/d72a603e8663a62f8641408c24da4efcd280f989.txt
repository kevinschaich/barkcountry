# Activities and experiences

## Elk Island National Park

Elk Island National Park offers something for everyone looking to connect with the natural world around them, from day picnickers to overnight campers.

[![A couple captures photographs from the Living Waters Boardwalk... Elk Island National Park](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/ab/elkisland/WET4/2023/feature/randonnee-hike-640-22.jpg?modified=20221129193402)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/elkisland/activ/experiences/randonee-hiking)

### [Hiking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/elkisland/activ/experiences/randonee-hiking)

Enjoy a hike on a park trail!

[![Visitors capture pictures of bison from a safe distance... Elk Island National Park](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/ab/elkisland/WET4/2023/feature/bison-640-22.jpg?modified=20221129193135)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/elkisland/activ/experiences/observation)

### [Wildlife watching](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/elkisland/activ/experiences/observation)

Keep an eye out for wildlife!

[![a bison grazes beside the road in Elk Island National Park](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/ab/elkisland/WET4/2023/feature/bison-rd.jpg?modified=20230920200548)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/elkisland/securite-safety/faune-wildlife)

### [Canoeing](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/elkisland/activ/experiences/canot-canoe)

Canoe around one of the many islands on Astotin Lake or stop by Beaver Bay for a picnic.

### [Camping](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/elkisland/activ/camping)
