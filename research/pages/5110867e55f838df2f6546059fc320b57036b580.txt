# Little Falls Campground

Little Falls Campground is a pleasant little campground with 3 tent sites with fire rings, complete with views of the Illinois River and the expansive mountains surrounding the Illinois Valley. The area offers access to the cascading Little Illinois River Falls, scenic views of the Illinois Valley and surrounding mountainous terrain. Located within the botanical-rich [Eight Dollar Mountain Botanical Area](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/eight-dollar-mountain-botanical-area), the site features specialized serpentine soil, unique Darlingtonia plants, wildflowers and pristine river swimming holes nearby.

The campground is co-located with the trailhead for the Little Falls Trail #1284 that connects to the Jeffrey Pine Loop Trailhead.

[![Vicinity map of Eight Dollar Mountain recreation opportunities](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r06/rogue-siskiyou/image/Eight%20Dollar%20Mountain%20Map_3.jpeg?h=b6236d98)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/little-falls-campground#)

## Reservations

All sites are first come, first serve.

**This site is fully cashless**.Visitors will need to pay on-site using the [**Scan and Pay**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/learn-more-scan-and-pay) feature of the Recreation.gov app. Download the [**Recreation.gov app**](https://www.recreation.gov/mobile-app) and add a valid payment method _before_ heading out on your adventure.

## General Information

There is an amphitheater with a large fire pit for group gatherings.

### Operational Hours

Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. until 6:00 a.m.

### Restrictions

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfires in developed fire pits only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Dogs must be on leash
- OHV/ATV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $10 per night

Additional Vehicle Fee: $5 per vehicle per night

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in the campground. All pets must be kept on a leash, and owners are required to clean up pet waste.
