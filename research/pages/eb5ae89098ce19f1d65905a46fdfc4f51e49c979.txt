# 6,000 (PCT Trailhead)

Information on [Pacific Crest Trail closures](https://closures.pcta.org/).

Popular trailhead open year-round. Dogs must be leashed. No campfires allowed.

**Amenities:** Two picnic tables. One single vault toilet. _No drinking water available._

**Winter play:** Slopes are commonly used for sledding and tubing.

## 6000' PCT Trailhead

[![View of snow-covered hillside as seen from the parking lot](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/angeles/image/6000%27%20Trailhead.jpg?h=5b82fd31)](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/angeles/recreation/6000-pct-trailhead#)

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Year-round

### Operational Hours

Day use hours are 6 a.m. to 10 p.m.

### Restrictions

[**Know Fire & Other Restrictions Before You Go!**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/angeles/alerts): These conditions can affect your entire trip. For example, _Extreme_ and _Critical_ fire use restrictions mean no campfires and no portable cooking systems/stoves. Bring cold food and no S'mores.

### Passes and Permits

Adventure Pass Required $5/day or $30/year. Interagency Passes also accepted. For more information, please visit our [**recreation pass page**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/angeles/passes).

### Fee Site and Info

Adventure Pass Required $5/day or $30/year. Interagency Passes also accepted. For more information, please visit our [**recreation pass page**](http://www.fs.usda.gov/main/sbnf/passes-permits/recreation).

### Pet Information

Dogs must be on leash.

36 CFR 261.8 (d): Possessing a dog not on leash or otherwise confined.

$150 fine
