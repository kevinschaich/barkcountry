# Teal Lake Campground

[**\*\*\*Information concerning the drinking water at this campground.\*\*\***](https://www.fs.usda.gov/media/254371)

Teal Lake Campground is located near Walden, Colorado and provides 17 individual campsites. Each site has a fire grate and a picnic table, and the campground offers a vault toilet. All individual sites are first come, first served, except for the group site, which can be reserved at [www.recreation.gov](http://www.recreation.gov/). It can accommodate up to 21 people, has 4 picnic tables and 2 campfire rings.

Teal Lake is a 50-acre lake surrounded by a rocky shoreline and pine-fir forest. While some sites provide shade, many were affected by the beetle kill and what was once forested sites, offering shade, have now been thinned and lack shade. Black bear, bobcat, coyote, elk, mule deer and moose are just some of the many animals that inhabit the Medicine Bow-Routt National Forests. Teal Lake offers trout fishing and only electric and non-motorized boats are permitted. Off-road vehicle enthusiasts have easy access to the Grizzly-Helena Trail nearby. Hiking is great in the nearby Mount Zirkel wilderness, though hikers must drive to the trailheads. Open season is early June to late October.

To get to this campground, please note that GPS and other map apps do not give accurate directions causing many campers to get lost and arrive at destinations not near the Teal Lake Campground. It is advised to use the instructions step by step shown on this site only.

This site was approved for a fee change - [Click here for more information](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/mbrtb/recreation/mbrtb-recreation-site-fee-changes-information)

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Early June (weather dependent) - Mid to Late October (weather dependent)

### Restrictions

Dogs must be on leash in campground at all times.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $20 per night

$20/site, $40/group site, 21 person maximum. Exact cash or check only, payment instructions are found on the kiosk.

### Pet Information

Dogs must be kept on a leash.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 40.58508

**Longitude:** -106.6071

### Elevation

9,000 ft

### Directions

Please be aware that GPS and other map apps do not provide accurate directions. If traveling to this campground through Buffalo Pass, Buffalo Road conditions are rough and results in a much longer drive and requires 4WD.

From Walden, take Hwy 14 west for approximately 11 miles to Hebron, turn right on County Road 24 driving west. After approximately 10 miles, the road becomes Forest Road 60 and passes the Grizzly Creek Guard Station. Turn right on Forest Road 615, drive approximately 3 miles turning right into the Teal Lake Campground.
