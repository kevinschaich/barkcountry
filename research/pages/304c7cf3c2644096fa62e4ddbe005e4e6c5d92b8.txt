# Lafferty Campground

Current Conditions

Water system is out of service for the season.

Lafferty Campground has 8 campsites located along a small creek. It has a paved campground loop and is suitable for medium RVs. No hook-ups. The typical operating season for Lafferty Campground is mid-May through the end of September or early October.

## Reservations

All sites are available on a first-come, first-serve basis.

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Check in: 2:00 p.m.; Check-out: 11:00 a.m.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $10 per night

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in all campgrounds, picnic areas, and on trails. We recommend you **keep your dog on a leash**. Never leave them unattended. Dogs allowed to roam freely can frighten or injure wildlife, disturb other forest visitors, and create impacts to natural resources.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 44.939838

**Longitude:** -116.653893

### Directions

From Hwy 95 take the Council to Cuprum Road #002 (Hornet Ridge Road) twenty miles northwest to the campground.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Vault toilet(s)


### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

Potable water is typically available in the summer. It's always a good idea to travel with extra water for drinking and cooking.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are not allowed at this site.
