**The White River NF is in Summer Travel Season: May 21-November 22**

Off-road and off-trail motorized and mechanized travel is prohibited. E-bikes are allowed on roads and trails open to motorized vehicles. Contact the Ranger District you plan to visit for specific questions and on-the-ground updates.

**Summer Motor Vehicle Use Maps  (MVUMs)** are free and available online and at White River National Forest offices. These maps show forest areas that are designated as open, closed, or restricted to motorized and mechanized vehicles. Summer MVUMs also include seasonal closures. It is **your responsibility** to know where you can go, and the MVUM is your key resource.
