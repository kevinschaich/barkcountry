# Nahanni National Park Reserve

![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nt/woodbuffalo/2024-changes/01_11-nodrone/No-Drone_nobkgd.png)
Using recreational drones in Nahanni National Park Reserve is prohibited. Operating a drone within park boundaries may face law enforcement action and a fine of up to $25,000.

You must make a trip [reservation](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/nahanni/visit/inscription-registration) to visit Nahanni or Nááts'įhch'oh National Park Reserves overnight.

The Cirque of the Unclimbables’ granite spires rise out of the lush alpine meadow, at Náįlįcho (Virginia Falls) the South Nahanni River surges over a drop twice the height of Niagara Falls. Nahanni National Park Reserve, encompassing 30,000 square kilometers, is a designated UNESCO world heritage site. The Dehcho First Nations welcome adventurers to Nahʔą Dehé, land of peaks, plateaus and wild rivers.

### [Nahanni river trips](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/nahanni/activ/pagayer-paddle)

National Geographic ranks the Nahʔą Dehé trip among its Top 20 Tourism Adventures in the World. The reserve offers more than one river. Experienced paddlers also explore the Little Nahanni River and Flat River.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nt/nahanni/2022-site-refresh/activities/features/2022-NAH-2016-08-0002--640x360.jpg?modified=00010101000000)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/nahanni/activ/vol-flight)

### [Nahanni day flight experience](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/nahanni/activ/vol-flight)

A flightseeing excursion in Nahanni National Park Reserve provides access to icons of this UNESCO World Heritage Site and to remote areas few others will behold.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/nt/nahanni/2022-site-refresh/activities/features/2022-DSC02009-640x360.jpg?modified=00010101000000)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/nahanni/activ/escalade-climbing)

### [Nahanni alpine experience](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/nahanni/activ/escalade-climbing)

Nahanni combines wilderness with diverse peaks to create a mountaineering mecca for outdoor enthusiasts. Trekkers explore the valleys and mountain passes of the Ragged Range.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

[![Tehjeh Deé ](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nwt/nattsihchoh-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220102)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/naatsihchoh)

### [Nááts'įhch'oh National Park Reserve](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/naatsihchoh)

In the headwaters of Tehjeh Deé (South Nahanni River) Nááts'įhch'oh National Park Reserve is a place where culture and nature are intertwined. Nááts'įhch'oh offers whitewater paddling and off-the-grid hiking in the Northwest Territories for experienced adventurers.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nwt/2022-SENHS-2015-027-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220102)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nt/saoyuehdacho)

### [Saoyú-ʔehdacho National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nt/saoyuehdacho)

Saoyú-Ɂehdacho National Historic Site celebrates the traditional lifestyles of the Sahtúgot’įnę – “the people of the Sahtú.” Visitors to Canada’s largest National Historic Site learn about the teaching, healing and spiritual places as conveyed through oral history.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nwt/wood-buffalo-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220102)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/woodbuffalo)

### [Wood Buffalo National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nt/woodbuffalo)

Wood Buffalo National Park is our country's largest national park and one of the largest in the world. It protects an outstanding and representative example of Canada's Northern Boreal Plains.
