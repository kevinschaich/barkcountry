# Bring Your Dog for a BARK Ranger Hike at National Park

#### You are viewing **ARCHIVED** content

This content was published online before January 20, 2025.
Please note that this content is **NOT UPDATED**, and links may not work. For current information, visit [https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/news/index.htm](https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/news/index.htm).

**News Release Date:** July 16, 2019

Bring your leashed dog for a hike at Indiana Dunes National Park’s West Beach on Saturday, July 27, from 7:00 pm to 8:30 pm. The hike could be as long as three miles and will include walking along the beach. Don’t forget your leash, pick up bags and water. Your dog will receive a free BARK Ranger dog tag. West Beach is a spectacular area to see diverse habitats such as oak savanna, pannes, wetlands, and dunes, as well as sweeping views of Lake Michigan.

Meet at the West Beach parking lot located at 376 North County Line Road in Portage. Please note that there is a $6.00 per car parking fee charged from 8:00 am to 7:00 pm at West Beach. For more information on this or other programs at Indiana Dunes National Park, call 219-395-1882 or visit our website at [www.nps.gov/indu](http://www.nps.gov/indu) and our Facebook page at [www.facebook.com/IndianaDunesNPS](http://www.facebook.com/IndianaDunesNPS).
