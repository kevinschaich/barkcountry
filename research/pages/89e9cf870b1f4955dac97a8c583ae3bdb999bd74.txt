![A wood sign with Danger painted in white is at the caldera edge with lake behind. ](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/crla/crop16_9/6A357DF2-B432-23BA-89D2C51CA84110D5.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Keep abreast of current conditions and any alerts regarding danger, closures, and important park information.

[Entrance Fees](https://www.nps.gov/crla/planyourvisit/fees.htm)

![Cars in line at the Annie Spring Entrance Station](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/crla/crop16_9/793A8EDF-ACD9-A870-872214427CD95326.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Find out what it costs to enter the park and what NPS passes are available. See a list of fee-free days for 2023.

[Directions and Transportation](https://www.nps.gov/crla/planyourvisit/directions.htm)

![ a guard rail on the left curves with OR Hwy 62, on the right is a slow 25 mph sign ](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/crla/crop16_9/E827CC35-B584-F7F5-A9AF0DF652C61E32.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Reach the park from the West, South or North (closed winter) Entrance. Cell reception is limited, get directions before hand.

[Weather](https://www.nps.gov/crla/planyourvisit/weather.htm)

![Mostly cloudy lake view with a rainbow from the caldera rim to the top of Wizard Island, a volcano. ](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/crla/crop16_9/93CBEF6F-E72C-3E8D-FDD395DBD3BA3EAE.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Regardless of the time of year or season that you visit, check the forecast multiple times in advance. Conditions can change quickly.

[Enjoying the Park with Your Pet](https://www.nps.gov/crla/planyourvisit/pets.htm)

![Visitor with his dog at Rim Village](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/crla/crop16_9/7A698EE3-AC4A-617E-CBC419F709BB607A.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Important information about visiting the park with your pet(s), ways to keep them safe, and where to take them.

[Backcountry Camping](https://www.nps.gov/crla/planyourvisit/backcountry_camping.htm)

![trail and campground directional sign](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/crla/crop16_9/D6781959-09DE-CA63-DD2106766016F860.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Prepare for a wilderness adventure that could include snow, a brilliant night sky, or the sound of animals. Get help with trip planning.

[Cleetwood Cove Trail Rehabilitation](https://www.nps.gov/crla/planyourvisit/cleetwood-cove-trail-and-marina-rehabilitation.htm)

![An treetop view of the Cleetwood Cove section of Crater Lake with boat dock trail.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/crla/crop16_9/CF1F6750-945F-6FD0-AAC28F0A20FA23AC.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Cleetwood Cove Trail and Marina will be closed for 3 years for a major and much needed rehabilitation project.

[Lodging and Camping](https://www.nps.gov/crla/planyourvisit/lodging-camping.htm)

![Round wood logs painted dark brown support a brown wood sign stating the west entrance to the park](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/crla/crop16_9/3F3F6B8D-A511-B07F-BB1D07876DF45FD2.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

A list of lodging and camping options (year-round) within 20 miles outside the park boundaries, and distances to further-out communities.

[Things to Do](https://www.nps.gov/crla/planyourvisit/things2do.htm)

![People  at Watchman peak overlooking the lake, faces aglow from the setting sun. sun](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/crla/crop16_9/6882C76A-CC57-D0D9-A0590025190584E2.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Whether day-tripping or spending a week, find a way to explore, discover, and retreat into the natural beauty and history of the park.

[Trolley and Boat Tours](https://www.nps.gov/crla/planyourvisit/boat-and-trolley-tours.htm)

![At an overlook, visitors stand along a 2 foot native stone wall next to a trolley near trees](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/crla/crop16_9/EF690FAD-E030-B0BD-B17EEB4E5FEF5A97.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Choose a trolley or boat tour and enjoy the sights worry-free with an added bonus of a ranger's perspective.
