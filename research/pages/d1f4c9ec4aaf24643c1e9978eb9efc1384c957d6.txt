# Canebrake Horse Camp

Canebrake Horse Camp has 28 sites.

The Non-motorized Trail System can be accessed from Canebrake using the Home Trail, which goes around the campground.  There are approximately 40 miles of horse/hike/bike trails, ranging in difficulty from easy to difficult.

## Active Alerts

[Alcoholic Beverage Prohibition- Uwharrie National Forest](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/alerts/alcoholic-beverage-prohibition-uwharrie-national-forest)

## Reservations

[Make Reservations Here](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/233913)

Campsites can be reserved at recreation.gov. First come first serve sites are also available for purchase in person on a limited basis. Payment can be made by cash or check. Make checks out to the USDA Forest Service.  Payment must be made within 30 minutes of choosing your campsite.

## General Information

Map and brochures can be found [here.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/maps-guides)

### Operational Hours

Check-in Time is 4:00 pm and Check-out Time is 2:00 pm.

### Restrictions

Rules and regulations for camping can be found [here](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/northcarolina/recreation/camping-cabins).

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $20 per night

Double Site: $40 per night

Single Site electric hook-up Fee: $7 per night

Double Site electric hook-up Fee: $14 per night

### Pet Information

Pets must be on a leash.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 35.43977148

**Longitude:** -80.04716521

### Directions

From Troy, travel on NC 109N for 10 miles and turn left onto Mullinix Road (NC 1154). Follow Mullinix Road for about 2 miles to a "T" intersection. Turn right at "T" onto FR 544 (McLeans Creek Road) and travel about one-quarter mile to the campground on the right.

### Parking

Up to two vehicles are allowed at each site. Parking spurs are available for horse trailers.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

4 Flush toilets, 2 vault toilets, and 4 showers


### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

The camp provides water spigots and hydrants for drinking water.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are allowed at this site.
