# Sunlight Ranger Cabin

Beautiful scenic cabin sleeps eight, nestled in the Sunlight Valley. Three bedroom, one bath log cabin with a fully equipped kitchen and fireplace make for a very comfortable stay.

Stay limit is 16 days.

Food storage is required at the Sunlight Ranger Cabin - be prepared to store your food properly.

## Reservations

Sunlight Ranger Cabin is reservable via Recreation.gov. Reservations begin 6 months in advance.  For reservation or inquiries about reservation status, go to Recreation.gov.

## General Information

### Operational Hours

The Sunlight Ranger Cabin is available for rent May through September.  Reservations begin 6 months in advance.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 44.7278

**Longitude:** -109.5548

### Directions

The Sunlight Ranger Cabin is located approximately 40 miles northwest of Cody at 724 Sunlight Road. Take Highway 120 north from Cody approximately 16 miles, turn left onto Chief Joseph Scenic Highway 296, and drive west 22 miles up and over Dead Indian Pass. At the bottom of the pass turn left onto Sunlight Road 101, then drive west approximately 8 miles, the cabin will be on your left.
