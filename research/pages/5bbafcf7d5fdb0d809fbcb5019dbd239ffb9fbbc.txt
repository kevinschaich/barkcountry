# Lower Tonto Creek Campground

Current Conditions

No Firewood Sales. Walk-in tent sites only.

The Tonto Creek Campground has a large open-space parking lot and paved walkway makes it accessible with only a light hike to the creek. Families may enjoy fishing at both Tonto and Horton Creeks or pass time hiking through nearby Horton Creek Trail or Derrick Trail. Both of these trails access the High line National Recreation Trail (#31).

## General Information

- All campsites are walk-in, no vehicle access to site. Parking is available in lot only.
- No off-road vehicles allowed.
- Bear activity has increased due to drought conditions. For more information please visit [bebearaware.org](https://bebearaware.org/)
- 14-day stay limit.
- No firewood sales
- Dogs are allowed and must be kept on a leash.
- Please use trash receptacles or pack it in, pack it out.
- All campgrounds have posted Rules & Regulations at entrance kiosks. Please read and observe all rules.
- Sites are small, so trailers have to be short or only over camper type mounts.
- Quiet hours 10:00 pm to 6:00 am (running generators are not permitted during quiet hours).

### Seasons of Use

Open Season: April - October

### Operational Hours

Check in: 2:00 p.m.; Check-out: 12:00 p.m.; Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. until 6:00 a.m.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 34.330891

**Longitude:** -111.093784

### Directions

This site is 15 miles northeast of Payson. From Payson, travel 15 miles east on State Hwy. 260. Just east of mile marker 269 at Kohl’s Ranch, turn left (north) on Forest Road (FR) 289.
