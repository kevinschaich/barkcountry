[Boating in Biscayne National Park](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/planyourvisit/boating.htm)

![Silhouette of a boat heading out on the water into a glowing orange sunrise](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bisc/crop16_9/BAD3014B-A924-8472-CEA9E4793C01C12B.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

In a park that's 95% water, boating is the perfect way to explore.

[Canoeing and Kayaking](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/planyourvisit/canoeing-and-kayaking.htm)

![Silhouette of a person in a kayak paddling across glassy waters ](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bisc/crop16_9/D85099D1-1DD8-B71B-0BCB85721A0B789D.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Canoeing and kayaking are great ways to experience the mangrove-fringed shorelines and shallow bay waters.

[Coral Reefs](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/learn/nature/coralreefs.htm)

![an underwater photograph of several types of coral.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bisc/crop16_9/26732F86-F45B-AD00-FC29E58C1EB91446.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Learn about our most unique and diverse ecosystem in the park. What makes the coral reef special, and what challenges it faces.

[Biscayne Maritime Heritage Trail](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/12f2c70c87a44179b889ce5a1ed4256a)

![A mosaic image of a scuba diver at a shipwreck](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bisc/crop16_9/26901106-DF4B-F34D-3849FD3CF50CFC75.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

See 3D images of some of the park's shipwrecks and learn their stories through this interactive story map.

[Guided Eco-Adentures](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/planyourvisit/guidedtours.htm)

![Snorkeler swimming in crystal clear waters](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bisc/crop16_9/3DC97E03-1DD8-B71B-0BB322D3F4474DE6.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Guided eco-adventures depart from the Dante Fascell Visitor Center.

[Explore and Learn](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/learn/index.htm)

![Water winding through a mangrove forest](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bisc/crop16_9/F0A30C42-0CFD-046A-D65EF6769A2CAE4B.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Biscayne National Park protects coral reefs, mangrove forests, Biscayne Bay, Florida Keys and 10,000 years of human history.

[Mooring Buoy Locations](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/planyourvisit/mooring-buoys.htm)

![Boat attached to mooring buoy Fowey Rocks lighthouse in the distance](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bisc/crop16_9/C562F8ED-1DD8-B71B-0B9BDFCF35FA380A.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Mooring buoys help prevent damage to the fragile coral reef.

[Fishing and Lobstering](https://www.nps.gov/bisc/planyourvisit/fishing.htm)

![Two people fishing on a boat](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bisc/crop16_9/ECA8A43E-1DD8-B71B-0BD404E5EE1394E0.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

By understanding and following applicable fishing regulations, you can be an important steward of the resource.
