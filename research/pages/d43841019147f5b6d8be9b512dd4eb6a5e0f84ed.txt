# Falls Campground

The recently remodeled Falls Campground hosts 54 sites near Brooks Lake Creek Falls. Mature pine and fir trees surround the site and campers can enjoy an overlook of the falls.

Stay limit is 16 days.

Maximum spur length is 32 feet.

Food storage is required in this campground - be prepared to store your food properly. Bear boxes available.

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Typically Mid to late June (snow dependent) until approximately October 20th.

### Restrictions

[Food storage is required](http://chrome-extension//efaidnbmnnnibpcajpcglclefindmkaj/https://www.fs.usda.gov/Internet/FSE_DOCUMENTS/fseprd1112030.pdf)

### Passes and Permits

Interagency Senior/Access Recreation Passes provide a 50% discount to Single Campsites ONLY.

\*The discount applies only to the fee for the campsite physically occupied by the pass holder, not to any additional fee or campsite(s) occupied by members of the pass holder's party.\*
