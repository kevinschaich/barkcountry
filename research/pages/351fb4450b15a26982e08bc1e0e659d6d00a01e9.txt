![A hiker enjoys majestic views in the Chisos Mountains.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bibe/crop16_9/7314FB38-FDC1-882F-660FED074C3BA5E9.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Big Bend is a hiker's paradise. More than 150 miles of desert and mountains trails to explore!

[Nature](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/learn/nature/index.htm)

![Collared peccary, or Javelina, posing next to a prickly pear cactus.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bibe/crop16_9/320B0393-BAF3-2A86-42318BBCF0BDAF8D.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Delve into the park's flora and fauna, geology, ecosystems, and other natural features.

[Big Bend Birds](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/learn/nature/birds-of-big-bend.htm)

![A pyrrhuloxia resting on mesquite branches. ](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bibe/crop16_9/E8232D75-0BD7-55DF-BF0CC56B5662BAB0.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Big Bend is a world-famous location for birds and birdwatching. Learn more about the feathered wonders of Big Bend.

[Scenic Drives](https://www.nps.gov/bibe/planyourvisit/scenic_drives.htm)

![A paved scenic drive winds its way through rugged desert landscapes.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/bibe/crop16_9/3BC91A46-ACFE-BC02-BBF3233DFDF92161.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Hit the road and explore the park's gorgeous vistas. Paved highways and dirt roads provide access to an incredible array of experiences.
