# Activities and experiences

## Rouge National Urban Park

Join Parks Canada for a quiet, place-based observance of the **National Day for Truth and Reconciliation** on September 30 (9 a.m. to 3 p.m.) at Twyn Rivers Day Use Area.

[Click here](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/activ/calendrier-calendar/1c11b33b-669d-4845-ba38-fcdab30618a4) for event details.

Rouge National Urban Park is a “people’s park” with so much to discover in your own backyard! The park’s trails cover forests, thickets, meadows, wetlands, rivers and agricultural landscapes. Join us on a guided walk, paddle the river and marsh, photograph migrating birds or plan a family picnic.

Are you organizing a group visit to the park? Please contact [rouge@pc.gc.ca](mailto:rouge@pc.gc.ca) or 416-264-2020 to plan for a best possible experience.

## Activities

### [Guided walks](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/activ/guide)

Discover the park's natural, cultural and agricultural stories with our friendly and knowledgeable Parks Canada volunteer leaders and staff.

### [Calendar of Events](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/activ/calendrier-calendar)

Take part in events, special activities and memorable experiences in the park.

### [Volunteer opportunities](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/activ/benevolat-volunteer)

Become a volunteer today for unique and enriching experiences in the park.

### [Water activities](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/activ/eau-water)

Canoe or kayak the waters of Rouge Marsh and Rouge River to discover a rich array of wildlife.

### [Fall Colours](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/activ/automne-fall)

Embrace the colours and fall in love with Rouge National Urban Park this season.

### [Winter activities](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/activ/hiver-winter)

Discover winter activities in the park: hiking, snowshoeing, birdwatching, cross-country skiing and more!

### [iNaturalist](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/activ/inaturalist)

Share discoveries of plants and animals with other nature enthusiasts.

### [At-home activities](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge/activ/chezvous-athome)

Try one of our at-home activities to learn more about the park without leaving home!
