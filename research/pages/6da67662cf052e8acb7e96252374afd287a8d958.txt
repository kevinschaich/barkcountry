# Old Timers Day Use Area

Old Timers Day-Use Area, on the east shore of Vallecito Reservoir, is 1.5 miles north of the dam on County Road 501A (Forest Rd. 603). Nine shaded picnic sites are on a gentle hillside near the water. Amenities include picnic tables, restrooms and a trash dumpster.

This picnic area is open from Memorial Day until Labor Day, from 8:00am to 8:00pm daily.

Dogs are welcome but must be kept on a leash at all times.

First Come - First Served

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Between May and September.

### Operational Hours

Open daily from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. (the gate is locked at night)

### Fee Site and Info

Day Use:

$10 per vehicle per day

Maximum number of people per site: 8

### Pet Information

Pets are welcome but please keep your pets on a leash.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 37.3769

**Longitude:** -107.5605

### Directions

From Durango, CO: Travel 17 miles on Florida Road, County Road #240. Turn left onto County Road #501. After 4 miles, slight right onto Forest Road #603 for just under 1 mile. Day use area is located on the east side of Vallecito Reservoir.

From Bayfield, CO: Travel 14 miles north on County Road #501, then slight right (northeast) onto Forest Road #603 for just under 1 mile. Day use area is located on the east side of Vallecito Reservoir.
