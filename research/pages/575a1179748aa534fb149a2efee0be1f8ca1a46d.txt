# Interior Whistle Stop Day Use Area

The Interior Whistle Stop is a shady picnic area along Big Stony Creek featuring picnic tables, grassy areas, and a child-sized wooden train playground. Big Stony Creek is a stocked trout stream.

## General Information

### Seasons of Use

Open year round

### Operational Hours

Sunrise to Sunset

### Restrictions

When recreating on Forest Service Lands, follow the [safety and outdoor ethics](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/gwj/safety-ethics) recommendations.

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

### Pet Information

Keep your pet on a leash when in a recreation area or campground. The law requires dog owners to keep their dog on a leash with a length no greater than 6 feet and under physical control when within a recreational area, such as a campground or day use area (36 CFR 2616(j)).

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 37.41472222

**Longitude:** -80.59222222

### Directions

From Blacksburg, VA: Follow US 406 West for 15 miles. Turn right onto SR 635 and continue for approximately 9 miles. Whistle Stop Picnic Area is on the left.
