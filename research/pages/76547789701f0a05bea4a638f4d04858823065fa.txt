# Horse Creek Recreation Area

Horse Creek Recreation Area is located eight miles from Greeneville, off TN 107 north. This recreation area is nestled into a hardwood cove beside its namesake, Horse Creek.

There are facilities for camping and day-use activities including hiking, fishing, picnicking and swimming.  During the 1930’s, a rustic picnic pavilion and swimming area were constructed by the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) Company 1455, Camp Cordell Hull, stationed in Unicoi, TN.

The Horse Creek Recreation Area is planned to underago area rehabilitation and site wide improvements. Please visit [Horse Creek Recreation Area (arcgis.com)](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/176342f04758414f9ef0a861bb14f0c3) to learn more about the project.

## General Information

### Operational Hours

- The campground portion of the rec site is scheduled to open on May 5, 2026.
- The pavilion has been closed indefinitely due to damages. We apologize for the inconvenience.
- Make reservations on Recreation.gov [https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/10158738](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/10158738?tab=ratings)


### Restrictions

- Camping and picnicking are on a first-come, first-served basis.
- Group size is 5 people, 2 vehicles per site.
- No alcoholic beverages are allowed in the area.
- Food storage restrictions apply at all areas on the Cherokee National Forest. All food and beverages must be stored in an approved bear-proof container, your locked vehicle or hard-sided camper at all times unless being eaten, prepared, or transported.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $10 per night

- Camping fee is $10 per night.
- [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/123040) either online or call 1-877-444-6777.
- No fee for Day Use.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 36.1083

**Longitude:** -82.6568

### Directions

From Greeneville, take TN 107 north for six miles. Turn right at Horse Creek Road and follow the signs about two miles to the recreation area.

### Parking

Access to the campground is walk-in only. Park in the first loop to your left near the campground bathhouse and take a short walk over the pedestrian bridge to access the campground.
