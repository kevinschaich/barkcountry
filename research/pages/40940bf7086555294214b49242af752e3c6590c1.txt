# Welcome to Pike-San Isabel National Forests & Cimarron and Comanche National Grasslands

The Pike-San Isabel National Forests & Cimarron and Comanche National Grasslands covers nearly 3 million acres from western Kansas to Colorado's highest mountain peaks along the Continental Divide. This landscape offers a variety of ecosystems rich in history, geology, scenery, wildlife habitat and recreation opportunities.
