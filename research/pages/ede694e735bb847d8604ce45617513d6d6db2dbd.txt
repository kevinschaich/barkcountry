# Cedar Springs Yellow Post Site

One Yellow Post Site alongside Morris Ranch Road.

[Campfire permits](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/sanbernardino/safety-ethics/what-does-it-mean-if-i-have-california-campfire-permit) are required for any type of fire use—campfires, charcoal BBQs or gas stoves—but may be prohibited during certain times of the year. Check the current fire restrictions before heading out.

### Restrictions

- 8 persons maximum. 2 vehicles maximum.
- Avoid parking on grasses and vegetation at all times.
- Yellow Post campsites have a 14-day camping limit.
- No wood fires, charcoal BBQs, or charcoal stove allowed outside agency provided fire-ring
- No ground fires or building of rock-fire-rings permitted anywhere on Southern CA Forests!
- Pack out what you pack in and [**Leave No Trace!**](https://lnt.org/why/7-principles/)

### Passes and Permits

Valid [California Campfire Permit](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/sanbernardino/safety-ethics/what-does-it-mean-if-i-have-california-campfire-permit) required for use of fire ring and or gas / propane stoves and lanterns. Subject to fire use restrictions.

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are associated with this site.

### Pet Information

Dogs MUST be on a leash not longer than six feet, or otherwise under physical restrictive control \[ [36 CFR 261.16(j)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/chapter-II/part-261/subpart-A/section-261.16)\]. Read more info about [pet ethics](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/wilderness/ethics).

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 33.65416

**Longitude:** -116.589621

### Directions

From Idyllwild - Head toward CA-243 South for about 4 miles, then slightly left to CA-74 East for about 8.6 miles, turn left onto Morris Ranch Road for about 3.7 miles, the Yellow Post Site is on the right side of the road.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Restrooms are not available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are allowed at this site.

Horses must be hobbled or picketed at least 100 feet from streams, springs, meadows, trails or other campsites. Do not tether horses to trees. Before leaving an area scatter manure so it will decompose.
