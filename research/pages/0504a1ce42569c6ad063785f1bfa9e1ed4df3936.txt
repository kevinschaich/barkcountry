# Activities and experiences

## Georgian Bay Islands National Park

### Regular fees apply as of September 8, 2026

September 7 is the last day of [free admission and discounted camping and overnight stays](https://parks.canada.ca/voyage-travel/conseils-tips/choisis-canada-choose/admission-camping) with Parks Canada and the Canada Strong Pass.

#### Admission fee update:

Admission fee update: Outside of the Canada Strong Pass period (June 19 to September 7 2026), there is a [daily admission fee](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg/visit/tarifs-fees) for all visitors to Georgian Bay Islands National Park in addition to other applicable fees.

Hike or bike forest trails to secluded lakeside vistas on Beausoleil Island. Stay a while at a waterfront campsite and rustic cabins, or book a day visit with Landmark Cruises. Spend family time exploring these scenic shores.

**Important Notice:** This National Park is located on islands and is only accessible by boat. Day visitors must plan ahead and reserve boat transportation with [Landmark Cruises](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg/visit/navette-bateau-shuttle-boat) or with a [licensed private water taxi](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg/visit/directions). Campers must coordinate their own boat transportation or book a [licensed private water taxi](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg/visit/directions). [Cabin](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg/activ/camping/cabins-chalets) and [oTENTik](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg/activ/camping/oTENTik) reservations include transportation to the island.

## Services and information

### [Camping and overnight accommodations](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg/activ/camping)

Stay YOUR way on Beausoleil Island! Cedar Spring Campground, backcountry camping, rustic cabins and oTENTiks, there is something for everyone.

### [Boat Georgian Bay's stunning landscapes](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg/activ/camping/boat-nav)

Avid sailors can explore the isle and islets with docking facilities on Beausoleil Island.

### [Hike forest trails and granite hills](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg/activ/hike-randonne)

From north to south, Beausoleil Island offers flat winding trails and rugged rocky scrambles with stunning views.

### [Transportation for cabins and oTENTiks](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg/activ/camping/transport)

The shuttle boat service for cabin and oTENTik stays in the park has specific check-in and departure times.
