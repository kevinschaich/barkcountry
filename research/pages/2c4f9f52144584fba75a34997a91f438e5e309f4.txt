# Meadow Trailhead

The Meadow Trailhead is located at the junction of the Meadow Trail and the parking lot at Woods Canyon Lake Store.  THis trailhead provides access to the Meadow Trail and the Woods Canyon Lake Loop Trail.

The Meadow Trail is a one mile trail that travels from The Woods Canyon Vista on Forest Road 300 north to Woods Canyon Lake. The entire trail is paved and provides easy walking access from the campgrounds along Forest Road 105 to Woods Canyon Lake.  Due to the steepness of the trail as it descends toward Woods Canyon Lake, the last 1/4 mile is not considered ADA accessible.

## General Information

This trail is popular for walking and bicycle riding.  No horses or motor vehicles are allowed on this trail.

### Pet Information

All dogs must be on a leash at all times

### Contact Information

**Contact Name:** Black Mesa Ranger District

**Contact Phone:** [928-535-7300](tel:928-535-7300)

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 34.33318611

**Longitude:** -110.9434417

### Parking

Parking for this trailhead is shared with the Woods Canyon Lake Store. Additional parking is available in the overflow lot on Forest Road 105 on the ridge above the lake.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Vault toilets are located at the trailhead

### Water

Potable water is available at this site.

Potable water is available for purchase at Woods Canyon Lake Store

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are not available at this site.

Picnic tables are available nearby on the south side of the parking lot across from the Woods Canyon Lake Store.
