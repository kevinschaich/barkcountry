# Manti-La Sal National Forest and Interagency Partners Begin Reopening Public Lands

**Release Date:** July 22, 2026

**San Juan County, UT, July 22, 2026**— Public lands are integral to the communities, public and economy throughout San Juan County. As conditions on the Babylon Fire moderate, the Forest Service, Bureau of Land Management, National Park Service, in coordination with the U.S. Wildland Service, San Juan Sheriff, San Juan County Emergency Management, Utah Division of Forestry, Fire and State Lands, Utah Highway Patrol and Monticello and Blanding officials are looking at every opportunity to reopen public lands where it is safe to do so while not impacting ongoing fire response.

Starting tomorrow, Thursday, July 23, Monticello Field Office of Bureau of Land Management will remove closures from all currently closed areas except those from Brushy Basin Road north to the Forest Service boundary. This information will be under BLM Fire Prevention Order: UT-CYD-2026-0. The Monticello Ranger District of the Manti-La Sal National Forest is removing closures from the area south of Elk Mountain/South Elk roads (Forest Service Road 50092), southwest of the Powerline road (Forest Service Road 50084), and northeast of the Hart’s Draw road (County Road B101). Additionally, Dalton Springs Campground, Buckboard Campgrounds, and Foy Lake Road (Forest service road 50101) to Foy Lake, will be opened, but all other roads and trails around Foy Lake will remain closed. This information will be under Monticello Ranger District Closure Order #04-10-26-05-04. Please note that this reopened area is still under Stage 2 fire restrictions. Canyonlands National Park does not currently have any closures related to the Babylon fire.

Due to ongoing fire operations, fire impacts as well as potential flash flooding, closures remain in place for the Monticello Field Office of Bureau of Land Management lands, and the remainder of the Monticello Ranger District of the Manti-La Sal National Forest. Current closures with maps and full information for these areas can be found here: [Monticello](https://www.blm.gov/sites/default/files/docs/2026-07/BLM%20Babylon%20Fire%20Closure%20Order%20and%20Map.pdf) [Field Office](https://www.blm.gov/sites/default/files/docs/2026-07/BLM%20Babylon%20Fire%20Closure%20Order%20and%20Map.pdf), [Monticello Ranger District.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/manti-lasal/alerts/monticello-ranger-district-closure-order-babylon-fire-7172026)
