# West Lake Crockett

Located on the northwest shore of the 450-acre Lake Crockett, West Lake Crockett Recreation Area was completed in 1968. Lake Crockett was built in the 1930s as a recreation site for the area.

First come, First Serve 12 campsites available- with cash, check or Scan-and-Pay QR code onsite.

Visitors may fish from the shore.

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Quiet time is from 10pm-6 am

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $10 per night

**CAMPING**\- $10 per campsite, per night
