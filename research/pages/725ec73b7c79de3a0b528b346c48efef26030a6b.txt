The Pinnacles Campground is located near the Visitor Center. Backpacking is not allowed.

[Cave & Karst Systems](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/learn/nature/cave.htm)

![Large boulder suspended by red rock walls on either side.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/pinn/crop16_9/F356B7C5-0938-F39D-D26CE486588AFC40.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Pinnacles is known for its rare talus caves. Discover the geology that underlies these unique cave and karst systems at Pinnacles.

[Condors](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/learn/nature/condor.htm)

![Profile of a California condor head, with piercing golden eye, pink skin, and hooked beak.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/pinn/crop16_9/9B174B7B-98C8-0A1A-F90A715DEECF6F54.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Learn why and how Pinnacles works to protect these massive, majestic, and severely-endangered creatures.

[Keep Wildlife Wild](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/planyourvisit/kww.htm)

![A raccoon forages for food.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/pinn/crop16_9/34E9BE81-1DD8-B71B-0B5712FD74806B54.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Help protect wildlife by storing food properly and keeping your distance from all park animals.

[Red Legged Frogs.](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/learn/nature/rlfrogs.htm)

![The Red Legged Frogs](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/pinn/crop16_9/AEF769AC-1DD8-B71B-0B46E3A6EA0770B4.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Exercise caution while exploring the caves. Frogs mate at the Bear Gulch Reservoir and some may be on the dark floor of the cave.

[Physical Environment](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/learn/nature/physical-environment.htm)

![Rocky peaks set against a clear blue evening sky with a sliver of moon.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/pinn/crop16_9/25432694-AC20-C1F9-375382E6DEC78570.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Explore the non-living factors that shape the environment at Pinnacles, including geology, water and hydrology, climate, and air quality.

[Meet the Scientists](https://www.nps.gov/pinn/learn/meet-park-scientists.htm)

![Scientist looks through long lens Camera.](https://www.nps.gov/common/uploads/grid_builder/pinn/crop16_9/93D68F22-999F-95BB-A1A43704811B6FD4.jpg?width=640&quality=80&mode=crop&format=webp)

Get to know the people behind the science that we do here at Pinnacles.
