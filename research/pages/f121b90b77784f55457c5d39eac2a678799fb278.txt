# Stag Point

**Four-wheel drive access only.** One dispersed site at trailhead with fire pit and table only. At Stag Point 5 dispersed units. First come, first served. No water, no garbage services. **Pack it in/Pack it Out.**

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Open when accessible/weather permitting. **Snow may close access at any time.** Trees may be across road at any time.

### Restrictions

Check for campfire restrictions

### Passes and Permits

Camp Fire permit needed.

Check for campfire restrictions.

### Fee Site and Info

No Fees

### Pet Information

Pets must be on leash and attended at all times.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 39.793

**Longitude:** -121.082

### Directions

From Oroville: Head east on Oro Dam Blvd East.  Turn right on Olive HWY (162) take a right on Forbestown Road. Left on Lumpkin Road (Feather Falls). Then right on 22N27 Rd.   Then you will take a left on the 94 Road toward Tammarack Flat area, Then a right on 22N72. Then a Left on 22N80Y Road to Stag point the Trailhead. No trailers beyond this point.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Restrooms are not available at this site.

### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are not available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are not allowed at this site.
