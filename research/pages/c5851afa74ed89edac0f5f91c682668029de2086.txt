# Long Lake Recreation Area

Current Conditions

Boardwalk is closed.
The boardwalk in the Long Lake Recreation Area in the Washburn District of the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest will be closed until further notice so that updates can be made to help ensure safety. USDA Forest Service specialists are planning a temporary re-route. Information about the re-route will be provided when the details are confirmed. A long-term plan is also being considered.

The Long Lake Recreation Area features two picnic areas and a swim beach with buoys on 32-acre Long Lake, a clear-water lake just west of Washburn on Forest Road 251.

There is a large picnic area with a designated swim beach located at the east end of the lake. It is accessible with paved trails to the picnic area, pedestal grills, water and vault toilets. There is a large, paved parking area that provides access to OHV (ATV and UTV) trails.

The carry-in boat landing, located on the west end of the parking area (Forest Road 1890A), provides access to Long Lake that is great for panfish and bass. It is also very popular for paddling - both kayaks and paddleboards. There are picnic tables and pedestal grills at this location as well.

This is a great place to bring your family for a day and enjoy the sandy beach!

This area is also popular during the winter months for ice fishing and snowshoeing. Visitors also like to bring their dogsled teams and use the unplowed roads around Long Lake.

## General Information

### Restrictions

Restrictions for all National Forest System lands can be found by visiting [Title 36 Code of Federal Regulations 261](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?tpl=/ecfrbrowse/Title36/36cfr261_main_02.tpl) by clicking here. The Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest specific restrictions can be found on the [Orders of the Forest Supervisor page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/chequamegon-nicolet/alerts).

### Fee Site and Info

Day Use:

$5 per vehicle per day

$30 per annual

There is a $5 day use pass for this recreation site. The Forest also has an [annual pass available](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/chequamegon-nicolet/passes) for $30. The USDA Forest Service honors all four [Interagency Recreation Passes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/passes-permits/recreation-fees-passes) that may be applied at National Park Service, Bureau of Land Management, Bureau of Reclamation and U.S. Fish and Wildlife sites charging recreation use fees.

### Pet Information

- Pets must always be restrained or on a leash while in developed recreation areas.
- Pets (except guide dogs) are not allowed in swimming areas.

## Facility and Amenity Information

It is accessible with paved trails to the picnic area, pedestal grills, water, and vault toilets.

## Recreation Opportunities

### Water Activities

[![Swimming](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/opportunity-icons/swimming_icon.svg)Swimming](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/chequamegon-nicolet/recreation/opportunities/water-activities) [![Boating - Non-Motorized](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/opportunity-icons/non_motor_boat_icon.svg)Boating - Non-Motorized](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/chequamegon-nicolet/recreation/opportunities/water-activities)

### Swimming Info

The area has a designated swim beach that is sandy.

### Boating - Non-Motorized Info

Long Lake is popular for paddling - kayaking and paddleboarding.

### Fishing

![Fishing - Lake & Pond](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/opportunity-icons/lake_fishing_icon.svg)Fishing - Lake & Pond

![Fishing - Ice](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/opportunity-icons/ice_fishing_icon.svg)Fishing - Ice

### Fishing - Lake & Pond Info

Long Lake is popular for fishing. The lake has blue gills, small mouth bass, and other pan fish.

### Fishing - Ice Info

This is a great place to ice fish in the winter.

### Hiking

[![Day Hikes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/opportunity-icons/day_hike_icon.svg)Day Hikes](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/chequamegon-nicolet/recreation/opportunities/hiking)

### Day Hikes Info

There is a 1-mile trail around Long Lake.

### Picnicking

[![Picnicking - Single](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/opportunity-icons/picnicking_single_icon.svg)Picnicking - Single](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/chequamegon-nicolet/recreation/opportunities/picnicking)

### Picnicking - Single Info

The area features two picnic areas on each end of the lake.

### Winter Sports

[![XC Skiing/Snowshoeing](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/opportunity-icons/xc_skiing_icon.svg)XC Skiing/Snowshoeing](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/chequamegon-nicolet/recreation/opportunities/winter-sports)

### XC Skiing/Snowshoeing Info

The 1-mile Long Lake trail is popular for snowshoeing in the winter months.

### Outdoor Science and Learning

[![Viewing Wildlife](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/opportunity-icons/viewing_wildlife_icon.svg)Viewing Wildlife](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/chequamegon-nicolet/recreation/opportunities/outdoor-science-and-learning)

### Viewing Wildlife Info

The Long Lake trail is a great place to watch wildlife like loons, muskrats, eagles and much more!
