# Activities and experiences

## Grasslands National Park

### Fire ban in effect

This fire ban covers the entire East and West Blocks of Grasslands National Park including all front and backcountry campgrounds and day use areas.

[More information](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/securite-safety/bulletins/e4663531-224d-4656-a54c-4847664f6d6a)

[![Parks Canada oTENTik below the Milky Way at night in the Dark Sky Preserve, in Grasslands National Park.](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/sk/grasslands/WET4/2023/feature/camping-640-22.jpg?modified=20221129193711)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/activ/camping)

### [​Camping](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/activ/camping)

oTENTiks, tenting, RVs, or equestrian? Front country comforts or back country adventure, the options and views are endless!

[![Young adults out hiking on the 70 Mile Butte Trail at West Block, in Grasslands National Park.](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/sk/grasslands/WET4/2023/feature/randonnee-hiking-640-22.jpg?modified=20221129192516)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/activ/experiences/randonee-hiking)

### [Hiking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/activ/experiences/randonee-hiking)

Choose your own epic backcountry route or hike one of the many great trails in the park, including the fantastic 70 Mile Butte trail.

[![The sun sets on the rugged hills of the badlands in the East Block](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/sk/grasslands/WET4/2023/feature/badlands2-640-22.jpg?modified=20221129193657)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/activ/experiences/badlands)

### [Badlands Parkway](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/activ/experiences/badlands)

Take in the 11 km scenic driving experience that meanders along the rim of breathtaking Badlands and see some of Saskatchewan’s finest scenery!

[![View of bison on a hill](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/sk/grasslands/WET4/2023/feature/hiver-winter-640-22.jpg?modified=20221129193141)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/visit/saison-hivernale-winter-season)

### [Winter Season](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/visit/saison-hivernale-winter-season)

Visitors are welcome to visit Grasslands National Park during the winter season; however, it is important to be aware of what your options are when exploring the park.

[![Visitors wearing backpacks and snake gaiters follow a Parks Canada staff member. ](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/sk/grasslands/WET4/2024/rattlesnake/hike-640-24.jpg?modified=20240913190853)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/activ/decouverte-tours1)

### [Tours and programs](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/activ/decouverte-tours1)

Discover some of Canada’s rarest wildlife, darkest skies, astonishing dinosaur fossils, over 20,000 tipi rings, and varied landscapes.

[![Plains bison in September, in Grasslands National Park.](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/pn-np/sk/grasslands/WET4/2023/feature/bison-640-22.jpg?modified=20221129192732)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/activ/experiences/ecocircuit-ecotour)

### [Ecotour Scenic Drive](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/sk/grasslands/activ/experiences/ecocircuit-ecotour)

The Ecotour Scenic Drive (West Block) features stunning landscapes, rich history, and active conservation.
