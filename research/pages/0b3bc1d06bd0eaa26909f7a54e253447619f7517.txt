# Videos

Discover and enjoy the Manti-La Sal National Forest via YouTube.

![Prehistoric Artifact](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r04/manti-lasal/image/Artifact-Sm.JPG?h=91392671)

Volunteers help catalogue artifacts from hundreds of ancestral Puebloan sites on the flanks of Elk Ridge and the Abajo Mountains in southeastern Utah.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B84FimGEkS0)

![Brian Murdock Dinosaur Tracks](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r04/manti-lasal/image/Brian-Tracks-sm.jpg?h=af92a749)

A short trail on the Manti-La Sal National Forest leads to a spectacular view into Bull Canyon and includes several sets of dinosaur tracks.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=msH0NEWQS_c)
