# Fundy National Park

Experience the world’s highest tides along the Bay of Fundy, situated on the unceded territory of the Mi'gmaq people. Explore the bay as you paddle through the rising waters that reach up to 12 m or more, walk the otherworldly ocean floor at low tide, or venture inland on trails that lead to waterfalls deep in Acadian forests.

### Point Wolfe Covered Bridge will be closed from September 8th until December 2026 while repairs are underway.

## More places to discover with Parks Canada

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nb/Kouchibouguac-640x320.jpg?modified=20221126220757)](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nb/kouchibouguac)

### [Kouchibouguac National Park](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nb/kouchibouguac)

Golden sand dunes, estuaries brimming with life, warm ocean beaches, Mi’kmaq and Acadian culture, the starry spectacle of a Dark Sky Preserve and snowbound winter activities weave together the compelling tapestry of Kouchibouguac National Park.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nb/tourmartellodecarleton-carletonmartellotower-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220757)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nb/carleton)

### [Carleton Martello Tower National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nb/carleton)

Built around the time of the War of 1812, Carleton Martello Tower once protected New Brunswick’s most important port against American overland invasion. The site features restored barracks, interactive exhibits, and commanding harbour views.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nb/blockhausdestandrews-standrewsblockhouse-640x360.jpg?modified=20221126220756)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nb/standrews)

### [St. Andrew's Blockhouse National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nb/standrews)

Built just after the War of 1812 began, St. Andrews Blockhouse recalls an era of conflict along the New Brunswick harbour and US border, when townspeople united to protect family and community from American raiders.

[![](https://pcweb2.azureedge.net/-/media/WET4/proximite-nearby/nb/Beausejour-640x320.jpg?modified=20221126220756)](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nb/beausejour)

### [Fort Beauséjour – Fort Cumberland National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nb/beausejour)

Discover a time when Britain and France were caught in a tug-of-war for dominance in Acadie, and be moved by the stories of soldiers and settlers who lived and died around the historic star-shaped fort.
