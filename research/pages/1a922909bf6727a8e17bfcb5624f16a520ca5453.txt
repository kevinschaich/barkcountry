# Hurricane Campground

Located along a rushing mountain stream under the shade of hemlock and rhododendron, this campground offers a peaceful setting for quiet evenings around the campfire.  Hikers can walk from their campsite to the Appalachian Trail and many other nearby trails. This site is not recommended for large RVs.

# Hurricane Campground

# HURRICANE CAMPGROUND

Part of [George Washington & Jefferson National Forest](https://www.recreation.gov/gateways/1050)

## Overview

Hurricane Campground is one of the best kept secrets in southwest Virginia. Located along a rushing mountain stream under the shade of hemlock and rhododendron, this campground offers a peaceful setting for those looking to relax as well as visitors looking for outdoor adventure.

From the campsite, hikers can access many trails including the Appalachian Trail. This well-known trail extends more than 2,189 miles throughout the Appalachian Mountains across 14 states. It is a protected greenway and owns the title as the first National Scenic Trail.


## Facilities

Hurricane has drinking water, flush toilets, hot showers and a playing field. Large RVs are not recommended. Each site comes with a lantern pole, picnic table and campfire ring.


## Natural Features

The campground is perched along Hurricane Branch and Comers Creek, among colorful rhododendron. Several groomed, grassy fields unfold at the campground, but the campsites are nicely shaded by mixed woods of oak and hemlock. Hurricane Knob is nearby with an elevation from 2800 to 3050 feet.


## Recreation

Visitors frequently explore the Hurricane Knob Trail. The loop trail, at 1.1 miles, parallels Hurricane Creek for about half a mile, crosses two wooden bridges and ascends to the picturesque Hurricane Knob. The creeks are stocked with trout for a relaxing day of fishing.


## Nearby Attractions

Hurricane and Comers creeks run through the campground and surrounding areas for wading and trout fishing. Additional fishing options and excellent hunting opportunities are available near the campground. State fishing and hunting regulations and licensing requirements apply. Hurricane Knob Nature Trail offers a brisk 1-mile loop hike and there is easy access to the Appalachian Trail within ½ mile. The entire campground has a relaxing, rustic atmosphere and an open field allows for games and family fun.

## General Information

We welcome responsible volunteers to serve as Campground hosts. Hosts are provided a site with water, sewer, and electricity at no charge.

We are also looking for individuals or groups willing to _"Adopt a Trail"_ to perform light trail maintenance.

For more information about volunteer opportunities contact Mount Rogers Nation Recreation Area at [SM.FS.MRNRA@usda.gov](mailto:SM.FS.MRNRA@usda.gov) or **1-800-628-7202**

### Seasons of Use

Mid-April through late October.

### Operational Hours

Gate open 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.

### Passes and Permits

The America the Beautiful Interagency Pass series and the George Washington & Jefferson National Forests [Seasonal Day Use Pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/gwj/passes) are accepted here. The Virginia State Parks pass does not apply on the National Forest.

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $20 per night

Double Site: $40 per night

$3 per vehicle day use fee. $2 per shower (non-camper).

We currently accept reservations for some campsites at this campground, but you may use Scan & Pay through the [recreation.gov app](https://www.recreation.gov/mobile-app) to pay for a first come-first served campsite once you are on location and at your campsite.  Please scan the QR code at the fee station and follow all in-app and on-site instructions for Scan & Pay to be successful.

Cash or checks only accepted in the fee collection area. Some campsites are available for [reservation](https://www.recreation.gov/).

### Pet Information

Keep your pet on a leash when in a recreation area or campground. The law requires dog owners to keep their dog on a leash with a length no greater than 6 feet and under physical control when within a recreational area, such as a campground or day use area (36 CFR 2616(j)).
