# Bogachiel Rain Forest River Trailhead

**Provides access to**: Bogachiel Rain Forest River Trail #825 and Ira Spring Wetland Trail which create a loop option.

**Features:** Bulletin board for visitor information.

## General Information

Bogachiel Rain Forest Trail follows the Bogaciel River and enters [Olympic National Park](http://www.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/bogachiel-river-trail.htm) at 1.5 miles, where it becomes Bogachiel River Trail (No dogs allowed in Olympic National Park).

### Seasons of Use

Year-round

### Restrictions

[Wilderness regulations apply](http://www.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/wilderness-regulations.htm) in the [Olympic National Park](https://www.nps.gov/olym/index.htm).

### Passes and Permits

You must [obtain a wilderness permit](http://www.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/wilderness-trip-planner.htm) at an Olympic National Park office for all overnight trips in the Park.

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

### Pet Information

Pets are allowed **on leash** on the Bogachiel Rain Forest Trail. Once inside the [Olympic National Park](http://www.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/bogachiel-river-trail.htm) at 1.5 miles, where it becomes the [Bogachiel River Trail](https://www.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/bogachiel-river-trail.htm) pets are prohibited. No pets are allowed in the Olympic National Park.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 47.88202764

**Longitude:** -124.2755651

### Directions

From Forks, WA travel south on US Highway 101 for 5 miles then turn east on Undi Road, across from Bogachiel State Park, and proceed approximately two miles to the Undi Rd Bypass. Turn left (primitive road, all-wheel or four-wheel drive is best) and proceed approximately 1 mile, stay right and follow road another mile.  At stop sign turn to the left on FS Road 2932 and follow to trailhead.

### Parking

There is ample parking available.
