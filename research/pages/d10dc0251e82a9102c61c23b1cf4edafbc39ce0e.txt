# Lake Tahoe Basin Management Unit

_**Cover photo: Aspen along Angora Road. Forest Service Photo by Jonathan Cook-Fisher.**_

The Lake Tahoe Basin Management Unit Forest Supervisor’s Office in South Lake Tahoe is open Monday through Friday, 8-4:30 p.m. excluding federal holidays. Customers may also call 530-543-2600, during business hours. If you reach the recording, we're helping another customer. Leave a message with your name and phone number and we will get back to you as soon as possible. You may also [**email us**](mailto:sm.fs.paltbmu@usda.gov).

![Burning slash pile surrounded by snowy forest.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/legacy-media/laketahoebasin/Pile%20Burning.jpeg?h=aae27967)

Prescribed fires help reduce overgrown vegetation to protect local communities, infrastructure and natural resources from wildfires.

![Panoramic view of Lake Tahoe from the summit of Mt. Tallac on the Lake Tahoe Basin Management Unit.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/laketahoebasin/image/Lake%20Tahoe%20From%20Mt%20Tallac%20by%20Jonathan%20Cook%20Fisher%202026.jpg?h=827069f2)

Forest Service seeking feedback on proposed fee changes at recreation sites on LTBMU. Recreation fees help provide quality recreation to meet modern expectations and create sustainable recreation. Comments due Sep 29, 2026.

![Cards - Camping](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/legacy-media/laketahoebasin/Cards%20-%20Camping.jpg?h=7a498e30)

Enjoy the convenience of a full service cabin, developed campground or the secluded primitiveness of backcountry and wilderness camping.

![Nevada Beach](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/legacy-media/laketahoebasin/Nevada%20Beach.jpg?h=9c32743e)

Beaches and picnic areas are generally open Memorial Day weekend through mid-October. The open season depends on conditions, snowfall and may vary from year to year.

![South Shore from High Meadows featuring forest, mountains and Lake Tahoe.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/laketahoebasin/image/South%20Shore%20from%20High%20Meadows_2019_DBraun.jpg?h=be2253ff)

Helpful links for current road conditions, weather forecast, Daily Burn Status, prescribed fire activity, live web cams and more!

![Bear Canister Collage](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/legacy-media/laketahoebasin/Bear%20Canister%20Collage.jpg?h=11af8684)

Hard-sided bear canisters are required for overnight visits to Desolation Wilderness and bear-resistant containers are required in all other areas.

![Backcountry hikers navigate through a grassy section of the Tahoe Rim Trail with sky, snowy mountains and forest in the background.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/laketahoebasin/image/Guided-TRTA-Thru-Hike-in-Lake-Tahoe-hiking-in-Desolation-web.jpg?h=9c27b942)

The Tahoe Rim Trail is a 165 mile backcountry trail that circles Lake Tahoe and includes amazing vistas, alpine lakes, meadows and peaks.

![Tallac Hotshot uses hose to cool off downed tree on the Caldor Fire near South Lake Tahoe.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/laketahoebasin/image/Tallac%20Hotshots%20B.jpg?h=35272b31)

Herbicides are but one tool that help support Caldor reforestation objectives. Herbicide use is limited to areas where natural tree regeneration low in order to prevent the conversion of the area from a forested landscape to a shrub chaparral.

![Man rides ebike on trail with forest and mountains in the background.](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/nfs/files/styles/thumbnail/public/r05/laketahoebasin/image/eBike%20Tahoe%20NF.png?h=62142c79)

This highly anticipated project will expand e-bike (Class-1, pedal assist only) use and trail system access and connectivity on the Lake Tahoe Basin Management Unit. Work is expected to begin this summer.
