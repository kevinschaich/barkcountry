# Reef Ride Bike Days

|     |
| --- |
| ![View from atop a hill on a road with a line of red sandstone cliffs in sunlight in the distance.](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/images/Danish-Hill-north.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)<br>### An Event for Bikes and Pedestrians Only on the Scenic Drive<br>#### September 18 and 25, 2027<br>Save the Dates for two Saturdays in September 2027. Come enjoy the Scenic Drive on a bicycle or on foot. **From sunrise to mid-afternoon the Scenic Drive will be** **closed to vehicles** for this special event, allowing folks a chance to explore at a slower pace. Participants will experience stunning landscapes, including sheer sandstone cliffs and the western escarpment of the iconic Waterpocket Fold.<br>The Scenic Drive is a 7.9-mile (12.7 km) paved road that offers breathtaking views of the park's unique geological formations. The bikes-only portion will begin after the campground and provide access to Grand Wash, Capitol Gorge, and Pleasant Creek. Some bicyclists may also choose to ride the dirt roads into Grand Wash and Capitol Gorge, although these may not be suitable for road bikes. <br>During this event, vehicles will still have access to all of State Route 24 and the Fruita area, including the campground and Gifford House. For the safety and convenience of other visitors, vehicles should not line up at the gate in anticipation of the road reopening.<br>Elevation ranges from approximately 5,400 feet (1,646 m) to 5,977 feet (1,822 m) above sea level. <br>Parking for vehicles is available throughout Fruita. Designated parking areas south of the visitor center will be defined for great places to unload bikes and start your ride. <br>Bathrooms are available in the Picnic Area, at the end of Grand Wash Road, the end of the paved Scenic Drive, and the end of Capitol Gorge Road.<br>Safety Tips<br>- **Helmets:** All cyclists are strongly encouraged to wear helmets for safety. All children are required to wear helmets. Utah law on bicycle helmet use applies within the park. Class 3 e-bikes require a helmet for those under 21 and those under 16 are not permitted to operate a class 3 e-bike.<br>- **Hydration:** Bring plenty of water, as there will be few water sources along the route.<br>- **Visibility:** Wear bright clothing and use lights or reflectors to increase visibility.<br>- **Yield:** Cyclists should yield to pedestrians and runners and be courteous to all participants. Cyclists should follow the rules of the road and yield to oncoming bicycles. <br>- **Speed Limit:** Speed limits apply to bicycles. The speed limit on the Scenic Drive is 15 mph from the Blacksmith Shop to just past the turn to loop C of the campground. It is 25 mph for the remainder of the route.<br>- **Weather:** Check [weather](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/weather.htm) conditions before heading out and be prepared for sudden changes.<br>- **E-Bikes:** Ensure your e-bike is fully charged, as there are no charging stations available in the park.<br>- **Vehicle presence:** There may be a few NPS vehicles on the road for safety and maintenance work. Bicyclists should expect vehicle traffic after 2 pm. <br>- **No Cell Service:** There is no cell service in the park. Park staff will be positioned along the route to assist cyclists and hikers in case of emergencies.<br>![Cyclist riding along the scenic drive road through Capitol Reef National Park, with colorful layered cliffs in the distance under a clear blue sky](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/images/Scenic-Drive-Bike-Ride.jpg?maxwidth=1300&maxheight=1300&autorotate=false&format=webp)Cycling is a great way to enjoy Capitol Reef's Scenic Drive. <br>NPS / A. Huston<br>#### Route Options and Details<br>The Scenic Drive connects to several dirt roads; Grand Wash, Capitol Gorge, and Pleasant Creek. The conditions of these dirt roads can vary depending on recent precipitation.<br>Total round trip distances vary depending on where cyclists turn around and if they choose to bike the dirt spur roads. See the various route descriptions, distances, and elevation profile graphs to plan your day.<br>![graph plotting the Scenic Drive's elevation profile with distances across the x-axis and elevation along the y-axis. Elevation ranges from 5,400 feet to just over 5,900 feet.](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/images/Scenic-Drive-elevation-profile.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Elevation profile of Scenic Drive <br>**Scenic Drive**<br>The Scenic Drive is nearly 7 miles in length from the Picnic Area to the end of the pavement at the start of Capitol Gorge. The minimum elevation is 5,418 feet and the maximum is 5,973 feet. There are two major climbs, one cresting at Danish Hill, the other at Slickrock Divide. <br>![graph of the Grand Wash road elevation profile wit distances across the x-axis and elevation along the y-axis. The elevation ranges from 5417 to 5561 feet](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/images/Grand-Wash-elevation-profile_2.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Elevation profile of Grand Wash Road <br>**Grand Wash Road**<br>The Grand Wash road is a dirt road, 1.3 miles in length, leading to the Grand Wash and Cassidy Arch trailheads. The road follows the wash through a tall canyon. Bicyles are not permitted on trails and must remain on the road and parking area. The elevation ranges from 5,561 feet (1,695 m) at the junction with the Scenic Drive to 5,419 feet (1,652 m) at the parking area.<br>![graph of the Capitol Gorge elevation profile. Distances are along the x-axis and elevation along the y-axis. The elevation ranges from 5420 to 5646 feet](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/images/Capitol-Gorge-Road-elevation-profile.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Elevation profile of Capitol Gorge Road <br>**Capitol Gorge Road**<br>The Capitol Gorge Road is a dirt road, 2.4 miles in length, leading to the Capitol Gorge and Golden Throne trailheads. The road follows the canyon bottom as it twist and turns to the parking area and trailheads. Bicycles are not permitted on trails and must remain on the road and parking area. The elevation ranges from 5,646 feet (1,721 m) at the junction with the Scenic Drive to 5,420 feet (1,652 m) at the parking area.<br>![Elevation profile chart of Pleasant Creek Road, showing a steady climb from 5,680 feet to nearly 6,000 feet over 2.75 miles, followed by a gradual descent.](https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/images/Pleasant-Creek-Road-elevation-profile.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Elevation profile of Pleasant Creek Road <br>**Pleasant Creek Road**<br>The Pleasant Creek Road is a dirt road, 3 miles in length, extending from the end of the Scenic Drive to a parking area near Pleasant Creek. Bicycles are not permitted on trails and must remain on the road and parking area. The road climbs from 5,680 feet (1,731 m) to just over 6,000 feet (1,829 m) before descending slightly.<br>### Volunteer to Support this Special Event<br>[National Public Lands Day](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/public-lands-day.htm) is also the nation's largest single-day volunteer effort in service of public lands across the country. Interested in supporting this event as a volunteer? Please reach out to the park's [volunteer coordinator](https://www.nps.gov/common/utilities/sendmail/sendemail.cfm?o=4884C9BEA2C5A0B081B507AAFC058BA3568E088853A0&r=/care/planyourvisit/reef-ride-bike-days.htm) to learn more.<br>### Frequently Asked Questions<br>#### Are scooters, skateboards, roller skates, or similar items allowed?<br>**No.** Only bicycles and pedestrians are permitted on the Scenic Drive during the event.<br>#### Can I bring my dog?<br>**Yes.** Dogs are welcome but must be kept on a leash (6 feet or shorter), stay on the road, and remain within the developed areas.<br>#### Can I ride a horse?<br>**No.** Horseback riding or pack animal use is not allowed on the Scenic Drive or during Reef Ride Days.<br>#### Do I need a helmet?<br>**Yes.** All cyclists are strongly encouraged to wear helmets for safety—Utah law requires helmets for riders under 21. Class 3 e‑bike operators under age 21 must also wear a helmet, and riders under 16 are not permitted to use Class 3 e‑bikes.<br>#### Will there be restrooms along the route?<br>**Yes.** Restrooms are available at the Picnic Area, at the end of Grand Wash Road, the end of the paved Scenic Drive, and at the end of Capitol Gorge Road. <br>#### What about water and hydration?<br>**Bring plenty of water.** Water stations will be available along the route (locations TBD), but riders should still carry their own supply, especially on warm days.<br>#### Are there speed limits?<br>**Yes.** Bicyclists must obey posted speed limits:<br>- **15 mph** between the Blacksmith Shop and just past Loop C of the campground<br>  <br>- **25 mph** for the remainder of the route.<br>  <br>#### Is cell phone reception available?<br>**No.** There is no cell service in the park. Staff will be stationed along the route to assist with emergencies. |
