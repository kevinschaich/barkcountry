# Sunshine Bar Campground

Six lush campsites at Sunshine Bar Campground are located on the Wild and Scenic Elk River, and along the Wild Rivers Coast Scenic Bikeway.

## General Information

### Restrictions

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfires in developed fire pits only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Dogs must be on leash
- OHV/ATV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in the campground. All pets must be kept on a leash, and owners are required to clean up pet waste.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 42.71301

**Longitude:** -124.31001

### Directions

From Port Orford, OR, head north on Highway 101 to Forest Road 5325. Turn right onto Forest Road 5325, and proceed for 19 miles. Campground will be on the left.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

2 vault toilets


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Other Amenities

- Forest Service-approved fire rings. Know current fire restrictions before you go by visiting the Alerts and Notice page: [Alerts](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/alerts)
- There is no on-site garbage disposal. Please pack out what you pack in. For more on Leave No Trace Ethics, please visit [lnt.org](https://lnt.org/).
