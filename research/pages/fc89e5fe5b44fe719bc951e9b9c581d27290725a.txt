# Horse Riding and Camping

The wide open spaces of this high-desert forest are perfect for equestrian activities. Some trailheads and campgrounds offer corrals and horses are allowed on all Forest trails, just be prepared to share with other users

## Horseback Riding

Riders can enjoy a nice rides through the wilderness,  beautiful remote canyons and  in spring and early summer riders can enjoy beautiful wildflowers. Throughout the year hikers have the opportunity to see a variety of wildlife that range from big game such as Rocky Mountain Elk and Mule Meer to a variety of birds including turkey, grouse and water foul such as ducks and geese.

In spring and early summer riders should be prepared for wet and muddy conditions.

The trails are used by hikers and mountain bike riders, please be prepared to share the trail.

## Horse Camping

Please use certified weed free hay, remove any unused hay when leaving the area.
