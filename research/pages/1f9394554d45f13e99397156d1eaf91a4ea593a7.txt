# Be a Shenandoah BARK Ranger

|     |
| --- |
| ![Shenandoah National Park visitors with brown fluffy dog on a leash. Orange and brown tree mountains with natural stone wall is in the background.](https://www.nps.gov/shen/learn/management/images/SHEN_Vistors_with_Dog_8039906_3.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Shenandoah National Park visitors with dog on leash. <br>NPS Photo<br>### Become a B.A.R.K. Ranger!<br>Do you like exploring Shenandoah National Park with your canine friend? If so, stop by the nearest visitor center to pick up a copy of a B.A.R.K. Ranger pledge card and learn more about our B.A.R.K. Ranger program. Complete these steps below and a Park Ranger will issue your pet a B.A.R.K. ranger badge. <br>**1\. Read the B.A.R.K. Ranger principles.**<br>**2\. Learn the dog-friendly trails in the park.**<br>**3\. Take a photo of your pet doing one of these activities:**<br>- Hiking on a dog-friendly trial <br>- Disposing bagged waste<br>- Observing wildlife or a vista from a safe distance<br>- Attending a B.A.R.K. Ranger Program <br>**4\. Take the pledge!**<br>- My B.A.R.K. Ranger and I promise to follow the rules of B.A.R.K. and model good pet behavior while visiting Shenandoah National Park.<br>The B.A.R.K. Ranger program teaches dog owners about the importance of following the Rules of B.A.R.K. while visiting Shenandoah. Dogs completing this short program can be sworn in as B.A.R.K. Rangers.<br>![Bark Ranger cards which are handed out by Rangers at visitor centers.](https://www.nps.gov/shen/learn/management/images/Bark-Ranger-Cards_8040270_2.png?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)You can get your copy of the B.A.R.K. Ranger card at the Visitor Centers. <br>NPS Photo<br>### Rules of B.A.R.K.<br>**B - Bag Your Pet's Waste**<br>Pet waste can introduce bacteria that are harmful to wildlife and may contaminate streams. Always dispose bagged pet waste in the nearest trash receptacle. Do not leave bagged waste on trails. <br>**A - Always Use a Leash**<br>Keep your pet, wildlife, and others safe by always using a 6-foot leash. Other hikers and pets may be uncomfortable being approached by unfamiliar animals.<br>**R - Respect Wildlife**<br>Pets can startle wildlife, altering their behavior. Unleashed pets can put your pet and wildlife at risk. <br>**K - Know Where You Can Go**<br>Plan ahead and know where you can take your pet. Make sure you and your pet areprepared for your visit. |
