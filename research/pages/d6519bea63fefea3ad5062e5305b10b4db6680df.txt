# Sixmile Campground

Sixmile Campground has 4 walk-in campsites and fire rings located along the Wild and Scenic Illinois River defined by clear swimming pools and dramatic rocky shorelines. Six Mile Campground serves as a central hub for several outdoor attractions along the Wild and Scenic Illinois River. Immediately adjacent is the [Six Mile Day Use Area](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/sixmile-day-use), which is highly popular for its deep, emerald-green swimming holes, rock jumping ledges, and riverside picnic pavilions.

Located in the rugged Siskiyou Mountains, the landscape features unique serpentine soils and forested ridges of Jeffrey pine and Douglas fir. This deep river canyon offers stunning vistas, riverside boulders for sunbathing, and is a prime corridor for spotting bald eagles and river otters.

## Reservations

All sites are first come, first serve.

**This site is fully cashless**.Visitors will need to pay on-site using the [**Scan and Pay**](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/rogue-siskiyou/recreation/learn-more-scan-and-pay) feature of the Recreation.gov app. Download the [**Recreation.gov app**](https://www.recreation.gov/mobile-app) and add a valid payment method _before_ heading out on your adventure.

## General Information

### Operational Hours

Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. until 6:00 a.m.

### Restrictions

- Quiet hours between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m.
- Campfires in developed fire pits only
- Fireworks are prohibited
- Dogs must be on leash
- OHV/ATV use prohibited in the campground
- Maximum length of stay: 14 days

### Fee Site and Info

Overnight Use:

Single Site: $15 per night

Additional Vehicle Fee: $5 per vehicle per night

_As of 2026_

Overnight Use is $10 per site

### Pet Information

Dogs are welcome in the campground. All pets must be kept on a leash, and owners are required to clean up pet waste.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 42.29900001

**Longitude:** -123.73000001

### Directions

From **Selma, OR**, off of Highway 199 is the turn for the Illinois River Road (Forest Road 4301).  Take that road approximately 6 miles to the campsites. Just before the Six Mile Day Use Area you will take the spur road to the right to the parking area for the campsites.
