# Pets

|     |
| --- |
| ![Two Dogs on leash](https://www.nps.gov/viis/planyourvisit/images/Meredithsdogs184x184.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Dog Rangers Chewbacca & Princess Leia on leashed patrol with owner Ranger Riester <br>#### Pets<br>Service dogs are allowed in Virgin Islands National Park and leashed dogs are allowed on trails. <br>[Title 36 part 2 section 15](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/36/2.15) of the Code of Federal Regulations provides the rules on dogs. <br>\\* Pets are not allowed on beaches. [36CFR 2.15(a)](https://www.nps.gov/viis/learn/management/36cfr-2-15a.htm)(1)<br>This rule is in place to protect nesting sea turtles, shore birds and our visitors on the beach.<br>\\* Pets must be on leash no longer than 6' while in the Park. [36CFR 2.15 (a)](https://www.nps.gov/viis/learn/management/36cfr-2-15a.htm)(2)<br>\\* Pets can not be left unattended and tied to an object. [36CFR 2.15 (a)](https://www.nps.gov/viis/learn/management/36cfr-2-15a.htm)(3)<br>\\* Pet owners are responsible for removing pet waste appropriately. [36CFR 2.15 (a)](https://www.nps.gov/viis/learn/management/36cfr-2-15a.htm)(5)<br>To report any dog issues call 1-866-995-8467.<br>![Service Dog](https://www.nps.gov/viis/planyourvisit/images/servicedog_2_3.jpg?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)<br>#### [Service Animals](https://www.nps.gov/viis/learn/management/36cfr-2-15a.htm\#CP_JUMP_5494266)<br>A service animal is one that is specifically trained to aid a person with a disability.<br>\\* Service animals are welcome in the park but must follow many of the pet rules.<br>\\* Service animals must be on a leash and under control at all times.<br>\\* Pet owners are responsible for removing pet waste appropriately. 36CFR 2.15 (a)(5)<br>If you have any other questions about pets in the park please feel free to call the park 340-776-6201 or find a Ranger and ask them!! |
