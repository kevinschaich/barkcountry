# Welcome to the Tongass National Forest

The Tongass is the nation’s largest national forest and covers most of Southeast Alaska, surrounding the famous Inside Passage and offers unique chances to view eagles, bears, spawning salmon, and the breath-taking vistas of “wild” Alaska.

[Mendenhall Glacier Visitor Center](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r10/tongass/offices/mendenhall-glacier-visitor-center) [SE Alaska Discovery Center](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r10/tongass/offices/southeast-alaska-discovery-center-0) [Anan Wildlife Observatory Site](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r10/tongass/recreation/anan-wildlife-observatory-site)

[Fish Creek Bear Viewing Area](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r10/tongass/recreation/tours/fish-creek-bear-viewing-area) [Pack Creek Bear Viewing Site](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r10/tongass/recreation/pack-creek-wildlife-viewing-site) [Glaciers](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r10/tongass/natural-resources/glaciers)
