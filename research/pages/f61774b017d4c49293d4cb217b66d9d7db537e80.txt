# New Wisdom and Wise River District Ranger

**Release Date:** March 16, 2023

## New Wisdom and Wise River District Ranger

**Dillon, Mont., March 16th, 2023**— The Beaverhead-Deerlodge National Forest (BDNF) is pleased to announce the selection of Kristen Thompson as the new District Ranger for the Wisdom and Wise River Districts on the BDNF.

Thompson began her Forest Service career in 1996, on the Rogue River National Forest, as a Forestry Technician. She thought she had the best job in the world because she got to be outside in the woods all day, marking and cruising timber in southwest Oregon. Between her start on the Rogue River National Forest and her position as a Ranger with the BDNF, Thompson’s experience ranges from working in the Bureau of Land Management (BLM) as a state program lead, Forest Service fuels reduction projects, and several ‘details’ where she had the opportunity to temporarily fill positions such as acting Deputy District Manager, acting Field Manager and acting Associate District Manager for the BLM.

“I am honored to be selected as the Wisdom-Wise River District Ranger and I look forward to joining the Beaverhead-Deerlodge National Forest team,” said Thompson.

Thompson and her husband, Mike Rowberry, have been visiting and recreating in Montana for several years and are avid outdoors enthusiasts, who also enjoy music, cooking, and visiting family. They are excited about moving to the Big Hole Valley with their three dogs. Her official start date is March 24th, 2023.
