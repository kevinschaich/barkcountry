### Protected area. Enriching nature experiences.

Hiking is definitely the best way to discover Parc national du Mont-Orford. It's the perfect way to get access to numerous viewpoints, which are said to be among the most spectacular of the Eastern Townships region. In the fall, the forest presents a show featuring the flamboyant colours of the sugar maples. The park is notably home to white-tailed deer and Great Blue Heron, which you can observe in their natural habitat. There is also a large variety of amphibian and reptile species. Beside two beaches, vacationers have a wide range of fun-filled activities to choose from. Come fall, colourful sugar maples treat hikers to a blazing show. Come winter, the park is a destination of choice for cross-country skiing and snowshoeing fans.

Hiking trails form a network of over 80 km of trails laid out to offer you memorable moments of discovery. They are classified from easy to difficult.

You can also go backpacking on a portion of the trail network called [Les Sentiers de l'Estrie This link will open in a new window.](http://www.lessentiersdelestrie.qc.ca/), which crosses the park from north to south. The star attraction is the spectacular Des Crêtes trail.

Activities at Mont-Orford Mountain are managed by the Corporation ski & golf Mont-Orford. Access to the summit by foot is free. To access it by lift, check the opening hours and rates [online](https://montorford.com/en-ca/evenements/la-flambee-des-couleurs/hybridlift/).

Period: April 1 to November 30 (depending on freezing and thawing conditions)

Note that dogs are allowed on certain trails, from May 1 to November 30. See the [map (in French only)](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/docs/pq/mor/mor-carte-chiens.pdf) for details.

#### La Cavalière trail

You no longer need your car to travel from Lac Stukely sector to Lac Fraser sector! From now on, you can cycle between Le Cerisier Discovery and Visitors Centre and Opeongo Visitors Centre. The 10.6 km (one way) easy-level trail will enchant you with the beauty of the forest it crosses.

#### Des Trois-Étangs path

The Des Trois-Étangs is an easy-level 5.5 km bike path, to offer to the whole family at long last! The circuit begins at Le Cerisier Visitors Centre. Along the way, you can admire several types of forests and various lookouts at l’Ours, La Cuvette and Martin ponds.

#### Route verte

A section of La Route verte, this Quebec cycling path, crosses the park from east to west. It's over 10.4 km long and presents a high level of difficulty. [For more information This link will open in a new window.](https://www.routeverte.com/en/discover/)

Please note that La Castorie bike path (No. 14) is closed until further notice due to the condition of the pavement.

#### Bike rental

Bike rentals are available at Le Cerisier Discovery and Visitors Centre as well as at Lac Fraser Information Kiosk, depending on the opening hours of the rental centres. [Fatbikes](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/mor/index.dot#Iteme9af7cc5-0921-483e-b72d-8b1f309cc2a8) are available for rental at Le Cerisier Discovery and Visitors Centre, according to opening hours. [Rates](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/mor/tarifs.dot#cat_7)

**Important!** Electric bicycles are prohibited while electric pedal-assist bicycles are allowed on the trails. Please also note that electric scooters and unicycles are prohibited on the cycling paths.

### [![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/mor/accordeon/mor_escalade.jpg)Rock Climbing](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/mor/index.dot?language_id=1\#Item3123625)

![](https://www.sepaq.com/resources/images/pq/mor/accordeon/mor_escalade.jpg)

The Pic aux Corbeaux rock climbing cliff is for intermediate and expert climbers (some routes are also accessible to beginners. A 1,100-m approach path leads to the climbing area. No supervision on the site. **Dogs are not allowed.**

#### Reopening of the Pic aux Corbeaux climbing area

Thanks to the collaboration of users of the Pic aux Corbeaux sector following its closure, the nesting of the peregrine falcon couple was a success. The family left the nest. The Pic aux Corbeaux sector has been reopened since July 13, 2026. Hikers and climbers can resume their activities. Thank you for your cooperation and understanding!

#### Types of Climbing

Bouldering: Boulder climbing is allowed along the access trail to the cliff.

Cliff: Sports routes equipped with fixed anchors (bolted anchors and plates). Some 5.5 to 5.7 routes. A large variety of 5.9 to 5.12 b routes (difficult). Some mixed routes, with manual chocks and anchors. Sector of extremely difficult overhangs: for experts only. Some 5.13 b routes. In certain places calculate more than one length of rope. Highest route: 35 m.

#### General Recommendations

- Have training from an accredited organization specialized in the teaching and mastery of rock climbing techniques.
- Never climb alone.
- Bring your own specialized safety equipment for this sport. This equipment must be in good condition (rope, helmet, harness, climbing shoes, carabiners, descenders, crash pads, etc.).
- Bring a first aid kit.
- Bring a cell phone.
- Be familiar with the emergency procedures displayed in the parking lot and at the base of the cliff.
- Be ready to ensure your own evacuation, without outside assistance from the park and/or local emergency services.

#### Access

Parking lot off Route 112 as you head east. Exit 115, Highway 10.

### [![act_camping.gif](https://www.sepaq.com/contentAsset/raw-data/7738/fileAsset?language_id=1)Camping](https://www.sepaq.com/pq/mor/index.dot?language_id=1\#Item13021)

![camping_mor.jpg](https://www.sepaq.com/contentAsset/raw-data/16999/fileAsset?language_id=1)

Whether you are looking for the comfort of a serviced or semi-serviced campground or the charm of rustic campsites, a camping stay in the park is your guarantee of peaceful nights.

#### Lac-Stukely sector

This family sector features 273 serviced and semi-serviced campsites, including semi-shaded sites for tents and recreational vehicles. Near the camping area, you’ll find a beach, a watercraft rental centre, hiking trails, children’s playgrounds, picnic areas, and a small convenience store.

#### Le Vallonnier campground

14 tent platforms for rustic camping. This campground is accessible **only by foot** (4 km from the Lac-Stukely Visitors Centre and 2.5 km from the Grande Halte).

**Please note that the trails are in the forest and are not adapted for carts or bicycles.**

#### Lac-Fraser sector

This sector offers 148 serviced and semi-serviced campsites that can accommodate tents and recreational vehicles in a deciduous forest, including 50 sites with electricity. Near the camping area, you’ll find children’s playgrounds, picnic areas, and a watercraft rental centre. Lac Fraser beach is accessible by foot or bike (20 to 45 minutes from your campsite) or by car. There are hiking trails in the Lac Stukely sector (approximately 15 minutes by car).

Period: mid-May to mid-October (depending on the sector, subject to change without notice)

## Park access authorization

There is a limit to the number of visitors that can enter a national park on any given day. It is therefore **strongly recommended** that you purchase your right of access online before a visit or that you acquire an annual pass. This guarantees you access to the park, will ensure a smoother arrival at the site and avoid unnecessary travel when the maximum capacity is reached. It can be purchased up to 30 days before a visit. You may be denied access to a sector, a trail, or an activity if a parking lot is full or if the maximum capacity has been reached.

[Daily Access](https://www.sepaq.com/en/reservation/purchase/daily-access) [Annual Card](https://www.sepaq.com/en/reservation/national-parks/annual-card)

## Important

### Don't move firewood

A single piece of firewood can destroy millions of trees. Invasive insects and diseases can hide inside it. Buy it locally. Burn it on site. [Find out more This link will open in a new window.](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/plant-health/forestry/hazards-moving-firewood)
