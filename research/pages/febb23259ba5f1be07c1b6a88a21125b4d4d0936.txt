# Old Rim Trailhead

Old Rim Trailhead located on Forest Road 105 just north of Crook Campground. The Trailhead has parking for up to 3 vehicles in a roadside pull-out.

## General Information

No water or restroom is available.

### Operational Hours

Generally open May - November

### Fee Site and Info

### Pet Information

Dogs must be kept on a leash.

### Contact Information

**Contact Name:** Black Mesa Ranger District

**Contact Phone:** [(928)535-7300](tel:(928)535-7300)

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 34.31988889

**Longitude:** -110.9422111

### Parking

Parking for up to three vehicles in roadside pull-out on Forest Road 105 just north of Crook Campground.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are allowed at this site.

Horses and pack animals are allowed to travel through the area on the General Crook Trail but are not allowed in the nearby developed campgrounds or day use areas around Woods Canyon Lake.
