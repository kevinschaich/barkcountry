# Pets

| ![People hiking to a rocky overlook area. A person has a leashed dog in the foreground.](https://www.nps.gov/blca/planyourvisit/images/IMG_9101.JPG?maxwidth=650&autorotate=false)Leashed dogs are permitted on the Rim Rock Trail, Chasm View Nature Trail, picnic areas, campgrounds, parking lots, and paths to overlooks. <br>NPS Photo<br>## Visiting with Pets<br>Bringing a pet to Black Canyon of the Gunnison may limit your activities. Protect your pet, park wildlife, and other visitors by following these regulations:<br>- Leashed pets are permitted in picnic areas, campgrounds, and parking lots, on paths to overlooks, and along roads currently open to vehicles.<br>- Leashed pets are **only permitted** on the [Rim Rock Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-the-rim-rock-trail.htm) and on the [North Rim Chasm View Nature Trail](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/chasm-view-trail.htm).<br>- Pets **are prohibited** on all other hiking trails, inner canyon routes, within the wilderness, or on snowshoe/cross country skiing trails.<br>- Leashes must be a maximum of six feet (1.8 m) in length.<br>- Pet owners must bag and dispose of pet waste immediately. Do not leave bags along the trail for pick up later.<br>- Owners are responsible for their pet's behavior at all times. Fines may be given for pets that disturb wildlife or other visitors.<br>- Pets may not be left unattended or tied to an object.<br>- Pets may remain in vehicles for short periods of time (i.e. overlooks), but we recommend someone staying with them to ensure well being. Pets overheating in vehicles, even with windows cracked, is possible.<br>## Boarding Services<br>There are no kennels in the park. Boarding services can be found in the nearby communities of Montrose, Olathe, and Gunnison.<br>## Service Animals<br>Please visit the National Park Service's [webpage](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/accessibility/service-animals.htm) for detailed information on bringing service animals to NPS sites. |
