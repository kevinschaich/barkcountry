# Green Point Campground

## Gros Morne National Park

**Operating Season (open year round):** September 28, 2026 to mid- May, 2027 by self-registration

Green Point is a coastal escape, whether on your own, with a furry friend, or a car load of your favourite people. The campground offers spectacular ocean views with modern comforts and convenience. With direct view and access to the cobble beach, explore the banks of the Coastal Trail, and take in the sunsets in the stunted, wind-swept trees locally called tuckamore. Enjoy one of the best stargazing horizons in the Park, and then let the rhythmic sounds of the ocean lull you to sleep.

The campground is located near a traditional seasonal fish staging area, Green Point geological site, and offers access to the Coastal Trail. A short drive south brings you to the town of Rocky Harbour, Lobster Cove Head Lighthouse and a variety of trails. Head a little north to arrive at Western Brook Pond trail and boat tour.

#### Off-Season Camping

Green Point Campground is open year-round with reduced services in the off-season. From the October Thanksgiving weekend until the May long weekend, Green Point campground is un-serviced but open for camping by self-registration. During this time, the water is shut off, a pit toilet is available, the kitchen shelter is open for use, and the fee for camping is reduced. [See park fees here.](https://www.pc.gc.ca/en/pn-np/nl/grosmorne/visit/tarifs-fees)

#### Services and facilities

- Forested and ocean view campsites
- Serviced and unserviced sites
- oTENTik
- Kitchen shelter, flush toilets, shower facilities
- Fire pits on each site
- Wifi
- Kiosk
- Dumping station
- Off-leash dog park
- Access to hiking and coast
