# Clearwater Crossing Campground

Current Conditions

Open year round, snow dependent.

Off the beaten path, this is a small campground with stock facilities and public corrals. The nearby trailhead is primarily used for horseback riding and hiking into the Great Burn area.

View the site through the [interactive map](https://arcg.is/1jmbSu1).

## General Information

Each site has a picnic table and a fire ring. Rod & Connie French Conservation Camp is 1 mile from the campground.

### Seasons of Use

Open year-round, snow dependent.

### Restrictions

Please visit the [Forest's Alerts page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/lolo/alerts) for information on restrictions, in particular please review the [Food/Wildlife Attractant Storage Order](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r01/alerts/r1-2023-02-ncde-food-and-wildlife-attractant-storage-order) to help reduce the likelihood of human-to-bear conflicts.

### Fee Site and Info

No fees are required for this site

### Pet Information

Dogs must be under control at all times.

## Getting There

### Latitude / Longitude

**Latitude:** 46.909755

**Longitude:** -114.802018

### Directions

37 miles west of Missoula on Interstate 90. Take the Fish Creek Exit #66. Follow Fish Creek Road #343 south to West Fork Fish Creek Road #7750. Follow Road #7750 to the end. Follow the signs to Rod & Connie French Conservation Camp and go 1 mile beyond this camp to the campground.

### Parking

There is separate parking for horse trailers outside of the campground.

## Facility and Amenity Information

### Restrooms

Vault toilets


### Water

Potable water is not available at this site.

### Picnic Tables

Picnic tables are available at this site.

### Horse/Pack Animal Information

Horse/Pack animals are allowed at this site.

Public corrals are available near the horse trailer parking area. No stock allowed in the campground.
